# Physical scope and independent need

The proposed method supplies one actually occupied, exclusively designated campsite pitch-night for visitor-owned tent camping. Actual pitch area/time identity, surface and electrical/water/drainage configuration and the supplied common-facility responsibility are required. Grass, hardstanding and hookups are actual configuration qualifiers, not reasons for separate canonical methods. RV/rooftop-vehicle target equivalence, operator-provided overnight shelters, permanent residence/storage-only, and combined overnight-food-recreation camp packages are outside this narrower target.

UNSD CPC3 explanatory notes30June2025 printed355 classify tent/RV-space supply in63120. The actual method is narrower than this whole leaf. Current first-party Camping and Caravanning Club pitch-type/Sandringham pages and Ruby Country Camping own-tent page support pitch provision, customer gear distinction and real common facilities; provider dimensions, prices, guest limits, dates and equipment are not inventory defaults.

The complete440-record material index screen found no campsite-pitch method. Three current indoor private/whole lodging methods differ in supplied shelter and physical reference; the old camp-site-services leaf is only an empty legacy scaffold. Actual ground/space-time conservation, land/sitework/maintenance responsibility and shared sanitation versus visitor gear justify this semantic product method.

Full siteworks/common-buildings/equipment/reusables life, necessary idle/weather closure/grass-restoration resources, all real competing consumers and actual occupiedN attribution remain inside the responsible supply system. Actual land occupation, historical transformation and capital/upstream land interfaces require an exclusive boundary/stage matrix, native area/time/category evidence and no default land category. Missing quantities/UUIDs/ranges are explicit collection gaps; no checker result proves actual product identity or full-chain readiness.
