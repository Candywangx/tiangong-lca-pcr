The proposed semantic record covers actual occupied indivisible private hotel guestrooms with private bathrooms, a daily housekeeping regime and on-site guest response, using completed external or central room-linen care. CPC 3.0 63111 explicitly requires daily housekeeping; 63112 is less-than-daily and 63113 timeshare is excluded. Actual guest-declined housekeeping does not erase accommodation physically supplied. One Item(s) denotes one actual occupied room-night with actual cycle, guests and service qualifiers, not an order, guest-night or fixed 24-hour duration. This narrower route does not represent every 63111 establishment.

No reusable material hotel/linen methodology was found in the complete material index screen; relevant legacy leaves contain empty title-only scaffolds. This record supplies the distinct lodging responsibility-pool, standby/N0 closure, external laundry coverage and building/fitout/equipment/textile full-life method. HCMI historical operating-carbon boundaries and 2026 PEF consultation-introduction context are not adopted as full LCA standards or quantitative defaults. Concrete flow identities, actual property quantities and complete provider/asset/fate coverage remain explicit gaps; this draft does not accept a mapping or establish publication readiness.
