## 1. Product category and scope
Mechanically nailed fiberglass-reinforced three-tab asphalt shingle pitched-roof covering on a received dry accepted timber deck, including the actual selected underlay/leak barriers, edge/starter/field/hip-ridge details, flashing, conditional sealing/appearance finishing and owned ventilation accessories.
This is a bounded installation/construction service, distinct from manufacture of materials or the complete upstream building/fence/roof structure. CPC association is a proposed narrower relation subject to final independent review.

## 4. Process map and handoffs
- survey_receive: Survey accepted deck and receive roofing components. Approved actual product/design, dry compatible deck and nail holding, slope/roof geometry/ventilation/junction and access ownership; no substrate repair or tearoff.
- underlay_edges: Install underlays, leak barriers and edge drainage details. Actual selected underlay layers and leak barrier per product/slope/design, drip edges and compatible fastening/sealing; count all laps/cut losses.
- shingle_details: Lay field/starter/caps, flash junctions and integrate owned vents. Receive underlaid roof; nail actual fiberglass3tabshingles, actual cuts, starter/caps, wall/chimney/valley flashing, seals and owned vent component interfaces.
- finish_accept: Finish specified accessories, inspect sealing and accept. Actual specified accessory touchup/paint, final debris removal, verification of required seals/drainage/vent and junction completion; delays/weather/protection loads recorded through actual accepted gate.
Link same physical handoff by unique lot/event/surface/material state and period; receiving quantity reconciles actual sending quantity after upstream losses/returns/WIP. Rejected assemblies are removed/reworked, retained unaccepted WIP, documented usable exits or waste as actually evidenced. Rework event returns to producing/repair node, repeats actual inputs/energy/time; final unique accepted geometry counted once.

## 7. Allocation, stock, rework and period attribution
For each material and node, G=opening+receipts−closing_intact unissued stock. G is physical gross draw including usable U/X boundary exits. U and X are positive own-node return/external-transfer rows, mutually exclusive with closing/waste/retained/WIP and each other. Net consumed G−U−X is diagnostic/upstream burden reconciliation only; never create another negative input or credit. Unused material exit carries only its matching original unused material upstream burden; construction, rejected work, curing/waiting, plant, idle and cleanup burdens stay with lot. WIP and cross-node transfers preserve actual state and timestamp lineage. Complete acceptance-period lot attribution avoids arbitrary annualization; replacements/repeated events all remain actual lot costs. Shared infrastructure/service allocations enumerate all consumers and periods, remove direct measured portions once before any positive-weight residual assignment, sum to real master/bill. Unknown or zero-denominator residual attribution blocks resolution. No coproduced service arises from internal handoff or usable raw returns. Passive waiting is a timestamp/state condition, not invented operation; actual protection/heating/ventilation loads remain owned.

## 9. Data quality and gap treatment
Use site/lot-current primary measurements and actual component/manufacturer/design acceptance. Foreground raw events retain calibration, methods, intervals, units, material composition/state, custody, installed/removed/reworked/WIP and acceptance. Scope and source relevance are explicit; manufacturer examples never become universal factors. Each Range is a broad diagnostic reasoned_estimate pending empirical refinement, not permitted performance band, design limit, quantity default or proof of external identity. Report source uncertainties, unresolved services/support/substances and incomplete stock/water/meter closures. Candidate cannot become publication-ready with unresolved actual reference service or material/elementary identities; unknown species umbrellas require expansion.
