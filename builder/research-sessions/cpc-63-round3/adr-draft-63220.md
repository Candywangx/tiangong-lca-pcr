# Classification and physical methodology proposal

CPC3.0 printed page356 defines63220 as room or unit accommodation for workers in workers hostels or camps, usually short-term or seasonal. Group632's longer-period description does not establish continuous retained rooms for all workers. The official source retained at `builder/research-sessions/cpc-54-round6/sources/root-cpc3-explanatory-notes-30Jun2025.pdf` and the actual root extraction `builder/research-sessions/cpc-63-round3/root/cpc3-source-page-356.txt` were read.

Reuse `pcr.assigned-single-room-residential-place-days`, authored once by the63210 author, for the physically compatible worker-residence subset. Do not create a second canonical record on worker, employment, payer or tenancy identity. Root approved this shared scope; this per-code draft does not itself accept a mapping edge.

One counted output requires an actually supplied exclusive furnished single residential room for the declared sourced local place-day. Actual room/recipient assignment, access and ready condition, effective start/end/timezone/UTC-offset/DST, real service duties and fulfillment must agree. Neither a paid booking, roster, lease, storage of personal items, nominal room capacity nor a permanent-room marketing label proves service. No universal24hours, stay duration or occupancy ratio is implied. Daytime sleep for night-shift residents is an actual use qualifier, not automatically an absent night; shared/hot-bedded or sub-day turnover supply is not equivalent to this exclusive full-room product.

# Current first-party evidence and applicability

[Ski Louise worker housing](https://www.skilouise.com/contact-us/careers-employment/) describes housing allocation and confirmation, tenancy, and the actual Charleston Residence configuration: lockable single bedrooms furnished with bed and wardrobe, with shared kitchen, bathroom, laundry and cooking equipment. The same page separately describes shared-bunk and other configurations. Those other configurations do not automatically fit.

[Torbay hospital residences](https://www.torbayandsouthdevon.nhs.uk/about-us/working-with-us/employee-benefits/accommodation/) provide staff single rooms with furnishings, linen and lockable bedroom doors, sharing kitchen, lounge/dining and bathroom. The separately described self-contained studio is a different physical unit. [GOSH key-worker housing](https://www.gosh.nhs.uk/working-here/working-us/finding-somewhere-live/staff-accommodation/key-worker-housing/) corroborates actual cluster-flat room versus studio/flat distinctions; referral or eligibility alone is not fulfillment. These sources support physical operations and data questions, not measured inventory values, default lifetime or an assumption about every absent day.

[Stayover's current accommodation page](https://stayover.com.au/accommodation) distinguishes permanent rooms from ordinary departures; it also identifies night-shift areas, room servicing, laundry and included onsite food preparation. Its current [April2026 Chinchilla resident code](https://stayover.com.au/sites/stayover/files/2026-04/stayover-chinchilla_code-of-conduct_update-apr-26_a4_web-lr_final.pdf), linked by the operator's current code-of-conduct page, requires vacation of the room, key return and sign-out for R&R absences. Continuing charges without checkout do not prove physically delivered retained room-days. A [Civeo employee testimony](https://civeo.com/careers/apac-careers-utility-positions/) describes an actual permanent-room arrangement, while its [specific chef recruitment FAQ](https://civeo.com/careers/australia/apac-careers-campaign-250808-faq/) describes private rooms, meals and storage while home. Neither proves universal customer retention or full readiness/access during off-roster periods.

Continuous assigned absence may count only where actual room exclusivity, access and ready service continue. Vacated, returned-key, released, inaccessible, closed or repurposed periods do not count because charges continue. Actual room moves retain all segments under one continuing service ID; real released-room visitors, other residents, staff and day-users remain actual consumers in complete physical attribution.

An included food or transport duty cannot be silently removed to make an example fit. Full actual completed provider coverage or actual missing-stage integration is required. Genuine onsite kitchen production requires a reviewed actual route/module expansion; a lodging-only current method does not automatically cover the Stayover package. Employee travel or meals supplied under employment benefits are not automatically part of the room product: actual actor, package and responsible physical scope must decide and avoid both omissions and double counting.

# Reuse screen and proxy relation

The current443-record material catalog was fully parsed and screened by identity, path and title. All six existing material accommodation methods were compared through actual English scope, identity, reference and measurement sections. Hotel occupied room-nights require daily housekeeping; visitor apartments require private kitchen/bath and occupied visitor unit-nights; lock-off villas require dynamic parent/child combined space; visitor dormitory beds are independently supplied shared berths; tent pitches supply space rather than furnished rooms; recreational camp participant-sessions require an actual overnight-food-activity programme bundle. None expresses this continuous residential single-room supply. Building construction/retrofit methods concern capital outputs rather than accommodation. Old CPC-derived student/worker placeholders are not material methods. The shared63210 record is therefore reused instead of duplicating it.

The proposed edge is `proxy`:63220 includes worker rooms and units with varied physical and turnover regimes, while the shared physical method is restricted to eligible single-room residential delivery and crosses student/worker beneficiary categories. This is partial overlap, not exact coverage or a simple subset relationship for the entire purpose-neutral method. It does not claim all63220 worker hostels/camps, all tenancy periods, shared beds, whole dwellings, paid absent intervals or full catering bundles.
