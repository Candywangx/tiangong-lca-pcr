# CPC 3.0 63391 mapping decision

## Source leaf and target

- Official label: Event catering services. UNSD CPC Version 3.0 explanatory notes, 30 June 2025, printed pages 356–358: [official PDF](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf).
- Canonical PCR: `pcr.offsite-prepared-onsite-finished-plated-meal-service-occasions`.
- Proposed relation: `proxy`. The specific-event contractual/customer-location classification and this purpose-neutral physical method overlap. The method is bounded to actual offsite preparation, transport, onsite finishing and complete plated meal supply; the classification includes other routes and physically identical ongoing-contract services are not excluded from the methodology merely by contract label.

## Semantic boundary

The PCR addresses one actual recipient's fulfilled complete plated meal-service occasion of one evidenced equivalent menu/composition/portion/service-version. It requires actual component/course supply, offered/declined/partial/accepted/reversed states, recipient/place, physical gate and time. Contract planned covers, payment, capacity and swallowed food cannot replace actual fulfillment. No universal course count, nutrition, portion, temperature, duration or yield is imposed.

Two actual nodes own offsite ingredient composition/preparation, protective packing, dispatch and attributed transport; then onsite receipt, finishing/portioning/plating/holding/supply/clear/return. Cold assembly is permitted where actually performed; not every component is heated or mixed. The same selected receipt-gate native state/quantity/transferID appears on both sides. Dispatch versus receipt differences retain actual transport/holding losses. Dirty reusables and usable food have real old-state reverse transfers, actual washing/rework/storage and source/receiver responsibility. Internal receipts/returns are not new external manufacturing inputs or co-products.

Pure onsite preparation, dropoff-only, beverage-only, grocery/wholesale and whole event/lodging/entertainment products are outside the selected product. Buffet/shared-component food requires actual complete plated-equivalence recipient/portion/menu/handoff evidence; label alone neither establishes equivalence nor a new PCR. CPC63392 and63393 ongoing supply exclusions remain classification context; the physical method is purpose-neutral, so this edge must not claim taxonomy-pure narrower or exact coverage.

Current material catalog screening covered all445 entries. Actual nearby camp kitchen methodology informs questions, but its final reference is an overnight-food-recreation participant-session and cannot be relabelled as a standalone meal. The approved stationary-on-site prepared/served meal method shares the recipient meal questions but lacks this selected offsite prepared-food transport/receipt and reverse-return route. The new method is justified by those physical handoffs and responsibility/measurement differences, not an event label or service style.

Primary actual operator pages support the qualitative route: [Krisp Events](https://www.krispevents.com/) describes own commissary preparation, delivery, onsite finishing, plated menus, own staff/rentals and setup/breakdown; [The Box](https://theboxeventsandcatering.co.uk/private-dining/) describes preparation in its kitchen, transport, onsite finishing/service and cleanup; [Silver Feast](https://silverfeastcatering.ca/) describes protected component/cold-chain/equipment dispatch, onsite ovens/hot boxes/plating, service and rental pickup/cleanup. [Sodexo's 2024 central-kitchen announcement](https://id.sodexo.com/newsroom/2024/sodexo-central-kitchen) supports the limit that central kitchens serve both events and daily employees; it is not evidence for an actual current client quantity, temperature or yield.
