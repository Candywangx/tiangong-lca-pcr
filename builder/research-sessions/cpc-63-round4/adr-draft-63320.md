# Draft classification decision

CPC3.0 63320 is meal serving with limited services: seating is provided without waiter service. The proposed target is `pcr.stationary-on-site-prepared-and-served-meal-services`, shared with qualifying CPC63310 cases. Root approved this shared semantic identity for authoring; this draft does not accept a positive mapping. Final independent review, root acceptance and the exact acceptance evidence remain required.

# Product, reference and physical scope

The bounded product is an actually fulfilled meal-service occasion of one declared compatible menu, supplied quantity, service version and physical configuration, prepared and served at a stationary site. Actual food receipt, storage, preparation/cooking, holding, portioning, serving, dining support, cleaning and dishwashing are real duties. Full-waiter, counter and self-service configurations can use one method when that complete physical scope fits; they retain their actual differences in actors, quantities, timing and service obligations. A worker serving hot food at a self-service counter is not proof of full waiter service. Customer carrying or clearing a tray does not erase operator washing, waste handling or dining duties.

The same actual stationary kitchen may fulfill a compatible declared service at a table, counter, window, curbside or drive-through. Actual packing, dining/warewashing duties and handover conditions remain explicit. Last-mile delivery enters only when declared actual responsibility/gate and completed native provider/route coverage establish it. App table delivery does not prove full waiter service; no-seating configuration alone does not force new methodology. This cross-mode scope does not accept additional classification edges.

A final reference requires actual fulfilled menu/components/quantities and service-occasion handover records, including any actual beverage/refill obligations and reversals. Order, payment, checkout, table capacity, planned covers or eaten-food mass alone does not establish delivery. Different menu, composition, portions, duration and package obligations cannot be treated as interchangeable meals without reviewed physical equivalence. Native food quantities, stocks, state and all fates remain independent from the counted service reference. Partial or failed duties retain their resources and real disposition, without a fictional complete meal.

The present route excludes off-site central preparation with transport and on-site finishing, mobile dining cars/vehicles, industrial packaged-food manufacture and whole residential accommodation/recreation packages unless separately reviewed physical scope expansion is completed. Event or ongoing-contract labels alone do not create another method; a genuinely different preparation/transport/finishing handoff can. A concession canteen can have a physically compatible on-site route while remaining classified outside63320. This mapping does not claim63393 or63399 cases as63320.

Actual supplied-state food and losses, wet/dry and preparation/cooking/holding/served/refill/remaining/plate/preparation/unserved views, packaging and real reusable/tableware life must be quantified without duplicate whole/component views. Actual upstream supply, full site/building/land/fitout/kitchen/dining/equipment/textile/reusable manufacture-install-maintain-replace-end-of-life responsibility across all users and periods cannot be replaced by an operations-only picture. One complete real provider scope or actually completed exclusive missing physical stages is required. Necessary idle/failure/cancelled/zero-output duties retain actual owner, source/carry/receiving and final resolution. Native land area/time and from/to events are separate from counted asset equivalents.

Every independent numeric operand must have its own unit, basis, method, state, sample time and source. Actual stock draw and unused-return methods are exclusive alternatives; used recovery is an old-state transfer with no automatic new-manufacture deduction or avoided-production credit. Actual contained wastewater is waste; measured water vapour to air is an elementary flow; individual pollutant substance/medium requires evidence. An unexplained residual is neither an emission estimate nor zero.

# Why proxy and why shared

The semantic method crosses the classification boundary between physically qualifying full-waiter63310 and limited/self-service63320, while it is bounded to the reviewed stationary on-site preparation and service route. CPC63320 can include other physical preparation/provision configurations. Neither scope contains the other in full: `proxy` describes the supported intersection. Seating, waiter status, brand, contract, customer purpose and classification leaf alone do not justify independent canonical identity.

The current445-entry material catalog was screened before authoring, with actual near-neighbour product/reference/measurement/boundary reads. The existing residential multi-activity camp method has a real kitchen node but its final product is the complete lodging-food-recreation participant-session; it cannot supply a standalone meal methodology unchanged. Hotel and resident-room methods have accommodation references; food and kitchen-equipment wholesale records have commercial consignment references and exclude preparation/use. No current whole method covers this final standalone on-site meal service. One shared canonical method is therefore proposed, not a separate63320 method.
