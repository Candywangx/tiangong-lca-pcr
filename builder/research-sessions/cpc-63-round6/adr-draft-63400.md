# CPC 63400 standalone hot espresso drink service mapping

Official CPC 3.0 explanatory notes, printed pages 356–358, distinguish beverage service without prepared foods from meal services with related beverages. Subclass 63400 includes alcoholic and nonalcoholic beverage serving and excludes vending and meal-related beverage packages.

The proposed PCR covers actual stationary onsite preparation, extraction, formulation and hot drink presentation/recipient handoff, with complete actual supporting duties. Its reference is one physically fulfilled equivalent declared recipe/portion/presentation/service-version drink service. It excludes meal-related beverages already inside a meal reference, cold/tea/filter/vending/sealed retail/alcoholic products, moving/offsite routes and whole lodging/entertainment/passenger packages. The mapping is narrower than the complete subclass.

Current Costa coffee, allergen and cup pages and Starbucks espresso descriptions establish qualitative actual espresso extraction, additions/steaming and recipient presentation. Manufacturer route evidence corroborates technical questions without establishing target equipment or inventory. Recipes, quantities, yields, temperatures, factors and lifetimes require actual records. No brand-specific product identity is created.

The full current material catalog was screened. Stationary meal methodology expressly excludes beverage-only; offsite plated and operator-loaded meal methods have different products and gates; camp/rail methods are whole bundles; sealed coffee wholesale is B2B bean handling and excludes brewing. A separate method addresses the actual standalone drink gate, coffee extraction/spent grounds, water/steam/milk stocks and full service responsibility.

This draft does not accept a mapping. Root review must verify both languages, native inventory and all selected module obligations, explicit identity/data gaps, projection, target checks and final repository validation before any accepted edge is added. Scientific review and final concrete flow identity verification remain separate from this candidate-methodology mapping decision.
