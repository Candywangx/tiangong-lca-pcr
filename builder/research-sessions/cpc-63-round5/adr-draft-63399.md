# Proposed code-specific semantic decision

Reuse `pcr.stationary-on-site-prepared-and-served-meal-services` for the bounded intersection of CPC 63399 and an actual stationary onsite kitchen supplying a complete declared equivalent meal-service occasion at its real takeaway/window/drive-through recipient gate. A no-seating or no-waiter label does not create another physical methodology. The existing method already covers compatible table, counter, window, curbside and drive-through handoffs with actual packaging, preparation, cleanup and full-life responsibilities. No new canonical PCR is justified by this leaf.

This draft does not accept a mapping edge. Root owns independent acceptance, mapping/catalog integration and final selected-target validation. Root is the sole canonical owner for the classification-reference additions; all existing methodology, cards and rules remain unchanged.

# Classification and actual-route evidence

UNSD CPC3 explanatory notes, printed 356–358, were personally read for 63399 and its neighbours 63310, 63320, 63391–63393 and 63400. [Official explanatory notes](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf) classify takeaway/refreshment stands and normally no-seating food outlets together with other heterogeneous routes: actual premises-prepared vending and mobile vehicle/cart food preparation and immediate service. Off-premises-prepared vending meals/snacks are excluded to 6242. Classification is coverage evidence and supplies no recipe, route quantity or module truth.

The current operator's [Eating at Gunwalloe](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cornwall/gunwalloe/eating-at-gunwalloe) page describes a fixed takeaway kiosk, says there is no seating area, and identifies onsite kitchen preparation of most food and drink. The [current property page](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cornwall/gunwalloe) retains the seasonal operating kiosk and actual hot-food offerings. The [operator access statement](https://nt.global.ssl.fastly.net/binaries/content/assets/website/national/regions/cornwall/places/gunwalloe/pdf/gunwalloe-access-statement-.pdf), dated 17 January 2024, describes a kiosk window/counter and a visitor bench, with no formal seating or picnic tables. The bench is disclosed: this decision does not claim there is literally no seat anywhere on the property.

These sources establish a real bounded intersection worth reviewing. They do not prove that every sold dish is prepared onsite, that a hot snack equals a complete meal, or that a particular physical dataset has completed this method. In particular, supplied Callestick ice cream, a beverage-only purchase and bought-in resale cannot be silently treated as the reference product. Actual menu-specific onsite preparation and the declared complete equivalent meal package must be demonstrated before use. Operator-level [National Trust food and beverage roles](https://www.nationaltrustjobs.org.uk/where-you-could-work/food-and-beverage/) corroborate ingredient-based onsite preparation and service; they supply no Gunwalloe recipe or quantities. The separate [Lyme food page](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cheshire-greater-manchester/lyme/eating-drinking-and-shopping-at-lyme) gives an actual cooked-to-order and boxed-pizza example for the packaging question, but its accessible seating elsewhere and material claims are not transferred to Gunwalloe.

A possible alternative example was rejected as current no-seating proof. Chick-fil-A's [2024 Jodeco opening release](https://www.chick-fil-a.com/press-room/chick-fil-a-opens-first-elevated-drive-thru-concept) explicitly describes an elevated kitchen, no dine-in and actual drive-through meal handoff. Its [current location page](https://www.chick-fil-a.com/locations/ga/jodeco-road) also lists dine-in hours. That contradiction remains unresolved; it is not dismissed as a template error. Likewise, the previously reviewed 2021 Chipotle digital-kitchen design had patio seating and cannot establish current no-seating operation. No current no-seat claim relies on these examples.

# Why proxy reuse

All 447 current material-catalog English titles were screened. The actual closest methods' scope, functional unit, reference, measurement and boundary were inspected. The stationary method already has the selected physical kitchen and recipient gate. The offsite-prepared onsite-finished plated method requires two real responsibilities, dispatch/transport/receipt and onsite finishing; the camp method's final product is an overnight-food-recreation participant-session. Neither is a substitute for the selected standalone onsite meal service.

The proposed relation is `proxy`: the broad classification contains mobile, vending, ice-cream/cake and other cases outside this selected meal method, while the purpose-neutral PCR also spans seating/service modes outside 63399. This is not whole-leaf coverage or an assertion that either entire scope contains the other. Exclude mobile vehicles/carts, onsite-prepared vending, beverage-only, bought-in resale and nonmeal ice-cream/cake service from this bounded edge. Any such actual route requires its own substantive applicability review, rather than broadening the existing PCR to complete the classification.

# Independent methodology review

The actual English and Chinese method, all 32 cards and 12 collection protocols, eight selected module YAML records and their 32 obligations were reviewed. The reference is one actual fulfilled compatible menu/component/recipe/portion/service-version occasion at the complete recipient physical gate. Ordering, payment, kitchen availability, planned covers and eaten-food mass do not prove fulfillment; actual partials, declined optional components, refills, reversals and already supplied food remain distinct records.

One real onsite operation retains recipe composition/treatment, holding, protective presentation, supply, kitchen wash and cleanup. Absence of a dining room does not erase actual preparation-equipment washing, sanitation, water or waste duties, nor does it impose fictitious customer plate washing. Full upstream supplies/losses and all-user, all-period site/building/kitchen/equipment/reusable manufacture-install-maintain-replace-EOL, native land duties and actual receivers remain mandatory. Actual complete provider coverage or completed exclusive missing-stage integration is required once; neither a blanket purchased meal nor a future supplement hides the real kitchen.

Actual ingredients, materials, food states, species, receiving media and units must expand to concrete exchanges. Air water vapour is Elementary Mass; contained effluent and captured grease are Waste. Count support establishes Item(s) counting only. All 32 concrete identities remain unresolved, with final concrete identity/property/unit/native quantity verification mandatory. The unchanged cards reuse the preserved round4 original prewrite/freeze and later eight-module correction/binding history. No new lookup, fabricated freeze, detail confirmation, database absence or allowance reset is claimed. Round4 made one actual initial reference search, which failed for unavailable OAuth; the other 31 initial plans remained unexecuted.

Every independent numerical operand has its own unit, basis, method, state, sample time and source. Food wet/dry/water states, actual water stocks versus meter readings, concentrate/solution/active/water, stored/installed/recovered fluids and whole-equipment contents remain distinct; component and terminal views are nonadditive. Tare-only receipts precede unused returns. Gross drawn supply plus paired unused-return output or net consumed supply without that output is exclusive; used recovery and reused frying oil do not receive new-material credits. Necessary empty vehicle duties survive zero cargo.

Direct physical trace precedes actual compatible full-consumer resource attribution, followed by one positive same-profile target denominator. Necessary idle, failed, cancelled, closed and zero-output responsibilities retain source/carry/receiver/final resolution. Full asset life uses observed plus sourced compatible remaining use or actual termination, rather than annual target output or nominal capacity. The physical all-output ledger retains actual native outputs before attribution. Independent other meals remain evidence in target-attributed LCI; any genuine joint output needs an actual PCR-specific decision and native physical quantity divided once by the same positive target denominator, without a second resource target share.

All new local evidence is qualitative and retained here and in research. Actual dish preparation, complete equivalent meal gate, packaging quantities, cleaning route, stocks/fates, supplier/provider/asset/land/receiver responsibilities and numeric values are mandatory foreground gaps. Unavailable generic ranges and unresolved identities do not become zero or guessed factors. EN/ZH rules and deterministic English projection remain aligned. Candidate/authored methodology, reviewed Chinese and pending scientific review remain unchanged; this is neither a measured final dataset nor publication readiness.
