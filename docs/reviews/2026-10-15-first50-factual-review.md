# Factual review of the first 50 audited candidate PCRs

The bounded review covered the first 50 records in the agreed audit order: groups 1 and 2, then the first 10 records of group 3. The fixed input is fork PR #20 merge `840a3be19e2e42ffb5c5bd101f6dc7ce8a222ae1`. All 200 canonical input file hashes were checked against that commit. The upstream integration starts at `tiangong-lca/pcr:main`, commit `176c63d6b115f3116a2ceda7172833ef0f83f7df`; it does not merge the fork history.

The review read English sections 1–11, grouped repeated inventory metadata, compared key Chinese facts, conditions and formulas, and checked 280 bounded claims against original publisher, regulator, research or manufacturer material and dimensional/elemental reasoning. General rules and selected admission conditions were distinguished from empirical industry or site facts. Source access and review took place on 15 October 2026. A failed download was not treated as successful evidence access: where needed the same original was read through web PDF access, or an identified distribution copy was matched to the original bibliographic identity.

## Findings and corrections

| Scope | Finding | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #1 | Confirmed English dimensional error: concentration × interval volume × duration repeats the time factor; Chinese already said volumetric flow rate. | Both languages now specify compatible concentration × volumetric flow rate × time integration, or interval flow-weighted concentration × measured interval volume without another time factor. |
| #1, #3, #8, #15, #29, #31, #42, #48 | Eight confirmed repository-state errors: accepted mappings described as unaccepted, absent or currently unmapped. | Retain the narrower physical scope and locate acceptance decisions in the canonical mapping. Related pending/candidate wording in #13, #16, #22, #24, #46 and #49 is clarified without promoting methodology. |
| #1 | Evonik title used legacy PDF metadata rather than the current original cover. | Use the visible cover title; retain the stable source id and undated status. |
| #43 | FPL-RP-707 allocation §1.4 cited printed p.9; it is printed p.8 (PDF p.10). | Correct the page locator in both languages. |
| #14 | USGS 1910 supports historical retort and cost context, not modern LCI allocation policy. | Mark the hierarchy as an authored, limited LCI adaptation and add GHG Product Standard 2011 Chapter 9 p.63, Tables 9.1–9.2. Physical quantities remain unallocated. |
| #39 | The stated pMDI-water CO2 mechanism is correct, but the cited 2021 handbook locator did not explicitly establish it. | Attribute the mechanism to Frihart et al. 2023 pp.732–733, Figure 14.19; retain 2021 for general adhesive, moisture and architecture context. No site reaction extent or fixed yield is inferred. |
| #26, #28, #29 | Clarify conservation of whole mass/elements versus reactive wood/resin components and molecular water. | Distinguish element conservation from evidenced formation/consumption, preserve residuals, actual extent and unknown terms, and forbid invented balancing emissions. #29 retains the 12/44 CO2-to-carbon conversion. |
| #30, #32, #44 | Clarify percent versus fractional moisture and volumetric flow rate versus accumulated gas volume. | State the percent basis in #30 and the two equivalent dimensionally consistent emission forms in #32/#44. The latter ambiguities were not counted as additional confirmed quantity errors. |
| #44 | True H2O/component closure requires a method valid for the UF panel matrix. | Keep the operational oven-test result separate, disclose non-water volatile or continuing-cure loss, retain unresolved interference, and distinguish dry panel solids from pure dry wood. No correction factor is invented. |
| #38 | JRC document citation year 2016 and repository release 17 January 2017 are both real dates. | Label both dates; do not count 2017 as a false year. |
| #48 | Ambient humidity is not purchased process water, but physical moisture accounting must retain actual crossings. | Preserve inlet/outlet humidity and measured net moisture uptake/release without assuming a nonzero or zero amount. |

The confirmed-error count is one dimensional error, eight repository mapping-state errors and two bibliographic/locator corrections. Source-support gaps and precautionary clarifications above are reported separately. Independent readback checked all 23 changed PCRs against their final English/Chinese and generated projection snapshots; no new blocking issue was identified within the repaired claims.
