## Arithmetic counterexamples inspected

These are synthetic review examples, not plant data or default PCR values.

- **Moisture:** 120 kg wet wood at dry-basis moisture u=0.20 contains 100 kg oven-dry wood and 20 kg water; dividing by 0.80 would incorrectly treat the same number as wet-basis moisture.
- **Normalization:** a raw input total of 200 kg and positive accepted production of 100 kg reports 2 kg/kg once, not 0.02 kg/kg after a second division.
- **Finished stock:** opening finished diapers 20 kg plus new accepted Q=100 kg, with dispatch 90 kg and closing stock 30 kg, gives 20+100=90+30. A consolidated ledger does not count Q as an extra external output. For a direct measurement of Q, no second dispatch-stock correction is applied.
- **PM:** 8 mg/m3 on 100,000 matching m3 gives 0.8 kg. On identical bases, cumulative PM10=1 kg and PM2.5=0.4 kg means middle bin 0.6 kg; adding cumulative PM10 again double counts.
- **Joint burden interpretation:** if 100 upstream burden units and 10 machining units are genuinely shared by 90 kg primary product and 10 kg saleable coproduct, mass allocation gives 99 and 11 burden units. Physical outputs remain 90 and 10 kg.

The last example highlights an optional wording clarification for sleepers: explicitly state that relevant shared incoming-material and reception burdens travel into the joint burden pool. Existing wording does not expressly exclude them or mandate the erroneous alternative, so this was not reported as a confirmed error or used to rewrite the method. Likewise, supporting combustion total-mass accounting must consistently include actual air/reactant contributions, inlet humidity, inert gases and excess oxygen; the PCR does not prescribe a contrary closed-sum equality. No invented oxygen exchange is added to force closure.

## Evidence and source limitations

Original evidence was reviewed for its stated limited use, not as a universal recipe or plant observation.

- [USDA Wood Handbook, FPL-GTR-282 (2021)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-A13-PURL-gpo158673/pdf/GOVPUB-A13-PURL-gpo158673.pdf): moisture/density, machining, drying and durability context, with chapter/page locators retained in the PCR. No default sleeper mass, density or service life.
- [CORRIM Northern Lumber LCA, revised July 2025](https://corrim.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/LCA-Report-Northern-Lumber-Report-07222025.pdf), especially sections 3.2.4 and 3.2.6: regional process and allocation precedent. No regional survey values transferred to all sleepers.
- [EPA sawmill particulate screening memorandum](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2016-09/documents/spmpteef_memo.pdf) and [AP-42 wood boilers](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2022-03/c1s6_final.pdf): route/species/particle screening; maximum potential emissions are not actual LCI, and no numerical factors are imported.
- [Pampers Swaddlers producer disclosure](https://www.pampers.com/en-us/products/pampers-swaddlers-diapers), [component explanation](https://www.pampers.com/en-us/about-us/quality-and-safety/article/whats-in-a-pampers-diaper), and [manufacturing/traceability explanation](https://www.pampers.com/en-us/about-us/quality-and-safety/article/manufacturing-process), checked 2026-10-17: limited design/material and traceability support. Marketing performance and proprietary plant quantities are not adopted.
- [UK Environment Agency SC010018/SR2 (2008)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7c4054e5274a1b00422810/scho0808boir-e-e.pdf), sections 1.2-1.3 and figure 1.1: historical component and lifecycle context, without default masses or impacts.
- [JRC Pulp and Paper BREF (2015)](https://bureau-industrial-transformation.jrc.ec.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2019-11/PP_revised_BREF_2015.pdf), printed pages 49, 196 and 670: supplier AD convention, kraft process and qualitative paper-cutting dust context. The live endpoint could not be retrieved by the reviewer; the retained original was checked against its recorded SHA-256 and these passages inspected. It does not establish diaper/SAP emissions or factory-specific quantities.
- [European Commission PEF Annex I, section 4.5](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2021-12/Annexes%201%20to%202.pdf): generic multifunctionality hierarchy, without claiming complete PEF/CFF conformance.

Remaining dataset prerequisites include actual site/lot quantities and calibration; supplier reference bases; the sleeper heat/chip-volume/NOx compatibility conditions; diaper SAP and purge identities; and complete grade, composition, water/carbon and monitoring evidence. Existing manifest disclosures remain binding. Report-only range warnings do not justify adding unsupported numbers.
