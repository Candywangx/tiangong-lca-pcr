## Independent critique of proposed plan

- PASS with additions: exact35 four-file bundles and latest upstream baseline is sound; freeze CSV identities and hashes before changes, inspect upstream existence/current status and abort accidental replacement of published records.
- MISSING: inspect evidence credibility, original native official names/properties/units and item-specific applicability, not just UUID shape/EN-ZH parity. Mark unavailable sources and unresolved identity debt explicitly; do not invent evidence.
- MISSING: per-record review matrix with bilingual full-rule condition/action context, conserved arithmetic counterexamples, source-specific findings and fix/recheck trace. Candidate states and draft translations must stay truthful.
- MISSING: preserve all pre-existing accepted mapping objects/ADRs and baseline aliases semantically; review narrower replacement versus legacy identity, unmapped exclusions and unreviewed chain controls. Do not blindly regenerate accepted decisions.
- MISSING: check both required inventory gates in all35 manifests, exact reference-output selection, collection citation identity and mass/water/carbon basis; packaging/stock/allocation checks alone omit measurement/identity traps.
- MISSING: full local validate/docs build is necessary but insufficient to claim target integration qualified. Need actual final-source lane selection and fresh sealed-product/native/browser/SEO results; mappings/ADRs/shared derivatives may select full rather than current-PCR-only data lane.
- MISSING: public inspectable sanitized receipts/excerpts linked from PR; .reports is normally untracked and inaccessible to bot. Distinguish proof not downloaded, reviewer access limitations, authored claims and inspected successful output.
- MISSING: source movement invalidation: rebase/merge current main and rebuild/recheck final union; bind PR head and tested tree and preserve old failed attempts. Never waive budgets/drop content to fit build.
- OUT OF SCOPE: product version/tag, publication, provider changes and root workspace integration unless separately authorized. A fork PR submission should accurately report those as later handoffs rather than add unrelated work.
