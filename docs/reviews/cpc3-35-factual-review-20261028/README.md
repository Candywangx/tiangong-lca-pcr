## Findings addressed

- Six packaging bridges now admit first standalone non-purchased receipts while separating paid standalone/bundled procurement and proven same-item external returns. Borrowing does not invent a new manufacture or erase real service burdens.
- Three thermal rules measure supply and return mass independently; a single-mass enthalpy difference requires equal-mass proof and complete boundary closure.
- The 31413 shipment-gate method carries original production vectors through raw/WIP/unshipped FG instead of dividing unmatched current activity by current dispatch.
- The 32520 score method retains unique first-release Qc independently of later damage, rework, returns and repeat dispatch; loss/state-change legs are disjoint.
- The 32300 and 32420 organic-species equations distinguish matched inlet/outlet masses from gross outlet observations and retain missing/negative/uncertain evidence.
- The 37570 plain-board scope includes ordinary same-formulation Hatschek thin-layer accumulation while continuing to exclude externally faced/dissimilar composite boards.
- Four selected Chinese flow labels match current official native names, retaining applicable concentration/route restrictions in the complete cards. Redundant lifecycle frontmatter and classification-status prose now agree with the authoritative manifest/current mapping; scientific/translation debts are preserved.
