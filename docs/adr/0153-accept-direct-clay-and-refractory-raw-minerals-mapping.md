## Evidence and methodology

US EPA AP-42 11.25 Clay Processing (January 1995) original PDF pp.1–3 and 8 was visually checked against bounded text. Clay types, process table, kaolin dry/wet refining, ball/fire-clay preparation, bentonite/fuller-earth/common-clay processing and thermal/particulate scope support route conditions. Historical yields, moisture, temperature, particle-size and emissions are not imposed as default quantities. IFC Mining (10 December 2007) supports actual deposit extraction, development/closure and water/waste/emission management. WCO establishes product identity only; it is not evidence of a refractory synthesis recipe.

Seven conditioned processes contain 28 atomic exchanges. Each dataset fixes clay species or refractory mineral/phase, source, market state, grading and actual route. Mining, mechanical preparation, wet purification/activation and drying/thermal conversion are partitioned; raw wet slurry does not acquire assumed drying/calcination. Sulfuric acid, confirmed sodium dithionite and sodium carbonate each have distinct conditional input cards. The term hydrosulfite alone establishes neither salt identity nor dose. Supplied kyanite and kaolin inputs are distinct; each actual other mineral/additive requires its own identity. Actual alumina and silica synthesis feed are separate conditional rows for a confirmed raw-mullite route, not an invented universal stoichiometric recipe.

Positive accepted net as-received kg D is independently weighed. Slurry D includes declared carrier water with separately measured solids. Wet-basis free moisture is separate from mineral structural water and calcination loss; dry/slurry conversion requires matched assays and density. Mineral/oxide solids, additives, rejects, stock and actual phase conversion are reconciled separately from water circulation/evaporation/discharge. Structural-water release has its own measured thermal-balance protocol. Aluminosilicate conversion alone does not justify calcination CO2; actual fossil combustion, mineral carbonate and organic-carbon releases require their correct origin and separate records. Development/closure is charged once over disclosed measured lifetime output; internal dust/slurry return receives no automatic credit.
