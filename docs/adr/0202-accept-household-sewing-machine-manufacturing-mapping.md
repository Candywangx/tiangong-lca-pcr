# Accept household sewing-machine manufacture

Decision UTC: 2026-10-02T00:13:36.040551Z
PCR: `pcr.metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.special-purpose-machinery.household-sewing-machines`
Status: candidate; authored methodology.

Accept CPC 3.0 44814 as an exact edge to configuration-specific complete household sewing machines manufactured at the factory gate. Mechanical/electronic electric machines, manual/treadle heads and manufacturer-designed domestic sewing/embroidery combinations or overlock machines retain their actual principal function and supplied interface. Industrial machines, book-sewing machines, separately sold furniture/parts/needles and garment sewing services require separate scope. No stitch-speed cutoff establishes household identity.

Independent source review read acquired original Janome 712T instruction-book pedal and treadle-installation pages, original Singer 4423 metal-frame versus stainless-bedplate body, original Brother SE725 combination and 1034D serger bodies, and original JUKI DDL-8700 industrial counterexample. Janome book 749-800-011 (E-N), Printed in Taiwan, is undated; an externally required table is not automatically delivered BOM. Marketing speed, operating power, listed accessories and consumer maintenance instructions are neither universal factory recipes nor manufacturing quantities. Cast aluminum, cast iron, stamped steel and polymer routes remain conditional on actual drawings/grades and performed operations.

Independent direct read confirms 633bed4f-5fd3-4590-b645-8d8daeedaa00 as finished manufactured Product flow at plant with Mass/kg and official Chinese 家用缝纫机. Electricity 50657322-939c-4829-a87b-47c093bfa6a7 applies only to actual CN below-1-kV user consumption supply, with actual provider/year; specific materials, modules, wastes and releases retain explicit identity/provider and empirical-range gaps. Industrial sewing identity and contradictory mixed-part comments are rejected.

Aligned bilingual methodology contains nine processes and 73 atomic cards, with finite measurement pass, 730 performed, complete coverage and zero skipped. Bought complete frame, housing, motor, control, gears, stitch modules and supplied accessories count upstream once; actual in-house alternatives require individual feedstock and process burdens. Supplied bobbin/thread/cover/tray is separate from consumed factory sewing-test thread/fabric and waste. Same accepted cohort Q/N then Q/D, calibrated net D and M=D/N exclude packaging, rejected output and externally owned table. Projected tables preserve own-term physical assays, wet/dry moisture and density, water stocks/reactions/paired returns, solvent non-air fates with capture distinct from destruction, independently measured supply/return heat on common enthalpy datum with provider returns once, residual utility reconciliation and negative-residual investigation. Species-resolved concentration×dry-flow×time and actual abatement retain NOx-as-NO2-equivalent distinct from measured molecular NO2.

Acceptance establishes methodology category semantics rather than publication readiness, empirical factory evidence or interchangeability of sewing functions. Root/runtime and independent original-author full validation are separate recorded delivery evidence.
