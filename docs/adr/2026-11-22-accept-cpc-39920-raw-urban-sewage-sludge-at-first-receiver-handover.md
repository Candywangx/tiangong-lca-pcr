# CPC 39920 raw urban sewage sludge at first receiver handover

Accept a narrower association for a traceable consignment of raw, unstabilised sludge originating from urban wastewater treatment, transferred from an external plant in bulk and first accepted by the receiving treatment works before mixing or treatment. For the documented UK route, retain waste code 19 08 05 and source and receiver acceptance evidence. Measure the physical wet net mass separately from dry solids, transport work and treatment fees.

Exclude local sludge at the receiver, industrial or drinking-water sludges, other material the same permit allows, treated cake, dried sludge, and stabilized sludge suitable for fertilizer. A transport invoice, downstream land use or an annual destination percentage cannot establish an eligible lot by itself.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39920.json).
