It supplies no universal100km boundary,reference unit,crew exclusion or LCA formula. The actual product here is one real transported person receiving the declared completed transport segment or evidenced single transport itinerary under its actual endpoints,state,trajectory/orbit,duration,quality and included safety/return/recovery obligations. Internal stage separation,coast,docking,carrier release or constituent return phases do not create extra final units. Actual separately fulfilled compatible transport segments retain their own function and attribution; incompatible endpoint/quality classes require subdivision or an evidenced transparent mix.

NASA Commercial Crew's actual source calls astronauts crew while delivering their transport to/from orbital destinations. A crew label,salary,piloting/monitoring/research duty or dual role cannot automatically remove transported recipients from N. Distinguish real transport recipients,operation-only support and dual roles from actual contract/role/fulfilment evidence; one real person-function is counted once.
