# CPC 3.0 65118 mapping decision

## Source leaf and target

Proposed relation: `exact`.

## Semantic boundary

Cover actual road carriage of live animals as cargo, with terrestrial, aquatic, domestic/companion/captive/wild and other actual species subject to their applicable conditions rather than a convenient single species/truck. Exclude original rearing/breeding/growing, animal commodity sale, traction-animal supply/asset lifecycle, non-road carriage, passenger outputs, slaughter/product manufacture, and dead-cargo carriage as a separate initial freight function.

Transported animals remain custody cargo outside manufacturing exchanges and are never assets consuming original rearing burden or animal useful-life allocation. Record actual care during carrier responsibility: feeding/watering, holding/rest, ventilation/thermal/containment, fitness/inspection/veterinary/biosecurity, handling and cleaning, actual excreta/sanitation/body waste and releases. Aquatic conditions may require real transportwater/oxygen/quality/state and discharge responsibility; transported water contributes payload but not animal liveweight.

One reference is one actually completed, predeclared qualified road live-animal consignment carriage obligation with real species/count/identified cohort/liveweight/state, route/distance/duration, care/quality and full outcomes/completion disclosure. Headcount, liveweight, crate count, trips and tkm cannot silently replace this functional service. Mortality/rejection/partialfailure/rescue retain burdens and actual outcomes; no mortality-free compliance is assumed. Positive equivalent N and function strata are mandatory.

Live-count custody and dead-body custody are separate balances. A death is neither automatic disposal nor completed service: body ownership, return, discard and receiver require actual evidence. Preserve original animal cargo condition and actual transport-period biological emissions without lifetime rearing emissions. Equipment, containers and actual infrastructure retain their own full lifecycle/all-user/provider responsibilities, independently of cargo animals.
