# CPC 64131 mapping decision

Proposed canonical record: `pcr.rail-sightseeing-itinerary-service`, semantic path `transport-services/passenger-transport/rail-sightseeing-itinerary-service`. Draft only; the author adds no accepted edge. The retained classification leaf scaffold is not a material method.

UNSD CPC 3.0 Explanatory Notes (30 June 2025), pp.360–361, 64131, identifies sightseeing passenger rail transportation. It does not prescribe a reference quantity, traction mix, duration, menu or common emission rate. Source: https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf .

Propose **narrower**: actual participant sightseeing itineraries on locomotive-hauled or self-propelled powered wheel-on-rail equipment, with real electric, diesel and steam branch obligations. The function is the completed observation/interpretation experience over the whole declared rail sequence, stops, class, duration and included content. One actual participant completing that itinerary is one Count item. A constituent outward/return leg or reboarding inside that same itinerary does not create another completed tour. Tickets, day passes, reserved seats, party bookings, train movements and passenger-km are different quantities.

The existing ordinary passenger-rail record explicitly excludes sightseeing and steam. Its actual train-duty, native measurement, provider and capital interfaces can inform this method; replacing the sightseeing product with an ordinary A-to-B rail leg would delete the fulfilled experience and included services. Conversely, a train journey chosen privately for its pleasant scenery does not automatically become an operator-fulfilled sightseeing product. Actual service purpose, observation/interpretation quality and fulfilment must be evidenced independently from the classification label or ticket description.

Actual NPS Steamtown Train Rides HTML retained on 21 October 2026 describes narrated heritage-yard experiences and longer excursions, while some short-ride tickets remain valid all day. Mechanical, weather, crew and equipment conditions can alter operations. This supports separating ticket entitlement, attendance and actual experience completion; no published price, operating schedule, duration or occupancy becomes a universal PCR factor. The retained page says updated 10 October; a search cache said 8 October, so only the actual snapshot date is used. Source: https://www.nps.gov/stea/planyourvisit/trainrides.htm .

Royal Gorge Route's dated 2023 group guide, actual full PDF p.2 read through the web reader, distinguishes an out-and-back scenic itinerary and differing included versus extra dining by class. It is a qualitative case, not a current contract, recipe or duration assumption. Actual food/drink/guide/interpretation/stop contents must be independently collected. Its beverage wording is internally ambiguous and is not used to prescribe inclusions. Direct local acquisition failed; no retained original PDF/hash or verified local fragment is claimed for that source. Source: https://www.royalgorgeroute.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/RGRR-Group-Reservation-Form-V7.-4.26.23.pdf .

Steam is a materially relevant branch rather than an omitted legacy technology. IPCC 2006 Vol.2 Ch.3 §3.4 introduction, pp.3.39–3.40, describes diesel, electric and tourist steam locomotives and directs steam combustion toward conventional boiler methods. Vol.2 Ch.2 supports actual fuel/technology and actually burned activity. The PCR adopts no default power, deterioration, full oxidation or emission factor. Actual coal grade/received moisture/carbon/sulfur/ash, ignition and other fuels, hot starts/standby, water/treatment/steam/condensate/blowdown, captured bottom/fly ash and unburned carbon must be independently evidenced. Steam water is an actual elementary water-to-air exchange where supported, not a waste-treatment stream; collected boiler liquid, ash and airborne particles remain separate.

The method includes actual traction/support, empty/shunting/failed/cancelled/zero-output duties, whole sightseeing support and actual included incidentals; all actual participants, staff roles, class and other product users remain distinguished. N is positive actual completed participant itineraries, not tickets. Actual independently fulfilled altered/partial products require explicit separate boundaries; no fictional output is created for idle or failed duty. Shared duty uses a complete compatible all-user denominator rather than default seats or fare shares.

Every manufacture/restoration/refit/installation/maintenance/renewal/retirement and included-provider cell has one owner. A delivered manufacture UUID represents only its actual supply gate; complete-life duty allocates that coverage and does not create a whole-life tour. Initial complete assemblies and the same included components are exclusive. Heritage age, donation or financial depreciation do not establish zero physical burden. GHG Product Standard 2011 supports transparency/subdivision/physical allocation; expanded capital, restoration and tour-bundle scope and collection equations are explicit PCR choices rather than a capital mandate falsely attributed to that standard.

Purchased rail-leg or passenger-distance results require actual compatible supplied legs/distance before cohort attribution and division by N; only an already equivalent per-completed-tour result retains its exact normalized basis without another division. They replace only their exact supported duty/provider cells and never become the sightseeing output or duplicate foreground energy/assets/emissions. Whole fuel blend and replacing physical component inputs are exclusive; fuel supply, withdrawal, actual burn, spill/recovery and transfer remain distinct. External AC export is separate from internal regeneration/storage and cannot receive duplicate netting/credit.

Cable-only rail, human/animal traction, overnight accommodation and whole multimode holiday products remain separately assessed methodology needs, not silently covered 64131 routes. The current actual evidence and independent service boundary support a powered rail sightseeing itinerary, including genuine meal/guide/interpretation branches, without creating one PCR per traction technology or class. A later scope change needs its actual mechanism, full fulfilment object and additional material interfaces reviewed. No exhaustive leaf coverage is claimed.

Twenty-one concrete conditional cards are accompanied by mandatory row-addressable expansion obligations for every actual missing ingredient, chemical, energy, asset, waste, species/medium and provider. Twelve locations bind ten unique verified UUIDs; nine concrete identities remain unresolved after bounded lookup. Count support does not identify a sightseeing-service UUID. Raw steel/coal equivalents, general freight or passenger-distance services, mixed coach classifications, industrial plant residues, direct discharges and steam heat do not automatically match actual completed tours, equipment or collected generation streams. No missing evidence is filled with an invented range or UUID.
