# CPC 39332 source-segregated silver-copper sheet offcuts for refining

Accept a narrower association for clean solid offcuts generated while cold mechanically cutting a traceable uncoated silver-copper sheet lot into jewellery blanks, first accepted as feed for a specified refinery. Record the actual remaining usable sheet, saleable blanks, eligible offcuts, foreign material, rejections and losses separately. Exclude unused stock, finished blanks, plated or soldered scrap, filings, mixed sweeps, precious-metal ash and electronic waste.

The October 2026 CPC 39332 note identifies HS 7112.92 and 7112.99. The selected non-gold, non-platinum offcuts enter the other-scrap branch 7112.99. Verify alloy assay, absence of gold/PGM or coating, custody and agreed receiver state. The first product release, transfer of ownership, payment on assay and return of refined metal are different events.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39332.json).
