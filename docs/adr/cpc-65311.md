# CPC 3.0 65311 mapping decision

## Official leaf and target

[UNSD CPC 3.0 code 65311](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2100/65311) includes scheduled and nonscheduled transportation by air on behalf of postal and courier services. The actual official note is retained and read.

## Scope and reference

Cover the full actual air-carrier mail function for postal/courier customers, including direct and transfer routes, dedicated freighter, passenger belly/combi, small aircraft and all other actually qualifying propulsion/configuration. Neither international UPU-designated-operator traffic nor jet aircraft defines the whole category. Carrier-responsible custody, actual airport handling/security/care and supporting duty are included. Integrated posting/collection/independent postal sorting/last-mile delivery, passenger service, independent nonmail air freight, space freight, original contents manufacture/trade and bare aircraft/ULD rental/manufacture are not the reference product. Common company brands or invoices do not merge distinct provider gates, and pure ground-only carriage is not relabeled as air service.

The reference `1 Item(s)` is one originally specified air-carrier letters/parcels consignment obligation completely fulfilled and accepted at its receiving operator/agent handoff. Mandatory original manifest articles/receptacles and actual quantities/state, route/legs/airports, custody duration, timing/security/integrity and actual care conditions define the function. Contemporaneously documented independent accepted segments/amended obligations may count their own specified function once; failed scope and quality cannot be retrospectively reduced. Articles, bags, dispatches, ULDs, scans and aircraft movements are not interchangeable service counts. Unlike obligations require strata or a disclosed bounded mixture; Count normalizes and does not replace actual payload, distance/time or causal allocation.

Actual public Count property `01846770-4cfe-4a25-8ad9-919d8d378345` references group `5beb6eed-33a9-47b8-9ede-1dfe8f679159`; reference `Item(s)` factor 1 and countable-case support are independently verified. This supports the independently defined count function, not an air-mail service UUID. An unresolved actual reference output remains registered, without relabeling road/mass-distance/material/equipment identities.

## Physical and lifecycle methods

The actual operating aircraft/engine/configuration and all passenger/baggage/other-cargo users govern each flight. Full attributable start/APU/taxi/queue, takeoff/climb/cruise/descent/landing, ground power/terminal/transfer/security/cold duties and empty/offloaded/failed/diverted/returned/recovery duties are retained. Passenger belly mail is not assumed free marginal freight; dedicated cargo is not assumed mail-only. Code sharing and repeated consignment/scan records do not create extra physical flights or completed outputs.

Postal-article mass, its own declared packaging, external receptacle tare, ULD/pallet/net tare and other users are distinguished. Nested gross values cannot be added again; tariff/volumetric weight is not actual material payload or a universal density conversion. Customer mail contents remain custody stocks, not consumed manufacture inputs. Actual accepted outward delivery, outward return/transfer, discard/direct release and closing stock are exclusive; offloaded/refused/retained mail and unresolved losses require actual evidence. Actual moisture changes use measured wet bases and explicit water-change closure, not inferred dry loss.

Actual jet/avgas/SAF blends/other propulsion fuels and origins are separate from ground diesel; fuel uplift differs from burn. Actual initial/operator/customer stock consumed bears compatible fuel upstream once irrespective of ownership/payment. Own APU/generator/battery/bleed-air/recovered energy is internal conversion, not duplicate purchased energy. Actual cold materials, refrigerant and de-icing/cleaning chemicals keep native supply/stock/recovery/release/receiver gates; not every consignment requires every branch.

Actual source/phase/trajectory altitude and receiving medium are preserved. Certification LTO/static data are not automatic cruise or other-propulsion factors. NOx as NO2-equivalent, aggregate HC as CH4-equivalent, carbon/CO2, smoke number, particle mass and nvPM number remain distinct reporting bases. Known high-altitude emissions do not use unspecified-air UUIDs merely for reuse. Non-CO2 characterization, contrails and noise impacts are not invented mass exchanges or universal CO2 multipliers; actual physical inventory needs its own native support and stated assessment scope.

Same-cohort full all-user useful duty governs aircraft/engine/APU/battery, ULD/bags/gear, GSE and airport/terminal/security/power/cold assets. Include uncovered manufacture/install, maintenance/overhaul/replacement and retirement once, preserving initial assembly/parts/provider coverage. Reusable stock and actual empty returns close; native provider quantities/gates replace exact covered cells only. No aircraft/engine/bag lifetime, load factor, empty ratio, fuel coefficient, chargeable-density or recovery credit is invented.
