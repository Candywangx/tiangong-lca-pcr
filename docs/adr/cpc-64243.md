# CPC 64243 draft mapping decision

The actual UNSD CPC3.0 Explanatory Notes30June2025, printed363, defines `International scheduled air transport services of passengers` with aircraft of any type including helicopters and accompanying passenger baggage/items carried without extra cost. This code requires actual **international** and **scheduled** service qualification. For scheduled leaves the actual predetermined route/schedule applies; non-scheduled leaves expressly exclude sightseeing64134 and aircraft-with-crew rental66031. This decision does not invent technology limits from available UUIDs. Actual original: https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf .

## Semantic product and proposed relation

Propose `pcr.air-passenger-leg-service`, **broader** than this individual leaf, pending independent main review. Its ordinary operator-fulfilled passenger conveyance method covers the union of domestic/international and scheduled/non-scheduled services. Code and commercial timetable labels alone do not require duplicate canonical methods. Actual international/scheduled applicability is mandatory when this particular mapping is used; the method also covers other geography/schedule leaves. No positive edge is accepted by this draft.

One reference is one actual passenger completing the declared boarding-to-final-alighting air service leg with actual OD, class/quality/duration, stage membership and included baggage/cabin/ground content. A full ticket/return/connection itinerary is not silently one leg; technical stops do not create additional through-passenger outputs. Actual independent connection legs remain distinct; flight-number/aircraft changes alone cannot establish genuine new fulfilment. Infant/nonrevenue actual passengers count; on-duty crew are support, transported repositioning crew roles require evidence. Passengers, seats, movements, flight stages, hours, cargo/mail and pkm remain native distinct quantities. Complete period/stage/all-user closure precedes positive actual N once.

## Method and evidence

The method retains actual fixed-wing/rotor flight interfaces and other real same-conveyance mechanisms through mandatory native technical row expansion. No universal commercial-jet template, aircraft mass, fuel/cargo/cabin factor, occupancy, useful life or generic range is supplied. Actual all-stage/ground/APU/holding/diversion/failed/empty/repositioning duties, airport/heliport/deicing/handling/cabin support, manufacture/install/refit/maintenance/replacements/retirement and actual species/height/medium are explicit. Known altitude is not erased to fit unspecified-air UUIDs. Purchased per-leg/hour/pkm/handling results scale actual compatible native activity and replace only exact documented provider cells; Count service is not an asset/freight UUID.

Read actual CPC363 and Eurostat air-statistics manual v17July2024 §§1.2–1.5 distinctions for passenger/crew/infants, takeoff-to-nextlanding versus onflightOD and transit/transfer. These dated statistical definitions are not LCA quantities: unknown-OD fallback, seat adjustments, passenger-unit cargo convention, unsuccessful/zero-traffic reporting omissions and statistical aircraft exclusions are not adopted. IPCC2006Vol2Ch3§3.6 supplies actual engine/fuel/phase/altitude questions; ICAO ICEC actual13.1August2024 operational CO2 calculator is not complete lifecycle/nonCO2 evidence or universal rotor factor. GHGProduct2011 boundary/data/subdivision/allocation principles inform explicitly authored full-lifecycle Count-leg formulas. Reused FAA2024glider/balloon and actualZeppelin technical case provide conditional physical interfaces only, not proof every use is ordinary passenger transport.

## Functional exclusions and honest readiness

Aerial-viewing sightseeing has a different whole-programme function; customer-controlled aircraft-with-crew availability, reservations without fulfilment, primary training/adventure/jump or independently fulfilled medical treatment and freight-only output are different accountable objects. Passenger medical/accessibility status alone does not exclude conveyance; actual included support remains. Codeshare sale does not add another operated transport output. Included baggage/items are not automatically cargo coproducts; separately fulfilled cargo/mail/other services require real subdivision or causal allocation with sensitivity.

Candidate status, grade/gate-specific fuel/asset/collection identity gaps and actual site native data/provider/lifecycle requirements remain explicit. Exact public proof can be reused; earlier bounded rejection queries are not reset. Missing UUID/numeric range is readiness evidence, not arbitrary product exclusion.
