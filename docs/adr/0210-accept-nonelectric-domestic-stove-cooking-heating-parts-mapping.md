# Accept non-electric domestic cooking and heating appliance parts mapping

- Decision: accepted
- Decided at UTC: 2026-10-02T18:03:19.424081Z
- Decision-maker: codex-direct-pcr-author

CPC 3.0 `44832` maps exactly to `pcr.metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.special-purpose-machinery.parts-of-stoves-ranges-grates-cookers-barbecues-braziers-gas-rings-plate-warmers-and-si-c6e01f0a`, a candidate authored methodology for dedicated supplied parts of non-electric domestic iron/steel stoves, ranges, grates, cookers, barbecues, braziers, gas-rings, plate-warmers and similar appliances. Actual cooking and room-heating architectures remain within the full category rather than one convenient stove-door model.

Delivered output is a configured part, module or identical kit, not its complete host appliance. Original Jotul foundry/assembly evidence and Weber exploded cooking-appliance parts show independent supplied states. The material of one grate cannot establish the non-electric host identity, as the inspected electric-barbecue counterexample shows. Glass, seals, liners, valves and ambiguous kits require documented principal use and item-specific classification review; general-purpose or independently classified parts, industrial/electric/central-boiler parts and complete hosts are not selected automatically.

Both languages collect matched-period Qattr/Dnet by calibrated accepted part mass, retain accepted part or kit count as a cross-check and exclude packing/reject/test-media mass. Actual make/buy states avoid repeating embedded production. All atomic identities were queried; adopted UUIDs were directly checked for supplied interface, chemistry, type, state, property, units and official Chinese name. Unresolved identities and future factory records remain candidate gaps. No catalogue weight, recipe, fuel rate or lifetime is adopted. This positive category mapping does not publish the methodology or certify future datasets.
