# CPC 64117 mapping decision

## Original classification evidence

UNSD CPC Version 3.0 Explanatory Notes, 30 June 2025, printed/PDF pages 358 and 360, places the subclass in urban/suburban land passenger group 6411 and includes human- or animal-drawn passenger conveyance, including rickshaws and pack animals, with an operator. Rental without driver belongs to 73114. Source: https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf

Proposed canonical record: `pcr.human-and-equid-passenger-conveyance-service`, under `transport-services/passenger-transport/human-and-equid-passenger-conveyance-service`. The canonical record covers the actual human/equid conveyance function across evidenced urban/suburban or interurban routes. Its animal route covers working horses, donkeys and mules. The reference, actual duty/care and lifecycle methods are not intrinsically urban-only; actual geography remains a mandatory service qualifier rather than a fixed factor. This boundary is chosen from the physical service and its care/allocation needs independently of a classification leaf. Other animal species, motor/electrical assistance, no-operator rental, booking-only activity and standalone freight remain excluded. The proposed relationship is **proxy**, pending independent semantic review: the canonical product crosses geography while excluding unassessed animal species within the leaf, so the two sets are not nested. Apply the 64117 edge only to its urban/suburban parent-group 6411 context within one city or contiguous cities. Other actual geographies require their own classification decision; this proposal establishes neither all-species nor unconditional whole-leaf coverage. Do not narrow or widen the canonical product merely to obtain a simpler mapping relation.

## Material method and denominator

The record describes one actual passenger completing the declared operator-provided conveyance itinerary in native Count / Item(s), with actual origin/destination, route/stops/return boundary, mode, configuration, occupancy, quality and period. It does not count tickets, seats, vehicles, animal-days or a default passenger-distance. Existing motorized bus, rail and integrated rail/bus records do not cover the human/equid biological and manure accounting problem.

Human-powered and animal-supported routes retain different actual inventories. A pack ride does not automatically demand a cart; an animal-free human route does not demand animal feed. Empty, waiting, failed and nonworking support stays attributable without inventing completed outputs. Complete vehicle supply is manufacture-only; delivered native quantity is allocated with supported same-asset lifetime duty, while replacements, maintenance and retirement remain separately owned.

The animal route explicitly includes allocated pre-entry rearing/training, actual post-entry feed/water, daily maintenance and rest support, shelter, veterinary/farrier requirements, enteric/respiratory carbon, manure and retirement responsibilities. A live-animal first-entry product is not a full-life traction-service identity. Actual period care is not amortized a second time by lifetime duty. Species, diet, moisture, animal-days, manure systems, native carbon/nitrogen basis and actual receiving subcompartments must be evidenced.

Ordinary human subsistence is excluded as a disclosed PCR service-boundary convention, while actual service-specific supplied consumables and any evidenced incremental work nutrition are assessed independently. This is not a measured zero, food-to-labour fuel conversion or justification to omit animal maintenance. Comparisons require functional/boundary equivalence and sensitivity to that asymmetric convention. External WOAH/IPCC/GHG sources provide their stated care, pathway and allocation context; the custom service formulas and human boundary are explicitly authored PCR choices.
