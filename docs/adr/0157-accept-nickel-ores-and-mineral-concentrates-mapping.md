## Decision

Accept CPC3.0 14220 as exact to pcr.ores-and-minerals-electricity-gas-and-water.metal-ores.nickel-ores-and-concentrates. Cover nickel mineral ores and concentrates, including sulfide, lateritic oxide/hydrous-silicate and other independently confirmed nickel-category mineral ores, including nickel-cobalt/nickel-copper grades. Mining and actual mineral preparation are bounded by the explicitly selected mine, plant-loading or included receipt gate. Do not narrow the category to sulfide concentrate or impose flotation on laterite or ore-only output. Other mineral states and novel extraction routes require their own confirmed geological identity, actual route and additional exchanges, not borrowed sulfide intensities.

Qualified actual thermal preparation belongs only where the output still has independently confirmed ore/mineral-concentrate identity, with measured phase and sulfur/water/offgas balance. Do not assume every calcine is ore. Smelting matte, ferronickel/nickel pig iron, dissolved leach solution, chemically precipitated mixed hydroxide/sulfide, refined nickel chemical compounds and metal have different identities and product gates. Commercial use of the word concentrate does not make chemically precipitated MHP/MSP into geological mineral concentrate. Copper/cobalt/precious-metal reference products declared under other categories remain distinct; recycled metal and industrial slags are not mineral ore merely because nickel-bearing.

The owner authorized candidate completion without Harness and one fresh independent worktree per PCR. Product-boundary mapping acceptance is not independent methodology review or publication approval.

## Evidence and resulting methodology

British Geological Survey, Bide/Hetherington/Gunn, Nickel Mineral Commodity Profile, September2008 original PDF pp6–9 was acquired and retained; bounded original text was read, and original PDF7 processing description and PDF9 complete laterite flow diagram were rendered and inspected. Sulfide mining and actual crushing/screening/magnetic separation/grinding/flotation/dewatering are distinguished from laterite mining and downstream metal processing. Historic grade/recovery, dimensions, temperatures, leach conditions and market/feasibility claims are not adopted. The report is qualitative route evidence, not an assumption that all laterite can undergo sulfide flotation. Actual limonite/saprolite horizons, clay, free versus bound water, Fe/Mg/Si and metal assays determine preparation and collection scope.

WCO HS2022 Chapter26 original PDF pp1–2, Note2 and heading2604 support mineral ores/concentrates and matte/residue distinctions. The retained original and bounded hash-checked fragment were reused; this is identity evidence, not a new HS mapping or legal classification certification. IFC Mining2007 supplies generic water/drainage, waste, pollution-control and development/closure guidance. Search-found Nickel Institute publications returned403 for actual original retrieval and are not adopted as verified source evidence.

Eight conditioned processes contain37 atomic exchange cards. Sulfide and laterite geological resources have distinct unresolved elementary identities; Product-flow ore and environmental nickel oxide cannot substitute for them. Actual standalone laterite washing/screening/scrubbing/sorting has separate supplied laterite, electricity, purchased wash water, coarse reject and fines-sludge cards. Actual concentration has supplied nickel-cobalt ore, electricity, steel grinding balls, purchased water and individually conditional potassium amyl xanthate, MIBC, quicklime and anionic polyacrylamide. Named reagents are collection candidates requiring actual supplier formulation/circuit evidence; none imposes a universal recipe or dose. Actual copper concentrate is a separately weighed co-product, while embedded copper/cobalt in one nickel bulk concentrate is not counted again as independent physical output. Other actual chemicals/media, land exchanges, mineral resources, energy and pollutants need individual additional cards.

Mining diesel/electricity/ANFO and waste rock are separate. Tailings slurry, coarse laterite reject, wash fines, water-treatment sludge, collector dust and externally treated wastewater remain distinct. Direct river abstraction differs from purchased water and internal reclaim. Included delivery fuel has its own gate condition and does not duplicate outsourced transport burden. Actual dissolved-nickel release uses matched concentration and discharge volume with receiving compartment/background uncertainty; managed-tailings nickel is not automatically environmental release. Actual fossil CO2/SO2/PM10 are measured or traceably calculated at the foreground gate, excluding downstream smelter/refinery emissions.

D is independently weighed positive accepted net as-received mineral-product kg. Measured wet-basis free moisture w gives dry mineral mass D*(1-w); measured dry-basis nickel fraction g gives contained nickel D*(1-w)*g. D remains the common inventory denominator. Hydrous laterite minerals retain bound water as mineral phase; state drying/assay method and observed dehydroxylation rather than silently redefining free moisture. Match solids and Ni/Co/Cu input/output/stock balances separately from new/recycled/evaporated/discharged water. Recovery requires matched dry masses and assays after stock adjustment, not concentrate grade or refinery yield.
