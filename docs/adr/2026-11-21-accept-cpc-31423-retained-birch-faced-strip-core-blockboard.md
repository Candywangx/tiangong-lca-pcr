# CPC 31423 temperate-birch-faced softwood strip-core blockboard

The library accepts a narrower CPC 3.0 31423 association for four temperate birch veneers, verified as Betula pendula or Betula pubescens, around a single pine or spruce solid-strip core, with alternating physical axes, unfinished faces and no tropical outer ply. The 23 October 2026 UNSD white-cover publication places the corresponding blockboard explicitly in 31423.01 under the standard five-digit parent (Annex, paragraphs 138–143 and printed p.928). The standard 31413 note still expressly includes blockboard and permits other central-core materials. The five physical layers alternate 0/90/0/90/0. Removing the solid core leaves four veneer axes 0/90/90/0. That sequence alone cannot resolve how to read alternate veneer sheets. The specific sectoral child and its parent rule support this limited interpretation alongside that counterevidence. Verify the actual Betula pendula or Betula pubescens faces and crossbands, pine or spruce strip core, crossed axes, adhesive, supplier and finished state. The Paged technical sheet documents a birch option without identifying the species in a particular lot.

This decision supersedes the current effect of the [10 November hold](2026-11-10-hold-blockboard-cpc-associations.md) for 31423. The 6 November acceptance and its supersession remain historical. The governing five-digit parent comes from the CPC 3.0 publication, with the two-digit sectoral suffix retained only as supporting evidence. The WCO HS 2022 blockboard branch 4412.52 corroborates the physical distinction. A generally applicable UNSD correction or final HS–CPC correspondence for these competing notes was not established. This is a scoped library decision.

The independent reviewer inspected the retained methods and hash-verified UNSD originals. The primary controller separately read the published October files, competing standard notes, original physical construction evidence, historical decisions, and native/measurement qualifications. Current state is candidate with authored methodology and reviewed Chinese. Independent scientific expert approval, study-specific qualification and publication remain outstanding.
