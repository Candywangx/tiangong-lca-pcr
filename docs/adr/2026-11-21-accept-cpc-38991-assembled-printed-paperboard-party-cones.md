# CPC 38991 assembled printed paperboard party hats

Accept a narrower association for an open-bottom printed paperboard cone worn for a celebration, released with a cured emulsion-bonded seam and attached elastic chin cord. An actual order must show the conventional sheetfed printing route, board, converting and assembly. A blank sent flat for the recipient to assemble has a different production endpoint. Ordinary headwear and a conical container require different intended functions.

Use 1 kg net first-accepted assembled hats of the same declared design and condition. Record actual accepted units and measured net mass, fitness, first release and rejects. Account for the bill of materials, cutting and print losses, inactive and rejected jobs, real drying or setting exchanges, stock and later custody. Keep the packaging mass and supply burdens separate from net hat mass.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/38991.json).
