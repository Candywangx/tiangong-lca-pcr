## Decision

Accept CPC 3.0 12011 as exact to pcr.ores-and-minerals-electricity-gas-and-water.crude-petroleum-and-natural-gas.crude-oil: conventional reservoir crude, well development, lift, separation, produced-water management and production-gate supply. Exclude oil-sand/shale recovery and synthetic crude upgrading. Offshore extension requires field-specific evidence; the onshore IFC source does not establish its inventory.

Accept CPC 3.0 12012 as narrower to pcr.ores-and-minerals-electricity-gas-and-water.crude-petroleum-and-natural-gas.petroleum-oils-and-oils-obtained-from-bituminous-minerals: oils from oil sands, in-situ bitumen recovery and oil-shale retorting, with actual upgrading when needed for the declared synthetic-crude state. SIEC 4500 includes other non-conventional oils and standalone hydrogen. Coal/gas conversion and standalone hydrogen are explicitly outside this authored boundary. The normalized label and UN CPC explanatory notes, printed pp.56–57, establish this distinction; they do not supply process quantities.

The repository owner requested direct candidate authoring without Harness. This direct-author decision accepts bounded classification applicability; it does not claim independent methodology review, publication approval or completion of the wider statistical category. Existing accepted edges are preserved. The parent12010 remains a differentiated umbrella record rather than a substitute for route-specific selection.

## Readiness and evidence

Both targets have authored English and aligned Chinese, atomic exchange cards, finite collection protocols and deterministic projections. Conventional crude removes mineral feed, spent shale, oil-sand tailings and upgrading. Mineral-derived oils remove reservoir petroleum resource, associated reservoir-gas output and well-development cards. IFC onshore production-separation evidence applies only within its stated context; NRCan mining/in-situ oil-sand processing and EPA archived oil-shale retorting support their specific routes. Parent content is prior evidence, not an independent original source.

The reference is net product mass, with water, sediment, density/temperature and added-diluent corrections explicitly disclosed. Missing identities and independent range evidence remain review items and must be resolved before dataset completion or publication. Shared utilities, waste treatment and emissions are not counted twice.
