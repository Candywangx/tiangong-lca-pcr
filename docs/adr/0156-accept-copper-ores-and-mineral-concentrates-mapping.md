## Decision

Accept CPC3.0 14210 as exact to pcr.ores-and-minerals-electricity-gas-and-water.metal-ores.copper-ores-and-concentrates. Cover copper mineral ores and concentrates, including sulfide, oxide, native-copper and mixed ores, surface/underground mining and standalone concentration at the explicitly declared mineral-product gate. Do not narrow the category to sulfide concentrate or force flotation on ore-only/native/oxide routes. Confirm the identity and allocation of copper-category mixed-metal bulk concentrates. Include any actual thermal preparation only when its output remains a confirmed normally prepared mineral ore/concentrate feed; transformed phases, sulfur/offgas balances and actual additional exchanges are required. This is not automatic acceptance of every calcine as ore.

Separate mineral ores/concentrates from copper-bearing industrial slag/dross/scrap, smelting matte, cement copper, metal products, leach solutions and refined compounds. Exclude transport-only services and other declared metal-category reference products. Copper content alone cannot make a slag, freight service or refined metal into this mineral product.

The owner authorized candidate completion with one fresh independent worktree per PCR and no Harness production. This accepted product-boundary decision does not establish independent methodology review or publication approval.
