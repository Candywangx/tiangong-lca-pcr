# CPC 39130 mechanically dewatered fresh wet potato starch pulp

Accept the narrower association for fresh wet potato starch pulp obtained by mechanical dewatering of the separated potato fibre residual stream and released unblended and unfermented for a named cattle-feed use. The accepted boundary excludes primary starch and other starch processing products, generic potato processing waste, mixed feed, ensiled pulp and dry pellets.

Use measured net wet product mass at first whole-lot release, with separate product-state assays and treated-stock accounting. Follow the actual supplier hand-off and inherit documented joint-process burdens once. Trace delivery, returns and their services to the actual release cohort. The PCR retains candidate authored methodology and aligned Chinese.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39130.json).
