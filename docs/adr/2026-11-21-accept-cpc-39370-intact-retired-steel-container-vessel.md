# CPC 39370 retired steel container vessel at first recycling-yard acceptance

Accept a narrower association for an identified retired commercial steel container vessel, first accepted intact by the specified ship-recycling facility before structural cutting. Bind the hull identity, actual configuration at handover, owner/custodian, withdrawal from service, documented decision to dismantle, receiver acceptance and stated dry-mass convention.

The October 2026 CPC3 explanatory note links 39370 to HS heading 8908. A vessel sold for continued operation, a charter handback, loose steel and recovered equipment, and a contract to tow or dismantle are different products or services. The owner’s historical recycling cases establish the existence of this boundary but do not establish a current vessel’s mass, condition or regulatory approval.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39370.json).
