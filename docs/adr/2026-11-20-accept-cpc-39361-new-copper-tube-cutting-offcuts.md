# CPC 39361 new copper-tube cutting offcuts

Accept a narrower association for separately collected solid tubular offcuts generated during the actual hand cutting of qualified new, uncoated, unalloyed copper tube into useful lengths. The declared product first meets a named copper recovery receiver’s clean material-feed agreement. Old pipe, wire, mixed or coated metals, chips, ashes, smelted material and treatment services fall outside this scope.

The unit is 1 kg of whole net accepted offcuts at first release. Incoming tube retains attributable upstream burdens. Useful lengths, chips and offcuts have separate physical accounts. Manufacturing burdens follow the documented allocation hierarchy. Delivery and returns refer to the original cohort. Grade certificates, measured quantities, receiver agreement and compatible native product identity require foreground evidence.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39361.json).
