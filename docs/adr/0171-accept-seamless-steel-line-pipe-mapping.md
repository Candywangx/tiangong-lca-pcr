## Decision

Accept CPC 3.0 `41281` as an exact link to `pcr.metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.basic-metals.line-pipe-of-a-kind-used-for-oil-or-gas-pipelines-seamless-of-steel`. The normalized classification covers seamless steel pipe for oil or gas pipelines. The methodology retains actual carbon, alloy and high-chrome feed/grade qualifiers and conditional hot piercing/rolling or extrusion, cold finishing, heat treatment, inspection, hydrotesting and contract coating. It distinguishes solid feed from supplied hollow tube and links supplier-completed operations instead of repeating their foreground burdens.

Welded pipe, drilling casing/tubing/drill pipe, noncircular profiles, fittings and installed pipeline construction/use are excluded. The classification link does not establish suitability of a particular grade, delivery condition or manufacturing route. It never establishes conformity with an unseen API or other order specification.
