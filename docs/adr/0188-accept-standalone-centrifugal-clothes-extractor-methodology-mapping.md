Parent independently reviews bilingual scope, original manufacturer evidence, current UN category, direct product/utility identities and finite measurement rules. The decision accepts correspondence to the named centrifugal category; it does not automatically classify every household device as 44911 or create any other edge.

Current CPC3 explanatory pages 239 and 241 name both 44812 household washing/drying and 44911 centrifugal clothes driers without resolving every household model. The methodology therefore requires actual design, sole/principal function, marketed use and authoritative classification review for individual household assignments. Original WCO HS2022 Chapter84 separates centrifugal clothes-dryers at 8421.12 from washing machines with built-in centrifugal driers at 8450.12 and other textile drying machines at 8451. The method keeps independently evidenced household and industrial configurations distinct and excludes integrated washing, heated/heat-pump drying, unrelated centrifuges and separately supplied parts.
