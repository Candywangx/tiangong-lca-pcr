## Evidence and methodology

Original BGS Salt January2006 PDF pp.6–7 and USGS Salt2018 (June2023 advance release) PDF p.2/printed63.1 were acquired and registered before extraction, relevant text read and pages visually inspected by the author. Parent review checked the hash-bound fragments and original route text. Historical UK purity, yields, extraction fractions, production quantities, waste totals and no-waste assertions are not defaults. USGS brine statistics represent anhydrous salt content, not as-received solution mass. Sea-water supply has independent foreground measurement protocols. The salt physical gate excludes downstream chemical manufacture.

Eight processes contain42 individual exchange cards. Purification chemicals, anticaking additives, electricity, steam, gas, fuel, water, brine/sludge/screen rejects and actual receiving-environment pollutant species remain separate conditional rows; further actual flows must be added individually. Source methods do not impose a universal additive recipe, yield, salinity or weather-driven evaporation factor.

Positive D is independently accepted net as-received kg of one chosen product, excluding packaging/rejects/returns. Solid dry solids = D*(1-w), dry-basis NaCl mass = D*(1-w)*x; aqueous solution NaCl = D*c. Sea-water D uses directly weighed net output or matched shipment volume times measured density at batch temperature/composition. Total salinity is not pure NaCl assay. D remains the common denominator, and internal brine circulation cancels. Reconcile water/ionic inputs, actual precipitation/evaporation/seepage, coproduct bittern/co-salts, wastes, inventory changes and uncertainty. Gross/background paired marine releases are declared; natural intake chloride is not automatically new pollution.
