Complete and partial bought units embed completed upstream work once; subsequent site manufacture is separate. Every atomic identity was actually queried and adopted UUIDs independently checked for state, type, native properties/units, chemistry, classification, supplied state, geography/provider and official Chinese names. Supported rules require stream-specific assays, water and solvent fate closure, actual post-control species measurements, residual-only utilities and independent gross supply/return heat.
