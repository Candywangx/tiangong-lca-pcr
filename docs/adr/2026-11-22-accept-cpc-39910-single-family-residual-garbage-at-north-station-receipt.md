# CPC 39910 single-family household residual garbage at first North Transfer Station receipt

Accept a narrower association for a traceable lot of mixed Seattle single-family residential residual garbage after source separation, first accepted at the North Transfer Station tipping floor. Establish household origin, collection route, material and receiver records. Weigh the whole as-received mixture.

Exclude commercial, multifamily and self-haul loads, separately collected recyclables and organics, industrial and clinical waste, sewage sludge, ash, later consolidation and downstream disposal. Transport and treatment services cannot stand in for the physical residual.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39910.json).
