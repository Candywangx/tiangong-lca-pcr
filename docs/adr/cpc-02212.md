# CPC 3.0 02212 mapping decision

## Semantic product boundary

This PCR governs foreground data packages for unprocessed buffalo milk at the producing dairy farm gate, whether handed over warm or chilled by that farm. Declare buffalo species, farm, herd/lactation period, raw state, temperature, fat/protein or solids, and accepted/rejected mass. Exclude milk from cattle or goats, pasteurization, standardization, consumer packaging, independent cooling centres and post-handover transport. Live buffalo are a separate product.

## Decision

Retain the accepted `exact` relation from CPC 3.0 `02212` (Raw milk of buffalo) to `pcr.agriculture-forestry-and-fishery-products.live-animals-and-animal-products-excluding-meat.raw-milk-of-buffalo`, including its recorded decision attribution and time. The candidate's flow cards carry their own identity, quantity, range, route and validation conditions. Unresolved identities require concrete verification before final exchange creation. Shared-asset references have been removed from the migrated record. The accepted classification edge does not establish publication readiness, UUID completeness or complete measurement-check coverage. The generated projection is rebuilt from both canonical Markdown files.

## Current scope clarification (2026-10-09)

The current candidate PCR implements the [official CPC 3.0 02212 explanatory note](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2100/02212): the final species-specific raw-milk product is unskimmed/not partly skimmed and has a measured milk-fat mass fraction of at least 3.5% (35 g/kg milk). Lower-fat milk is outside this declared CPC-linked scope, including naturally lower-fat lots. Composition must be evidenced for the actual handover lot; missing evidence does not establish eligibility. This is a classification boundary, not a milk-quality pass limit or an instruction to alter composition. Reference normalization remains the actual measured net milk mass.

This dated clarification aligns the current scope with the retained exact relation. It does not rewrite the historical decision attribution or time, create another mapping edge, or establish methodology/translation approval or publication readiness.
