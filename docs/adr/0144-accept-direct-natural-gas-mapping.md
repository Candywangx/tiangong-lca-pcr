## Decision

Accept CPC 3.0 12020 as exact to pcr.ores-and-minerals-electricity-gas-and-water.crude-petroleum-and-natural-gas.natural-gas-liquefied-or-in-the-gaseous-state. The normalized classification and CPC explanatory notes identify natural gas in gaseous or liquefied states. The methodology fixes one actual gas or LNG product and one declared gate for each dataset, separating integrated reservoir recovery from supplied-gas processing and standalone LNG regasification. Manufactured coal gas, separate petroleum-gas products, standalone hydrogen and customer combustion are excluded. Shipping and transmission enter only within the declared gate; supplier datasets carry upstream stages outside that gate. Offshore field recovery requires field-specific evidence instead of inheriting onshore operations.

The repository owner authorized direct candidate completion without Harness, with one fresh worktree per PCR. This decision accepts classification applicability; it does not constitute independent methodology review or publication approval.
