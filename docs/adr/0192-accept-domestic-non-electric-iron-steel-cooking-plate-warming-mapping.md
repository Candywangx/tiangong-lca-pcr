# Accept domestic non-electric iron or steel cooking and plate-warming methodology mapping

Decision time: 2026-10-01T21:03:50.615768Z.

Accept CPC 3.0 44821 as an exact positive methodology edge to `pcr.metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.special-purpose-machinery.cooking-appliances-and-plate-warmers-non-electric-domestic-of-iron-or-steel`, a material candidate for domestic iron/steel cooking appliances and plate warmers with non-electric principal cooking heat. The current original CPC hierarchy distinguishes non-electric parent 4482 and adjacent heating-only, electric thermal and non-domestic categories. The reviewed method retains gas, solid-fuel and liquid-fuel architectures, cast iron, stainless sheet and mixed iron/steel assemblies. Ancillary electric ignition or controls do not independently decide principal thermal function. Combined cooking/heating and ambiguous hybrids require actual functional and authoritative classification review; this mapping is not an automatic ruling for an individual marketed appliance.

The actual ESSE original wood-fired cooker pages support cooking with incidental heat as a counterexample to blanket heating exclusion. Actual Rangemaster gas-hob and factory-guide originals support stainless/gas and cast-iron supports, conditional pressed/cut/washed/polished/enamelled steel, folded frames and repeated factory testing. Actual AGA and Rayburn originals support cast-iron melting, reusable packaging, oil/gas cooking and separately controlled heating configurations. General original foundry guidance supplies conditional process decomposition only. Electric neighbour products, whole-company environmental percentages, brochures' weights/output/times and marketing claims of carbon-free HVO are not manufacturing quantities or emissions factors. Standalone plate warmers remain covered with explicit unresolved actual design and supplier evidence; an integrated warming oven does not establish standalone manufacture.

No directly read product UUID establishes the full reference or concrete supplier interfaces. The reference identity remains explicitly unresolved with verified Mass property, mass unit group and kg support; unrelated steel and elementary methane hits are rejected. Grades, chemicals, glass, refractory, seal, purchased component, fuel, waste and emissions identities and empirical ranges remain actual collection gaps. Missing identity neither narrows the category to a searched model nor establishes dataset completeness.

Both language renderings and the deterministic structured projection require same-configuration calibrated accepted net mass D, accepted count N and M=D/N, with attributable period Q including reject/rework burdens. Packaging, rejected output, free test media and food are excluded from D. Purchased finished enamelled bodies, burners, valves and embedded materials carry manufacture once; only actual remaining local operations are added. Factory combustion/tests remain foreground while later household cooking fuel and food service are downstream.

The projected water, contained-species, solvent and utility rules use every term's own moisture/assay and wet/dry basis, stocks, reactions, returns and combined uncertainty. Capture is not destruction; an unresolved solvent residual is not an air release. Shared utilities are only unassigned residual after process loads, with generation, exports and storage reconciled and negative residual investigated. Supplied steam mass times its actual common-reference enthalpy and separately measured condensate return establish MJ; no default enthalpy or kg-as-energy substitution applies. CO, NO, NO2 and particle fractions require species/size-specific evidence and fossil/biogenic carbon is retained separately.
