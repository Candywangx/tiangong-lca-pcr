# CPC 34330 basic chromium sulfate tanning powder

Accept a narrower association for commercial basic chromium sulfate tanning powder made on the documented inorganic reduction and drying route and delivered in lined paper packaging. Require the actual grade, intended tanning function, state, packaging, lot and supplier records. Other inorganic salts, colour preparations, botanical extracts, mixed organic tanning agents and tanning operations have different product boundaries.

Measure net powder at the first accepted factory-gate delivery for the declared cohort. Distinguish that delivery from newly released material, stock transfers, physical dispatch and returned powder. Document actual basicity, moisture, chemical and environmental analytical bases. Neither the manufacturer’s grade specifications nor a historical patent supplies lot quantities.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/34330.json).
