# CPC 64119 mapping decision

Proposed canonical method: `pcr.cable-propelled-passenger-ride-service`, semantic path `transport-services/passenger-transport/cable-propelled-passenger-ride-service`. The author proposal below was independently reviewed and accepted locally as recorded in the acceptance section.

UNSD CPC 3.0 Explanatory Notes (30 June 2025), p.360, subclass 64119, includes scheduled cable-operated services such as funiculars, teleferics and ski lifts; other scheduled mechanized land passenger vehicles n.e.c.; and non-scheduled vehicles with driver n.e.c. Accompanying luggage, animals and items carried free, and included incidental guides/food not separately charged, remain part of the actual service. The note excludes sightseeing-bus services under 64132. Source: https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf .

Propose **narrower**, limited to actual scheduled cable-propelled transport fulfilling a completed person-segment with matching mechanism, endpoints, geometry, period, direction, quality and incidental contents. The canonical scope stays within the first included family and does not include unrelated classifications. Ordinary leisure travel alone does not make a segment a sightseeing product. A separately fulfilled sightseeing/entertainment experience, resort admission/access or availability engagement is outside this transport reference. No exhaustive 64119 coverage is claimed.

The full leaf was assessed before English authoring. Aerial cabin/chair, funicular/guided rope haul and surface ski tow all deliver movement of a person by a common powered rope installation. EU 2016/424 as adopted, Article 3(1),(3),(7)–(9) and Annex I, provides actual whole-installation and suspended/towed/track-guided mechanisms and common component interfaces. This dated technical taxonomy supports writing questions, not universal installed configurations or current legal advice. Their reference fulfilment, complete common-drive measurement, all-user duty allocation and installation lifecycle can share one method; differing carrier names alone do not warrant duplicate PCRs. Actual aerial ropes/grips/towers/empty returns, funicular track/wheels/fixed-track braking, and surface-tow prepared path/tow device/required equipment remain mandatory independent branch obligations, not interchangeable assets.

Surface tow requires actual person engagement/disengagement and appropriately equipped users. Attributable corridor preparation, snowmaking/grooming, water/additives, land effects and necessary skiing equipment need actual service responsibility and justified lifecycle boundaries. Ownership alone does not make necessary gear zero. A T-bar is not the concrete platter-hanger example. A cable-driven guided people mover qualifies only if its actual mechanism and classification match; its marketing name alone is insufficient. Material human/animal propulsion or other non-cable mechanisms require separate semantic review.

Other scheduled mechanized and unspecified non-scheduled n.e.c. routes cannot be assigned a universal cable method from a residual classification description. They remain explicit source/method gaps rather than being deleted, automatically declared covered, or split into invented PCRs. Additional actual mechanism and fulfilment evidence must determine reuse of reviewed passenger methods versus a materially needed new record. The retained 64119 leaf scaffold is not a material method. Existing bus/car/rail service methods do not by themselves cover a coupled rope-drive installation with these branch interfaces.

One reference item is one actual person completing a declared boarding-to-alighting or tow-engagement-to-disengagement segment, with N positive and actual distance/elevation/direction/quality declared. It is not a carrier, offered seat, resort pass, operating hour, passenger-km or available facility. Count support permits persons without resolving a service UUID. Failed/partial/rescue/zero-output duties, empty carriers, return directions, starts, standby, shutdown and tests remain in their causally justified pools before normalization. Whole coupled drive quantities and all relevant users are measured first; passenger-km, mgh, advertised capacity or assumed occupancy do not establish electricity demand.

Complete actual infrastructure, ropes, carriers, drives, controls, rescue equipment, necessary path/gear/support, upstream supply, maintenance, renewals, relocation/reuse and retirement remain owned once. Whole-installation initial supply and the same initial component bill are mutually exclusive. Delivered manufacture flows convey only verified supply coverage; complete-life duty is an allocation denominator, not a whole-life transport claim. Uncovered operation, replacements, installation and retirement remain separate. Gross electricity import/export, internal regeneration/storage, losses and brake dissipation differ; gravity/counterweight assistance is not automatically exported electricity. Fuel supply, stock withdrawal, actual combustion, spill/recovery/transfer differ; use a compatible whole blend plus composition ledger or replacing physical components, never both.

The manufacturer lifecycle PDF, printed pp.16/20, supports qualitative installation/material/operation/maintenance questions. Its recycling-excluding PCF boundary and promotional comparisons do not become this complete PCR boundary or numerical factors. IPCC 2006 Vol.2 Ch.2 supports actual stationary-source fuel/technology/activity and burned-fuel basis, not a default mobile factor, oxidation value or complete lifecycle result. GHG Product Standard 2011 chapters 7–9 supports transparent boundaries, subdivision and physical allocation; expanded capital/branch/incidentals scope and collection equations are explicitly authored PCR choices.

Twenty conditional atomic cards preserve native quantities. Ten locations reuse eight exact verified public identities; the Count ride service, specific carriers, finished/retired rope, complete motor, mineral formulation and collected wash-water identities remain unresolved where bounded lookup did not establish compatibility. A motor candidate measured in kg cannot silently bind a complete Item(s) motor without supported conversion and gate. Raw wire, furniture seats, source-specific factory waste, treatment/discharge streams and distance services are not acceptable substitutes. Missing numerical ranges do not authorize invented rates or deletion of actual collection/expansion requirements. Checker pass does not establish final scientific approval, actual quantities, complete identity coverage or publication readiness.
