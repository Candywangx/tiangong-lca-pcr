# CPC 38994 polypropylene hair comb

Accept the narrower association for a complete manual hair comb, made as one solid part from unfilled virgin polypropylene by injection moulding through first bare-comb quality release. The official leaf explicitly covers combs. The selected boundary excludes brushes, electrothermic apparatus, other materials and construction routes, and repackaging of purchased finished combs.

Documented first release sets the measured net mass denominator. Later stock and service activity follows the original release cohort. Actual factory receipts must substantiate the material grade, route, quantities and exclusions. The material remains candidate authored methodology with aligned Chinese. See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/38994.json).
