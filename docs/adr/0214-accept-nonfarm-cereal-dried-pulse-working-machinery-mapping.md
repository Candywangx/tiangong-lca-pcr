# Accept non-farm cereal and dried-pulse working machinery mapping

- Decision: accepted
- Decided at UTC: 2026-10-02T19:10:46.924925Z
- Decision-maker: codex-direct-pcr-author

CPC 3.0 `44513` maps exactly to `pcr.metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.special-purpose-machinery.machinery-used-in-the-milling-industry-or-for-the-working-of-cereals-or-dried-leguminou-52304b89`, a candidate authored methodology for complete non-farm cereal milling and dried-pulse working machinery. The full category retains roller, stone, hammer and impact routes, husking/dehulling/splitting, whitening/polishing, milling-purpose sifting and grain conditioning/dampening. Farm-type machines and independently classified cleaners/sorters/graders, parts, dryers, thermal food machines, packaging/transport/storage units require separate boundaries. A complete processing-line advertisement cannot classify every unit. HS2022 Chapter84 specifically retains grain dampening in8437;8437.10,8437.80 and8437.90 remain distinct.

Original Diorit brochure en10/19, Europemill stone-mill body, Satake HR10DDF-T February2022 No7028-00, KB polisher and Buhler pulse/plansifter pages establish contrasting configurations. Mineral-composite stones, rubber rolls and stainless/polyurethane sieve structures cannot silently inherit a universal cast-metal BOM. Catalogue weights, powers, capacities, food output, yields and service life are not factory defaults.

Both languages use measured common-period accepted equipment masses and attributable exchange totals including rejects/rework/factory trials. Bought completed frames and other modules embed their upstream once; supplied partial states and actual onsite work remain explicit. Factory trial grain is separate from user processing and from accepted machine net mass. All atomic identities were actually queried; adopted UUIDs were directly checked for native type, state, property/units, chemistry, classification, supplied interface and official Chinese name. The seed-wheat and luggage-classified film candidates were rejected; LDPE foil retains its actual noncellular/nonadhesive/nonreinforced/nonlaminated supplied grade. Species including measured ordinary-air PM10 and molecular NO2 require their own actual emission evidence, not a default concentration or NOx-equivalent substitution. Unresolved identities and future foreground data remain candidate gaps. This positive mapping does not publish the PCR or certify a future dataset.
