Existing scheduled-bus and rail records do not represent this actual on-demand passenger-car duty pool.

Propose **proxy**, pending independent review. The intended intersection is actually fulfilled chauffeured passenger-car point-to-point transport for a declared passenger party. Mandatory actual purpose, accountable operator, hire model, party/passengers, OD, duration, distance, geography, vehicle/configuration, physical completion and lifecycle qualifiers prevent classification labels or billing units from substituting for the function. The canonical method also serves a qualified point-to-point function in the other leaf, while this leaf includes separately fulfilled vehicle/chauffeur availability and whole multi-destination hire engagements. This is an overlap, not a globally nested narrower/broader relation. Do not claim exhaustive leaf coverage.

One completed person-leg is a declared PCR service object. It is not one party/booking, taxi movement, driver, vehicle-hour, passenger-km or money payment. The reference Count support does not resolve a passenger-service UUID. Actual incomplete/failed/zero-passenger duties, dispatch/pickup/cruising/waiting and required support remain in their causally justified service pool; every actual passenger and other user remains in compatible allocation denominators. Real private/driver travel and multi-platform overlaps require explicit ownership rather than automatic inclusion/exclusion or duplicated quantities.

The shared record is justified by matched passenger transport fulfilment, not a claim that all taxi and chauffeur contracts are identical. The2025high-volume-for-hire utilization notice distinguishes passenger-carrying, waiting-dispatch and pickup duties; its monetary rates, utilization percentages, financing/residual assumptions and geographic policy restrictions are not LCA factors or physical lifetimes here.

Concurrent/independently hired cars have separate real single-car records and comparable strata; chauffeur changes and replacement vehicles preserving the same single-car service responsibility do not automatically create extra output.
