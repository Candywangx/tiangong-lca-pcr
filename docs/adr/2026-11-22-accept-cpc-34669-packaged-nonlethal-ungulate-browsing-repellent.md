# CPC 34669 packaged liquid ungulate browsing repellent at original first release

Accept a narrower association for a registered commercial liquid non-lethal ungulate browsing repellent at its unique original finished-product release. The reference is one kilogram of net finished product, excluding the primary container, closure, label, outer carton and pallet. Trace the actual first event across all producers/contractors and link opaque upstream production and primary packaging to the same perimeter.

The October 2026 UNSD row links frozen 34669 to HS 3808.99. The 2026 EPA letter identifies TRICO registration 71637-2. A primary field-trial report independently confirms the commercial finished product existed. Historical US customs decision W968435 treats a different retail vertebrate-repellent product as 3808.99; limit the comparison to the type of function. The study must resolve any special 34666 exclusions and applicable jurisdiction/label edition.

US finished-stock/label records can support first release before physical shipment. Separate retained stock, previously released returns and any later legally relevant change in packaging or labels from new product mass. Actual additional packaging, storage, return and other services retain their burdens. The method records candidate readiness only.
