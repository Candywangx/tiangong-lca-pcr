## Decision

Accept CPC 3.0 15310 Natural sands as exact to pcr.ores-and-minerals-electricity-gas-and-water.stone-sand-and-clay.natural-sands. This natural-origin product category needs distinct excavation/dredging, grading, moisture, mineralogy, salinity and optional industrial-beneficiation rules. It covers natural siliceous, calcareous and other mineral sands from terrestrial and permitted river/lake/marine deposits, at the declared raw, washed/classified or dried supply gate. It excludes deliberately manufactured sand from nonsand rock, recycled concrete, gravel as reference, resin-coated sand, synthetic silica and downstream mixtures or extraction services. Liberation of naturally cemented sand requires documented natural-grain origin.

The owner authorized candidate completion without Harness, using a new independent worktree for each PCR. Mapping acceptance concerns classification applicability; independent methodology review and publication are pending.

## Evidence and methodology

US EPA AP-42 11.19.1 (November 1995), original PDF pp.1–3 including the construction-sand process diagram, was inspected against bounded extracted text. It supports excavation/dredging, handling, washing/classification/dewatering, conditional industrial purification/drying and particulate/combustion-release scope. IFC Construction Materials Extraction (30 April 2007) supports actual dust/water/waste controls and attributable development/restoration. No historical moisture, particle size, slurry solids, dose, emission factor or energy intensity is adopted as a universal site value.

Six conditioned processes contain 22 atomic exchange cards. Extraction-only sites can bypass washing; ordinary construction sand does not inherit industrial chemicals or drying. Sodium silicate, sulfuric acid and dryer natural gas are separate conditional inputs; each actual other chemical or fuel needs its own row. Joint separated gravel receives explicit allocation and is excluded from reference sand D. New make-up water is separated from circulation; slime, impurity rejects and wastewater have distinct measured states and fate. Saline washing has a conditional chloride release with receiving-water identification and matched concentration/volume collection, not assumed seawater composition. Actual aquatic/land transformations and all other releases require specific additional rows.

Positive accepted net as-received kg D is independently weighed. Dry solids = D*(1-w) uses measured wet-basis moisture with 0 <= w < 1, without changing normalization denominator. Volumetric sale records need measured bulk density, compaction and moisture. Mineral solids, stocks, grades, slime and water are separately reconciled. Development/restoration is attributed once over measured lifetime output; internal recycling creates no automatic avoided-product credit.

Direct-read Elementary/Mass UUID 0d7a3ad3-6556-11dd-ad8b-0800200c9a66 identifies quartz sand / 石英砂 and applies only to actual quartz-sand geological resources, never all natural sand minerals or a supplier product. Reference product identity remains unresolved: the available silica-sand product has ambiguous Chinese/English treatment descriptions and does not establish the whole natural-sand boundary. The cone-crushed manufactured-sand/gravel product is incompatible. Specific unconfirmed flows and missing independent compatible ranges remain explicit review needs.
