# CPC 34662 registered mancozeb wettable powder at first qualified factory release

Accept a narrower association for registered 70% mancozeb wettable powder, a finished civilian fungicide before its first qualified commercial release. Count one kilogram of the whole net finished product with separate environmental accounts for actual external packaging, utilities, residuals and the upstream whole-product supply.

The UNSD correspondence places fungicides under HS 3808.92. The Chinese registration record establishes this holder, product, form and grade. Regulatory batch/quality release records distinguish a first manufacturer or licensed contractor release from already released stock, distribution or a second packing service. Keep actual product and site permissions tied to the period.

Use a single first-release denominator, reconcile unreleased and released stock, and retain separately the physical movement of packaging and the carrying of environmental burdens. Technical mancozeb, dispersible-granule forms and multi-substance products lie outside the scope. Mapping acceptance does not approve the candidate scientifically, release a dataset or qualify a particular lot.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/34662.json).
