# CPC 39940 resin-contaminated spent acetone at first off-site regeneration acceptance

Accept a narrower association for a source-segregated, non-halogenated spent solvent whose principal solvent is acetone, originating in cleaning uncured polyester resin from hand tools in an open-mould fibre-reinforced plastics operation. The reference is the net mass of the whole wet liquid first accepted as feed at an authorised off-site solvent regeneration receiver. Actual identity, contamination, custody and receiver qualification must be established for each study.

Arkansas permit revisions R11 and R13 document actual collection and off-site recycling in a fiberglass boat facility. Condition 37 provides an independent measurement model for the whole waste and a composite laboratory assay. The earlier R4 describes on-site reuse. These revisions refer to the same facility at different times. Federal reports corroborate the industrial route; the site-specific shipment, first acceptance and measured inventory remain study inputs.

Account independently for first accepted material, logistics, component masses, retained inventory and releases. No output for recovered solvent, distillation service, waste sludge or an inferred VOC/credit falls under this mapping. Acceptance concerns the relation to the material method. Scientific and translation reviews remain pending.
