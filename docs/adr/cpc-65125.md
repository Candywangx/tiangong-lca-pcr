## Semantic boundary and reference

Cover actual loose dry-solid railway carriage across open/covered/hopper/gondola/suitable special wagons and actual gravity/bottom/rotary/mechanical/pneumatic handling. Declare carrier, terminal/silo/receiver/compressor/filter handoffs and actual loading, locomotive/consist/shunting/yard/linehaul/interchange, dwell, empty/reposition/failure/cleanup responsibilities. Exclude packaged or intermodal-container service, liquid/gas/slurry, animals/mail, independent nonrail functions, cargo mining/manufacture/milling/trade and bare wagon rental.

Reference1tkm is actual qualified completed net as-carried cargo kg times eligible rail segment km /1000. Original predeclared completed accepted portions or independently documented contemporaneously accepted segments/amended service qualify; earlier travel of later-failed cargo does not automatically become successful work, and failed scope cannot shrink retrospectively. Tare/gross vehicles, empty/yard loops, duplicated interchange/bills and later absent cargo do not add output. Burdens of failed/support/empty duties remain allocated. Cargo/state/moisture/quality/path/route strata constrain comparisons; unsupported wetting cannot inflate output.

`tkm` is exactly the spelling alias of independently verified native `t*km`=1000`kg*km`, with actual property `118f2a40-50ec-457c-aa60-9bc6b6af9931` and unit group `3620148f-c5db-48ce-9065-a10092089aca`. Final TIDAS exchanges use actual native unit name`t*km`; this support does not identify a service UUID.

Customer cargo is custody stock, not a consumed goods-production exchange. Every observation records wet mass and measured wet-basis fraction with M_dry=M_wet*(1-w), or evidenced compatible conversion. Explicit actual water additions/uptake/drainage/evaporation and water in retained/delivered streams reconcile wet/dry/water ledgers; wet mass/moisture need not remain equal. Captured/returned/retained/discarded/released dust fates are exclusive, with actual material/composition/size/medium and receiver. Fine fractions/constituents cannot duplicate total PM. Cargo coal differs from actual burned fuel. Actual all-user equipment lifecycle and native provider covered cells remain unique, with no universal factors or lifetimes.
