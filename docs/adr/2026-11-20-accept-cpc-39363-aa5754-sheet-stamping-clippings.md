# CPC 39363 single-grade AA5754 sheet stamping clippings

Accept the narrower association for bales of segregated solid AA5754-O sheet clippings generated by a documented stamping and trim or punch operation. The declared product is first released to the applicable single-alloy remelter grade. Its source lot, temper, bale and receiver approval are traceable. Dross, mixed alloys, old cans, finished ingots and baling already purchased scrap fall outside this production boundary.

Use 1 kg net dry whole-alloy clippings after deducting separately measured packaging, oil, water and removable foreign material. Manufacturing retains the applicable burden of incoming sheet, the stamped product and all scrap outlets. Post-release logistics and returns use the original cohort. The source grade rules and buyer-specific documents support the scope. Each study must confirm the operative conditions and actual measurements.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39363.json).
