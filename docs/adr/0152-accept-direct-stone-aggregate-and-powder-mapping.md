IFC Construction Materials Extraction (30 April 2007) supports attributable site development/restoration and water/dust/waste management. Historic particle sizes, moisture, capacities and emission factors are not adopted as universal quantities.

Six conditioned processes contain 25 atomic exchanges. Purchased raw stone and natural gravel are separate inputs and cancel for integrated internal transfers. Crushing power, screening/washing power, grinding power, new wet-grinding water, replacement steel grinding balls and actual dryer gas are separate. Each actual flotation reagent, explosive, fuel, packaging component, habitat-specific land/aquatic exchange and pollutant requires its own named row. Ordinary gravel is not required to be blasted/crushed; coarse aggregate is not assigned powder drying. Slime, rejects, beneficiation residue, disposed collector dust and wastewater retain distinct states and fate. Internal oversize and dust returns receive no new resource charge or automatic avoided output credit.

Joint grades and sand/gravel retain unallocated inventory and causality/alternative allocation evidence; purchased offcuts require the supplier allocation/cut-off convention, not automatic zero burden.
