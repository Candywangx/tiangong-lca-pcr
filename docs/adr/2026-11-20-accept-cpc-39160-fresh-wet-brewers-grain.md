# CPC 39160 whole fresh wet barley-malt brewers grain

Accept the narrower association for whole freshly separated wet barley-malt brewing grain, with actual cereal adjuncts disclosed. The eligible endpoint is a first-quality-released unblended wet single ingredient for a named cattle-feed use. Conditional mechanical pressing preserves that identity. Distilling residues, ordinary milling bran, wine lees, other brewery residues and preserved or blended products are outside the selected scope.

Use measured unpackaged wet mass at the first conforming lot release. The reference retains actual supplier joint-process evidence and inherited environmental loads. Distinguish physical capture from burden allocation and trace dispatch services to the original release cohort. The PCR retains candidate authored methodology and aligned Chinese.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39160.json).
