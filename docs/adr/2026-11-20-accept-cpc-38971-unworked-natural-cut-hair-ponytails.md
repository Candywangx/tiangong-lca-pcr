# CPC 38971 purchased unworked natural-colour cut-hair ponytails

Accept the narrower association for purchased, clean, thoroughly dry, natural-colour, unworked human ponytails received with their original cut-end direction preserved and first accepted as raw material. Trace the seller lot to authorized source collection and first tying and cutting. Match actual supplier cleaning, cutting and delivery burdens to that same lot. A previously accepted equivalent hair lot cannot create a second release through resale or repacking.

Record 1 kg net accepted hair, excluding bands and shipping articles, at its first material release. Weigh each lot, retain quality and origin evidence, and separate refused lots returned to the sender, unreleased stock and original manufacturing burdens. The buyer’s technical intake, a haircut service, a donated lot and the later manufacture of a wig have different functions.
