# Accept gas fired Mannheim bulk powder potassium sulphate

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `34632` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.basic-chemicals.potassium-sulphate-sulphate-of-potash`.

The method covers refined KCl and concentrated sulfuric-acid solution in the gas-fired Mannheim route, actual cooling/powder finishing and absorption of HCl as a separately accepted aqueous co-product. Natural/brine, Hargreaves, wet metathesis, double-salt, granulation/coating and other fuel/control variants remain excluded. Whole accepted supplied SOP kilograms and whole HCl-solution output are preserved; nutrient mass, pure salt or active acid do not replace them, and allocation does not scale physical products.

Native KCl volume, acid concentration, actual gas reference state and CN electricity require compatible measured supplier bases. The official CPC34231 class allows sulfuric acid; the actual explanatory defect is its SO3-oxidation comment, not a proven acid-classification conflict. Complete raw-period pools, WIP/stock burdens and unique positive P are normalized once. Supported Chinese headers/input separators preserve all existing source, calculation and quality fields. The Mannheim salt reaction forms no net water; actual combustion/process moisture and vapour already have distinct evidence-backed accounts. Twenty-six adopted identities and two specific waste gaps remain.

Independent controller review checked raw state-100 flow records, official bilingual names, types, media and property-to-unit-group chains, with any source metadata inconsistency retained explicitly for clarification. Physical identity does not establish supplier/background compatibility or actual quantities. The record remains unpublished `candidate/authored_methodology`, author-aligned in Chinese and pending independent scientific review. This association neither covers the complete classification leaf nor approves scientific methodology, empirical completeness or publication.
