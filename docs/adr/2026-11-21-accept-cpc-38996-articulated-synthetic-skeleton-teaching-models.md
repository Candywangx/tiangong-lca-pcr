# CPC 38996 articulated synthetic skeleton teaching models

Accept a narrower association for complete life-size synthetic skeleton models supplied as mechanically articulated teaching articles with the specified pelvic support and wheeled stand. Establish the intended demonstration-only use, order configuration, component inventory and first complete release. Natural specimens, toys, replacement stands and devices supplied for a clinical intervention have different product functions.

Use the measured net mass of individually identified complete models of the same released configuration. Record accepted count, assembled net mass, losses and support components as specified. Distinguish the documented factory hand assembly and release from protective disassembly for shipping and recipient setup. Check actual leg, support, accessory and reusable package custody through each handoff.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/38996.json).
