# Dedicated scope

This PCR applies only to onsite preparation and handoff of declared complete takeaway meals and related beverages at a stationary facility providing neither seating nor waiter service. Record actual site seating/service arrangements and the protected-food recipient handoff gate. Seated limited service, full waiter service, ongoing contract/concession service, event and transport catering, mobile carts/vehicles, vending, beverage-only or snack/ice-cream/cake-only operations, and resale of bought-in meals are outside this selected route. Their occurrence at a shared site requires separate output and resource records; it is not zero activity.

# Reference flow

1 Item(s) of Actually completed stationary no-seating takeaway meal occasion. Actual output row: `no_seating_takeaway_meal_service`. Count support does not establish a concrete service UUID.

# Scope review outcome

The canonical identity is exclusive to this CPC code. Its reference product name and actual product-output row agree in both languages; the normalization is 1 Item(s), without inferring a standard meal size, residence-day duration or fixed commodity equivalent. The explicit dedicated classification gate excludes other purposes/service modes from the target denominator while retaining their actual resource use in shared pools. Each scope is narrower than the entire CPC leaf; unsupported routes remain outside the declared use. No new fixed service UUID is asserted.

The original shared physical-accounting rules remain applicable only inside this dedicated scope. Candidate inventory completion and whole-repository qualification remain separately required; accepted mapping is not a substitute for either.
