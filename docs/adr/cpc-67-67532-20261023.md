# CPC 3.0 67532: Vessel salvage and refloating services in inland waters

Decision: accepted locally at 2026-10-23T09:49:59.061Z by codex-cpc67-local-semantic-review.

Accepted relation: `narrower` from CPC 3.0 `67532` to `pcr.distributive-trade-services-accommodation-food-and-beverage-serving-ser-18ff339a.supporting-transport-services.vessel-salvage-and-refloating-services-in-inland-waters`. This is an individual prospective mapping decision after author/main-agent review, retaining the pre-existing scaffold ID and directory.

Covered services: Inland vessel/cargo salvage, raising, righting and stranded-vessel refloating with identified recovery handoff

Excluded services: Standalone towing/pushing; coastal salvage; life rescue/fire/police; ordinary transport; standalone shipbreaking/clean-up

Reference: 1 item of Completed qualified inland vessel or cargo recovery task.

Required qualifiers: task/object IDs; vessel type/dimensions/displacement and cargo identity/mass; distress/sunken/capsized/stranded initial condition; depth/current/water-level/visibility; freshwater or actual salinity; riverbed/contamination baseline; bridge/lock/access constraints; stage plan and actual route; craft/crane/pump/diving/cutting equipment; real power/load/time; standby/failed attempts; success endpoint/custody handoff; support movement and supplier boundary. Different service configurations cannot be pooled without demonstrating equivalent function, conditions and boundary.

Source-conflict decision: the current UNSD 67532 note both includes and excludes towing distressed vessels in inland waters, with transport towing separately located at 65229. This acceptance covers only the unambiguous vessel/cargo recovery, raising, righting and refloating subset. Standalone distressed-vessel towing is excluded. Necessary support movement within actual recovery is counted once without establishing the applicability of an independent tow. The narrower edge preserves this source ambiguity.

Methodology need: Survey and establish initial condition → actual stabilization/recovery stages → verified state/custody handoff → attributable site clearance/return. The retained record defines category-specific service output, activity collection, atomic exchanges, upstream/asset boundaries, allocation, calculation and validation. The classification leaf alone did not establish these methods.
