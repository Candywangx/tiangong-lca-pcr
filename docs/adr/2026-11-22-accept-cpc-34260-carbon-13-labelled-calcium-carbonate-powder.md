# CPC 34260 carbon-13-labelled calcium carbonate at first quality release

Accept the narrower association for one kilogram of net, chemically finished nonradioactive carbon-13-labelled calcium carbonate powder at its documented original quality release. Exclude ordinary carbonate, natural-range reference materials, other isotopes or compounds and previously released bulk repacked for sale.

The UNSD explanatory table and HS chapter 28 note 6 establish the classification boundary. The original product listing and an independently reported purchase of the same SKU establish a real labelled powder. A dated statement from the same supplier identifies a quality-review event. A study must trace any earlier bulk release through the responsible supplier and verify the qualifying lot, including its separate chemical-assay, isotope-composition and mass records.

Attribute original quality release once per cohort. Retest, resale, shipment or transfer of already released stocks creates no new reference output. Retain actual physical transfers and environmental burdens for each relevant period, including third-party services and original packaging within the declared boundary.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/34260.json).
