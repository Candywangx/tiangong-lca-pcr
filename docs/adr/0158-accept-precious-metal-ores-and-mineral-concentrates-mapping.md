## Decision

Accept CPC3.0 14240 as exact to pcr.ores-and-minerals-electricity-gas-and-water.metal-ores.precious-metal-ores-and-concentrates. Cover gold, silver and platinum-group mineral ores/concentrates, including Pt, Pd, Rh, Ru, Ir and Os rather than gold or platinum alone. Include actual lode/placer, surface/underground and placer-dredging extraction, standalone geological-ore preparation, and actual mineral-specific gravity/magnetic/dense-medium/flotation concentration. Each dataset fixes its mineral product, metal suite and chosen mine/loading or explicitly included receipt gate. Mixed precious/base-metal mineral concentrate requires confirmed category identity and allocation; traces of precious metal do not automatically reclassify a declared copper/nickel/lead/zinc concentrate.

Actual thermal/oxidative preparation is included only when the output remains independently confirmed normally prepared ore/mineral-concentrate feed with phase/element/offgas balance and individually added actual reactants/gases. Do not automatically classify every refractory calcine as ore. Leach solutions, pregnant carbon/resin, amalgam, chemically precipitated metal/salts, matte, anode slimes, dore/bullion and clean metallic precious-metal reference products are distinct gates. Recycled jewellery/catalysts/electronic scrap and industrial residues need secondary-material methodology. Commercial use of concentrate cannot establish mineral identity after metallurgical processing.

The owner authorized candidate completion without Harness and a new independent worktree per PCR. This accepted semantic product-boundary decision does not establish independent methodology review or publication approval.

## Evidence and resulting methodology

British Geological Survey Platinum Mineral Commodity Profile (September2009) original PDF pp14–15 was retained and bounded original text read, with PDF14 rendered and inspected. It distinguishes mineral-specific comminution, magnetic/dense-medium separation and flotation from smelting/refining and explains the role of mineralogical variation. No historical moisture cutoff, stage count, recovery, thermal parameter or market claim is adopted.

EPA Volume6 Gold Placers Part1 (October1994), original PDF pp18–19 was read and original PDF18 rendered. It distinguishes actual placer excavation/washing/gravity/magnetic concentration and water/settling/tailings management from bullion processing. EPA530-R-94-013 Volume2 Gold Chapter1 (August1994), original pp28–30 was read and PDF29 flotation description/national-statistics headers and footnotes rendered. Lode flotation/gravity mineral preparation is separated from amalgamation/cyanidation and downstream metal recovery. Historic national totals, grade/yield, stripping ratios, equipment dimensions, technology shares and legal claims are excluded from PCR defaults.

WCO HS2022 Chapter26 original pp1–2, Note2 and heading2616 distinguish silver and other precious-metal mineral ores/concentrates. The retained original/hash-checked bounded fragment was reused. This is identity evidence, not a new HS mapping or legal classification certification. IFC Mining2007 supplies generic actual mine-water, waste, controls, development and closure rules without product-specific factors.

Eight conditioned processes contain43 atomic exchange cards. Geological gold ore, silver ore and specifically confirmed platinum-bearing chromitite resource are separate unresolved elementary identities. Other PGM deposit lithologies need their own resource cards; a generic product ore or pure metal is not an elementary resource. Shared gold/silver-bearing geological ore must not have its mass counted twice. Standalone alluvial, gold, silver and platinum-bearing chromitite feeds remain individual products with supplier burdens; other PGM feed states likewise require individual identities. Actual placer preparation separates electricity, purchased water, coarse reject and fines sludge. Actual lode/PGM preparation separately represents electricity, steel balls, conditional ferrosilicon medium, water and conditional potassium amyl xanthate/MIBC/quicklime/polyacrylamide. Every actual formulation and circuit use needs foreground evidence, not an imposed reagent recipe. Other actual media, chemicals, oxygen, energy, land changes and pollutants each require individual additional cards.

Actual independently recovered chromite and copper mineral concentrates are weighed co-products. Embedded Au/Ag/PGM/base metals in one bulk concentrate are elemental indicators, not duplicate physical product masses. Mining waste rock, placer coarse reject/fines, final flotation tailings, water-treatment sludge, collector dust and external-treatment wastewater remain distinct. Purchased water, direct river abstraction and internal reclaimed water have different roles. Actual TSS/sediment and dissolved arsenic releases use matched concentration/volume and receiving compartment with background uncertainty, not all managed tailings solids/metals as environmental emissions. Fuel/offgas emissions belong only to actual foreground operations through the chosen gate; optional delivery avoids duplicate provider fuel.

D is independently weighed positive accepted net as-received mineral-product kg. With measured wet-basis free moisture w, dry mineral mass is D*(1-w). Each precious-metal dry-basis assay a_j is explicitly g/t, giving contained element kg = D*(1-w)*a_j/1000000. A dimensionless mass fraction g_j instead gives D*(1-w)*g_j. Percent, ppm, g/t, troy ounces and metal kg are not silently interchangeable. D remains the mineral inventory denominator. Match solid/metal/water balances, sampling/assay uncertainty and stock changes; recovery requires matched feed/output dry masses and individual assays, not concentrate grade or downstream bullion/refinery yield.

Direct-read Product/Mass UUIDca49fc61-8575-4bf4-b072-f96ce6efd56c identifies Gold Ore / 金矿石, production mix at plant. Use only compatible actual gold-ore mineral and plant loading/receipt state. Silver/PGM ores, concentrates, placer variants and thermal states need their own exact identities. The database description of cyanidation does not require that downstream operation for an ore product gate. Generic all-metal feed, tin concentrate and unrelated environmental-pesticide search results cannot substitute for a precious-metal ore/concentrate product.

Retain unallocated multi-metal inventory and justify subdivision, physical causality or actual payable terms/prices/treatment charges and sensitivity for economic allocation. Precious-metal grade alone is not an allocation formula. Supplied/old-stock geological feed retains supplier allocation/justified cut-off and actual rehabilitation burden. Attribute development/closure once by measured lifetime accepted output. No automatic virgin-metal, disposal or internal-recycle credits. Unconfirmed identities and absent compatible independent empirical ranges remain explicit candidate review needs.
