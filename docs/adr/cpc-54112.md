# CPC 3.0 54112 mapping decision

## Source leaf and target

- Official source leaf: General construction services of multi-dwelling residential buildings.

- Proposed relation: `narrower`; this draft alone does not accept an edge.

## Semantic boundary

Whole existing multi-dwelling apartment building integrated energy retrofit using factory-prefabricated insulated facade/window and roof systems, heat-pump heating/hot-water, heat-recovery ventilation and roof PV, ending at joint project acceptance. Named retained qualified apartment building with at least3dwellings; actual surveyed structure, occupied/vacated work state, prior services and retained/replaced schedule. Site integration of factory prefab facade/window/roof packages, actual connections/support/airtightness/weather/moisture/fire details and heatpump/HRV/distribution/electrical/PV/control systems; testing/retests/defectclosure and signed whole-building retrofit acceptance. Whole factory packages exclude already-covered constituent BOM purchases. Own actual removal/returns/waste, provider-paid resources, lifting/scaffold/tools and refrigerant/commissioning fates. Exclude new whole building, facade-only/roof-only/heatpump-only maintenance, uninstalled modules, enlargement/additional storey/dwelling, and future operating-energy/netzero/avoided-impact promises.

Actual route and handover gate: Actual survey of load capacity/thermal bridges/moisture/fire/airtight envelope and compatible services; designed project-specific performance targets; installed anchorage/weather/air/water/fire interfaces and actual required blowerdoor/ventilation balancing/heatpump-hydronic/electrical-PV-protection-control tests; commissioned integrated systems, defectclosure and signed owner acceptance. Annual NetZero is not proved by construction handover. No universal PHPP2007 numeric criterion, panel dimensions, sensor count, apartment count, lifetime or energy saving.

Reference and comparability context: N>0 unique complete accepted building retrofit IDs; not dwellings/facade panels/storeys/m2/kWp/kWh/tests. Record dwelling count and measured floor-area convention as qualifiers/comparability only. Gross metered test electricity imports/exports, retained generation/circulation/load losses separate with resource ownership/no automatic net credit. Precharged factory refrigerant is not repurchased; separately measured field topup/recovery/contained waste/actual species-medium releases need independent fates; no default leak or future use credit.

Reuse assessment: Existing multi-dwelling asset PCR is new site-assembled whole asset at completion and excludes separately sold service/later replacement scenarios. No coupled existing-building survey/prefab-envelope-to-existing-thermal/ventilation/heating/PV compatibility, historic removal states, refrigerant-fate or integrated retrofit commissioning. Individual wallcover/scaffold/roof/electric services can be bounded upstream inputs, never substitutes for the whole accepted intervention.

This semantic record requires retained-asset intervention gates and resource/state attribution specific to this route. It does not claim every possible route in the classification leaf.
