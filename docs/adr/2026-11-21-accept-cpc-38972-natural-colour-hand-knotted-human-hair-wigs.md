# CPC 38972 natural-colour hand-knotted human-hair wigs

Accept a narrower association for a newly made complete wearable wig with natural-colour human hair hand-knotted into a cut-and-sewn, uncoated polyamide 6.6 lace cap. The order must establish the actual hair state, cap material, construction, fixed accessories and first acceptance tests. Bought-in finished wigs, removable hair extensions, raw ponytails sold on their own and independent repair services have different terminal products.

The reference is one kilogram of conditioned net first-accepted complete wigs of the specified configuration. Record the actual count, individual measured net masses, original release events and evidence of fit, seam and knot quality. Reconcile hair preparation, cap cutting, hand knotting, rejects, stock and finishing up to first acceptance. Keep later services and packaging custody traceable to the relevant cohort.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/38972.json).
