## Source leaf and target

- Official leaf: General construction services of industrial buildings; UN CPC 3.0 Explanatory Notes, 30 June 2025, printed p. 283. Includes repair/alteration/restoration of ordinary factory/plant/workshop buildings; excludes mining/power/chemical/specialized-manufacturing facilities.
- Target: `pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.construction-services.existing-single-storey-precast-concrete-industrial-building-roof-and-cladding-connection-seismic-retrofit-services`.
- Proposed relation: narrower. No accepted edge is created by this record.

## Semantic boundary

- Include one completed coordinated roof and cladding connection seismic-retrofit intervention on a retained assessed single-storey precast RC factory or ordinary production/assembly workshop.
- Exclude new whole assets, rebuilding, generic warehouses, specialized plant civil facilities, isolated bolt/anchor supply, equipment restraint-only, cladding-only maintenance and future operation/risk claims.
- Actual approved connection scheme, retained column/foundation/load-path assessment, installation, required tests/inspection, as-built verification and defect-closed engineer/owner signoff govern completion. Count the building intervention once.
- Existing industrial-building PCR owns a whole new site-assembled asset and omits this retained-state-to-coordinated-connection-retrofit gate. General installation and material inputs do not replace it. The new PCR covers only a reviewed repair/alteration subset of 54121. ReLUIS industrial poster supports route roles, not universal case quantities.
