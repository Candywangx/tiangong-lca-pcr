# CPC 39350 rough-cast remelting ingots from alloy-steel fine turnings

Accept a narrower association for remelting scrap ingots made by remelting segregated fine machining turnings from an alloy-steel source and rough casting them into ingots for steelmaking feed. Verify scrap history, absence of further working, required cast form, visible defects, chemical exclusions and first positive acceptance. The method excludes grinding dust, unmelted loose or compacted scrap, regular steel billets and finished castings.

The reference is net dry, newly accepted ingot mass for the cohort. Examine machining allocation, actual melting equipment and any outsourced handoff with feed, yield, stocks, transport and supplier burdens. No port of entry, electricity provider, steel chemistry, furnace technology or national production mix is established by the customs decision.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39350.json).
