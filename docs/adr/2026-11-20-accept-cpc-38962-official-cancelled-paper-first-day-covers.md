# CPC 38962 official cancelled paper first-day covers

Accept the narrower association for the first formation and release of an official paper first-day cover. A genuine stamp affixed to the finished paper envelope receives its authorized first-day cancellation as a collectible. The physical object, postal operator and issue are identifiable. Mint stamps, ordinary unused stationery, customer-owned cancellation services and resales of completed covers remain outside the product boundary.

USPS offers the specified finished collectible. Canada Post independently distinguishes an official first-day cover from separately supplied stamp-affixing, cancellation and return services. Count each newly accepted bare cover once at its net physical mass. Model separately purchased input freight and service activity against actual provider scopes, and carry lot-specific work in progress and failed-attempt burdens into their proper accounts.
