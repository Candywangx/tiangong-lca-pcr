# Mapping decision

Map CPC 3.0 `64134` (Sightseeing services by air) to `pcr.air-sightseeing-flight-programme-service` with the independently reviewed `exact` relation accepted locally below. The record remains candidate methodology with explicit dataset-readiness gates.

The actual UN explanatory note (printed p.361) includes sightseeing passenger air transportation services. The independently defined product is an operator-fulfilled participant aerial-viewing flight programme, qualified by actual duration, viewing/interpretation quality, trajectory/altitude, mechanism and all included content. One completed programme per actual participant is distinct from a seat, ticket, party charter, flight movement, hour of availability or passenger-km. A balloon programme need not promise a predetermined landing airport. Actual included launch, ground transfers, recovery/chase, interpretation and incidental services belong to the same fulfilment boundary.

# Method and route assessment

Fixed-wing aircraft and rotorcraft have actual engine/fuel/flight-phase/trajectory interfaces. Gliders require actual aerotow including towplane return, winch/auto launch or selflaunch energy/rope/recovery; absence of an onboard engine is not zero lifecycle burden. Thermal balloons require actual burner/fan/cylinder/envelope/basket/ground support. Gas balloons require actual lifting-gas storage/fill/topup/vent/leak/recovery, ballast and receiver; hydrogen and helium are distinct from burner fuel. Airships require actual propulsion plus lifting gas/ballonets, mooring/mast/winch/truck/hangar/ground responsibility. Actual hybrid/superpressure/other mechanisms fulfilling the same viewing function require complete native-interface evidence and independent row expansion before application; they are not excluded just because source examples or UUIDs are unavailable.

These branches share a fulfilled passenger viewing programme and the same auditable completion/duty/provider/all-user-life allocation logic. Mechanism-specific native quantities and releases remain conditional obligations, not an aircraft-class averaging factor. Thus separate PCRs solely for aircraft labels would duplicate the fulfilment method without resolving real inventory differences. Existing ordinary passenger methods provide physical/provider interfaces only and do not define this whole observation programme.

Ordinary A-to-B transport with a passenger's private tourist interest, customer-controlled aircraft-with-operator availability, reservations without fulfilment, and primary piloting/training or active jump/adventure/competition functions are distinct accountable products. Equipment/activity names alone do not decide function: actual contract and responsibility do. Independently fulfilled multiactivity holiday packages need actual subdivision or a separately reviewed composite method; included incidental support cannot simply be removed.

# Evidence and limitations

Read retained CPC3 actual note; IPCC2006 Vol2Ch3 §3.6 aviation introduction and phase/fuel guidance; FAA Balloon Flying Handbook 11B actual2024 ch2/6/8/11 and Glider Flying Handbook 13B actual2024 ch7; actual Papillon, Hot Air Australia and Zeppelin operator FAQ originals. Source cases establish physical questions/actual products, not universal operating values or a guarantee every flight using that equipment is sightseeing. FAA ch11 mainly addresses zero-pressure gas balloons; actual other architecture interfaces remain required, not presumed absent. ICAO ICEC actual v13.1 August2024 is only a scheduled-flight operational CO2 calculator and supplies no universal sightseeing lifecycle/nonCO2 factor. GHG Product2011 chapters7–9 support transparency/subdivision/allocation principles; complete capital/support boundary and formulas are explicit PCR design choices.

Full actual lifecycle responsibility includes machinery/ground/site capital, installation, maintenance, replacements, retirement and all attributable unsuccessful/empty/repositioning/standby duty. Known flight altitude/medium is not erased to fit a UUID. Physical inventory gaps and LCIA characterization limits are separate. No default fuel mixture, occupancy, life, emission factor, range, gas purity/density or duration is supplied.

This is semantic function coverage, not a claim every operator/aircraft dataset has complete data. Conditional concrete UUID gaps and actual technical/quantity/coverage requirements stay explicit in the candidate manifest. A particular site's missing data blocks its complete application, without manufacturing a product-scope exclusion. The root review confirmed this exact function relationship before accepting the mapping; the author did not edit shared mapping or indexes.
