# CPC 64115 mapping decision

UNSD CPC3.0 Explanatory Notes (30June2025),p359,subclass64115: Taxi services. Source: https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf

Canonical candidate: `pcr.on-demand-passenger-car-journey-service`, semantic path `transport-services/passenger-transport/on-demand-passenger-car-journey-service`. Retained taxi/car-with-operator classification directories are empty scaffolds; supplied-car manufacture and car rental without operator are different products. Existing scheduled-bus and rail records do not represent this actual on-demand passenger-car duty pool.

Propose **proxy**, pending independent review. The intended intersection is actual motorized passenger-car taxi point-to-point service; a non-scheduled airport car transfer qualifies only with matching fulfilment. Mandatory actual purpose, accountable operator, hire model, party/passengers, OD, duration, distance, geography, vehicle/configuration, physical completion and lifecycle qualifiers prevent classification labels or billing units from substituting for the function. The canonical method also serves a qualified point-to-point function in the other leaf, while this leaf includes motorcycle taxis, larger non-scheduled shuttle configurations and other unassessed vehicles. This is an overlap, not a globally nested narrower/broader relation. Do not claim exhaustive leaf coverage.

One completed person-leg is a declared PCR service object. It is not one party/booking, taxi movement, driver, vehicle-hour, passenger-km or money payment. The reference Count support does not resolve a passenger-service UUID. Actual incomplete/failed/zero-passenger duties, dispatch/pickup/cruising/waiting and required support remain in their causally justified service pool; every actual passenger and other user remains in compatible allocation denominators. Real private/driver travel and multi-platform overlaps require explicit ownership rather than automatic inclusion/exclusion or duplicated quantities.

The shared record is justified by matched passenger transport fulfilment, not a claim that all taxi and chauffeur contracts are identical. CPC describes general distance/time billing differences; that alone does not establish different functions. DfT's2023England-only guidance, section2Question2 and3.2, describes a typical chauffeured A-to-B function; it supports conditional semantic reuse, without exporting licensing law or proving every hire is a transport leg. A separately fulfilled vehicle/chauffeur availability period or whole multi-destination engagement stays outside this reviewed method and needs its own later semantic assessment. No duplicate PCR is created merely for a classification code.

New York TLC's2025taxi field dictionary distinguishes meter times, passenger number, distance, OD and payment/void fields; it is an example collection structure, not universal occupancy or proof of actual completion. The2025high-volume-for-hire utilization notice distinguishes passenger-carrying, waiting-dispatch and pickup duties; its monetary rates, utilization percentages, financing/residual assumptions and geographic policy restrictions are not LCA factors or physical lifetimes here.

Review requires atomic actual fuels/materials/wastes/species/native media; exact provider-stage ownership; manufacture-only vehicle/battery gates with complete-life all-user allocation; independent uncovered maintenance/retirement; no lifecycle-zero assumption for EVs; bilingual/projected rule and native denominator equivalence; and final actual evidence before use. Draft only: author adds no accepted edge, and checkerpass does not establish publication readiness.
