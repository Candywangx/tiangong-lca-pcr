# Accept natural langbeinite cold leach bulk fertilizer

The semantic PCR covers natural retained K2Mg2(SO4)3 fertilizer from mined ore by cold impurity leaching/washing, debrining, actual drying/sizing and uncoated bulk dispatch. It excludes other minerals, synthetic sulfate blends, heavy-media/flotation, added binders and chemical conversion with KCl to K2SO4.
