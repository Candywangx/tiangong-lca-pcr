Covered services: Transportation/warehousing/distribution logistics planning/design/support deliverables, including multimodal plans; independently demonstrated non-intermediary transport-document issue/acquisition

Excluded services: Passenger/freight intermediation; sea/air freight agents; on-behalf-client organization/forwarding or customs brokerage; actual carriage/handling/storage; public authority decisions; goods production

Required qualifiers: job/provider and client; positively evidenced non-intermediary planning/document role and separability; no client forwarding/carrier procurement/customs brokerage; family or eligible package; actual activity and acceptance scope; start/end and effort; modes/routes/legs/handoff nodes; consignments and cargo attributes as descriptors; counterparties and responsibility limits; logistics network/sites/horizon/scenarios/constraints/deliverables; document types/identifiers/master-house relation/versions/copies/languages/channels; procurement versus issue and completion evidence; corrections/rework; computing/network configuration and suppliers; site/travel/asset boundaries; period and allocation. Different service configurations cannot be pooled without demonstrating equivalent function, conditions and boundary.

Counterevidence decision: the same UN explanatory notes place sea-freight forwarders, air-cargo agents, organization/forwarding of consignments on behalf of clients and customs brokers under 85630, while 67910 lists multimodal arranging/organizing and excludes intermediation. This narrower acceptance covers positively demonstrated logistics planning/design/support and separable independent non-intermediary transport-document functions. It does not resolve every arranging/forwarding boundary. Absence of vehicles, fee structure or a coordination label is insufficient; inseparable client-forwarding/carriage-procurement/customs-brokerage work is excluded.
