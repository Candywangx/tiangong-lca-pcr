## Semantic boundary

Full man- or animal-drawn road-vehicle freight, all actual cargo, cart and draft species states. One actual accepted consignment leg with quantity, OD/distance/terrain/time and quality declared. Human/draft animal supports traction and is not cargo or final output. Excludes passenger, motorized, unvehicled carrying/pack-back, bare hire, independent courier function and separately classified 65118 live-animal transport service. Carried live cargo is distinct from the draft animal providing traction and is not automatically excluded from an otherwise qualifying drawn-vehicle freight leg; actual function/equipment and classification resolve overlap without duplicate outputs. Includes actual husbandry/feed/water/manure, full animal/cart/path/support lifetime and causal empty/failed/rest duties. Human baseline/extension explicitly defined; no invented metabolism or animal defaults. Native supplier coverage exclusive, all real outputs and users retained.
