Ferruginous manganese dry Mn >=20% criterion applies only to that expressly specified boundary; no universal ore purity is imposed. Heading2613 admits both roasted and unroasted Mo concentrates. Registered/read EPA1994 Lead-Zinc section1.4/Table1-4 and BGS2011 tungsten Figure3/beneficiation supply distinct conditional circuits. Parent visually inspected BGS original p9 Figure3 and read p10 gravity/flotation/magnetic/electrostatic separation, distinguishing concentrate from APT/oxide/metal manufacture. No historical grade, reagent dose, recipe, recovery or intensity is adopted; other families require actual independently collected route/phase records. IFC mining covers controls, development/closure, water and residues.

Six processes73 atomic cards separate every named resource/supplied ore, actual utility/reagent, admitted roast, waste and species release. Independently weighed accepted whole-product positive D kg is the denominator. Dry D*(1-w), contained element D*(1-w)*gE and explicitly converted oxide-equivalent assays remain distinct observations, not normalization to contained metal or assumed pure mineral. Geological contained-element resources are distinct from supplied bulk ore. Reconcile element-by-element and dry solids/water/stocks; actual roast includes input/output phase/sulfur/oxygen/water/SO2/dust/captured-residue balances without universal reaction/yield. Mixed concentrate constituents are not automatically separate sold co-products; causal or justified actual matched economic allocation requires accepted output/specification/fate and retained unallocated totals. No automatic backfill, aggregate, metal or waste-treatment credits.
