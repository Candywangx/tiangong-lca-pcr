# CPC 39141 raw wet bagasse

Accept the narrower association for untreated, undepithed, loose wet sugarcane bagasse from the last juice extraction outlet through documented industrial material receipt. The selected product is the separated cane fibre. Exclude beet pulp and other sugar wastes, downstream pith separation and conversion, treated or densified material and fuel as the reference function.

The reference denominator is measured wet mass at first conforming release. Carry original upstream burden once and reconcile holding, transport and receipt to the original release cohort. Actual receiver quality, moisture and supplier records define each study application. The PCR retains candidate authored methodology and aligned Chinese.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39141.json).
