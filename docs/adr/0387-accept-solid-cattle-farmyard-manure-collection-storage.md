# Accept solid cattle farmyard manure collection and storage subset

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `34654` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.basic-chemicals.excreta-of-animals-useful-for-manure-fertilizer-and-fuel-preparation`.

Only bulk moist solid cattle excreta with declared existing straw bedding and urine, scrape collection, solid storage, actual foreign-material removal and agricultural-use supply. Composting, digestion, forced drying, sanitisation and fuel routes are excluded. EPA and IPCC sources establish process and measurement context without default emission factors, nutrient grade or agricultural equivalence. Incoming waste/residual/co-product status and actual farm-gate value, upstream method and inherited burdens must be evidenced. PEF supplies a bounded hierarchy and conditional cattle example, not universal dairy/IDF/CFF or zero-burden defaults. Net vapour requires matched atmospheric water inflow/outflow or independently validated measurement; rainfall is separate. Native NMVOC speciation restrictions, complete raw-period burdens, actual supplied wet mass and one final normalization are preserved.

Independent controller review checked raw state-100 flow records, official bilingual names, types, media and property-to-unit-group chains, with any source metadata inconsistency retained explicitly for clarification. Physical identity does not establish supplier/background compatibility or actual quantities. The record remains unpublished `candidate/authored_methodology`, author-aligned in Chinese and pending independent scientific review. This association neither covers the complete classification leaf nor approves scientific methodology, empirical completeness or publication.
