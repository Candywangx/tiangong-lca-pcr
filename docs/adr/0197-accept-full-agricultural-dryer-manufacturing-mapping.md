Accept CPC 3.0 44518 as an exact edge to manufacture of complete equipment principally designed for agricultural-product drying. Batch/recirculating grain dryers, continuous-flow grain/seed equipment and dedicated fruit/vegetable/leaf-product cabinet configurations remain distinct. Actual direct/indirect combustion, electric, steam/water and passive/assisted solar interfaces are conditional and need actual design evidence. A grain tower is not a proxy for every family. Mixed-use and independently supplied assemblies require principal-purpose/supply review; other-material dryers 44912, whole processing plants, civil works, crop production and drying services are outside this equipment boundary.

Independent review read original Cimbria continuous-flow brochure, actual October 2024/EN footer, modular galvanized sections, conditional stainless/DOCOL1200 option, fan/burner/sector-valve discharge interfaces; OPICO retained batch/recirculating source with separate LPG/diesel configurations and factory-fit dust options; and rendered FAO 2008 fruit/vegetable solar cabinet page 60 with wood, polymer/glass and tray counterexample. Specific thicknesses, dimensions, crop throughput, moisture removal and consumer-season fuels are not manufacturing factors. Historical manuals support conditional architectures rather than a default current recipe. Original EPA cover establishes EPA-453/R-02-006 February 2002; its appendix characterization date is separately 30 September 1998. Incorrect shared edition and preliminary verification records were superseded with honest original-bound registrations.

The bilingual record has seven processes and 68 atomic cards, actual make/buy and supplied-module/retained-fill boundaries, test crop only when actual factory trials occur, and configuration-specific calibrated accepted net mass with period Q/N and Q/D normalization. All 68 bilingual IDs match; finite inspection passes 680 checks with 136 rows and zero skipped. Projected balances include each material/species term own assay and wet/dry basis, each water input/product/wet waste/sludge/wastewater/stock term own moisture/density, reactions and paired internal returns, combined uncertainty, solvent capture versus destruction and no unexplained residual-to-air. Shared utilities are unassigned residual after process meters with generation/export/storage and negative-residual investigation. Purchased steam uses separately measured delivered and returned kg times each own MJ/kg enthalpy on the same datum or calibrated net heat metering. CO, NO and NO2 need species-specific evidence; NOx-as-NO2 conventions are retained separately.

The full-category output UUID remains disclosed unresolved, with verified Mass/kg support; unrelated straw dry matter and drying labour are not equipment references.
