# CPC 35450 original sealed civil finished product at first EU market placement

Accept the narrower association for a complete factory-sealed civil finished product within the prepared-product subset of CPC 35450, at its first commercial making available for distribution, consumption or use on the EU market. Use administrative and environmental evidence for the individual finished product. The previous exclusions of a stand-alone packaging service and the first-manufacturer-release boundary remain in force for their original scopes.

The classification sources identify the leaf. The first Union market event is supported by Directive 2014/28/EU Article 2 and the Commission Blue Guide section 2.3. The act of placing an individual product on the market and its custody change can occur on different dates. Separate them in the accounting records and reconcile the physical perimeter without generating a fictitious stock movement.

Use a reconciled net-article reference mass and a verified original environmental exchange inventory for the opaque finished product. The screened programme records distinguish product-stage burdens, packaging and later modules. Keep packaging mass and attributable packaging burdens on their respective bases, retain genuinely covered burdens once, and link subsequent corrections to the original eligible cohort. Programme declarations alone cannot qualify the study or establish an actual exchange amount.
