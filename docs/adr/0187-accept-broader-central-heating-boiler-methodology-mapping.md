# Accept broader heating-boiler methodology for CPC 44825

Decision: accepted at 2026-10-01T20:04:35.479121Z by codex-direct-pcr-author.

CPC 3.0 44825 maps to `pcr.metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.special-purpose-machinery.central-heating-boilers-for-producing-hot-water-or-low-pressure-steam`, a candidate authored methodology, with relation `broader`: the methodology spans complete hot-water and low-pressure-steam central heating boilers, including evidenced electric-resistance designs, while the original CPC parent 4482 expressly covers non-electric domestic cooking and heating equipment. Only the corresponding non-electric portion is classification-matched to 44825. Actual electric equipment requires separate authoritative classification review; this decision accepts no other edge. Leaf-title omission of the word non-electric does not override the parent boundary. Canonical methodology identity remains classification-independent.

Parent review independently reads the actual UN original around pages 239–241, the JRC foundry original fragment, the bilingual canonical boundary and direct rejected flow. Original Weil-McLain 80 PDF page 1, ACV E-Tech W PDF page 6 and Viessmann CI3 PDF page 2 are independently rendered and inspected. They establish cast-iron water/steam, steel electric, stainless condensing and distinct delivered component and factory-test alternatives. Actual publisher Vitoligno 150-S HTML supports wood-fired and refractory alternatives. These documents do not establish universal mass, efficiency, pressure, recipe or factory energy factors. The JRC general foundry decomposition applies only to actual in-house casting, never to all boilers.

The methodology excludes separately supplied parts, radiators, heat pumps, standalone domestic water heaters and high-pressure industrial steam generators. Actual delivered configuration determines sealed fluids/fillers retained in net product mass; free test water and packaging are excluded. Measured accepted net period mass D and accepted count N define M=D/N, q_item=Q/N and q_ref=Q/D, preserving reject/rework burden. Cast sections, completed heat exchangers, burners, pumps and controls are either purchased with upstream burden once or actually made with separate foreground constituents and operations.

Factory hydraulic and actual firing tests include water, fuel, power, condensate, emissions and repeats before delivery. Later heat-service fuel and efficiency are separate. Utility rules reconcile assigned process/test loads and only residual shared demand, with matched imports, generation, fuel, exports, stocks and actual uncertainty; negative residuals are investigated, never clipped. Purchased steam uses measured delivered MJ or each measured supplied/returned mass in kg times its own state-specific enthalpy in MJ/kg at a common reference. Supplier fuel is not duplicated as purchased heat. Water, metal/species and solvent balances use each term's own content, assay, wet/dry basis, stocks, reactions, returns and uncertainty. NOx reported as NO2 is an equivalence convention, not pure NO2 identity; actual NO/NO2 evidence is retained. Particle size fractions and sampling definitions require separate verified exchanges rather than unknown-size dust substitutions.
