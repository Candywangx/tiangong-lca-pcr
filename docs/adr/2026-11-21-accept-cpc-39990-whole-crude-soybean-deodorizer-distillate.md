# CPC 39990 whole crude soybean deodorizer distillate for constituent recovery

Accept the narrower association only for the traceable whole crude soybean distillate collected separately in a single scrubber of the chemical refining route and first released in bulk at the refinery tank outlet for contracted tocopherol or sterol recovery. Exclude physically refined fatty-acid distillate, selectively fractionated condensates, recycled oil, isolated sterols, pooled unspecified streams, brewery effluent and rejected or internally reworked quantities.

The October 2026 UN note connects the frozen 39990 leaf to HS 3825.90. The US Federal Circuit majority in ADM and the subsequent CBP application distinguish the commercially valuable unseparated residue from prepared derivatives. These historical findings establish the selected product family. Each actual facility must establish lot identity, chemical route, recovery destination, residual quality, noninfectious status and applicable safety and waste conditions.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39990.json).
