# CPC 35460 civilian handheld maritime distress-signal product at first French market placement

Accept the narrower CPC relationship for a new, complete civilian hand-held maritime distress-signal article at its first qualifying placement on the French national market. Assess the finished product as an opaque unit using environmental and administrative records. Distinguish its first French placement from factory release abroad, subsequent physical shipment, later declaration and end-of-life collection.

The original UNSD classification and HS heading 3604 support the product identity. The French environmental scheme distinguishes the hand-held group in its 2022 and 2024 reports, and separately reports declared unit counts and tonnage estimated from category averages. The declared reference requires original net finished-article mass for the selected cohort and explicit packaging scope. National category averages or the simultaneous collection of unrelated old units do not measure the selected cohort.

Limit the selected cohort to documented paid or free onward supply, with a physical custody perimeter that includes the actual custodian when the French event occurs. Reconcile national events outside that perimeter and direct domestic own use separately without creating inventory receipts. A first national placement can change status with no movement. Keep supplier environmental coverage, attributable actual service increments, opening stock and old-cohort returns separately traceable.
