# Accept crystalline potassium nitrate conversion association

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `34646` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.basic-chemicals.potassium-nitrate`.

The bounded method covers uncoated crystalline fertilizer KNO3 from purchased solid NaNO3 and qualified granular KCl by aqueous conversion, hot salt separation, crystallization and indirect drying. Generic manufactured KNO3 Product/Mass/kg is limited by the declared product and route; native CN electricity and Chinese-plant KCl grade/location constraints are retained. Whole product, wet mixed-salt coproduct, packaging and steam-condensate physical quantities stay distinct from attributed burdens. Salt exchange creates no assumed stoichiometric water; actual period quantities, stocks/WIP and accepted output normalize once.

Physical identity does not establish supplier/background compatibility or actual quantities.
