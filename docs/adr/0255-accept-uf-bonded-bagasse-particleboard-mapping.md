# Accept a bounded bagasse particleboard association

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31433` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.bagasse-particleboard-uf`.

The method covers uncoated flat panels whose only lignocellulosic furnish is sugarcane bagasse, bonded with supplied UF resin and ammonium sulfate hardener, with aqueous paraffin emulsion and hot pressing. It excludes wood blends, wood-fibre facing, flax/bamboo furnish, binderless processing, other resins and coatings. A new semantic identity avoids promoting the mixed-furnish leaf into an indiscriminate feedstock selector. Official CPC 3.0 inclusion notes explicitly include bagasse particleboard.

Brito et al. (2021), DOI 10.5039/agraria.v16i1a8783, supports the untreated experimental route. The reported P2 failure is retained: no modern market grade, recipe, press setting or performance default is inferred. USDA FPL-GTR-80 (1994) supplies historical distinctions only. EPA AP-42 bagasse (1996) and the acquired natural-gas chapter marked 10/26 support distinct combustion sources without adopting emission factors. European Commission C(2021) 9332 Annex I section 4.5 supports the allocation hierarchy. Bagasse's useful fuel status does not justify zero upstream burdens or automatic displacement credit.

The reference `58b62091-9855-4631-9076-823e8ba59238` explicitly describes adhesive-pressed wood or other lignocellulosic particles. Its additional description of a wood-fibre-faced variant does not make that variant mandatory for the broader identity. The PCR requires pure-bagasse, unfaced board and one m3 of accepted net solid board at the measured gate state. Supplier route, density/moisture and volume correspondence are separately required; the identity is not a generic background proxy.

All 34 rows were searched; 25 rows use 24 distinct verified identities and nine remain specifically unresolved. All 16 elementary rows have verified outdoor-air unspecified identities. Wax emulsion, pith/fines, cured residues, ash fractions and cleanup wastewater retain their specific gaps. Separate material, water and origin-specific carbon accounts precede allocation; internal fuel transfers and purchased bagasse are not double counted. The complete measurement check performed 173 checks with none skipped.

The independent controller checked raw state-100 identities, English/official Chinese names, flow types, media and the reference property-to-unit-group chain. Quantity and supplier/background compatibility remain separate from identity verification. The unpublished record is `candidate/authored_methodology`, author-aligned in Chinese and pending independent scientific review. This association neither covers the full classification leaf nor approves scientific methodology, empirical completeness or publication.
