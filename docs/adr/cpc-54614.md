# Accepted decision: CPC 54614

Accepted target: `pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.construction-services.passive-outdoor-terrestrial-tv-antenna-installation-services`. Accepted relation: `narrower`.

The UN CPC 3.0 explanatory notes identify Residential antenna installation services, including residential antennas, satellite antennas, in-building cable-TV lines and lightning conductors. The target covers only first installation of a fixed passive outdoor directional residential terrestrial-TV antenna with one coax receiver lead-in, actual mast/mount and installation-specific separate mast/coax grounding or bonding to an existing suitable building interface, followed by aiming, acceptance test and signed installer handover. It therefore cannot represent the entire classification leaf.

Satellite, indoor, amplified, rotating, multiple-outlet or distribution services, towers, new lightning-protection systems and earth electrodes, old-equipment removal and later operation/maintenance are excluded. CPC membership does not establish a reusable methodology boundary. The current catalog reuse screen found no material PCR with this same output, handover and acceptance responsibility; component overlap with cable works or complete buildings does not supply that service scope.

Manufacturer assembly, package-inclusion and support guidance supports the selected integration route and distinct mast/coax responsibilities. It does not provide universal supply quantities, electrical design, reception guarantees or asset lifetimes. Permanent antenna/balun/hardware and separate mast/coax inputs are reconciled; bundled parts cannot be supplied twice. The one node owns actual corrections/retests, material exits and handover, with exact resource/period attribution evidence required.

All 15 flow identities remain unresolved; exact Mass and Number-of-items support chains are verified separately, while Energy and hourly Duration support remain unresolved. Candidate content and target checks do not constitute final binding or publication readiness.

The target checker passed 79 performed checks, zero skipped and 13 disclosed Range warnings; repeat sync was deterministic. Review repaired unsupported manufacturer attributions to lifecycle/measurement/identity methods and aligned the official classification title. The PCR remains candidate/authored_methodology with reviewed Chinese translation, zero fixed and 15 unresolved flow identities; unresolved Energy/conditional Duration and actual quantity/resource evidence preclude publication readiness. The accepted decision records semantic mapping only; shared mapping/catalog integration is a separate transaction.
