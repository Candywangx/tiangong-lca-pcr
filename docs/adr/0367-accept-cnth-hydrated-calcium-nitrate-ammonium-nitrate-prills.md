# Accept CNTH-derived hydrated calcium nitrate ammonium nitrate prills

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `34614` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.basic-chemicals.double-salts-and-mixtures-of-calcium-nitrate-and-ammonium-nitrate`.

The method receives wet calcium-nitrate tetrahydrate and adhering mother liquor from the nitrophosphate route, followed by melting, gaseous-ammonia neutralisation of residual nitric acid, evaporation and prilling. Actual calcium nitrate, ammonium nitrate, structural/free water and impurity composition must establish the hydrated mixed-salt product. Pure calcium nitrate, ammonia-free lots, limestone CAN and dry blends remain excluded. Whole supplied hydrated product kilograms are distinct from dry active salt or nutrient mass.
