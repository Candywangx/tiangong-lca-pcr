# CPC 39310 segregated coarse hot-strip mill scale for sinter feed

Accept a narrower association for loose, moist, coarse oxide scale from one segregated non-alloy Chinese continuous hot-strip rolling source, after wet descaling, gravity separation and physical drainage, at its first documented quality release to a specified sinter receiver. The finished feed requires a documented legal transfer basis and actual particle, chemical, oil and moisture acceptance evidence.

CPC 39310 includes scalings from iron or steel manufacture. Its October 2026 note links HS 2618 and 2619. This material falls on the scaling side of 2619. Granulated slag, metallic scrap, rough-cast remelting ingots, fine oily sludge, dust and briquetted feed are outside the scoped method. The receiver, grade and composition must be measured for the study.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39310.json).
