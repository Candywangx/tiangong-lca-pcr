Status: candidate; authored methodology.

Accept CPC 3.0 44813 as an exact edge to complete domestic electric textile overblanket and underblanket manufacture at the factory gate. Passive blankets, standalone parts, medical therapy pads, clothing and seat heaters require distinct principal-function review. Unusual actual heater or battery designs require their own BOM and supplier evidence rather than exclusion solely for an unresolved identity.

Independent review read actual Beurer UB60 original body confirming a mixed-fibre nonwoven, removable controller/power connection and temperature protection; actual heated-throw body; and independent original US6888112B2 and US8698045B2 technical disclosures for conductive-yarn and coaxial cable alternatives. Patented polyester/stainless-filament or polyethylene examples do not establish every manufacturer's recipe. Model fibre percentages, net weight, consumer washing, marketing watts-per-hour and use duration are not factory inventory factors. The two Beurer pages share a publisher and are not independent sources.

Independent direct read confirms db63276c-3e0f-4fe9-80de-b45ff12d515b as manufactured finished Product flow with full electric-blanket identity, Mass/kg support and official Chinese 电热毯. Unspecified plant geography/provider remains a required collected qualifier. Generic winding wire does not establish blanket heater conductor/insulation/sensing identity; all specific inventory UUID/provider and empirical ranges remain disclosed candidate gaps.

The aligned bilingual record has six processes and 34 atomic cards, with 340 finite checks passed, complete coverage and zero skipped. Purchased fabric, heater, sensor, controller, cord and connector interfaces count embedded manufacture once; conditional in-house alternatives require actual individual grades and operations. Same-configuration accepted calibrated net mass, Q/N then Q/D and actual cohort reject/rework burden govern normalization. Projected collection and calculation rules retain each water-bearing term own moisture/density, raw-period water closure with stocks/reactions/paired returns, each species own assay, capture distinct from destruction and no unexplained residual-to-air, and reconciled unassigned electricity with generation/export/storage and negative-residual investigation.

Acceptance establishes category semantics only, not publication readiness or a default BOM. Concrete datasets require actual site, supplier, route, testing, chemical and quantity evidence. Integrated runtime and independent root/original-author full validation remain separately recorded.
