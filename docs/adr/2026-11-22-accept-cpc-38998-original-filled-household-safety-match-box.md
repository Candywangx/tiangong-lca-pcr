# CPC 38998 original filled household safety-match retail box

Accept the narrower association for one kilogram of complete, original, factory-filled, closed and unchanged household safety-match retail boxes at their first finished-product release. The functional inner and outer retail box are included in that mass. Shipping cartons, multipack films and pallets are accounted for as separately identifiable packaging when present. A wrapping around several retail boxes does not establish that each box was individually sealed.

A historical manufacturer photo identifies the distinct Solstickan Hushållsask format. The independent foundation accounts separately for this household presentation. The producer used one matchbox in its aggregate production-accounting unit. A study must establish its particular SKU and lot, the initial release, measured intact mass and useful companion count without opening the product. Nominal contents, charitable payments and group-level environmental intensities do not establish these values.

Keep original manufacture and its environmental inventory opaque. Separate the first-release and subsequent custody books, count each original article and each previously incurred environmental exchange once, and reconcile purchased, non-purchased and returned ordinary packaging within a single physical perimeter.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/38998.json).
