# CPC 39212 undyed sheep-wool yarn clearing cuts for recovery

Accept a narrower association for the segregated, still-spun yarn cuts generated while winding and clearing undyed, unwaxed sheep-wool singles. The recovered material has an identifiable collection and first quality-release gate. The actual yarn provenance, species, fibre content, winding source and receiver specification qualify each study lot. Combing noils fall under 26140 and garnetted stock falls under 39213.

The reference kilogram is net of all tare at the first documented lot release. Later dispatch, returns, outsourced handling, resale and internal transfers retain the original cohort. Reconcile gross wool, moisture, inherited oils, rejects and stocks before assigning common winding burdens. Require independent site and receiving evidence for the specified recovery route.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39212.json).
