# CPC 39270 source-segregated baled LDPE cast-film edge trim

Accept a narrower association for segregated edge trim from natural-colour, uncoated, unprinted, single-layer LDPE cast film, still unprocessed except for separate collection and cold baling, first released as feedstock against the receiver’s acceptance specification. Verify the actual source lot, polymer, foreign matter, state and commercial handover. Retain the original semantic PCR identity.

The October 2026 CPC 39270 note identifies HS 3915. Its ethylene-polymer branch covers eligible scrap. WCO chapter 39, notes 6 and 7 exclude single thermoplastics transformed to primary forms. Exclude usable film rolls, internal regrind, post-use mixed packaging, laminated film, startup purge, virgin granules, reprocessed pellets and toll-conversion services.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39270.json).
