## Physical and lifecycle methods

Actual vessel/convoy propulsion and auxiliary cold loads are distinct across sailing, lock approach/passage/queue, tide/current/draft restrictions, terminal/loading/dwell, empty reposition, return, rescue and failure. Cooling continues throughout actual carrier custody. Shore AC/DC/heat/cold, onboard conversion/compressors/heat-driven systems, pumps/fans/defrost, purchased ice/PCM/cryogen and onboard manufacture have exclusive source coverage. Internal power/cold/heat recovery is not an extra purchased input. Actual customer initial-stock fuel consumed during responsible operation bears compatible fuel upstream once regardless of title/payment, without charging the whole opening stock or original cargo manufacture.

Product and air/fluid temperature histories, original inlet quality and receiver acceptance are recorded; air setpoint or compressor operation alone does not prove completion. Cargo remains freight rather than service-production feed. Exclusive actual delivery/outward return/waste/direct release/closing stock prevents retained or damaged goods from counting twice. Measured wet-basis moisture at each observation supports `M_dry=M_wet*(1-w)`, or an evidenced compatible conversion; actual water addition/uptake/drainage/evaporation enters wet-mass closure. Ice melt is not automatic cargo loss. Actual reaction components/element balances explain respiration where present without invented loss factors.

Refrigerant, secondary fluid, ice/PCM and cryogenic supply/charge/makeup/recovery/destruction/release/closing stocks are separated. Charged loops are not fully consumed each voyage, and recovered material is not also emission. Substance, origin and real receiving medium govern final release identity. Inland intake/discharge never defaults to freshwater; ballast/cooling/melt/bilge/cleaning/sewage/cargo streams and captured waste have exclusive actual fates. Same-cohort full all-user useful duty governs native vessel/hold/cold equipment/gear and terminal/shore/lock/navigation infrastructure, with uncovered manufacture/install, maintenance/docking, replacement and retirement once.
