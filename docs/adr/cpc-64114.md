# CPC 64114 mapping decision

## Source and actual intersection

Actual local school/college or enterprise scheduled motor-bus service; scheduled urban/suburban airport shuttle. The leaf explicitly includes pupil home-school and school-school transport including rural areas: do not force a rural route into the interurban class merely because of rural geography. A public general route merely calling at an airport is not automatically an airport-shuttle product. Preserve the actual airport purpose, timetable, geography and access condition, rather than imposing an exclusive-user rule from another jurisdiction.

## Canonical reuse and material method review

Reuse `pcr.scheduled-bus-passenger-journey-service`, `library/pcrs/transport-services/passenger-transport/scheduled-bus-passenger-journey-service`. The actual function remains one person completing one boarding-to-alighting leg on one scheduled motor bus/coach, native1Item(s). Special-user eligibility, school/shift/airport calendar, operating attendants and safety/heating support add mandatory collection and quality questions; they do not change the native service function, quantity equations, provider-stage ownership, causal allocation or complete-life manufacturing-duty model. Creating a second PCR only from purpose, contract or CPC classification would duplicate this method. The pre-edit semantic comparison and baseline hashes are retained in the author audit and will be independently reviewed.

The amendment preserves public-service cases and their accepted64112/64221 proxy relation. Public/school/employee/airport strata stay functionally separate, with actual distances, timetable, completion N and actual persons. Children receive no assumed adult/seat equivalence; operating drivers/attendants do not become extra passengers. Transport support is distinct from education, employer production or flight outcomes. A reserved-capacity, whole-vehicle-hire or availability output is a different function and is not invented or converted to counted completed passengers here.

## Proposed set relation

Propose **proxy**, draft pending independent review. The canonical PCR spans both public and special-purpose local/interurban motor-bus services outside this individual leaf. The leaf describes broad road/shuttle service rather than proving equivalence to the assessed motor-bus configurations; its note does not explicitly enumerate tramway/trolleybus. Together with the canonical record crossing public and interurban service outside this leaf, a qualified proxy is proposed rather than an unsupported whole-leaf exact or nested-set assertion. Require the actual per-code purpose, route/geographic clause, actual vehicle configuration and declared lifecycle gate before use. This does not claim exhaustive subtype coverage.

A predetermined timetable, repeated operation or contract alone cannot prove special-regular rather than charter service: the adjacent CPC64223 charter note also describes predetermined routes/timetables. Inspect actual continuing delivery function, target users and organiser/carrier responsibilities. EU1073/2009 adopted Articles2/5 give a dated jurisdiction-specific comparison only, not a universal CPC/legal-compliance test or a transplanted licence rule.
