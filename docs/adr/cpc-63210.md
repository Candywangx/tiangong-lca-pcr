# Dedicated scope

This PCR applies only to self-catering private single-person furnished bedrooms assigned to students in residences attached to schools or universities. Verify both the actual student accommodation purpose and the institution-residence relationship for each service interval. Summer visitors, conference participants, worker accommodation and other residential purposes are separate outputs, even when the same room is reused. Physical student occupancy eligibility does not replace evidence of actual received handover and maintained exclusive access/readiness. Whole apartments, shared sleeping rooms, family rooms and catered production routes are outside this selected single-room route.
