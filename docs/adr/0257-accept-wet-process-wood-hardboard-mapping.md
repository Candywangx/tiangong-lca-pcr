# Accept a bounded wet-process wood hardboard association

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31442` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.hardboard`.

The method covers hydraulic wet forming followed by wet hot pressing of wood hardboard, with explicitly declared binder recipe and optional heat/oil treatment state. It excludes dry MDF/HDF, wet-formed mats dried before dry pressing, low-density insulation fibreboard, nonwood furnish and coated/laminated assemblies. The retained unpublished hardboard scaffold matches this method boundary. The CPC 3.0 hardboard leaf supports this narrower route without implying complete hardboard coverage.

EPA AP-42 10.6.4 (2002) distinguishes wet forming/wet pressing and recipe-specific PF, wax and alum. USDA Wood Handbook (2021), chapter 11, supports some binder-free boards and conditional linseed/tung/tall-oil tempering. EPA-600/2-79-008 (January 1979), printed pages 13 and 15, supports wet broke return, white-water reuse and saveall possibilities. The original rendered pages were independently inspected. These historical methods provide no universal recipe, water demand or recovery rate. EPA analytical originals define COD, BOD5, TSS, phenol and free-formaldehyde measurement bases; no discharge limit is inferred.

The reference `2b213638-a6da-4337-8972-72e4afe18969` has verified Mass/kg and no contrary dry-route restriction. The HDF synonym does not authorize a dry-MDF proxy. One kg of accepted board at recorded dispatch moisture, with actual density, thickness, binder and treatment qualifiers, defines the reference. Separate stage ledgers retain white-water/fibre loops and wood/water/carbon balance before allocating environmental burdens.

All 39 rows were searched; 35 use 35 distinct verified identities, while saturated supply steam, tung oil, external-treatment wastewater and process sludge remain unresolved. All 21 elementary rows were directly verified, including five water-unspecified emission identities. Actual direct receiving-water releases and external treatment transfers remain distinct. NOx identity `f79d0f8f-2b0e-49cb-bed0-b1ea0fbd8625` is retained with its monitoring/LCI/LCIA mass definition explicitly pending: NO2-equivalent and actual NO+NO2 mass are not automatically interchangeable, N2O is excluded and separate NO/NO2 cannot be duplicated. The complete measurement check performed 198 checks with none skipped.

The independent controller checked raw state-100 identities, English/official Chinese names, flow types, media and the reference property-to-unit-group chain. Quantity and supplier/background compatibility remain separate from identity verification. The unpublished record is `candidate/authored_methodology`, author-aligned in Chinese and pending independent scientific review. This association neither covers the full classification leaf nor approves scientific methodology, empirical completeness or publication.
