## Semantic boundary

Include actual special tank-truck/tank-semitrailer road carriage of crude oil, natural gas, refined petroleum products and other bulk liquids/gases. Ambient, heated, pressure or cryogenic state and actual propulsion remain conditional routes, not mandatory assumptions. Cover actual custody/collection through accepted delivery and causally assigned loading, pumping, discharge, empty/positioning/failed work, cleaning/changeover, support and asset lifecycle responsibilities.

Exclude intermodal tank-container service, packaged drums/cylinders, dry bulk, pipeline/rail/water/air/space carriage, cargo manufacture/trade and bare rental. Resolve actual offered service and tank configuration before selecting adjacent records; this is not a general freight proxy. Customer cargo and vehicle fuel are distinct custody inventories even where chemically identical. Retained heels are closing stock, outward returns are uniquely identified transfers, and each loss has one actual waste or environmental fate.

Reference: one actual completed qualified tanker consignment delivery, with actual cargo identity, delivered amount/state, origin/destination and distance, temperature/pressure/quality, period, positive equivalent completed N, full duties and allocation disclosed. It is a service Count with independently verified support identities, not a tkm relabelling, cargo commodity, vehicle manufacture or arbitrary pump cycle. Failed/partial/refused work retains burdens under declared completion rules.
