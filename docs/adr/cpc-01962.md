# CPC 01962 Fresh Cut-Flower Mapping Decision

## Decision

Accept a `narrower` CPC 3.0 `01962` edge to
`pcr.agriculture-forestry-and-fishery-products.products-of-agriculture-horticulture-and-market-gardening.cut-flowers-and-flower-buds`.
The PCR covers fresh, undyed cut flowers and flower buds, including their flower-stem content in bouquets, wreaths,
floral baskets and similar articles whose principal function is supplied by those flowers. The PCR excludes dried,
dyed, bleached, preserved and materially transformed flower routes, living plants, planting material, artificial
flowers, extracts and edible flowers sold as food. Accessories remain attributable inventory inputs.

The classification label is broader than this explicit fresh, undyed scope. `Exact` would overstate the evidence.
This accepted edge makes a scoped material candidate discoverable; it does not establish methodology for every
preservation state within the classification, current external certification, activation, publication or dataset
identity linking. A consumer must still match the actual product, route and boundary to the PCR.

## Evidence and limits

- [UN CPC 3.0 01962](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2100/01962)
  names cut flowers and flower buds including bouquets, wreaths, floral baskets and similar articles. Its displayed
  label does not restrict the category to this PCR's fresh, undyed subset. No unsupported preservation-state
  explanatory note or CPC-to-HS equivalence is asserted.
- [Wageningen report 2024-023](https://research.wur.nl/en/publications/product-environmental-footprint-category-rules-for-cut-flowers-an/),
  DOI `10.18174/549543`, identifies the dated 2024 FloriPEFCR technical source already cited in this material PCR.
  The publisher record was checked; the attempted current full-text download returned HTML, so this review does
  not claim a new complete reading of that PDF. Inherited flower-specific rules are retained as dated evidence;
  the repaired balances and residual allocation choices are explicitly specified in this independent PCR.
- [FSI benchmarking guidance, July 2024, sections 2–3](https://fsi2030.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/20240701-FSI-FloriPEFCR-Benchmarking-guidance.pdf)
  distinguishes benchmarking and formal compliance. Neither this guidance nor continuing public use proves a
  renewed validity period. Current PEF/FSI conformity requires separate method/database/version verification.
- General allocation evidence is the WRI/WBCSD Product Standard (2011), Chapter 9. Managed-soil N2O and lime/urea
  CO2 pathways use IPCC 2019 Refinement, Volume 4, Chapter 11, under the applicability and tier rules in the PCR.
  They do not supply site quantities, yields, stem masses or unsupported default recipes.
- A read-only TianGong search on 2026-10-19 and direct read of `487c493e-53fd-46a5-ae32-51c3ecc2bb7e` confirmed
  that **Dyed flower** has a different treatment boundary and mass reference. It was rejected as the fresh stem
  identity. No matching fresh reference UUID was verified. A later read-only canonical support-row export
  independently confirmed Number of items and its Item(s) unit group. The 50-card identity review bound ten cards
  to five verified flow identities (generic electricity, R134a product, LDPE film, generic truck-freight service and
  LUC CO2 resource uptake); forty cards, including the fresh reference product, remain explicitly unresolved.
  Candidate-specific fibre composition, source, receiving compartment, service gate and route restrictions were
  not generalized away. Detailed query and readback evidence is retained in `docs/reviews/cpc-01962-20261019/`.

## Required repairs applied before acceptance

1. Fix the reference to one fresh stem at consumer purchase and link it uniquely to `delivered_stem`. Harvest,
   conditioning and first dispatch retain measured node counts and stocks, with downstream losses applied once.
   A raw node contribution cannot independently claim conformance; only a complete linked cradle-to-grave system can use the purchased-stem denominator. Missing downstream data leaves raw contributions unnormalised.
2. Replace combined material/utility cards with 50 atomic exchange cards across six stages. Specify actual-product,
   formulation, equipment and destination collection obligations without assuming that an illustrative input is
   actually used. Project those obligations into machine-readable data-quality requirements as well as Markdown.
3. Include decorative use and its purchased-stem/post-use-biomass hand-offs. Require measured mass/moisture and
   material-specific fate evidence, separate treatment-service inputs from waste transfers, and prevent duplicate
   treatment emissions and automatic recovery credits.
4. Reconcile opening/closing stocks, counts and transfers; component water, internal circulation and discharge;
   direct versus indirect nutrient pathways and elemental-to-compound conversion; and refrigerant stock/recovery
   versus actual release. Purchased energy CO2e is not a local CO2 exchange.
5. Include perennial establishment/non-bearing burdens and service-life output; aggregate raw burdens and outputs
   before averaging. Define the full residual allocation denominator, nutrient uptake over rotations, and net useful
   heat/electricity attribution for CHP. These explicit PCR choices do not claim external scheme certification.
6. Retain precise unresolved identities and unavailable quantitative screens, candidate maturity and aligned Chinese
   translation. Missing real data must be collected under these fixed methods, never invented or treated as zero.

## Associated methodology closure

The follow-up repair fixes one complete-system conformance contract across scope, reference, process coverage,
collection and dataset profile. It adds route-specific first-release pesticide balances (with the dated PEF
open-field convention restricted to that route), phosphorus loss methods, and the direct-LUC screening/temporal
attribution of Product Standard Appendix B. LUC release/eligible uptake use separate carbon dioxide
cards; the eligible uptake resource identity has been independently verified. Freight retains physical consignment
work separately from the total-leg service allocated by its actual
mass or volume constraint; quantity, dataset utilisation and provider boundaries must remain compatible. Water
ownership distinguishes purchased supply from foreground abstraction. Site evidence gaps remain explicit and
none of these changes establishes current external PEF certification.
