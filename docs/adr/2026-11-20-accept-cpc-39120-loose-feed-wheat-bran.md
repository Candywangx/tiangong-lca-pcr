# CPC 39120 unpelleted feed-grade wheat bran from dry roller milling

Accept the narrower association for wheat bran produced as a separate co-product of dry roller flour milling and released as a loose unpelleted single feed material. The selected scope excludes formulated, fermented or pelletized output, different milling fractions, starch and brewing residues, and bran released for retail human food.

Use net as-released mass including the batch's measured moisture. Link the milling and joint-process evidence at the actual bran fraction hand-off and retain the original release cohort through gate service. Supplier, lot, feed eligibility and inventory values require study evidence. The PCR retains candidate authored methodology and aligned Chinese.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39120.json).
