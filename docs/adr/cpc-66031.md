## Semantic boundary

Covers rental services of passenger aircraft or aircraft suitable for passenger and freight,including helicopters,with crew. Preserve the full category across actual aircraft types,propulsion and configurations;not limited to jets,airlines,a provider brand or a complete ACMI insurance/maintenance bundle. Determine that the immediate supplied function is qualified aircraft-plus-crew availability from the original/genuine amended agreement and actual operation.

Passenger/freight suitable aircraft are assigned from the actual offered hire function,original cohort,configuration/hired capacity,roles and gates;aircraft type,payload on one flight,invoice title or flight number alone cannot classify dual use. Resolve an ambiguous or mixed-function obligation explicitly without silently choosing a passenger or freight category or counting the same hire twice. Ticketed passenger carriage or freight carriage sold as consignment transport,bare aircraft lease,crew-only labour and aircraft manufacture are outside this reference service.

Passenger-and-freight suitability must be evidenced for the actual hired configuration and accepted function; it does not require both passengers and freight to move. A freight-only hire outside this passenger or combined-suitability boundary needs its own applicable PCR.

The parent activity is actual crewed passenger or passenger-and-freight-suitable aircraft hire. These services are generally time-based and frequently involve several destinations;neither statement creates a minimum flight,leg,destination,load or distance gate. Qualified accepted standby may have zero flights,zero passengers and zero payload. Declare lessor/operator technical,AOC and captain safety responsibility separately from lessee commercial programme/directions as actually supplied;commercial direction does not imply technical control. Positioning/ferry,diversion,ground/APU work,off-hire failure,recovery,substitute and return remain causal activities.
