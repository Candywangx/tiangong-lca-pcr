# Dedicated scope

This PCR applies only to onsite meal preparation and serving at a stationary limited-service or self-service establishment that provides seating but no waiter service. Record the actual seating provision, ordering/collection or self-service arrangement, recipient and completed package. Mere seat availability does not establish food delivery. Full waiter service, facilities without seating, concession canteens or ongoing contract food service, event catering, transport-operator catering and beverage-only service are excluded. Other service modes at a shared kitchen remain separate outputs and real shared-resource consumers.

# Reference flow

1 Item(s) of Actually completed seated limited-service meal occasion. Actual output row: `seated_limited_meal_service`. Count support does not establish a concrete service UUID.
