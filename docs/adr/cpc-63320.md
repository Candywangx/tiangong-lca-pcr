# Current semantic decision

Accepted narrower mapping for CPC 63320 to `pcr.distributive-trade-services-accommodation-food-and-beverage-serving-ser-18ff339a.accommodation-food-and-beverage-services.meal-serving-services-with-limited-services` after the per-record eight-dimension review.

Official CPC 63320 covers limited-service or self-service meals with seating and no waiter service. It excludes concession-operated canteens, food service normally without seating and waiter service, and beverage-only service. The official note does not impose a stationary-site condition. Selected PCR scope: Only stationary onsite prepared meal service in limited/self-service establishments providing seating but no waiter service. Exclude full waiter service, no-seating service, canteen concessions and contract-catering outputs classified elsewhere, moving dining and beverage-only service. The stationary onsite route and exclusion of moving dining are additional method restrictions, not official CPC 63320 exclusions.

The clarification distinguishes official classification rules from the retained selected method; it does not change the accepted edge, decision-maker or acceptance time. Official source: [UNSD CPC 63320](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2100/63320).

This is a proper physical subset of the official subclass: the PCR requires all subclass eligibility conditions plus its specific configuration, preparation route and reference gate. It does not claim the entire subclass. The previous cross-category proxy rationale is superseded. Other categories remain shared-resource consumers only; they are not target outputs. Unknown classification eligibility blocks application.
