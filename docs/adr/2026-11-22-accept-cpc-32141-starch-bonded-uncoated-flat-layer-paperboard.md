# CPC 32141 starch-bonded uncoated composite flat-layer paperboard

Accept a narrower association for first accepted flat composite board made by joining finished flat paper or paperboard plies with starch adhesive. Require documentary confirmation of the uncoated, unimpregnated surfaces, supplied stock form and original gate. Finished boxes and artwork mounts are outside the flat-board product.

The UNSD and HS classification material supports the original coordinate. Commercial evidence supports starch-bonded flat multi-ply board, while the historical photographic case independently documents conversion from previously manufactured rolls and subsequent sheet finishing. Require actual records to bind grade, supplier boundary, inventory and moisture.

Use original net wet-basis board mass at the documented first acceptance, keeping packaging, external receipts or returns, utilities and retained inventory separate. An earlier finished roll brought into a converter does not justify duplicating its mill inventory.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/32141.json).
