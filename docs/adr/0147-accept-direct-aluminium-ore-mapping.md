## Decision

Accept CPC 3.0 14230 as exact to pcr.ores-and-minerals-electricity-gas-and-water.metal-ores.aluminium-ores-and-concentrates: aluminium-bearing ores and physically beneficiated concentrates through one declared mining or processing gate. Bauxite is representative, not a requirement that every aluminium mineral inherit its extraction or washing technology. Other minerals need actual mineralogy, route evidence and distinct resource/product identities. Exclude alumina chemicals and Bayer refining, aluminium metal/smelting, calcined refractory products and finished abrasives. Downstream chemical conversion is not ore beneficiation in this authored boundary.

The repository owner authorized candidate completion without Harness, one newly created worktree per PCR. This acceptance concerns classification applicability; it is not independent methodology review or publication approval.

## Methodology and evidence

International Aluminium Institute's retained original Mining: Process webpage describes surface mining, topsoil/overburden removal, extraction/haulage and either as-is ore or crushing/washing before stockpiling and refinery handover. It explicitly establishes that not all mines wash bauxite. IFC Mining guidance (2007), verified original pp.1 and 4, supports site-tailored water, waste-rock/tailings, drainage and closure coverage; no numerical discharge limit is adopted.

The candidate has aligned bilingual methodology, five conditioned processes and 19 atomic exchanges. Net accepted as-received product mass D is the denominator. Moisture and dry-solid assays reconcile wet product, rejects and wash fines; contained alumina is separately derived from matching dry-basis assays and is not the reference quantity. No universal grade, reactive-silica fraction, yield, wash intensity or resource-to-product ratio is asserted.

The directly verified Product/Mass reference UUID bd0c6203-d2b3-4e7f-8602-027aa93e87df identifies Washed Bauxite, official Chinese baseName 洗后铝矿石. It applies only to that confirmed washed state. Unwashed products and other aluminium minerals require distinct actual flow identities; the representative UUID cannot silently relabel them.

Development/closure is allocated once by disclosed attributable lifetime saleable output. Primary resource and supplied-ore processing are alternate starting conditions. New water, internal recycle, waste handling and actual pollution controls are reconciled without duplicate provider or treatment burdens. Unconfirmed resource, explosive and residue identities and absent independent ranges remain explicit candidate review needs. The finite bilingual check is complete (98 checks, zero skipped), and repeated projection generation is byte-stable.

The author commit contains only this PCR's four canonical files. Shared mapping, aliases and catalog/coverage are serial integration work. Verify runtime guidance and full repository validation after regeneration, preserving the unrelated known runtime-retirement test failure.
