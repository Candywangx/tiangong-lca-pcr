# CPC 39290 unground natural muscovite sheet-fabrication trimmings

Accept a narrower association for clean unground trimmings generated while cutting or punching unbonded natural muscovite sheet. The reference is the first accepted, unpackaged bulk handover at the producing workshop against a real buyer specification. Record original sheet and workshop, provenance, state, custody, net as-received mass and transfer evidence.

The October CPC3 note places mica waste (HS 2525.30) within 39290. Natural mineral feed and mica powder are separate. Exclude still-useful sheet offcuts kept for fabrication, preliminary mine dressing, fabricated or bonded mica products, mica paper, mixed demolition debris and ash. Separately document blade, moisture, captured dust and actual air releases.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39290.json).
