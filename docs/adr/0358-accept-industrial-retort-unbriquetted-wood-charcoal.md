# Accept industrial retort unbriquetted wood charcoal

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `34510` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.basic-chemicals.wood-charcoal`.

The method covers untreated non-coniferous roundwood converted in closed industrial retorts with internal off-gas combustion for process heat, followed by cooling, screening and bulk loading. The reference is 1 kg complete as-delivered unactivated, unbriquetted charcoal at measured moisture; fixed carbon is a constituent, not the product denominator. Open pits, briquettes, activation, treated wood, other feedstocks and commercial chemical co-product recovery are outside this subset.
