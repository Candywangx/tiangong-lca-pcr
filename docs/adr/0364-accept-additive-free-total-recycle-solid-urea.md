# Accept additive-free total-recycle solid urea

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `34611` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.basic-chemicals.urea`.

The method covers additive-free uncoated agricultural-grade bulk solid urea from NH3/CO2 total-recycle synthesis, concentration and separately declared prilling or granulation datasets. UAN, delivered solutions, coating/anticaking formulations and specialised purification remain excluded. The reference is the complete accepted supplied kilogram including measured moisture/biuret/impurities, not pure urea or nitrogen mass. The producer evidence supports a real prill option rather than a universal additive-free recipe.

Gas reference m3 are converted on measured composition/state and supplier conventions before species kg or element balances; a mass fraction never converts m3 directly into kg. Actual net reaction-water formation/consumption is independently evidenced and distinct from a closure residual. Complete raw period totals, failed/zero-output/rework, unique accepted lots and stock/WIP burdens share one positive denominator and one normalization. The CO2 Product-flow identity is conditionally retained with its contradictory waste/point-of-generation name metadata disclosed; actual origin, processing, custody point and burdens remain required, with no zero-burden inference.
