# CPC 39260 first purchase of a retreadable used truck tyre casing

Accept a narrower association for an individually inspected, previously used, whole commercial truck pneumatic tyre casing accepted for retreading at the seller’s location. The casing is presented loose and free of rim, tube and flap. The first commercial acceptance, payment and transfer of title close the quality-release gate. Lot evidence must establish the specific condition, grade, source, prior use and absence of additional preparation.

Account for the condition and net mass of accepted casings, with actual inspection effort, unsuccessful visits, rejected presentations and received stock recorded separately. Subsequent transfer or retreader inspection produces no second casing credit. Definitively unusable rubber received for disposal and a finished retread are different physical states.
