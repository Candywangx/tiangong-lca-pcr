## Semantic mapping

The intended intersection is urban/suburban rail within one city or contiguous cities, including underground/elevated rail, open to the public, using actually evidenced electric or diesel traction. The PCR counts one completed boarding-to-alighting person-leg with actual origin/destination, class, distance, timetable and period. It explicitly preserves real accompanying luggage/animals/vehicles and separately sold freight, food and sleeping outputs in the coverage/allocation ledger. Tramways, sightseeing and mixed-mode through itineraries are outside its reference-service scope.

Proposed relation: **proxy**, pending independent review. The canonical method also covers interurban rail, while excluding unresearched steam/other traction within the leaf. Therefore it is neither an exact whole-leaf match nor a simple narrower/broader nested set. Select the actual CPC service class and traction before use; no automatic full-leaf, all-technology or per-passenger-km coverage claim is allowed. Do not broaden the scope or create a duplicate record merely to change this relation.

## Method review questions

- Confirm actual completed persons versus ticket/pass/capacity counts and single-leg versus itinerary boundary.
- Confirm actual energy state, empty/shunting/standby/cancelled duty and regeneration gross/net/export ownership.
- Confirm provider coverage and manufacture-only asset gates, complete-life all-user duty, maintenance and retirement without duplicate fuels/emissions.
- Confirm actual shared/ancillary output allocation, species/medium and physical waste gates.
- Confirm bilingual/projected identity and measured native denominator, independently verified Count support, atomic/localized inventory and bounded UUID evidence.
