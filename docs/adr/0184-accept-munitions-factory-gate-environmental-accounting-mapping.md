CPC 3.0 44740 maps exactly to `pcr.metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.special-purpose-machinery.bombs-grenades-torpedoes-mines-missiles-and-similar-munitions-of-war-and-parts-thereof-2a511aac`, a candidate authored methodology for non-operational environmental accounting of the declared delivered article or part. Parent review independently checks the current UN category, bilingual boundary, original environmental reporting evidence and finite dimensional rules. Classification correspondence does not establish complete inventory coverage of a concrete dataset.

The broad category includes the named munitions and ammunition articles and parts, including shot and wads. One actual article family and delivered state is declared for each measured reference output; unrelated weapons, services and post-use remediation are separate. The methodology is restricted to supplier environmental interfaces, site utilities, releases, wastes and delivered-gate accounting. Its partitions are accounting boundaries, not a manufacturing sequence. It contains no energetic formulations, operational fabrication or loading instructions, operating settings, geometry or performance optimization.

Actual accepted net period mass D defines the kg reference and inventory normalization, with rejected/reworked burdens retained in numerators. Water closure includes moisture, opening/closing stocks, actual reaction water, product and wet waste, paired returns, discharge and evidenced evaporation with applicable density and uncertainty. Each contained-metal balance term uses its own matched wet/dry assay. Each energy carrier reconciles imports, measured generation and opening stored energy against measured allocated demand, exports, measured closing stored energy and evidenced losses. No unidentified loss is manufactured from a residual. Purchased steam uses verified delivered MJ or actual mass times state-specific enthalpy relative to a common reference, less actual condensate return using its own enthalpy. Internal fuel generation and purchased energy are not duplicated.

The original EPA munitions overview is environmental contamination context only. EPA TRI annual selective reporting supports media/species and transfer distinctions, not a complete factory LCI; unreported flows are unknown rather than zero. The EU environmental-footprint allocation source provides general accounting guidance, not manufacturing process evidence. Each actual supplier/site environmental interface register requires independent review; confidential or missing essential evidence leaves concrete dataset validation incomplete.

The actual Mass property/unit group and kg are verified. Missing identities and numeric ranges remain explicit unresolved evidence requirements rather than proxy defaults.
