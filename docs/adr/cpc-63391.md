# Accepted semantic mapping decision

Official63391 is specific-event food preparation/supply and excludes ongoing63392/63393. This reviewed purpose-neutral route is actual offsite preparation/transport plus onsite finishing and plated complete recipient supply; it can physically apply across event/ongoing labels and excludes other event formats. Partial overlap is proxy, not a taxonomy-pure narrower edge.

One actual recipient/occasion/version complete plated menu is the reference; actual supplied component/portion/acceptance gate governs output, not contract covers, bookings, prepared availability or eaten mass. Two actual kitchen responsibilities retain paired receipt-state food and reverse usable/dirty transfers, transport/holding/empty-duty coverage and actual completion. Same food cannot reappear as external additional manufacture; other independent outputs retain raw native evidence without a second allocation. Full all-user assets/land/providers/receivers, native stocks/fates and final concrete identity evidence remain mandatory. Pure onsite, dropoff-only and unreviewed non-equivalent shared/buffet routes are excluded; event label alone never defines a new method.
