# CPC 39931 expired pharmacy-owned paracetamol tablets at first internal waste handover

Accept one narrower association for expired paracetamol-only finished tablets from pharmacy-owned stock at the first documented internal handover into segregated waste custody. One kilogram means net whole-tablet mass at that gate. Verify actual unfitness, discard, internal receipt, non-hazardous status, finished product identity and lot for the place and reporting period.

The October 2026 UNSD correspondence links the frozen 39931 identity to HS 3006.92. WCO Chapter 30 Note 4(k) includes pharmaceuticals unfit for their original purpose. An Irish pharmacy audit and regulator support this internal stock-to-waste route. A separate original study supports the existence of expired finished paracetamol tablets. These sources identify different samples and provide no joint traceable lot or emission factor.

Distinguish the stock transfer from any later external service. Retain inherited exchange burdens, the first-event mass and actual incremental work. Patient returns, medicine manufacture, stock still eligible for medicinal supply, other clinical waste, waste solvents and chemical-industry waste fall outside the scope. The accepted mapping has no effect on scientific or translation approval.
