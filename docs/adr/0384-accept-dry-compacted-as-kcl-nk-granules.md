# Accept dry compacted ammonium sulphate potassium chloride NK granules

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `34647` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.basic-chemicals.fertilizers-containing-two-nutrients-nitrogen-and-potassium-other-than-potassium-nitrate`.

Only binderless uncoated dry AS/KCl composite granules made by dry mixing, roll compaction, crushing, sizing and recirculation, supplied in bulk. JP2019043797A and Sahut original context establish this distinct route; their numerical recipes, density, force, yields and performance claims are not defaults. Each accepted granule contains both nutrient feeds, distinguishing it from adjacent physical blends of separate finished granules. Native NK product identity excludes potassium nitrate and does not impose a recipe; anomalous optional pesticide wording is not an adopted ingredient. Whole supplied mass, N/K/S/Cl, actual salt/water species, separate stock ledgers, repeat-pass burdens and compatible native-volume conversions are retained.

Independent controller review checked raw state-100 flow records, official bilingual names, types, media and property-to-unit-group chains, with any source metadata inconsistency retained explicitly for clarification. Physical identity does not establish supplier/background compatibility or actual quantities. The record remains unpublished `candidate/authored_methodology`, author-aligned in Chinese and pending independent scientific review. This association neither covers the complete classification leaf nor approves scientific methodology, empirical completeness or publication.
