# CPC 39110 raw inedible bovine bone at first nonfood supply release

Accept a narrower association for an identified wet batch of raw bovine bones from meat cutting or slaughter, first released as whole, unworked or simply prepared bones for a documented eligible nonfood industrial destination. Identify the actual animal source, bone state, supplier, collection and original release. Confirm the lot belongs to the product route.

Exclude edible soup or food bones, other species or mixed offal, discarded waste, rendered or degreased bone products, powders, ash, gelatine, food-market feedstocks and objects cut to final shape. Earlier animal food eligibility, a named collector or a commercial price cannot by themselves establish the intended destination of these bones.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39110.json).
