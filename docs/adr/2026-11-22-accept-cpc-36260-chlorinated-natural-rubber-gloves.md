# CPC 36260 chlorinated powder-free natural rubber gloves

Accept a narrower association for the first quality release of non-sterile, unsupported, powder-free vulcanized natural-rubber gloves on the documented chlorinated route. A study starts at the receipt of already matured, compounded natural-rubber latex. Confirm the glove material, finish, route, non-sterile state and actual release inspection with lot records.

The reference is net glove mass in the newly accepted cohort. Reconcile gloves previously in finished inventory, rework, packing materials, actual process energy and on-site emissions. Separately determine upstream coverage of the purchased compound and material-specific packaging supply.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/36260.json).
