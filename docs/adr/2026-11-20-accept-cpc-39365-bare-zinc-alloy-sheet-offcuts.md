# CPC 39365 bare zinc-alloy sheet fabrication offcuts

Accept the narrower association for clean, source-segregated, solid offcuts of one identified bare bright-rolled zinc-base alloy sheet lot from roofing blank fabrication. Cutting and notching preserve the original alloy. The product is released as feed to an identified zinc remelter subject to that buyer’s alloy and contamination requirements.

The reference is 1 kg net whole accepted alloy at first release. The original sheet, usable roof blanks and offcuts share attributable production history. Keep actual process burdens and stock movements, independent lot assays, receiving records and original-cohort logistics. Confirm the receiver’s actual terms and distinguish the alloy from pure zinc clippings, galvanized steel, ash and dross.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39365.json).
