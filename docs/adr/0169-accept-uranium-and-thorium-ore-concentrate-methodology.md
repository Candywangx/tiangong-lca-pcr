## Evidence and measurement

Registered/read IAEA1892 original PDF39–40/printed30–31 supports actual thorium-bearing monazite dry/wet mining, spiral/gravity followed by drying/electrostatic/magnetic mineral separation; parent visually inspected39 and read40, keeping downstream rare-earth/thorium chemical stages separate. Mixed heavy-mineral sand or traceTh is not automatically a thorium reference ore. WCO STCE original60/printed58 and footnote65 were visually reviewed. IAEA TRS359 original physical preconcentration/radiometric sorting and conditional acid/alkaline recovery and TECDOC595 water/dust/radon/waste/closure support independently actual routes, not universal recipes, grades, dose, activity or yield.

Seven processes62 atomic cards distinguish deposit/resources, uranium and thorium mineral feed, conditional qualified leach/recovery, actual reagents/utility, conditioning, controls, residues and gate acceptance. Independently measured positive accepted whole-product D kg is the denominator; measured free water/drymass, elemental U/Th, actual phases/oxide-equivalent and uranium isotope fractions have separate bases. An isotope fraction within U is not a whole-ore fraction. In-situ recovery records actual dissolved extraction/wellfield solution balance and creates no imaginary mined-solid intermediate. Reconcile whole product, element-by-element, water, stocks and actual residue fate. Each radionuclide exchange records individually measured Bq, compartment, reference date/decay/disequilibrium and uncertainty; no equilibrium or activity/dose defaults. Wastewater uses independently metered m3 and paired assays/activity; its method/unit column error was corrected in both languages during parent review. Radionuclide activity remains distinct from wastewater transfer volume and managed residues; no double counting or inferred discharge from source activity.

Joint accepted monazite/zircon/ilmenite/rutile or other products require independent identity/specification/fate, subdivision or documented causal physical allocation, otherwise observed matched economic shares/sensitivity and unallocated totals. Radioactive residues are not automatically valuable co-products; closure and long-lived tailing/wellfield scope remain attributable. No avoided-product/disposal credits.
