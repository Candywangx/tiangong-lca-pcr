Covered services: Actual marine vessel/cargo salvage, wreck raising, righting and refloating with accepted physical outcome

Excluded services: Standalone towing; inland salvage; routine transport; public search/rescue/fire/police; ordinary scrapping and subsequent repair/storage

Reference: 1 item of Marine vessel salvage task.

Required qualifiers: casualty/task identifier; ship and cargo extent/composition; damaged/sunken/capsized/stranded state; exact coastal/open-sea location and water depth; seabed/weather/tide/current conditions; objective and acceptance criteria; actual pump/lift/buoyancy/diving/cutting configuration; team and operating/waiting periods; mobilization/demobilization routes; failed attempts; physical completion/custody interface; initial pollution stock and incremental releases; recovered property and waste fate; contractor boundary; asset utilization and electricity origin. Different service configurations cannot be pooled without demonstrating equivalent function, conditions and boundary.

Source-conflict decision: the current UNSD 67531 explanatory note both includes and excludes towing distressed vessels, while 65219 separately covers coastal/transoceanic transport towing. This acceptance is limited to the clearly stated physical vessel/cargo recovery, raising, righting and refloating subset. Standalone distressed-vessel towing receives no positive applicability claim. A necessary support movement inside that defined recovery is counted once as a component, without deciding the classification of an independent tow. The narrower relation preserves this unresolved source conflict.

Methodology need: Document casualty baseline/objective → actual mobilization and recovery operations → verify physical completion and transfer custody; attributable demobilization retained. The retained record defines category-specific service output, activity collection, atomic exchanges, upstream/asset boundaries, allocation, calculation and validation. The classification leaf alone did not establish these methods.
