# CPC 39149 fresh unmolassed pressed wet sugar-beet pulp

Accept the narrower association for mechanically pressed wet sugar-beet extraction pulp released fresh as a single feed material. Eligibility requires confirmed sugar-beet origin and fresh delivery state. The scope excludes added molasses, drying, pelleting, ensiling, bagasse and mixed feed.

The reference basis is measured net wet mass at first conforming release. The recorded hand-off determines whether pressing occurs in the foreground. Supplier burdens and period stocks retain lot provenance. Record actual gate storage and dispatch services against the lots served. The PCR retains candidate authored methodology and aligned Chinese.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39149.json).
