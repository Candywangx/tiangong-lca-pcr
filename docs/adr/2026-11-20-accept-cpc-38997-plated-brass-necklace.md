# CPC 38997 plated brass necklace

Accept the narrower association for complete gold-plated brass heart necklaces, finished and assembled from purchased base-metal components through first quality release. The route includes outsourced electroplating with actual supplier responsibility and receipts. The scope excludes precious-metal substrates, mechanically clad metal, pearl and gemstone jewellery, and separately traded components.

The reference quantity is the measured net mass of complete first-released necklaces. Include the externally provided coating operation once and retain actual stock and provider boundaries. Source limits and unresolved flow identities remain explicit. The PCR retains candidate authored methodology with aligned Chinese.
