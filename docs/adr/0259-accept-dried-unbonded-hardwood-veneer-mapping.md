# Accept a bounded dried unbonded hardwood veneer association

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31513` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.veneer-sheets-and-sheets-for-plywood-and-other-wood-sawn-lengthwise-sliced-or-peeled-of-4450a2e5`.

The method covers rotary-cut or sliced, dried and delivered non-tropical hardwood veneer no thicker than 6 mm, without adhesive joining, backing, composing or bonding. It excludes thin sawn sheets, green veneer sales, plywood, LVL, coniferous and tropical reference products. Reviewing CPC 31511–31513 together avoided separate identities based only on species; this actual reference supports only a narrower 31513 association. The other two classification leaves remain unmapped.

USDA Wood Handbook FPL-GTR-282 pages 255–256 and 323–324, EPA AP-42 10.5 pages 1–4, the separate wood-boiler chapter 1.6 pages 1–3, and CORRIM western plywood page 16 support bounded production, moisture, combustion and allocation rules. Softwood survey quantities, EPA factors and historical efficiencies are not transferred to a hardwood default. The explicit dry-mass allocation choice retains node/period and sensitivity requirements.
