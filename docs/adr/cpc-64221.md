# CPC 64221 mapping decision

## Source and canonical identity

UNSD CPC Version 3.0 Explanatory Notes (30 June 2025), printed pp.358–359 and362, subclass 64221: Interurban scheduled road transport services of passengers. Source: https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf

Canonical record: `pcr.scheduled-bus-passenger-journey-service`, at `library/pcrs/transport-services/passenger-transport/scheduled-bus-passenger-journey-service`. Existing bus manufacturing PCRs describe supplied vehicles, while the integrated urban multi-mode PCR describes itinerary integration; neither supplies this completed single-mode scheduled passenger-leg function. Reuse one scheduled-bus method for urban/suburban and interurban services because their completed-person-leg measurement, actual route-duty attribution, energy/maintenance/asset lifecycle and provider gates share the method. Actual strata stay separate; no new record is created solely for the code.

## Proposed semantic relation

Propose **proxy**, pending independent review. The intersection is actual motor-bus/coach passenger service between non-contiguous cities, on a predetermined route and timetable, open to all users. Require the actual code-specific route condition, actual passenger boarding/alighting OD and distance distribution, actual propulsion and complete-lifecycle boundary before selecting it. Count one completed unlinked passenger leg, with a verified Number of items/Item(s) support chain; neither vehicle-km nor passenger-km is relabelled Count.

The canonical PCR also includes urban/suburban bus service outside this individual leaf, while this CPC leaf includes tramways, trolleybuses and similar vehicles outside the explicitly researched motor-bus scope. The two sets therefore overlap; neither a narrower nor a broader edge describes them globally. Do not infer complete subtype coverage. Tramways and overhead-wire trolleybuses remain uncovered by this proposal; an actual other vehicle configuration requires its own reviewed applicability decision. The proxy is usable only with the mandatory motor-bus and geographical route filter, not by automatic whole-leaf selection.

Accompanying luggage, animals and similar items at no additional cost remain within the passenger function without increasing person-count outputs. Special-purpose routes, charter/exclusive hire, taxis, on-demand services, tour products, freight and integrated multi-mode itinerary outputs are outside the canonical reference-service scope.

## Review requirements

- Confirm completed persons against boarding/alighting and incomplete/cross-period records; retain empty/deadhead, zero-passenger and failed operated duties in their proper service pool. N must be positive; assumed occupancy never substitutes for counts.
- Confirm actual energy technology, charging gate/losses, stock closure and all shared users; hybrid, hydrogen, other fuels and other actual identities must expand independently when present.
- Confirm delivered vehicle/battery identities represent only verified manufacturing/supply scope. Complete-life duty is an allocation denominator, not proof of use/retirement coverage. Independently add uncovered maintenance/retirement/treatment; inclusive providers replace their exact covered exchanges.
- Confirm actual waste gates, independent substance/medium emissions, size-class overlaps and collection obligations for all missing actual identities. No generic range or factor is invented.
- Confirm bilingual and structured alignment, atomic flow cards, official Chinese names where supplied, actual native units and direct-read UUID proof. Bus service identity remains explicitly unresolved where only a distance-based worker-transport candidate is available.
