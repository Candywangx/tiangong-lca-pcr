# CPC 39150 preheated cocoa bean shells at the boiler material gate

Accept a narrower association for the complete cocoa-bean shell fraction removed after preheating dry beans and before alkalising or roasting the separated nibs. The material reaches its first documented acceptance as a whole shell supply for an identified biomass boiler. Require the origin, separation point, actual supplied condition and positive material receipt. Cocoa pod husks, coffee residues, shell char and heat supply have different boundaries.

Use the net as-supplied shell mass and record its actual moisture, composition and contaminants. The processing company’s historical use of shells in its own boiler establishes a real material use. Any sale, external receiver, combustion inventory or return of heat requires its own evidence and process allocation.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39150.json).
