Official CPC 63310 includes full-restaurant meal services in restaurants and lodging or transport facilities, including trains, ships and dining cars. Beverage-only service without prepared food belongs outside this subclass. Selected PCR scope: Only stationary onsite prepared meal service with full waiter service to individual customers seated at tables, counters or booths. Exclude limited/self-service without waiter service, no-seating takeaway, concession or contract-catering outputs classified elsewhere, moving dining facilities and beverage-only service. Stationary onsite preparation and the exclusion of mobile dining are this PCR's method choices; eligible transport-facility meals remain within CPC 63310 but outside this selected method.
