# CPC 39215 garnetted fibre from navy cotton jersey cuttings

Accept a narrower association for unspun, mechanically reclaimed fibre from pre-consumer, single-source navy-blue cotton single-jersey production cuttings. Qualify the feed by fibre content and previous fabric state, then document the actual opening route and the first quality release of the recovered loose fibre. Non-garnetted cotton waste, blends, yarn, cloth, wiping rags and chemically regenerated fibres have other boundaries.

Weigh net fibre at first release, retaining actual moisture and dye or finish in the material account. Trace later storage, repeated deliveries, returned fibre and rework to the original release. Keep real incoming moisture, added water, dry fibre, fibre-bearing residues and emissions in separate observed physical records. An instrument or pooled yield from a subsequent spinning study requires independent applicability evidence.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39215.json).
