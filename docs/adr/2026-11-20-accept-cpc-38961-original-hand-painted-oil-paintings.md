# CPC 38961 original hand-painted oils on purchased primed cotton canvas

Accept the narrower association for a newly made, original, physical oil painting painted by hand on a purchased, acrylic-primed, stretched cotton canvas. The specified direct, solvent-free two-colour route and retained wooden stretcher define the product. Original-created work, release state and the physical subject of the transfer must be documented.

Record actual whole works and their masses. The kilogram is a reporting denominator for the complete accepted objects. It conveys no aesthetic equivalence. Separate reproduction fees, digital licences, restoration services, framing and prints from the physical first release, and attribute actual later delivery and returns to the original work.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/38961.json).
