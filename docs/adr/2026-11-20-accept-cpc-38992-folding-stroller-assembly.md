# CPC 38992 folding stroller assembly

Accept the narrower scope for assembly and first complete-unit quality release of a single-seat manually pushed folding stroller from purchased subassemblies. The whole stroller is within CPC 38992. Separately traded parts, dolls’ carriages, invalid carriages and powered or multi-seat variants are outside the selected scope.

The reference quantity follows the calibrated net mass of independently documented first releases. Purchased subassembly burdens remain upstream, and subsequent handling and packaging preserve the original release cohort. Manufacturer sources support the construction and bounded operation without a common factory, catalogue weight or safety certification claim. The PCR remains candidate authored methodology with aligned Chinese.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/38992.json).
