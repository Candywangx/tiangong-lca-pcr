Covered boundary: This PCR specifies one homogeneous actual SKU per dataset: dry uncooked spaghetti made from durum-wheat semolina and water, no egg, fortification, filling or sauce, in one original heat-sealed printed polypropylene film consumer bag labelled 500 g. Actual net fill, film mass, ink/additives and secondary packaging BOM must be measured or supplier-verified; 500 g is a format label, never a measured fill or allocation factor. It covers walk-in final-use retail through a non-specialized store, supplier-unloaded cartons at the store receiving gate, manual unpacking and hand-trolley movement, dry protected ambient shelf/backroom holding, cash checkout with handwritten plain-paper receipts, and on-site handover/acceptance. No retailer opening, repacking, cooking, drying, milling or pasta production occurs. A damaged pack may be segregated and opened only for recorded waste separation, never offered as the qualifying product. The route has natural ventilation, fixed electric lighting, no powered handling, refrigeration, mechanical HVAC, dehumidification, fumigation, electronic checkout or retailer-operated transport. Eligibility requires inspection and complete operation/asset/work-order/utility evidence confirming these facts; a mere absent invoice is insufficient. Different SKU, ingredients, fill format, laminate/box package, loose pasta, fresh/chilled/frozen pasta, specialty store, delivery, donation/salvage production or powered route is outside this PCR until a bilingual named-inventory and protocol extension is authored. CPC 62129 also includes many unrelated goods; group 237 supports only this narrower subset.
