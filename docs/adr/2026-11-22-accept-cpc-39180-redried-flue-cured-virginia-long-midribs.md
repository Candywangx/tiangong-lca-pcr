# CPC 39180 redried flue-cured Virginia long-stem supply

Accept a narrower association for separately supplied, unblended long midribs removed from flue-cured Virginia tobacco, redried and conditioned, and first released to the declared industrial receiving specification. Verify the real product state, producer release, moisture basis and receiving use. Net as-supplied stem mass is the reference.

Screening by length is within this scope. Ineligible materials include lamina, a mixed leaf or fines lot, dust sold separately, cut or pulverised material, expanded stems, extracts, reconstituted tobacco and finished smoking products. Keep the upstream joint separation and redrying once in the responsible provider.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39180.json).
