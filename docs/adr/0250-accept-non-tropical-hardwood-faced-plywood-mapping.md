# Non-tropical hardwood-faced all-veneer plywood

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31413` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.plywood-consisting-solely-of-sheets-with-at-least-one-outer-ply-of-non-coniferous-wood-1a331a12`. The method covers integrated manufacture of flat, uncoated, PF-bonded all-veneer panels with at least one non-tropical hardwood outer ply and no tropical plies. It includes untreated-log conditioning, peeling/slicing, veneer drying, PF preparation, crossed layup, hot pressing, trimming/conditional sanding and packing. On-site gas/clean-chip heat, purchased tap water and external wastewater treatment have explicit boundaries. Other core constructions, adhesives, surface treatments, purchased-veneer assembly and the outer-ply classes 31411/31412 are excluded.

Promote the matching unpublished retained scaffold in place. The single finished product reference is `897daeae-8210-4a8e-af21-7ad112411dc5`, Mass/kg. Its formal identity specifies the target outer-ply class and its wider descriptive text expressly includes veneer plywood. Inclusion of other core structures is not evidence that this all-veneer subset is absent; actual layer, species, adhesive and surface qualifiers remain mandatory. This decision does not resolve the wider official CPC 3141/3142 title-versus-inclusion inconsistency or accept blockboard mappings.

EPA AP-42 10.5 and USDA Wood Handbook support veneer architecture, adhesive distinctions and process/emission boundaries. CORRIM western/southern softwood surveys support bounded collection questions and physical-versus-burden accounting, not hardwood numerical parameters. Separate boiler evidence and FAO solid/bulk volume concepts are used within their stated limits. No density, recipe, energy, emission factor, yield, service life or downstream scenario is imported as a universal default.
