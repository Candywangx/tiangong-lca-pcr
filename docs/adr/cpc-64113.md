# CPC 64113 mapping decision

## Evidence and semantic scope

The original UNSD CPC Version 3.0 Explanatory Notes (30 June 2025), printed/PDF page 359, defines subclass 64113 as mixed-mode urban/suburban passenger transportation over predetermined routes and schedules. The source permits combinations of rail, land and water modes. Source: https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf

The proposed canonical record is `pcr.integrated-urban-multimodal-passenger-journey-service`, under `library/pcrs/transport-services/passenger-transport/integrated-urban-multimodal-passenger-journey-service`. It covers an accountable, physically delivered, scheduled urban/suburban heavy-rail-and-public-bus integrated journey. Water combinations and other unassessed modes remain outside this narrower scope. Tramway is not silently treated as heavy rail. Booking-only aggregation, unrelated ticket bundles, single-mode services, charters, sightseeing and interurban itineraries are excluded.

Proposed relationship: **narrower**, pending independent semantic review. The classification leaf is broader than the actual authored service. Neither this PCR nor an accepted edge would establish complete whole-leaf coverage for every possible mode combination.

## Why this record is material

A single-mode completed passenger leg is an input or physical component of this service. Its boarding-to-alighting denominator does not represent an integrated through journey. This record requires actual person/itinerary completion linkage, preservation of transfers without extra final outputs, a closed mode/provider/burden/period coverage ledger, and exclusive ownership of provider-included versus expanded burdens. These are material method differences; the canonical identity is not created solely from a CPC number.

The reference is one actual completed linked person-journey in native Count / Item(s). Tickets, boardings, constituent legs and passenger-distance remain distinct quantities. DfT NTS trip/stage definitions and FTA boarding definitions inform these distinctions; the actual completion/linkage rule and bookkeeping equations are explicitly authored PCR decisions. Actual origin/destination, route, schedule, passenger class, distance, service quality, inclusion and period are required. No fixed journey-to-km conversion, occupancy, emission factor, asset life or numerical range is supplied.

Each provider/leg/cohort/burden/period cell uses a compatible complete provider result or explicit physical foreground expansion, with only proven uncovered components added. Asset manufacture remains manufacture-only; its delivered native quantity is allocated using supported same-asset/cohort lifetime duty, while other maintenance, replacement and retirement phases need their own quantities and non-overlapping coverage. Expansion requirements retain actual carriers, materials, species/medium, wastes, station/guideway assets and digital support, with unique row IDs and mandatory evidence.

## Identity and review limits

Twelve atomic cards are authored. Eight card locations reuse five distinct independently verified Tiangong identities, with exact official Chinese names and Mass/Energy/Volume support. Count support is independently verified. Four concrete identities remain unresolved: purchased completed bus person-leg, purchased completed heavy-rail person-leg, the integrated reference service, and the actual complete low-floor battery-electric urban bus at its manufacturing gate. Single-mode passenger-distance, freight, components and generic vehicle records were not substituted for these identities. Missing identity or quantity remains a production gate rather than a zero exchange.

Scientific review remains pending. The English source, Chinese rendering and deterministic projection are candidate/authored_methodology. The author adds no accepted mapping edge. The root reviewer must assess this individual decision and the PCR before any acceptance; checker pass alone does not establish external identity verification, complete actual foreground data or publication readiness.
