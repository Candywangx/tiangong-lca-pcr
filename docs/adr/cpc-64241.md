# CPC 64241 draft mapping decision

The actual UNSD CPC3.0 Explanatory Notes30June2025, printed363, defines `Domestic scheduled air transport services of passengers` with aircraft of any type including helicopters and accompanying passenger baggage/items carried without extra cost. This code requires actual **domestic** and **scheduled** service qualification. For scheduled leaves the actual predetermined route/schedule applies; non-scheduled leaves expressly exclude sightseeing64134 and aircraft-with-crew rental66031. This decision does not invent technology limits from available UUIDs. Actual original: https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf .

## Semantic product and proposed relation

Propose `pcr.air-passenger-leg-service`, **broader** than this individual leaf, pending independent main review. Its ordinary operator-fulfilled passenger conveyance method covers the union of domestic/international and scheduled/non-scheduled services. Infant/nonrevenue actual passengers count; on-duty crew are support, transported repositioning crew roles require evidence.
