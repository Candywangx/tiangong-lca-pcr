# Fresh cultivated sugar kelp within CPC 04932

The retained classification scaffold could not supply a material method. Accept one narrower positive edge to the candidate method for fresh, raw, whole cultivated *Saccharina latissima*: spore nursery, seeded marine longline culture and maintenance, harvest, drainage, cooling and the first documented food-quality release, with separately attributed post-release services. The PCR keeps its existing unpublished identity.

CPC 04932 also includes other food-suitable cultivated seaweeds and algae and frozen, dried or ground states. This edge does not establish methodology coverage for those species, states or routes. Exclude wild harvest, nonfood biomass, extracts, and processing or packaging routes outside the stated boundary. The contemporary site and receiving-market food-quality conditions must be checked independently; neither the mapping nor the PCR is food certification.

The reference is one kilogram of newly released net wet kelp tissue after a declared drainage protocol. Exclude attached/free water, ice, packaging, twine and fouling; disclose measured salt and dry matter without removing tissue salt from net tissue mass. Preserve one normalization, failed and zero-output loads, inherited WIP/finished-stock burdens and actual packaging/service records. No universal yield, dosage, carbon-sequestration credit or nutrient-substitution credit is accepted.

The native Algae flow supports only generic Product/Mass physical identity. Its opposite wild/unfit classification metadata and unknown owner intent remain explicit in the reference context, inventory descriptions and data-quality rules. It does not certify cultivated origin, species, fresh state, food suitability or a compatible upstream provider. Actual suppliers and foreground quality records require separate compatibility evidence.

The method uses Redmond et al., *New England Seaweed Culture Handbook: Nursery Systems* (CTSG-14-01, 2014), [complete nursery handbook](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/44208/noaa_44208_DS1.pdf); Concepcion, DeRosia-Banick and Balcom, *Seaweed Production and Processing in Connecticut* (CTSG-20-01, 2020), [historical food-hazard guidance](https://seagrant.uconn.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/1985/2020/01/Seaweed-Hazards-Guide_Jan2020_accessible.pdf); and the [ILCD detailed guide](https://eplca.jrc.ec.europa.eu/uploads/ILCD-Handbook-General-guide-for-LCA-DETAILED-GUIDANCE-12March2010-ISBN-fin-v1.0-EN.pdf), conditional allocation guidance. These sources support their stated topics, not a common supplier, a universal inventory recipe or present-day regulatory approval. The classification boundary is read against the [30 June 2025 CPC 3.0 explanatory notes](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf).

This decision accepts deterministic category selection only. Methodology remains candidate/authored, Chinese remains aligned, scientific and translation approval and publication remain separate. Auxiliary identity gaps, unavailable general ranges, provider matching and future foreground collection obligations remain visible. No edge is accepted for CPC 04352 or 04934: the cold-water shrimp terminal/method holds and nonfood algae product/classification holds are unresolved. Local or hosted engineering qualification cannot resolve these scientific gaps.
