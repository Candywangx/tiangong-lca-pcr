# CPC 3.0 01253: Onions

Decision: accepted at 2026-10-20T02:23:29.335Z by codex-pcr-content-review.

Accepted relation: `narrower` from CPC 3.0 `01253` to `pcr.agriculture-forestry-and-fishery-products.products-of-agriculture-horticulture-and-market-gardening.onions`.

## Semantic product boundary

The PCR retains its existing scope: fresh-market dry-bulb onions of Allium cepa L. Cepa group, excluding green onions, shallots and onions intended only for industrial processing. The repair does not broaden the eligible product set, add an industrial-processing route or change the declared reference quantity.

## Reason for the relation

The official CPC 3.0 leaf identifies the product category without this PCR's fresh-market/industrial-destination restriction. The PCR therefore covers a proper subset of the leaf; `exact` overstates that coverage. The mapping, manifest classification reference and bilingual category statement use `narrower` consistently. Goods outside the retained scope require other applicable methodology rather than implicit extension of this PCR.
