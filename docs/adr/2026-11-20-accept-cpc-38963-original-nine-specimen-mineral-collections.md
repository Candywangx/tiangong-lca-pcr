# CPC 38963 curated nine-specimen natural mineral collections

Accept the narrower association for first preparation and assembly of a complete nine-specimen natural mineral collection with verifiable mineralogical collectors’ interest. Each specimen must be identified and linked to its original labels, documented provenance and the collection rationale. Its physical condition, assigned kit and identity must survive custody and release.

The roster ranges from talc through corundum with a genuine specimen of each named type and no diamond. A manufactured hardness pick, model, or teaching-only test set cannot satisfy this boundary. Weigh complete first-released sets with their integral identification, tray and box. Retain separate transport packaging, original-cohort service and failures. Verify actual specimen and lot evidence before using a foreground dataset.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/38963.json).
