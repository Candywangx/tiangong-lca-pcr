# CPC 3.0 02291 mapping decision

## Semantic product boundary

This PCR covers unprocessed ovine milk from a dairy sheep herd at the actual producing-farm handover. Warm raw and on-farm chilled raw milk are alternative final states, with exactly one selected per lot. Exclude heat-treated, formulated, independently skimmed or partly skimmed milk, collection-centre processing and downstream dairy products. The current CPC-linked product scope implements the measured-fat exclusion described below. Record breed, herd, lactation phase, temperature, first conditioning and cooling status. Sources: `un-cpc-3-2025`, `fao-small-ruminant-dairy`, `fao-leap-small-ruminants-2016`.

## Current scope clarification (2026-10-09)

The current candidate PCR implements the [official CPC 3.0 02291 explanatory note](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2100/02291): the final species-specific raw-milk product is unskimmed/not partly skimmed and has a measured milk-fat mass fraction of at least 3.5% (35 g/kg milk). Lower-fat milk is outside this declared CPC-linked scope, including naturally lower-fat lots. Composition must be evidenced for the actual handover lot; missing evidence does not establish eligibility. This is a classification boundary, not a milk-quality pass limit or an instruction to alter composition. Reference normalization remains the actual measured net milk mass.

This dated clarification aligns the current scope with the retained exact relation. It does not rewrite the historical decision attribution or time, create another mapping edge, or establish methodology/translation approval or publication readiness.
