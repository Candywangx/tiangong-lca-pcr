# CPC 38995 standalone passive plastic replacement end cap for a utility lighter

Accept the narrower relationship for a new, dedicated, passive plastic end cap first released as a separate replacement part. The method is restricted to its material and environmental inventory at that product boundary. A lighter without fuel remains a complete product if all its constituent parts are present. A loose end cap is also distinct from a protective sleeve and from an outer packing box.

CBP’s August 9, 2023 decision N334329 separately classified the exact cap described in the applicant’s prospective import. The contemporaneous U.S. tariff schedule counts pieces under the relevant national statistical line. A study needs original lot-specific evidence for the actual standalone first release and its net mass. No tariff entry, sale or product mass for a particular lot is inferred.

Reconcile the same first-release quantity in the unreleased and released stock accounts. Preserve external unreleased transfers, previously released incoming goods, true returns and gross outbound movements, with later service burdens assigned once. Case-specific inventory requires measured bare cap mass and original acquisition, release, packaging, utility and freight records.
