## Semantic identity and sharing

Propose the new semantic `pcr.chartered-coach-group-transport-mission-service` at `library/pcrs/transport-services/passenger-transport/chartered-coach-group-transport-mission-service`. Its function is one operator-accountable completed whole-group transport mission with agreed actual party,itinerary/stops/return,duration,quality and acceptance. This is materially different from existing scheduled-bus one-person/one-vehicle completed leg and from rental/available vehicle hours. Several coaches,replacements,segments and waiting activities may fulfil one mission; they do not manufacture extra mission outputs. Invoice/contract/party size/capacity is not mission count. The mission unit and all collection/formula rules are explicit PCR designs,not quantities mandated by UNSD or EU definitions.

Local and long-distance charter use the same mission completion,native inventory,empty/failed duty,provider ownership and whole-life asset allocation methods. Actual food/water/safety/driver rest and overnight work support are mandatory when included; independent hotel/tour/guide products have explicit function boundaries and included components cannot be dropped. The method excludes final sightseeing experiences,all-inclusive tour/accommodation packages,booking-only and renter-controlled availability. A charter carrying a party to a museum is not automatically a sightseeing experience.
