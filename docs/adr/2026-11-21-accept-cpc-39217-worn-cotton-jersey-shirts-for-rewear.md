# CPC 39217 bulk worn cotton T-shirts for direct rewear

Accept a narrower association for appreciably worn adult single-layer, short-sleeved, round-neck cotton-knit T-shirts. The sorted garments retain their identity and function. They pass inspection to a documented receiving-market specification and are first released in bulk sacks for direct rewear. Record any necessary washing and minor repair that actually occurred.

Measure one kilogram net of packaging on the first positive release. Identify the original cohort on subsequent dispatch or return. Document prior production and use, collection, preparation, sorting rejects, actual energy and packaging. The classification decision supplies no default avoided new garment, useful life, site yield or scientific approval.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/39217.json).
