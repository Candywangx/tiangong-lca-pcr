# Accept manufactured bulk aqueous ammonia solution subset

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `34652` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.basic-chemicals.ammonia-in-aqueous-solution`.

Only bulk whole aqueous NH3 solution manufactured by closed absorption/blending of separately supplied manufactured anhydrous ammonia and DI water. Complete retained primary Hill Brothers page extractions and independent Yara context support this route; failed local publisher-PDF downloads are not represented as acquired PDF originals. One kg is accepted actual plant-gate handover of the whole solution at measured assay/state, not pure NH3, nitrogen or a separate pure NH4OH compound. Opening and closing stocks and their burdens are reconciled; retained finished stock is excluded from handover Q. No reaction-water default, universal concentration/density, cooling COP or emissions factor is adopted. Aqueous waste and purchased cooling service remain specific auxiliary identity/provider requirements.

Independent controller review checked raw state-100 flow records, official bilingual names, types, media and property-to-unit-group chains, with any source metadata inconsistency retained explicitly for clarification. Physical identity does not establish supplier/background compatibility or actual quantities. The record remains unpublished `candidate/authored_methodology`, author-aligned in Chinese and pending independent scientific review. This association neither covers the complete classification leaf nor approves scientific methodology, empirical completeness or publication.
