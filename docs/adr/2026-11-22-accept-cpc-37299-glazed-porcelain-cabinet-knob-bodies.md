# CPC 37299 glazed porcelain cabinet knob bodies

Accept a narrower association for a fired and glazed, non-structural porcelain cabinet knob body. The method begins with purchased plastic porcelain body and clear aqueous glaze, follows shaping, drying, biscuit and glaze firing, then ends at the release of the packed ceramic body. Establish purity of this component boundary with the drawing, lot and actual hardware exclusions.

Use first accepted net mass of the ceramic body as the reference. Keep packaging, kiln supports, moulds and hardware outside that mass. Measure the purchased body and glaze, firing energy, actual rejects, emissions and consumable lives on the production cohort.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/37299.json).
