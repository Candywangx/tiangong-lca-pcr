# Accept CPC 44914 printing and bookbinding machinery methodology

Decision: accepted at 2026-10-01T19:37:28.508883Z by codex-direct-pcr-author.

CPC 3.0 44914 maps exactly to `pcr.metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.special-purpose-machinery.bookbinding-machinery-machinery-for-type-setting-and-the-like-printing-machinery-and-ma-1de7f354`, a candidate authored methodology record. This decision follows independent parent review of the current UN classification, the bilingual canonical boundary, measured reference and inventory rules, direct flow records, and original manufacturer evidence. It does not infer methodology merely from a classification leaf.

The methodology covers complete binding, typesetting, printing and printing auxiliary machinery, including analogue and digital families, plate/cylinder preparation and object/container printing. Office sheet-fed offset machines and separately classified textile/yarn printing, peripherals, papermaking machinery and parts require their own boundaries. The original classification includes “warp printing machines for repetitive printing” while also excluding textile and yarn printing. This ambiguity is preserved in both languages and unresolved metadata: a concrete device must receive classification review based on its delivered function; its name alone cannot admit or exclude it.

Actual configuration-specific accepted net output D, accepted unit count N and measured M=D/N normalize actual period inventories. Rejected and reworked production remains in numerators. Purchased completed modules and conditional in-house routes are mutually reconciled. Factory test consumables and power are included only when actually consumed before delivery. Water, species, solvent and carrier balances include individually matched assays, moisture, opening/closing stocks, returns, reactions and uncertainty; shared utilities are residuals after assigned process/test meters, with negative residuals investigated rather than clipped.

Direct reference UUID b31260db-89a4-4b18-a5b2-90f54a2f9936 is the full category Product/Mass interface. Electricity UUID 3d76981f-964a-4865-b588-0e067a2a1163 applies only to its actual Chinese medium-voltage supply interface. The generic machined-parts candidate is rejected as insufficient for completed component burden. Remaining identities and numeric ranges are explicit unresolved evidence requirements, not invented defaults.

Primary evidence comprises the current UN CPC, actual Horizon binding documentation, HP official LEP material, current Heidelberg company and CtP pages and the publisher's historical production account. Manufacturer examples and historical routes establish conditional interfaces only, never universal recipes, weights or factors. Three registered Heidelberg challenge/consent payloads are excluded from evidence and retained in the external rejection record; no claims rely on them.
