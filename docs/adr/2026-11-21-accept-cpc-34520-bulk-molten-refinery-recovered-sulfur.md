# CPC 34520 bulk molten refinery-recovered sulfur

Accept a narrower association for elemental sulfur recovered at a refinery and supplied molten in bulk by the specified route. Identify the actual supplier, the declared grade and the first qualified receiver handoff. Keep the purity and moisture measurements separate from the net mass of the delivered molten product. Exclude sulfur sold in granulated form, sublimed, precipitated or colloidal sulfur, independently operated downstream storage and resale.

Record the first saleable production release separately from net qualified delivery at the declared product gate. Reconcile inventories and independently evidenced return, rejection and re-delivery events. Apply outsourced service burdens once at the actual ownership and operational boundary. Link the site configuration, physical flows and measurement methods to the relevant matched period and product cohort.

See [the independent review](../reviews/cpc3-115-resume-20261120/34520.json).
