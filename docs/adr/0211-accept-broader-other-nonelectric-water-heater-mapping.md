# Accept broader other non-electric water-heater methodology mapping

- Decision: accepted
- Decided at UTC: 2026-10-02T18:10:41.143794Z
- Decision-maker: codex-direct-pcr-author

CPC 3.0 `44827` maps as broader to `pcr.metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.special-purpose-machinery.other-non-electric-water-heaters`, a candidate authored methodology for complete other non-electric instantaneous or storage water heaters. Current CPC leaf links HS 2022 8419.11 and 8419.19; solar 8419.12 remains separate. The methodology retains commercial or industrial configurations only after actual principal-function and supplied-state review, so it does not assert exact equivalence with the domestic CPC parent coordinate.

Independent original Rinnai gas-instantaneous, Bradford White gas-storage and indirect, and Bock oil-heater bodies establish contrasting architectures. Auxiliary ignition, fans, controls and pumps do not by themselves establish a principally electric heating function. Generic indirect tanks, separate exchangers/burners and multi-service boiler hybrids require actual classification review. Installer supplies and shipping weights excluding burner/controls cannot silently establish a complete factory-gate configuration or net equipment mass.

Both languages use measured configuration-specific accepted mass and common-period Qattr/Naccepted/Dnet. Make/buy states prevent embedded production duplication; factory fuels and emissions remain separate from user hot-water service and installation. All atomic identities were queried, and adopted UUIDs were directly checked for type, state, chemistry, native units, supplied interfaces and official Chinese name. Board, pallet and polyol grades retain their actual identity restrictions. Each measured emission source is assigned once. Unresolved identities and future factory data remain candidate gaps. This positive mapping does not publish the PCR or certify future datasets.
