## Semantic boundary

This PCR covers rental services of ALL types of self-propelled freight vessels for coastal and transoceanic water transport WITH CREW, explicitly including tankers, bulk dry cargo vessels, cargo and freight vessels, tugboats and FISHING VESSELS. The immediate supplied function is actual operated vessel availability under a genuine hire obligation, with declared vessel or hired portion, configuration, capacity, time, crew/operator responsibilities and gates. It is not limited to ordinary cargo ships, time charter or one fuel, propulsion, towing or fishing technology. Vessel type alone does not prove the function supplied to the immediate customer.

Reference is one independently completed and accepted original operated freight-vessel hire engagement with positive actual accepted availability time H, original or genuinely chronologically amended configuration/capacity/control, region/duty and delivery/redelivery/quality/replacement gates. Count denotes the accepted hire obligation, not a tonne-kilometre, cargo shipment, completed tow, fish catch, voyage, invoice or crew shift. Actual permitted standby may fulfill availability with zero sailing, cargo, towing or catch. Retain H and capacity for coherent comparison and causal attribution; unlike hire durations/configurations/functions are not equivalent merely because each counts as one. An accepted replacement fulfilling the same obligation does not create another reference item.

Exclude rental/leasing of vessels WITHOUT CREW (73115), inland freight-vessel hire, passenger-vessel hire, operator-only labour and vessel manufacture. Distinguish operated availability from freight carriage, a towing outcome or fish production sold as the immediate product. Charter terminology, crew presence, cargo/tow/catch success, or fuel payer alone cannot decide classification. Record actual contract substance, operator/master/crew employment, navigation/maintenance/safety control, commercial directions, counterparties and handoffs. Independent downstream transport, towing or fishing production remains a separate boundary; genuinely owed work and support under the hire remain included.

Keep actual crew/readiness, standby, positioning, sailing/manoeuvring, port/anchor/berth, delivery/redelivery, cleaning, failure/recovery and accepted replacement duties. Include actual cargo/tank/pumping/temperature-control support, towing equipment/work and fishing gear/operation/preservation only where evidenced as owed by the hire; no positive tow, catch or cargo is required. Original duties and genuine amendments retain failed/cancelled/unavailable periods and burdens. Actual consumed fuel/energy and upstream supply/charging, including initial/customer-held stocks, belong once regardless payer/title after exact provider reconciliation. No universal hire clause, manning limit, fuel factor, yield, stock quantity or lifetime is supplied.
