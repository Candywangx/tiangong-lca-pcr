# Accept non-electric domestic iron/steel room-heating appliance mapping

- Decision: accepted
- Decided at UTC: 2026-10-02T17:24:19.772601Z
- Decision-maker: codex-direct-pcr-author

CPC 3.0 `44822` maps exactly to `pcr.metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.special-purpose-machinery.stoves-grates-braziers-and-similar-non-electric-domestic-appliances-other-than-cooking-3665f7e2`, a candidate authored methodology for complete non-electric domestic iron/steel stoves, grates, braziers and similar non-cooking appliances. Actual wood, oil and gas, cast and fabricated bodies, inserts and open-grate configurations remain conditional; no one model is the universal recipe.

Cooking and plate warming, electric heating, central boilers/radiators, industrial furnaces and separately supplied parts are outside this boundary. Hybrid cooking/heating and motor-fan hot-air equipment require actual principal-function review; non-iron/steel whole appliances do not qualify merely through one metal component. Original UNSD CPC 3.0 explanatory notes and inspected manufacturer original bodies establish the reviewed boundary.

Both languages use configuration-specific accepted net mass with measured common-period Qattr/Naccepted/Dnet. Bought complete modules embed upstream manufacture once; factory fuel/testing is separate from installation and lifetime combustion. All atomic basic-flow identities were actually queried, and adopted UUIDs were directly checked for type, state, supplied condition, reference property and official Chinese name. Unmatched identities and future factory measurements remain explicit candidate gaps. This acceptance establishes a positive category mapping and does not publish the PCR or certify future datasets.
