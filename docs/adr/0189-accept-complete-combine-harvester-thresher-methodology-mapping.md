# Accept complete combine harvester-thresher methodology mapping

Decision time: 2026-10-01T20:32:50.890590Z.

Accept CPC 3.0 44122 as an exact positive methodology edge to `pcr.metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.special-purpose-machinery.combine-harvester-threshers`, a material candidate record. The reviewed semantic boundary is complete machinery integrating crop harvesting, threshing, separation and cleaning, including self-propelled and trailed configurations. Rotary, straw-walker and hybrid systems remain distinct conditional architectures. Standalone threshers, independently supplied headers/parts, other harvesting machinery and supplying tractors are outside this record. Current CPC explanatory notes identify the named subclass and its neighbours but contain no explicit subclass-specific exclusion sentence; the integrated-machine distinction is this semantic review, not a quoted classification ruling.

Original John Deere and CLAAS product evidence supports equipment configuration and delivered-header boundaries. Historical CLAAS trailed-machine evidence establishes an architectural counterexample only. The original 1995 Pennsylvania permit supplies a bounded agricultural-equipment coating example, while the original Kappa CLAAS case supplies actual factory acceptance, diesel exhaust capture, demand ventilation and recovered-heat evidence. These sources establish no universal factory chemistry, recipe, machine mass, energy intensity or emissions factor. A malformed Deere mass is excluded from quantitative use.
