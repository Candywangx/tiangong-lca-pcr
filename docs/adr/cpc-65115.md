## Semantic boundary

Cover the complete household-removal and qualifying ancillary function, including actual packing-only, carrying-only or in-house obligations, not just house-to-house motorized road freight. Conditional road, manual handling, hoist/access, disassembly/reassembly, packing and equipment duties occur only when actually furnished under the obligation. No positive road-distance, delivered freight-mass or universal packing recipe is imposed.

Exclude office furniture/equipment removals65119, household-goods manufacture/trade, bare equipment rental and independently scoped disposal/storage or unrelated freight. Actual office/commercial users remain included in shared-resource allocation denominators where they consume the same equipment, though they are not this PCR's output.

One reference is one actually fulfilled, predeclared household moving or qualifying ancillary engagement with inventoried goods/condition, actual work and access/placement obligations, completion/quality, actual load/distance where applicable, period and positive equivalent output cohort. More vans, visits, packages, rooms or internal packing tasks do not multiply the final engagement output. Different obligations require explicit strata and comparability disclosure.

Customer household goods are custody inventory, not mover-bought manufacturing inputs. Damage, repair, retained/returned goods and discarded items have exclusive actual fates. Conditional protective packing is a distinct node with original/protected states, actual material and returnable-device life/stock/fate, and one paired internal task handoff. The final engagement can be packing-only without an invented haul.
