# PEF method requirements and EF 3.1 source bindings

This is a reusable candidate interpretation of the complete PEF method, not a product-category PCR. The authoritative recommendation, corrigendum, EF package and conditional interim guidance remain controlling sources. The sections below retain applicability, required action, evidence and professional-judgment boundaries together. A source citation is an exact source ID plus a locator into that source. The machine check records package conformance only; no study outcome follows from it.

## PEF-01 — Goal, intended use and applicable category rules

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §§2,3.1; eu-2026-interim:§§2.1,2.2

Define the study goal, reasons, intended application and audience, commissioner, limitations and any comparison/public communication. Identify whether a PEFCR formally developed with the Commission applies, its exact version, product boundary and validity. This common module is not a cement PEFCR and cannot establish conformity to a category rule. If none is established, use the no-PEFCR method branch and document that choice. Existence of a field is mechanically checkable; fitness of the goal and category-rule applicability requires professional judgment.

## PEF-02 — Function, declared unit and reference flow

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §3.2.1

Specify what function is provided, how much, how well and for how long; justify uncovered aspects, use applicable standards and SI units. Relate every inventory amount to the product quantity providing that function. For an intermediate product whose downstream functions are not determined, apply a justified declared unit (for example mass), and state the reference flow. A tonne of finished cement does not itself establish equivalent function, strength class or downstream service. Record product composition, route, performance, geography and time as applicable. Check units, identities and conversions mechanically; review whether the selected functional/declared unit and product scope are defensible.

## PEF-03 — Boundary and life-cycle stage applicability

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §§3.2.2,4.2–4.2.5

Map raw-material acquisition/pre-processing, manufacturing, distribution/storage, use and end of life to the study boundary; give a consistent process diagram identifying company-specific data, co-products, by-products and wastes. For intermediate products §4.2 excludes distribution (with justified exceptions), use and end of life; justify intermediate-product status rather than applying an arbitrary gate. Raw-material extraction, processing and transport to the production facility remain upstream requirements. Purchased clinker, gypsum, fuel or electricity do not become burden-free because they enter at the foreground gate. Document every exclusion and its reason and potential significance. Category-stage applicability needs professional judgment; the diagram and inventory bindings are mechanical evidence.

## PEF-04 — Inventory completeness and screening

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §§4,4.1,4.3,6.2

Inventory all relevant material/energy inputs, product and waste outputs and elementary emissions for each included process, with sources, amounts, units, allocation and recursive upstream links. Resolve intermediate flows to background systems until the aggregated inventory contains elementary flows and the reference product flow. If screening is undertaken, no cut-off is allowed during screening; use available primary/secondary data and refine iteratively. Distinguish a missing observation, measured zero, missing characterisation factor and justified non-applicability. An empty list, zero-filled placeholder or completed checklist does not demonstrate completeness. Review process/input/output coverage against goal and boundary.

## PEF-05 — Ordinary cut-off and data-gap exclusions are separate

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §§4.6.3,4.6.4; eu-2026-interim:§2.1

Avoid cut-offs. The ordinary §4.6.4 allowance is cumulative, up to 3.0%, with material, energy and environmental significance evidence; its further operative condition permits exclusion only for processes cumulatively accounting for less than 3.0% of material and energy flows as well as environmental impact in every impact category. Apply the foreground allowance in addition to cut-offs already included in background datasets, and disclose the evidence. Do not translate it into a mass-only rule or four arbitrary omitted upstream processes. Separately, a data-gap exclusion requires the applicable dataset hierarchy to be exhausted and the gap disclosed and reviewed as specified by its branch. The 2026 guidance does not waive investigation of EF and EL-ILCD availability. Record searches, alternatives, availability, suitability, significance and reviewer reasoning in study evidence. No module flag or dataset-unavailable assertion grants a consumer waiver.

## PEF-06 — Primary activity data and representative secondary data

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §§4.6.1,4.6.2,4.6.5.1–4.6.5.8,Tables 23–25

Use product-specific company activity data for the bill of materials and manufacturing, with measured quantities, collection period, facility/technology scope and transparent aggregation. Record secondary dataset identity/version, provider, geography, temporal and technological representativeness, completeness, methodological consistency and precision/uncertainty; apply the appropriate data-needs situation. All new datasets created in the study shall be EF compliant under the ordinary rules; the interim study-level DQR/submission exception does not itself remove new-dataset obligations. Under the ordinary EF-compliant-data rules, calculate and report new-dataset and whole-study DQR from technological (TeR), time (TiR), geographical (GeR) representativeness and precision (P): DQR = (TeR + TiR + GeR + P) / 4. Apply the Data Needs Matrix to every process and report its situation: company-operated (1), not company-operated but company-specific information accessible (2), or no such access (3). Table 25 requires company-specific datasets with DQR ≤1.5 for Situation 1 and Situation 2 Option 1. Situation 2 Option 2 requires company-specific transport activity and supply-chain-specific electricity/transport replacements in disaggregated secondary EF datasets, with DQR ≤3.0 and the prescribed criterion reassessment. Situation 3 uses aggregated EF secondary datasets with DQR ≤3.0; recalculate criteria for most-relevant processes and otherwise retain original scores. The two Situation 2 options are not hierarchical. For new company datasets assess activity data and direct elementary flows separately, identify the most-relevant contributions and apply §§4.6.5.2–4.6.5.3/Tables 23–24; do not replace their representativeness evidence with a self-assigned score. For whole-study DQR, weight each criterion of all most-relevant processes by relative contribution to the single overall environmental score under §4.6.5.8, then apply the DQR formula. The 2026 non-EF-data study-DQR exceptions in PEF-07/PEF-08 apply only when their actual conditions hold; they do not make ordinary DQR optional for an all-EF study. Quantities, criterion arithmetic and metadata can be checked; suitability, data quality and representativeness require substantive assessment. Unknown data quality is not a passing score.

## PEF-07 — 2026 hierarchy without a Commission PEFCR

Applicability: without-commission-pefcr

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2026-interim:§§1,2.1

While the interim guidance applies, first use available EF-compliant secondary datasets. If unavailable, use EL-ILCD-compliant datasets from one unit-process database if possible, otherwise multiple process databases; disclose their use in report limitations. Only if neither EF nor EL-ILCD datasets are available may the process be excluded, with an explicit limitation. Document the actual search and reasoned selection. Include the prescribed disclaimer that author-selected non-EF datasets may hinder comparability (use exact source wording in the study report). The temporary allowance can exceed the former 10% single-score share; it does not prove equivalence. Do not calculate study DQR for such datasets unless recalculating their TeR, TiR, GeR and P under this guidance. When the study actually uses non-EF-compliant datasets, a summary, main report and annex are required, and the guidance removes the requirement to develop, make available and submit an aggregated EF-compliant dataset to the Commission. Merely following the without-PEFCR branch does not exempt an all-EF-data study from ordinary reporting/dataset requirements.

## PEF-08 — 2026 hierarchy with a Commission PEFCR

Applicability: with-commission-pefcr

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2026-interim:§§1,2.2

For an established applicable Commission-developed PEFCR, retain its primary-data requirements and use EF-compliant secondary data first. If its specified EF dataset is unavailable, prefer Technical Secretariat-selected EL-ILCD replacements published in its annex/corrigendum; if none are selected, the study author selects EL-ILCD data, preferably from one process database, otherwise multiple. Disclose this selection and obtain study/report-verifier validation, with the required comparability disclaimer. Do not import the no-PEFCR process-exclusion permission into this branch. Respect §2.2 DQR and report provisions and do not recalculate the representative product merely because of this guidance. Establish actual category-rule applicability before using this branch.

## PEF-09 — Interim dates and validity

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: machine

Sources: eu-2026-interim:front matter,§1; eu-2021-2279-original:OJ identity; eu-2022-corrigendum:OJ 2022-05-23

Keep the 2021 recommendation identity, the authentic 23 May 2022 corrigendum and its corrected consolidated documentation distinct. The interim manuscript was completed in May 2026; its July announcement is a different event. It is recommended until the next EF database is available; the stated expectation of 2028 is not a guaranteed expiry date. Do not treat a server Last-Modified timestamp as a factor-method release. Reassess current applicability when later guidance or a new database is available.

## PEF-10 — Multifunctionality and allocation

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §4.5

Apply the prescribed hierarchy: avoid allocation through subdivision or system expansion where possible, then use a relevant underlying physical relationship, then another justified relationship such as economic allocation. Explain subdivision/system-expansion feasibility and causal relevance, products and co-products, allocation factors, data and sensitivity. Apply sector-specific provisions where applicable. Arithmetic closure cannot establish that a particular allocation is scientifically appropriate; arbitrary economic defaults must not replace the hierarchy.

## PEF-11 — Energy, transport, infrastructure and other modelling

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §§4.4.1–4.4.7

Assess the applicability of the complete §4.4 modelling requirements, including agricultural inputs, electricity hierarchy and contractual evidence, on-site generation, transport distances/modes/load/returns, capital goods, storage, sampling and use scenarios. Record a source-cited rationale for each inapplicable topic and the actual scenario/source for each applicable one. Grid geography and reporting year must match or carry justified limitations; contractual claims require their prescribed criteria. A generic electricity UUID or fuel-only foreground inventory is insufficient evidence for the upstream model.

## PEF-12 — Circularity and end-of-life conventions

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §§4.4.8,4.4.9

Where recycled inputs, production waste, recovery/recycling, reuse, refurbishment or product end of life apply, use the relevant Circular Footprint Formula and lifetime provisions with source-bound parameters and scenarios. Intermediate-product downstream-stage exclusions do not remove treatment of foreground production waste or recycled-input burdens. Explain allocation of recycled content, energy recovery and disposal and prevent double counting. Parameter presence and mass balances are mechanical checks; quality ratios, substitution and scenario relevance need professional judgment.

## PEF-13 — Greenhouse gases, removals and offsets

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §§4.4.10,4.4.11; jrc-ef31-update:§2

Distinguish fossil, biogenic and land-use/land-use-change climate flows and apply their specific accounting, timing and reporting requirements. Preserve direct process emissions (including cement calcination where relevant), fuel combustion, upstream energy/material burdens and supported removals; do not invent carbon uptake or net-zero assumptions. Offsets are not deducted from PEF results. EF 3.1 uses its source-bound updated climate characterisation; the recommendation year is not the factor version. Review stock/flow boundaries and carbon balance rather than inferring validity from an emission-factor match.

## PEF-14 — All sixteen primary categories and subcategory roles

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §§3.2.3,5.1; jrc-ef31-update:Table 1; jrc-ef31-package:ILCD/lciamethods

Apply all sixteen primary EF categories. source-bindings.json binds their exact official UUIDs, versions, units and source-member hashes, plus the nine subcategory methods. The three climate, four human-toxicity and two ecotoxicity subcategories are decompositions of their parent, not nine extra independent categories for normalisation/weighting or totals. Preserve the organic-cancer filename alias separately from its canonical XML UUID. Coverage of two selected methods cannot qualify a full PEF assessment. A legitimate known zero remains a characterised result with evidence; missing factors or unresolved mappings remain disclosed coverage gaps.

## PEF-15 — Characterisation, flow identity and geography

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §§4.3,5.1; jrc-ef31-package:ILCD/lciamethods

Classify elementary flows to the relevant categories and characterise using the pinned EF method. Match flow UUID/version, direction, reference quantity and units, compartments and any applicable geographic factor. Keep the original source factor location, alternative count and ordinal when selecting nonregional defaults. Absent/empty location and exact cache equivalence do not establish suitability for a study geography or permission to sum alternatives. Missing factors differ from an explicitly supported zero and from justified non-applicability. Record unresolved flow mappings, unmatched factors and suitability judgments; no silent zero substitution is permitted by this guidance.

## PEF-16 — Normalisation definitions and units

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §5.2.1; jrc-ef31-update:§4; jrc-ef31-package:other/lookLciMethodInformation.config

Normalisation is mandatory: divide each characterised primary-category result by its matching normalisation factor, with compatible reference units and the source global per-capita reference basis. Bind all sixteen records to method UUID/version and reference quantity; preserve decimal source values as strings. Report characterised alongside normalised results. Do not sum unweighted normalised results, which would impose equal weighting. The method XML flags normalisation=false and weighting=false describe the characterisation datasets, not an exemption from these PEF steps. Preserve the config ecotoxicity unit typo as source evidence while using the XML/JRC-supported CTUe semantics. Numerical implementation and rounding belong to the consumer.

## PEF-17 — Weighting and single-score interpretation

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §5.2.2 and footnote 60; jrc-ef31-package:other/lookLciMethodInformation.config

Weighting is mandatory. Multiply normalised primary-category results by the matching source weights; official percent weights require division by 100, whereas source-bindings.json already records fractions whose total is one. Do not divide those fractions again. Weighted categories may be aggregated to a single overall score in points, retaining characterised, normalised and weighted results side by side. Do not double-count subcategories or compare unlike declared units/scopes. Preserve the numerical scaling and score unit in consumer provenance rather than calling every dimensionless value a point or inferring public comparability from one scalar.

## PEF-18 — Robustness, hotspots and limitations

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: professional

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §§6.1–6.4

Assess completeness, consistency and sensitivity of boundaries, source selection, allocation and assumptions; refine the model when needed. Identify most relevant categories, stages, processes and elementary flows using the prescribed §6.3 procedures, including treatment of negative values, rather than an arbitrary top-N ranking. Draw conclusions consistent with the goal, data quality and uncertainties, and disclose limitations. Automated receipts and conformance fixtures cannot establish scientific robustness or demonstrate a complete real industry case.

## PEF-19 — Reporting, verification and claims

Applicability: all-pef

Check-kind: mixed

Sources: eu-2021-2279-corrected:Annex I §§7,8; eu-2026-interim:§§1,2.1,2.2

Prepare the PEF report and applicable supporting deliverables covering goal/scope, unit/boundary, inventory/data hierarchy and gaps, modelling choices, characterised/normalised/weighted results, interpretation, sensitivity and limitations. Apply the interim report/DQR/aggregated-dataset exceptions only in their documented branch. Studies disclosed to third parties require the prescribed independent verification/validation; retain verifier competence, scope, findings and statements. AI review, hashes, CI success and a module candidate/review field are not human verification, scientific approval, an EPD, or Commission approval of a cement PEFCR. Communication/comparative claims require their own applicable evidence and rules.
