# Electric filament or discharge lamps; arc lamps

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers factory-gate production of complete electric light-source products whose light is generated by an incandescent filament, a fluorescent low-pressure discharge, another gas-discharge route, or an arc. It covers replaceable and self-ballasted lamps when the light source is the product being reported. The foreground package shall keep filament, fluorescent, other gas-discharge, and arc routes separate through data collection and may aggregate them only with a disclosed mass-weighted production mix.

Covered examples include conventional and halogen incandescent lamps; linear, compact, induction, cold-cathode, and other fluorescent lamps; mercury-vapour, metal-halide, high-pressure sodium, low-pressure discharge, xenon and other gas-discharge or arc lamps; and comparable special-purpose lamps when their route and application are declared.

This PCR excludes LED or OLED light sources, LED modules, luminaires and lighting fittings, portable lamps with their own power source, illuminated signs, lamp parts sold separately, ballasts or separate control gear sold separately, candles, and tungsten filament or other intermediate materials sold as products. A luminaire, a narrow lamp model, a component, or one lamp technology shall not represent the whole category.

The reference dataset is a cradle-to-factory-gate foreground production package. Distribution, installation, use electricity, maintenance, and end-of-life are outside this reference dataset and require separately declared downstream scenarios. The PCR may supply a manufacturing-stage input to a broader cradle-to-grave study, but it is not itself a comparative lighting-service claim.
