# Modular stone-CNC vacuum workholding pod assembly

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Manufacture by assembly of a new standalone modular two-level vacuum workholding pod for a stationary CNC stone machining table. The defined route receives individually finished top/base plates, structural and air-distribution posts, friction pad, upper/lower perimeter seals, interface O-rings, straight push-in fitting and separate ordinary/vented screws. It includes incoming geometry/identity inspection, conditional qualified local minor preparation, matched assembly/seal seating/torque, upper/lower vacuum-path and geometric acceptance, independent complete net weighing and dispatch. Actual drawing, compound/material, interface and included component configuration are declared for each pod; a source illustration does not identify material alloy or polymer chemistry.

Exclude complete stone CNC machines, general vacuum pumps/generators/manifolds/hoses, lifting/handling suction cups, whole clamping tables or sets, independently sold replacement seals/pads/repair kits, refurbishment and customer stone-cutting services. Bought complete-pod resale/testing-only, integral one-piece or other host/material architecture requires separate applicability. Plate/post machining, casting and finishing, pad/seal compounding/moulding/vulcanization and screw/fitting production are upstream supplied-part processes, not implicit local operations. External host, workpiece, table, pump, test fixture and packaging excluded from product M. Actual manufacturing test support and rework burdens included; no operating life, holding capacity, machining yield or full cradle-to-gate assertion.

Existing mineral-and-cold-glass-working-machine-tools covers complete hosts and expressly excludes separately sold components. Existing broad tool/work-holder methodology covers broad dedicated holders and offers reusable make/buy, normalization and acceptance controls; it is relevant prior methodology, not an absent record. This narrower modular stone-pod assembly case needs independent matched two-level seal protrusion/contact datum, solid versus vented screw and air-post circuit routing, installed pad/compound/supplier scope, table-side versus workpiece-side leak acceptance and frame-free standalone net M. General chuck/closed hydraulic expansion rules do not establish these interface facts. The new candidate is a bounded subroute refinement, not another universal work-holder PCR or code-only duplicate. Mapping/category placement between44251 workholders and44252 host-specific accessories is unresolved scientific review; no accepted mapping claimed. No other author unfrozen canonical content inspected.

| rule_id | Applies to | Formula or rule | Inputs | Output | source_ids |

q_item is actual attributed exchange after issue/return/stock/rework balances divided by complete accepted same-configuration pod count. M comes independently from the same fitted net configuration. No capacity/load/catalog shipping mass or invented unit weight.
