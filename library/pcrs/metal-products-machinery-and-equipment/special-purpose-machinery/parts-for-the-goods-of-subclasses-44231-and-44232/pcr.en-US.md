# Dedicated parts for pneumatic, hydraulic, non-electric-motor and electric handheld tools

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR develops factory foreground data for complete accepted dedicated parts for BOTH pneumatic, hydraulic or self-contained non-electric-motor handheld tools44231 and self-contained electric-motor handheld tools44232. Preserve actual air, hydraulic and petrol hosts as well as corded and battery electric hosts, brushed/brushless and rotary/percussive/reciprocating functions where actual host fit is proven. The category is not limited to drill or battery-tool spares. One accepted unit is one complete supplied part configuration, not the complete host tool.

CPC44253 explicitly references both hosts. CPC44251 separately covers tool-holders for ANY handheld tool; removable chucks/holders, cutting blades/bits/abrasive wheels and independent accessories are individually reviewed. General motors/engine parts, bearings, screws, switches, batteries, chargers, cables and hoses are not automatically44253 references because they appear on an OEM spare list; separately classified goods may be compatible measured inputs to an accepted dedicated part. New manufacture differs tool repair/reconditioning, installation service and customer replacement/operation.

Bosch original April2025 catalogue contains actual corded GWS6-125 grinder and GST90BE jig-saw housings, armature/field, brushes, gearing, cords/switches and guards, GHO10-82 planer housings/belt/planer-head constructions, and GSR18V-50 cordless drill-driver stator/electronics/gearbox modules. Battery and charger alternatives remain independent supply/classification questions. Online spare-parts guidance requires actual model/article fit and sometimes complete assemblies rather than separate constituents. Older diagram issue dates do not become catalogue publication dates. Font-encoded catalogue numerical extraction is corrupted: readable model/part names were compared against actual PDF images; no catalogue prices, quantities or grease-tube pack volumes become manufacturing factors.

Chicago Pneumatic CP3450 drawings distinguish output-shaft/guard/rear-body/safety-lever variants, cylinder/rotor/spiral bevel gear, bearings and supplied vane/tune-up kits. The blade material and O-ring compound are unspecified; a kit count is not a universal part recipe. STANLEY BR87 OEM6/2019 manual, actually obtained through E.C.Hopkins distributor mirror after official URL access gaps, specifies hydraulic valve/spool/piston/flow-sleeve/housing and accumulator cylinder/diaphragm/chamber variants with a nitrogen-pressure decal, plus complete-only breaker-foot assemblies. Nitrogen identity presence does not prove pressure, fill mass or a universal factory test. General hydraulic components and tool holders still require their own item boundary.

Husqvarna K770 actual2017-11 parts list distinguishes crankcase/cylinder/piston/muffler assemblies and their inclusion sets, gaskets/bearings and configuration-dependent supply. Its actual service kit contains air filter, fuel filter and spark plug; petrol host rating/cutting depth, kit catalogue count and aftermarket life claims are not part-factory BOM, accepted mass or trial recipe. Engine/filter/ignition items may fall into their own general categories and cannot be accepted merely from compatibility.

Alumax explicitly manufactures aluminium HPDC power-tool wheel guards and impellers alongside machine-tool covers/tool pockets and compressor parts: only actual dedicated handheld-tool parts belong here, not its entire portfolio or the1.45kg turret-cover example. No alloy grade is specified. Hualong actual2009 project uses reinforced nylon and TPE secondary encapsulation for a power-tool housing; its718H/50-gauge steel and500000mould cycles concern the mould, not housing composition or tool lifetime. Reinforced nylon does not establish PA6/66, filler identity/fraction or all grades; TPE does not establish TPU/NR/SBS. Moulding/casting methods are conditional actual routes; bought finished blanks/housings and own raw manufacture are mutually reconciled interfaces.

Reference output is1kg calibrated accepted net dedicated-part mass. Dnet/Naccepted/M describe the same complete part configuration/cohort. Include only actual retained part hardware or proven supplied fill; exclude packing, rejects, consumed qualification media, complete host tool and customer workpieces. Collect Qattr for actual manufacture including rejects/rework and factory test burden. Conditional atomic cards prescribe identity and collection checks, not a universal recipe. All actual additional alloys/compounds/coatings/chain or engine subcomponents/test media/wastes/emitted species need their own bounded correct-role identity queries and measured records.

The actual8467 technical body also expressly preserves electric chain/circular/reciprocating saws, drills/hammer tools, grinding/polishing/sanding, screwdrivers/nut-runners/impactwrenches, routers/planers/shears and grass/weedtools, plus non-electric chain saws and petrol brushcutters/other horticultural tools and pneumatic/otherparts. These are host-scope examples, not a fixed partBOM. Chain/bar/lubrication or other actual dedicated subcomponents need their own supplied-item boundary, additional atomic identity queries and measured factoryrecords when present; standalone cutting/holder goods remain separately reviewed.

## 9. Validation Rules

| rule_id | Applies to | Rule | source_ids |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `identity` | dataset | Verify actual dedicated44253 accepted part and BOTH44231 air/hydraulic/non-electricmotor and44232 electricmotor host scope. Independently review model fit, holders44251, interchangeabletools/general motors/engineparts/batteries/chargers/switches/bearings. Each exchange own chemistry/grade/supply/nativeproperty/provider, unknown notzero. |  |
| `denominator` | all inventory rows | All inventory uses the same accepted cohort and common period. Verify calibrated accepted net mass and N; reject and packaging mass excluded. Check q_item=Q/N then normalization by same mean M; mixed configurations are invalid. |  |
| `double_count` | make_buy | Reconcile complete bought modules versus own materials and operations, retained fills/accessories versus factory consumption, paired internal transfers and external inputs. Count each actual burden once. |  |
| `water_close` | physical water records | For each term use its own measured water fraction, density and wet/dry basis: fresh and input moisture plus reaction water and beginning stocks minus final stocks, retained product, discharge and evaporation; internal returns cancel paired. Investigate measured closure against combined sampling/meter/allocation uncertainty; no universal tolerance. |  |
| `species_close` | material and chemical records | Close each contained metal/chemical separately using each input, product, scrap, sludge, liquid and release own matched assay and dry/wet basis, reaction stoichiometry and stocks. Gross mass is not contained element. No all-inventory mass rule applies to energy or transport. |  |
| `solvent_close` | solvent records | Distinguish retained solvent, recovered return, captured liquid/media, demonstrated destruction, wastewater/non-air residual and actual species air release. Capture is not destruction; an unexplained residual must be investigated, not assigned to air. |  |
| `utility_close` | energy records | Reconcile purchased imports, actual on-site generation, exports and storage changes with assigned fabrication/finish/integration/test/dispatch loads in the same period and units. Shared row ONLY unassigned residual; investigate negative residual against period, unit and combined measurement uncertainty without clipping. |  |
| `steam_close` | steam and condensate | Use supply kg times supply own MJ/kg and return kg times return own MJ/kg at measured pressure/temperature relative to common zero. If gross supply, subtract return once; if already-net invoice, do not subtract again. Keep physical steam/condensate mass balance independent from energy. |  |
| `species_emissions` | air releases | Validate every emitted species and compartment independently. Fuel carbon balance cannot alone establish CO or NOx. NO2 mass is not NOx reported as NO2 equivalent; keep reporting conventions and actual species identities distinct. |  |
