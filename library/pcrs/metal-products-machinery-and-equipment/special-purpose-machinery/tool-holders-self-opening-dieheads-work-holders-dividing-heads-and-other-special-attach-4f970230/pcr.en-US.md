# Tool holders, self-opening dieheads, work holders, dividing heads, special attachments and dedicated machine-tool parts

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This candidate covers the FULL category of tool holders, self-opening dieheads, work holders, dividing heads and other special attachments for machine-tools, tool-holders for ANY type of tool for working in the hand, and other parts/accessories for ALL class4421 hosts. It is not limited to metal CNC chucks. Full4421 includes any-material laser/light/photon beam, ultrasonic, electro-discharge/electrochemical/electron/ionic beam/plasma and waterjet removal hosts, plus metal machining centres/transfer machines, lathes, drilling/boring/milling, threading, grinding/finishing/removal, forging/forming/presses and nonremoving machine-tools. Review actual principal function, dedicated geometry/interface and supplied completion for each item. The explicit any-handworking-tool holder limb does not bring every powered hand-tool part into scope. Adjacent stone/wood/hard-material host parts44252 and other powered hand-tool parts44253 retain their own boundaries. Independent cutting dies/blades/bits, grinding tools, forming moulds, optics, ceramic/glass/material articles and general motors/pumps/bearings are not automatically reference products because used on a machine; actual dedicated complete mechanical/composite assembly versus independently supplied article requires item review. Source: `un-cpc-44251`.

RÖHM PRIMA describes threaded/conical and SDS mounting, gear-ring/key holding and hand-operated or stationary drill interfaces. EXTRA50/80 manufacture explicitly includes a steel clamping body CNC-milled from one piece, metal clamping sleeve, radial locking, carbide jaw inserts and individual factory inspection. These are specific product cases, not universal steel grade, carbide binder, insert count or recipe. Independent held drill bits remain cutting tools. RÖHM power chucks distinguish machine-actuated hydraulic/pneumatic cylinders, integrated pneumatic cylinder variants, ball-lock/draw-down and indexing options; manual versus powered clamping and external host cylinder versus included cylinder must be recorded. SCHUNK TENDO WZS supports manual actuation of a CLOSED hydraulic expansion clamping system and compatible intermediate sleeves. Hydraulic technology alone does not prove shipped oil volume, mineral-oil formulation or external hydraulic power pack. Sources: `roehm-hand`; `roehm-manufacture`; `roehm-workholder`; `schunk-tendo`.

RIDGID's actual self-opening head sheet identifies body/post, trigger/slide, roll pin, size bar, lock screw, clamp and spring-loaded throwout mechanism with model-dependent host fitting. Replaceable cutting dies are matched sets; the trigger automatically opens only in the stated pipe-threading cases and some sizes/bolt/straight threads require manual opening. The complete accepted head supply must disclose whether dies are actually included, individually classified or supplied separately; no shipment or wear-die quantity follows from replacement availability. A documented factory acceptance test thread is factory consumption only, not customer threaded-pipe production. Sources: `ridgid-diehead`.

Haas HA5C RevN June2006 identifies mechanical work-holding head plus controller, specially ground/heat-treated self-locking worm/gear and servo actuation; that specific case names aluminium-bronze worm gear and8620 chromium-nickel-molybdenum steel worm. Brushless AC and brushed DC alternatives and the actual compatible drive must be distinguished. The specific manual calls for synthetic Mobil SHC630 gear lubricant; a mineral-petroleum lubricating-oil anchor must NOT replace that synthetic grade without an independently compatible supply identity. Catalogue workpiece load, motor rating, angular speed, oil requirement or dimensions do not establish accepted attachment mass or manufacturing consumption. New part manufacture, used-part repair and whole host production are separate interfaces. Sources: `haas-index`.

Makino's actual wire-EDM sources identify removable PICO round guide assembly, upper/lower heads and energizing plates, with carbide contact plates, wire transport/tensioning and dielectric/flushing architecture. Guides/contact components as delivered complete dedicated parts are distinct from consumable electrode wire, ion-exchange resin, filters, independent raw carbide or the complete EDM host. Actual part manufacture/qualification may consume DI water or other evidenced media; customer EDM dielectric recipe, wire usage, cutting speeds and machining throughput are not part-manufacturing quantities. Sources: `makino-edm`; `makino-u3`.

OMAX's November2023 Revision1 parts book identifies MAXJET5 nozzle/mixing-tube collet, lock nut, chamber/disc, guard, filter assemblies and nitrile O-ring size variants, and states genuine parts are manufactured to their actual material-grade/dimensional tolerances and tested for functionality/reliability. Assembly, independent jewel/abrasive, spare/rebuild kit and whole waterjet host interfaces are distinct; the list does not prove all items shipped together. Precitec lists model/wavelength-specific protective windows and nozzle electrodes. Its actual note explicitly removes previously included O-rings from future protective-window deliveries. Thus neither a historical photo nor an available spare proves current included fill/accessories. A standalone optical window/ceramic tip keeps its material/optical-product boundary; a genuinely dedicated assembled mechanical cassette or head is reviewed separately. Sources: `hypertherm-waterjet`; `precitec-parts`.

WILA actual2024 catalogue distinguishes clamping/crowning/bottom tool holders from held bending tools, with hydraulic/pneumatic/manual options and model-family CR-MO steel/deephardened tool-slot construction. These are specific supply alternatives, not universal hardness, case depth, alloy chemistry, oil fill or catalogue mass. Dedicated metal-forming clamping/crowning/guide or other nonremoving-host parts remain within the full category when actual host and dedicated supplied interface qualify; independently supplied punch/die/mould and generic hydraulic hardware remain separate. Manufacturer architecture is qualitative evidence only. JRC2024 primary forging/foundry chapters support conditional heating/forming/heat treatment/mechanical processing and melting/moulding/casting/finishing routes, not one default route for all parts. Record actual make/buy starting state: raw/semi-finished stock, prepared blank, heat-treated precursor, complete bought part and new versus reconditioned supply cannot be collapsed. Own operations and complete bought embedded manufacture are counted once. The finite cards are conditional measured anchors; atomically extend all actual grades, coating/bond constituents, accessory components, qualification media and outgoing streams. Factory consumed trial stock/water/gases enter attributable Qattr only, never Dnet; reusable fixture stocks/returns are separate. Customer cutting/forming services and capacity are outside this part manufacturing reference. Sources: `wila-catalog`; `jrc-smithery-foundry`; `un-cpc-44251`.
