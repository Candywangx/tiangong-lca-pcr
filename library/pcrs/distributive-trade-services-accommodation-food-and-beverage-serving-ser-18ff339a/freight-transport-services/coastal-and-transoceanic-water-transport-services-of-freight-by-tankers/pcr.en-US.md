# Marine bulk-tanker freight transport work

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Cover coastal and transoceanic water freight carriage of crude oil and other bulk liquids/gases in special tankers, including actual natural gas/methane/LNG, LPG, refined petroleum, chemicals and compatible food or other bulk liquids. Actual ambient/heated/refrigerated/cryogenic/pressurized containment, propulsion and ship/shore or ship-to-ship transfer configurations are conditional; neither all tankers are oil-only nor all tankers refrigerated. Exclude inland-waterway-only freight, intermodal tank-container or packaged cargo service, dry bulk, independent pipeline/land/air transport, cargo production/refining/liquefaction/trade, bare vessel hire and independent floating storage or processing functions. Actual integrated conditioning needed for this carriage remains included, with independently supplied terminals/providers scoped once.

The functional product is qualified cargo carriage work with declared actual phase/quality/handover, not crude/LNG production, ship manufacture, ballast movement or an assumed evaporative loss allowance.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

The cp_expand_ protocols in section 8 are mandatory identification requirements, never selected flows or quantities to exchange. For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids. Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.
