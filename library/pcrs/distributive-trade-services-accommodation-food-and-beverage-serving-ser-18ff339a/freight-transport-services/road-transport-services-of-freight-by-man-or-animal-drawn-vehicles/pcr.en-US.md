# Human- or animal-drawn road freight service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This dedicated PCR covers actual freight transported by road in man- or animal-drawn vehicles. It preserves both human and draft-animal implementations without narrowing to one cargo, animal species or cart material. One actual accepted consignment leg has declared cargo quantity, OD/distance/terrain, duration and quality; equal service counts do not establish equivalence. Human or animal effort and the drawn vehicle are supporting means, not the transported product.

Exclude passenger transport, motorized freight, unvehicled carrying/pack-back animals, bare cart rental, independent courier final delivery and a separately classified 65118 live-animal transport service. Live cargo alone does not exclude an otherwise qualifying drawn-vehicle leg; distinguish the carried cargo from the traction animal and resolve classification by actual function/equipment without duplicate output. Actual loading, rest/recovery, husbandry, empty return, failed duty and full supported cart/animal/site life remain attributed. No numerical human metabolism, animal feed/emissions, workload or lifespan default is imposed.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

Each inventory card represents one independently identifiable product, material/formulation, waste stream, substance or supplied service at a compatible gate, property and unit. A complete supplied assembly or an actually collected mixture may have one identity; its already included constituents are not additional exchanges. The cp_expand_ protocols in section 8 are mandatory identification requirements, never selected flows or quantities to exchange. For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids. Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.
