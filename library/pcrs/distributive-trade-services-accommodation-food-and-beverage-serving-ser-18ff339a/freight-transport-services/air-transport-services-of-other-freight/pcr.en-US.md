# Air other-freight accepted delivery service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers air transport of individual articles and packages assembled and shipped in specially constructed shipping containers for ease of handling, and all other airfreight not elsewhere classified. Actual loose, unitized, unusual, temperature-sensitive, live-animal or dangerous cargo stays covered where it performs this freight function. Passenger belly, combi, dedicated freighter, scheduled/nonscheduled and every qualifying actual aircraft/propulsion configuration are conditional real routes, not exclusions. Special shipping-container carriage does not mean only one ULD standard or aircraft type.

The reference is delivery service for 1 kg of actual specified other freight independently accepted at the original declared air-carrier handover and quality. Declare cargo contents and intrinsic packaging basis separately from ULD/container/pallet/net/securing gear, carrier cooling materials such as dry ice, and other support. A genuine originally entrusted packaged article may include its intrinsic packaging once; adding carrier gear or refrigerant cannot enlarge accepted cargo. Declare route/each real flight leg, distance/time, aircraft configuration, actual environmental/care conditions and custody gates before quantification. Compare compatible cargo/function/basis/route/quality strata, not arbitrary cargo kg merely sharing a unit.

Exclude air carriage of letters and parcels on behalf of postal/courier services under its separate carrier function, independent space/other-mode transport, passenger transport as the studied final freight product, independent terminal/storage/forwarding/security functions beyond the declared carrier gate, and goods/packaging/animal rearing manufacture. Necessary carrier-owned consolidation/breakdown, loading/security interface, ground transfer, flight/ULD/cold/live-animal/dangerous-cargo support remains included. Actual shared passenger, baggage, mail and other cargo functions still participate in causal pools and asset life; an excluded final product is not a free shared user.

Entrusted cargo is custody, not ordinary cargo-production input. Arrival, loading, chargeable/volumetric weight, payment, capacity and ULD tare do not prove accepted delivery. Actual failed/off-quality/partial/diverted/returned/retained or lost cargo retains its real duty burden and evidenced fate; genuine chronological amended acceptance cannot retrospectively erase failure. Record real replacement responsibility once without charging the same replacement again to shipper, airline, insurer or handler. Actual required carrier support has native supply and lifecycle burden regardless of ownership/purchaser; opening/customer-owned fuel or energy is not free.
