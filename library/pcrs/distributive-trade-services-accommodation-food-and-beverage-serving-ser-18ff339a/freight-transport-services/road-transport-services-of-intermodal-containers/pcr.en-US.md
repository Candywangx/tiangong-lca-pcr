# Road intermodal-container freight leg service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers carriage by road of articles and packages assembled in purpose-built intermodal shipping containers. The service is road carriage of the containerized goods between the declared taking-in-charge and delivery interfaces. Container design, cargo type, road vehicle, propulsion, carrier geography, domestic or international route and actual handling or temperature-control conditions are declared parameters, not silent restrictions to a convenient dry-container or diesel subtype. A container does not have to change modes during the studied leg to qualify.

The reference is one completed laden-container road leg, not a whole international door-to-door multimodal consignment, an invoice, a container manufacture item, an empty-container positioning trip or a tonne-kilometre. Declare container identity, dimensions/type, cargo net and gross mass, contents and quality, actual road distance, elapsed duty, pickup/delivery gates and acceptance. A truck carrying several containers produces their independently accepted container legs; one container moving through several internal vehicle duties still produces one declared leg. TEU and rated capacity are descriptors, not automatic counts or delivery measures.

Exclude separately supplied rail, inland-water, sea, air or space legs; standalone forwarding, packing/unpacking, storage or terminal transfer sold as distinct functions; container/vehicle manufacture or rental as the final product; ordinary non-container freight, tank-truck, moving, letter/parcel, live-animal or refrigerated-vehicle service whose actual classification is another road subclass. Determine overlap from the actual supplied function and equipment rather than cargo name alone; the same physical event cannot be outputs of two transport categories. Actual road-haulier-controlled lift, lashing inspection, waiting, reefer connection and delivery support stay inside the leg when the contract and responsibility establish inclusion.

N counts only actual completed comparable laden-container legs with documented cargo and container handover and accepted promised scope. Preserve original promised scope, chronological amendments, damaged or rejected deliveries, cancellations and partial or failed duties. Never retrospectively redefine a failed leg as completed; an independently accepted amended output needs its actual scope and separate comparability. Retain causally attributable empty tractor/chassis/container positioning, waiting, detours, return and recovery burdens in the serving pool without inventing additional completed output. If N is zero, no finite per-leg intensity exists.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

Each inventory card represents one independently identifiable product, material/formulation, waste stream, substance or supplied service at a compatible gate, property and unit. A complete supplied assembly or an actually collected mixture may have one identity; its already included constituents are not additional exchanges. The cp_expand_ protocols in section 8 are mandatory identification requirements, never selected flows or quantities to exchange. For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids. Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.
