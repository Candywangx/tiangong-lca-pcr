# Other-goods pipeline delivery service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers pipeline transport of other chemical products, coal slurry and other goods not elsewhere classified, in their actual gaseous, liquid, slurry or other qualifying state. The full category is not restricted to one chemical, water-based coal slurry or one pumping technology. Actual cargo composition, phase, carrier medium, pipeline configuration, route, distance, elevation, pressure, temperature, duration, required quality and accepted custody gates define the service. Physical examples from U.S. pipeline sources illustrate real chemical states; they do not override CPC classification.

The reference is the pipeline delivery service for 1 kg of actual specified cargo delivered and accepted at its declared handover. Accepted mass throughput supports both actual batch and continuous carriage without inventing a transaction count. Declare the cargo mass basis before quantification: a genuinely contracted slurry or solution mixture retains that mixture basis; a contracted dry-solids or named constituent obligation uses its actual measured constituent basis with carrier medium excluded. These are different qualified service strata, not interchangeable denominators. Record actual composition, moisture and measured conversion; never switch basis retrospectively, count carrier/makeup/flush water as delivered solids, or compare unlike chemicals, routes, states and quality merely because kg is common.

Exclude pipeline services assigned to the natural-gas and crude/refined-petroleum subclasses, distribution of steam or water through mains, independent nonpipeline transport, and independent cargo manufacture, capture, storage, sequestration or use beyond the actual transport interface. Necessary carrier-accountable pressure/thermal support, slurry preparation, suspension, handling, dewatering or terminal duty remains included when it belongs to the actual promised carriage handover. A CCS cargo transfer is transport only; it does not automatically import capture/storage burdens or confer permanent-storage or avoided-emission credit.

Cargo receipts, tank/linepack stock, recirculation, internal transfer, return, rejected or contaminated material and environmental loss are custody/state records, not additional transport outputs or consumed cargo manufacture. Accepted delivery mass is independent of pumped circulation and nominated or invoiced throughput. Preserve original quality and genuine chronological amendments; failure, rejection or undelivered stock retains attributable duty without accepted output. Any actual cargo-replacement responsibility must be disclosed and counted once at its real owner rather than importing all ordinary cargo production.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

Each inventory card represents one independently identifiable product, material/formulation, waste stream, substance or supplied service at a compatible gate, property and unit. A complete supplied assembly or an actually collected mixture may have one identity; its already included constituents are not additional exchanges. The cp_expand_ protocols in section 8 are mandatory identification requirements, never selected flows or quantities to exchange. For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids. Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.
