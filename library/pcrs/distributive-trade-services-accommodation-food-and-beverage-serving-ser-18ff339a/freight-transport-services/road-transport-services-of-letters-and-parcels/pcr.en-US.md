# Nonrail land mail-carrier consignment leg service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers transporting letters and parcels by any qualifying mode of land transport other than railway, on behalf of a postal or courier service. The actual supplied function is a carrier carriage leg for that operator, evidenced by the transport agreement, load membership and carrier/operator custody interfaces. Road vans/trucks are examples, not a restriction to motor vehicles, diesel, long haul or one geography. Any actual qualifying nonrail land propulsion or conveyance requires its own native operation, support, assets and environmental records. A human-powered or animal-drawn carrier leg is not excluded solely by technology, but the explicitly excluded bicycle-messenger and courier/local-delivery functions cannot be relabelled as this service.

The reference is one completed actual carrier consignment leg between declared custody gates for an evidenced agreed load and quality, not one letter/parcel, postal dispatch, bag, cage, vehicle departure, invoice, route circuit or end-user delivery. Define actual load-manifest membership before operation: a carrier consignment may contain several receptacles, dispatches and mailpieces; none is automatically another consignment. A consignment may traverse several internal duties or equipment changes without multiplying the final output. Where actual independently supplied obligations have different gates, preserve their true boundaries. N counts accepted functionally comparable carrier legs; actual mail mass, dimensions, item/receptacle counts, distance and duration remain mandatory descriptors. No universal bag-size, parcel mass or item-to-tonne-distance factor is supplied.

Exclude railway mail carriage, air/water/space legs, bicycle messenger services, integrated courier delivery services and local delivery services identified in the CPC note. Also exclude the whole postal/courier end-user acceptance-sorting-delivery network, standalone forwarding/warehousing/sorting/packing, equipment rental/manufacture or mail contents manufacture as the final product. Physical proximity or an urban route alone does not decide the category: determine whether the actual supplied obligation is qualifying carriage on behalf of a postal/courier operator or the excluded delivery product. Include actual loading, scanning, sorting or holding only to the extent they fulfil this carrier leg responsibility; distinctly supplied sorting/storage remains a separate interface. Never count the same event as both full courier delivery and an additional complete carrier service without a non-overlapping supply relationship.

Prove actual carrier handover against original promised load, gates, time/security/handling quality and chronological amendments. Recipient acceptance of a missing or damaged load is evaluated against the real contract; payment, vehicle arrival or a scan alone does not prove full completion. Retain rejected, partial, cancelled, failed or misrouted duties and accountable return/recovery attempts. Never retrospectively shrink promised scope to manufacture success. Independently accepted amended or partial outputs need real scope and separate comparability. Causal empty movement, repositioning of vehicles and reusable mail receptacles, waiting and failures retain burden in their serving pool, not extra successful outputs; N=0 has no finite per-leg result.
