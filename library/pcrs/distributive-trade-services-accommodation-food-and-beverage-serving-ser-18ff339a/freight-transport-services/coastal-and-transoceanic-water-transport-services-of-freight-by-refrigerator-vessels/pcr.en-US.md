# Refrigerated coastal and ocean freight transport work

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This dedicated PCR covers coastal and transoceanic transport of frozen or refrigerated goods in specially refrigerated compartments, across the full actual refrigerator-vessel service. It includes actual conventional reefer holds and qualifying refrigerated-unit arrangements, rather than limiting the product to containers, one commodity or one cooling/propulsion technology. The product is qualified cold maritime carriage between actual carrier gates. Reference is 1 tkm of delivered accepted cargo net mass-tonnes times actual loaded maritime kilometres by segment, with cold quantity/quality and custody qualification. Ship/equipment/reusable packaging tare and service-added coolant are separate; capacity/TEU or sold goods are not this transport output.

The official leaf excludes coastal/transoceanic liquid-gas cargo transport under65212, even when that cargo is refrigerated. LNG or another gas actually consumed as ship fuel is an energy input, not an excluded liquid-gas cargo service. Exclude inland-waterway carriage, other finished modes, bare ship/equipment hire, independent cold storage and original cargo production. Actual contracted port/handling/cold interfaces remain included by real responsibility; independent providers enter once at declared gates. Resolve reefer-compartment versus intermodal/container and other service classification overlap by actual function/equipment/contract, without duplicated completed work; container presence alone neither defines nor excludes this scope.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

Each inventory card represents one independently identifiable product, material/formulation, waste stream, substance or supplied service at a compatible gate, property and unit. A complete supplied assembly or an actually collected mixture may have one identity; its already included constituents are not additional exchanges. The cp_expand_ protocols in section 8 are mandatory identification requirements, never selected flows or quantities to exchange. For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids. Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.
