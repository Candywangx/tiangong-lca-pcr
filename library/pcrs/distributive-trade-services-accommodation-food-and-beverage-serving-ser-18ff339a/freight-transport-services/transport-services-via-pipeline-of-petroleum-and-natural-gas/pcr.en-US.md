# Petroleum and natural gas pipeline transport work

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This dedicated PCR covers transport via pipeline of crude/refined petroleum, petroleum products and natural gas. Its product is actual qualified pipeline transport between declared receipt and delivery gates, not sale or production of the conveyed commodity. Full actual liquid/gas phases, composition, pressure, temperature, continuous or batch operation, pumping/compression and conditional transport-support duties remain in scope. Reference is 1 tkm of accepted delivered commodity mass-tonnes times its supported physical pipeline path kilometres, with actual path/period/cohort attribution; nominal capacity, inlet quantity, linepack stock or commodity energy alone is not delivered transport work.

Exclude mains distribution of natural gas under69120/86320 and LNG liquefaction/regasification under67990, as the official leaf directs. Exclude commodity extraction/refining/independent processing or sale, independent storage, and non-pipeline transport as finished functions. Transport-responsible pressure regulation, metering, heating/cooling, separation or treatment is included only in its actual contracted transport role; separate commodity-upgrading or distribution facilities remain separate with declared interfaces. Linepack needed for this pipeline duty is not automatically independent storage. Resolve actual petroleum-product classification and functional gates; do not extend this petroleum/gas PCR to unrelated pipeline substances.

For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids.
