# Other road freight service obligation

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers the full residual other-road-freight function: carriage of other freight in specialized vehicles not elsewhere classified, including concrete, tarred macadam and cars; office equipment, machinery and furniture removal services; and other freight not elsewhere classified in non-specialized road vehicles. Actual specialized configuration, ordinary vehicle, removal method, propulsion and geography are declared parameters, not restrictions to ordinary dry vans or one readily modelled commodity. Concrete/mix-condition carriage and actual office/machinery-removal functions remain in scope even though their necessary handling and work differ from a simple loaded road leg.

The reference is one actual completed scoped other-road-freight service obligation. Its original agreed object, cargo/items, origin/destination or removal interfaces, actual task membership, delivered state, time/quality and acceptance define the supplied service. For general/specialized carriage this may be an actual accepted load delivery; for office/machinery removal the agreed service can include real internal handling, dismantling, protection, lifting, carrying, reassembly and positioning at its accepted handover. Keep the true contractual supply extent rather than forcing every service to one road kilometre, tonne-kilometre, truck departure or item. A multi-vehicle/multi-task obligation is one output only if it is genuinely one accepted service, while independently supplied obligations retain their own gates.

Exclude armored-car services and freight functions assigned to other specific road subclasses, such as refrigerated-vehicle carriage, tank-truck carriage, intermodal-container carriage, man/animal-drawn freight, household furniture/goods moving, letter/parcel carriage and dry-bulk/live-animal carriage where their actual supplied function is specifically classified there. Office furniture removal must not be excluded by confusing it with the household-moving subclass. Carriage of cars is not manufacture/rental of cars, towing/repair or armored security merely because a car is involved. Other transport modes, standalone freight forwarding/storage, construction/installation or manufacturing products outside the actual qualifying service remain separate. Overlap is settled by real function/equipment and responsibility, not by renaming the same event or by cargo name alone.

N counts actual completed obligations within a functionally comparable declared stratum. State actual general/specialized/office-removal function, load/items and condition, duty/task extent, gates, mass/volume/count, actual road distance where occurring, internal handling exposure, duration and quality. Do not average unrelated office removal, car-carrier and hot-mix deliveries merely because they all have Count units. Preserve original promises, chronological amendments, failed/partial/rejected/cancelled deliveries or removals, and actual empty/repositioning/return/recovery work. A fee, invoice, arrival or individual completed subtask does not prove the whole obligation. Never shrink failed original scope retrospectively to invent completion; independently accepted partial/amended products need their actual scope and comparability. N=0 retains attributable burden without finite service intensity.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

Each inventory card represents one independently identifiable product, material/formulation, waste stream, substance or supplied service at a compatible gate, property and unit. A complete supplied assembly or an actually collected mixture may have one identity; its already included constituents are not additional exchanges. The cp_expand_ protocols in section 8 are mandatory identification requirements, never selected flows or quantities to exchange. For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids. Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.
