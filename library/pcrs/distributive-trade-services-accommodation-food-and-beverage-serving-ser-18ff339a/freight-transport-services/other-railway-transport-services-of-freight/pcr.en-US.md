# Other rail freight carriage engagement

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Cover railway carriage of cars, trucks and truck trailers, plus all other actual railway freight n.e.c. after resolving the dedicated refrigerated, tank-car, mail, intermodal-container and dry-bulk railway service categories. Actual open/closed/flat/low-floor/autorack/suitable special wagons, roll-on/roll-off, lift-on and other evidenced loading methods are conditional routes, not an autorack-only or new-car-only category. Exclude passengers and their accompanying vehicles (CPC64210), independent nonrail freight, cargo manufacture/trading/repair as an independent product, and bare equipment rental. Determine actual transport function and official classification before including borderline accompanied-freight/loaded-trailer/swap-body cases; a driver presence or a commercial intermodal label alone does not decide classification.

The reference describes the actual contracted carriage service, with material handling, cargo condition and all attributable duties. It does not represent a vehicle manufactured, a rental, a train motion or an undifferentiated railway tonne-km.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

Each inventory card represents one independently identifiable product, material/formulation, waste stream, substance or supplied service at a compatible gate, property and unit. A complete supplied assembly or an actually collected mixture may have one identity; its already included constituents are not additional exchanges. The cp_expand_ protocols in section 8 are mandatory identification requirements, never selected flows or quantities to exchange. For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids. Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.
