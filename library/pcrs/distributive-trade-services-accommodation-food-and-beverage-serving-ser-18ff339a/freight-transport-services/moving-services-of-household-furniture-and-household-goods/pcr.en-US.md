# Household furniture and goods moving engagement service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Cover household furniture and household-goods removal plus qualifying ancillary packing, carrying and in-house moving, including engagements whose actual obligation is only such an ancillary service. Road-powered, manual handling, in-house repositioning and actual lifting or packing routes remain in scope; not every engagement requires all branches. Exclude office furniture/equipment removal (65119), commodity manufacture/sale, bare equipment rental, independently scoped disposal/storage, and unrelated parcel or general freight outputs.

Declare one actual service engagement and accepted obligation: inventoried items, pickup/placement rooms and floors, access/stair/lift conditions, packing/protection/disassembly/reassembly roles where actually furnished, quality and completion gate. Fulfilment may be accepted in-house movement or packing handoff without a road journey or a freight-delivery event. No forced positive km, mass-distance, truck capacity or packaged-item count.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

Each inventory card represents one independently identifiable product, material/formulation, waste stream, substance or supplied service at a compatible gate, property and unit. A complete supplied assembly or an actually collected mixture may have one identity; its already included constituents are not additional exchanges. The cp_expand_ protocols in section 8 are mandatory identification requirements, never selected flows or quantities to exchange. For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids. Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.
