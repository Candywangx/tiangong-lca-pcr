# Road live-animal consignment carriage service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Cover actual road transportation of live animals as cargo, without restricting species, size, purpose, vehicle or containment to one convenient subtype. Terrestrial, aquatic, domestic, companion, captive/wild or other actually eligible animal cargo require their own condition evidence. Exclude animal rearing/breeding/growing, sale/ownership transfer, slaughter/product manufacture, passenger transport, non-road carriage and movement of dead cargo as a different freight function. Actual multimode providers cover only their road responsibility here.

Animals in this service are transported cargo, not working traction animals or productive capital. Record counts/ids/liveweight/condition and custody states outside manufacturing exchanges; no animal purchase, originalrearing burden or useful-life allocation of cargoanimals. Actual feed, water, life-support, sanitation, veterinary care and biological releases during carrier responsibility belong to this declared service interval, with separately disclosed loss/replacement responsibility rather than automatic entireanimal production.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

The cp_expand_ protocols in section 8 are mandatory identification requirements, never selected flows or quantities to exchange. For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids. Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.
