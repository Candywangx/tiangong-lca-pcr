# Air letter and parcel carrier handover service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Cover all scheduled and nonscheduled air transportation of letters and parcels performed on behalf of postal and courier services. The foreground is the actual air carrier/handler custody and contracted receiving handoff, including direct or actual transfer routes and supporting airport operations. Dedicated freighter, passenger belly, combi, small aircraft and any other actually qualifying propulsion/configuration are conditional routes; neither jet aircraft nor international UPU-designated-operator traffic defines the whole category.

Exclude integrated posting/collection/postal-network sorting/last-mile delivery as the reference product, passenger service, independent nonmail air freight, space freight, cargo manufacture/trade and bare aircraft/ULD rental/manufacture. Include only actual carrier-responsible handling, screening/transfer/care and support duties within the declared air carriage boundary. A common company brand or invoice does not merge the postal and air-carrier gates; independently supplied cells are assigned once by actual scope. Pure ground-only carriage cannot be silently relabeled air freight.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

Each inventory card represents one independently identifiable product, material/formulation, waste stream, substance or supplied service at a compatible gate, property and unit. A complete supplied assembly or an actually collected mixture may have one identity; its already included constituents are not additional exchanges. The cp_expand_ protocols in section 8 are mandatory identification requirements, never selected flows or quantities to exchange. For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids. Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.
