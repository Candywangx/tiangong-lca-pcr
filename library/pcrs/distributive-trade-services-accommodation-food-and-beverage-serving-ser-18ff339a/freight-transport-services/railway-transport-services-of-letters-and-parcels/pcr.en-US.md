# Postal-operator rail letters and parcels carriage service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This dedicated PCR covers transportation of letters and parcels by railway on behalf of postal or courier services. The product is the rail carrier function between actual operator/carrier custody gates, not an integrated postal/courier product from posting to final addressee. Full actual traction, mixed/dedicated trains, cars, receptacles, terminals and subcontracted arrangements remain in scope without assuming one operator, mail class or technology. Reference is 1 tkm of qualified carrier-handover mail-article work using actual article mass-tonnes times actual loaded rail distance by segment; article mass includes its intrinsic envelope/parcel packaging, and excludes reusable carrier receptacle and rollingstock/container tare.

Exclude end-to-end postal/courier intake, postage and final delivery as finished services; road/water/air carriage and other non-mail freight functions; independent storage and bare car/receptacle hire. Actual sorting, scanning, security, loading and transfers inside the contracted rail-carrier gate are included only with real responsibility; separate operator facilities/services are upstream once or explicitly disclosed. Rail mail carried in an intermodal unit is not automatically outside this mail-specific function: resolve actual operator contract/equipment classification overlap without duplicate final output. Original letter/parcel contents manufacture is outside transport product.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

Each inventory card represents one independently identifiable product, material/formulation, waste stream, substance or supplied service at a compatible gate, property and unit. A complete supplied assembly or an actually collected mixture may have one identity; its already included constituents are not additional exchanges. The cp_expand_ protocols in section 8 are mandatory identification requirements, never selected flows or quantities to exchange. For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids. Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.
