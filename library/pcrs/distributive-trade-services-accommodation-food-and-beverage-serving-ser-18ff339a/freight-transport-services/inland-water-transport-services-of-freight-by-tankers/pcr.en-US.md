# Inland tanker bulk-cargo delivery service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers inland-water transport in special tankers of crude oil and other bulk liquids or gases, including actual natural gas, methane, refined petroleum products and every other qualifying bulk cargo. It is not restricted to petroleum, LNG, dangerous goods, self-propelled vessels or one region. Actual cargo composition, grade, phase, temperature/pressure, tanker or tank-barge and pusher/tow configuration, route, distance, duration, required quality and accepted custody gates define the service. Actual waterbody and receiving compartment are recorded; inland navigation does not by itself prove freshwater.

The reference is inland special-tanker delivery service for 1 kg of actual specified bulk cargo delivered and accepted at its declared handover. Real measured accepted throughput represents the carrier function across actual liquids/gases without inventing a tanker-trip or consignment count. Declare the original cargo composition and mixture/constituent mass basis, phase, receipt/delivery gates, route/distance/time and required temperature/pressure/quality before quantification. Compare coherent cargo/state/route/gate strata, not unlike commodities because kg is common. Tank capacity, loaded mass, nominations/invoices, retained heel/vapour, returned cargo, cargo used as fuel or vented material is not additional accepted delivery.

Exclude independent sea/coastal, road, rail or pipeline legs, other inland freight functions outside special-tanker bulk carriage, and independent cargo manufacture, extraction, refining, initial liquefaction or downstream storage/use beyond the actual transport interface. Necessary carrier-owned loading/unloading, stripping, heating/cooling, pressure control, actual reliquefaction, inerting/purging, vapour return, cleaning, degassing and terminal duties remain included when owed by the actual carriage function. Regional inland-waterway examples provide limited qualitative context only; no marine-specific or regional rule is imposed on every inland route.

Entrusted cargo is custody, not consumed manufacturing input. Distinguish liquid/vapour phase change, retained heel, onboard or shore vapour return, recovery, actual waste and direct environmental release; evaporation inside a tank is not atmospheric emission. If actual cargo or boil-off is withdrawn for useful propulsion, heat or support, record its native fuel quantity and compatible upstream fuel supply once after exact provider coverage, regardless of cargo title or purchaser. Separately record actual original-species cargo withdrawal to GCU, flare or other energyless destruction/abatement and its actual reaction/exhaust species; this is custody/fate, not useful fuel, energy export or an automatic fresh cargo-manufacturing supply. Actual abatement fuel, energy, materials and real replacement responsibility remain included once. Initial/opening or customer-owned vessel/tug fuel and stored energy also require compatible native source supply/charging once; trace source, withdrawal and consumption without adding purchased and withdrawn quantities as duplicate supplies. Ordinary conveyed cargo manufacture is not imported in full. Failed or off-quality/partial duties retain burden without fictitious accepted mass or retrospective scope changes; actual replacement responsibility has its own quantity/owner once.
