# Rail live-animal consignment carriage and care service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers railway carriage of live animals, with the actual carrier-accountable care interval and accepted custody handover. The official category is not limited to cattle or other farm livestock: actual terrestrial, aquatic, companion, wild or other qualifying live-animal consignments require their own species, stage, condition, containment and care evidence. Declare actual rail vehicle/containment, train configuration, geography, propulsion, origin/destination, distance, duration and service quality. A particular domesticated-species guide or fish example does not restrict the category or prescribe conditions for every animal.

The reference is one actual completed scoped rail live-animal consignment carriage-and-care obligation. Original agreed animal/cohort membership, live status at taking-in-charge, species/life stage, care responsibilities, rail/care gates, duration/quality and accepted completion define the service. It is not one animal, one surviving animal, liveweight, a breeding/growing animal product, wagon departure, head-kilometre or a generic rail freight tonne-kilometre. A real consignment may span several wagons, handlers, yards or locomotive duties without multiplying its final output. Independent supplied obligations retain actual gates; compare only functionally coherent species/state/care/service strata rather than averaging incompatible consignments because Count is common.

Animals are transported cargo, not working traction assets or feed consumed to create service. Record animal identity/count/mass/state and every live/dead/returned/retained/escaped or unresolved custody transition separately from service completion. Mortality, injury, welfare nonconformance, arrival, payment or an acceptance scan alone does not determine the completed service count: assess actual original promised carriage-and-care quality and genuine chronological amendments at its real gate, preserving adverse outcomes and limitations. Never retrospectively shrink a failed promise or use survivor count to manufacture a successful denominator. If the original obligation is not completed, retain its attributable burden without a completed output; a genuinely independently accepted partial/amended service needs actual scope and separate comparability.

Include actual required loading/unloading, fitness/condition checks, handling, feed/water, ventilation/thermal support, aquatic water/oxygen management, rest, observation, veterinary/emergency and biosecurity tasks throughout the carrier care interval, including real rail shunting/formation, yard dwell, border delay, failed or recovery duties and actual handover gaps. Responsibilities may be split among carrier, animal owner, terminal, keeper or contracted care provider; assign one actual owner and complete necessary missing cells. Do not invent universal feeding, oxygen, space, temperature, rest, mortality or emission factors. Exclude independent road/sea/air legs, animal rearing/breeding/manufacture, slaughter or independently supplied quarantine/clinic functions beyond the declared rail-care interface; actual necessary in-scope care cannot be omitted by calling it an upstream animal-owner activity.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

The cp_expand_ protocols in section 8 are mandatory identification requirements, never selected flows or quantities to exchange. For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids. Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.

Use Q_target / R for an already assigned target total and retain q_ref unchanged for an already equivalent per-reference result. For a provider intensity first multiply by measured demand in its matching native unit. Apply an additional share only for a distinct, still-shared stage. R is the positive qualified output quantity in the same reference unit, cohort and gate. Retain failed and partial duties without enlarging R; for zero output retain the burden with evidenced carry-forward or final disposition. For owned assets use the same-cohort all-user lifetime duty and exclusive initial-component coverage. Delivered services replace only their documented covered stages. Unresolved applicable identity, quantity, support unit, fate or allocation denominator prevents final dataset completion. |  |
