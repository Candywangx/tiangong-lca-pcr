# Road tanker bulk-liquid and gas consignment delivery service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

The category covers road carriage of crude oil, natural gas, refined petroleum products and other bulk liquids or gases in special tank trucks or tank semi-trailers, including food liquids and dangerous or non-dangerous loads. Include actual pressure, ambient, heated or cryogenic containment and all applicable propulsion routes; none is a universal requirement. Tank-container carriage as an intermodal container service, individually transported packaged drums/cylinders, dry-bulk carriage, pipeline carriage, cargo manufacture or trading, and bare vehicle rental are different outputs. For permanently mounted tube/bundle systems, review the actual special bulk-gas tank-trailer function and official classification; the packaged-cylinder exclusion alone neither excludes nor establishes their eligibility. Resolve overlaps from actual offered carriage function and tank configuration; do not double-map one service.

The actual reference is one fulfilled consignment delivery with declared cargo, quantity, distance, gate and quality. It is not one tonne-kilometre, a tank movement, a commodity sale or an arbitrary vehicle trip. Compare Count results only for equivalent disclosed functions and retain actual distance and load distributions.

Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.
