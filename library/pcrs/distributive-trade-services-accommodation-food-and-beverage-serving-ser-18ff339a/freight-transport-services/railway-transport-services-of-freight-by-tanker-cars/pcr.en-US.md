# Rail tank-car bulk-liquid and gas freight transport work

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers railway carriage of crude oil, natural gas, refined petroleum products and other bulk liquids or gases in special tank cars, including actual ambient, heated, pressurized, liquefied or cryogenic configurations and all actual traction routes. Resolve permanently mounted gas-tube/battery equipment from actual special tank-car function and classification, not an unsupported blanket inclusion/exclusion. Exclude intermodal tank-container carriage as a container service, packaged cargo, dry-bulk tank-shaped wagons, road/pipeline/water/air/space transport, bare wagon rental and cargo manufacture/trade.

The actual reference is one tonne-kilometre of qualified completed rail-tanker freight work, with actual cargo state, rail segment net load and distance, conditions and custody/service gates. Tank-car Count, gross train mass-distance, bills, empty motion and pumping cycles are different measures. The PCR notation tkm is exactly the verified native t*km unit (1000 kg*km); final TIDAS exchanges use native unit name t*km.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero.
