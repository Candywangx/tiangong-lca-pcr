# Coastal and ocean container-ship freight-leg service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers coastal and transoceanic carriage by container ships of individual articles and packages assembled in specially constructed shipping containers designed for transport handling. All actual qualifying container types,coastal/ocean routes,ship configurations and propulsion remain in scope,including real dry,special or temperature-controlled containers when this is the actual container-ship function. A refrigerated CTU on a container ship is not automatically refrigerator-vessel carriage in specially refrigerated ship compartments; determine the real function and gate independently.

The reference is one actual completed and independently accepted laden-container carrier sea leg. The entrusted articles/packages,actual box identity/type/size,custody gates,coastal/ocean route,distance,time,required quality and original or genuine chronological amended service define the obligation. It is not one TEU,slot,ship departure,port call,container asset or generic cargo tonne-kilometre. Multiple ship segments or transshipment inside the same genuine carrier obligation do not multiply the final output;real independently supplied legs retain their own actual gates. Compare compatible box/cargo/route/time/quality strata,not all counted boxes as equal.

Exclude independent road,rail or inland-water legs,other actual vessel/commodity functions outside this container-ship category,and independent terminal/depot/storage or cargo/container/ship manufacture beyond the actual carrier interface. Necessary included loading,lifting,lashing,stowage,condition support,terminal or transshipment duties remain in the service when actually owed. Empty container/vessel repositioning,ballast voyages,waiting,anchor/berth,failed or return duties belong to their evidenced serving pool once;they are not relabelled as completed carriage of laden articles/packages.

Cargo is entrusted custody,not consumed manufacturing input. Preserve actual cargo/box loss,damage,partial or rejected handover,return,recovery and retained state separately from service completion. Arrival,payment,verified gross mass or a loading scan does not by itself establish original required service quality;failed obligations retain burden without completed output and cannot be retrospectively narrowed into success. Actual replacement responsibility has its own native quantity/provider owner once. Share each physical container over actual all-mode/all-user life rather than charging full manufacture to every sea/road/rail leg.
