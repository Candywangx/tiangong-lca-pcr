# Rail intermodal-container freight leg service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers railway carriage of articles and packages assembled and shipped in purpose-built intermodal shipping containers. Declare actual container design, cargo, rail carrier/custody interfaces, train/wagon configuration, railway and supply geography, propulsion and actual condition/handling duties. It covers the complete qualifying rail-container function, not merely dry containers, electric traction, one wagon arrangement or one corridor. A box does not have to change transport modes within the studied rail leg to qualify; container carriage remains distinct from a whole multimodal door-to-door product.

The reference is one completed rail carriage leg of one actual laden container at its accepted rail-service handover. This Count object is chosen because the independently entrusted box and its actual custody delivery identify a real supplied rail interface, not because all containers have equal cargo or burden. Declare box identity/type/size, cargo contents/net and gross mass, container tare, loading arrangement, actual rail segment distance, elapsed/dwell time, quality and pickup/delivery gates. Multiple boxes in one train or consignment are separate accepted box-legs only when their actual delivery records support them; changes of locomotive, consist, wagon, border or internal yard duty do not themselves create extra final outputs for the same declared box-leg. TEU, train departures and nominal wagon/container capacity are descriptors, not automatic service counts or quantities.

Exclude independently supplied road, sea, inland-water, air or space legs; standalone forwarding, warehousing, packing/unpacking or terminal transfer sold as separate functions; container/rolling-stock manufacture/rental as the final product; and non-container rail freight or actual separately classified refrigerator-car/tanker/dry-bulk/mail/live-animal functions. Determine overlap from the actual containerized supplied function, equipment and gate rather than cargo name alone. A real conditioned container on a flat wagon is not automatically excluded as refrigerator-car freight, nor is its cooling ignored. Include actual rail-carrier-accountable terminal lift/securing/inspection, shunting, dwell and custody support; independently supplied terminal or other-mode activities keep their documented interface and necessary coverage once.

N counts actual accepted functionally comparable laden-container rail legs in the declared cohort. Original promised box/load/gates/condition/quality and chronological amendments substantiate completion. Preserve damaged, rejected, partial, failed, cancelled and diverted duties plus causal empty locomotives/wagons/containers, repositioning, assembly/disassembly, shunting, waiting and recovery. Arrival, payment or a train movement alone does not prove box delivery; never retrospectively shrink failed promised scope to invent completion. Independently accepted amended/partial services need their own actual scope and comparability. A single box-leg must not be counted again at each internal terminal or traction change, and N=0 has no finite per-leg intensity.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

Each inventory card represents one independently identifiable product, material/formulation, waste stream, substance or supplied service at a compatible gate, property and unit. A complete supplied assembly or an actually collected mixture may have one identity; its already included constituents are not additional exchanges. The cp_expand_ protocols in section 8 are mandatory identification requirements, never selected flows or quantities to exchange. For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids. Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.
