# Inland refrigerated compartment carriage service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers the complete inland water transport function of frozen or refrigerated goods carried in specially refrigerated compartments. It includes actual river, canal, lake and qualifying inland estuary/tidal-reach operations, self-propelled refrigerator vessels and actual refrigerated compartment/barge services in push/tow convoys. It covers all qualifying goods and actual cold technologies, not fish only, one fuel or one vessel design. Declare the actual inland/coastal interface rather than infer mode from salinity or ship flag.

Include carrier-responsible cold custody and handling, actual loading/unloading and terminal transfers within the declared service, navigation/lock/waiting duties, propulsion/auxiliaries, shore and onboard cooling, sanitation, empty/reposition and failure/return duties, actual releases and uncovered asset life-cycle responsibilities. Loading or refrigeration supplied by another party is assigned once by its actual covered gate; ownership/payment alone does not define the boundary.

Exclude original cargo production/harvest/fishing/processing and trading, independent storage/freezing/processing outside the declared carriage, passenger transport, coastal/ocean refrigerated carriage, and equipment manufacture/rental as the reference service. Resolve adjacent tanker bulk-liquid/gas, container or other inland freight functions from actual equipment and contractual service before classification; cooling a bulk-gas tank or carrying reefer containers does not automatically establish this specially refrigerated-compartment category. Conversely actual LNG used as vessel fuel does not make the cargo a gas-tanker service.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

Each inventory card represents one independently identifiable product, material/formulation, waste stream, substance or supplied service at a compatible gate, property and unit. The cp_expand_ protocols in section 8 are mandatory identification requirements, never selected flows or quantities to exchange. For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids. Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.
