# Other coastal and ocean freight commission service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This dedicated PCR covers the complete other coastal/transoceanic freight function: letters/parcels carried on behalf of postal/courier services; dry bulk such as cereals, flours, cement, sand and coal; high-sea/coastal towing and pushing, including oil rigs, floating cranes, dredging vessels, buoys, hulls and incomplete vessels; and actual freight not elsewhere classified. Reference is one qualified completed carrier commission, whose physical cargo or towing/pushing task, gates, route, quantity/configuration and original acceptance criteria are fixed before operation. Mandatory separate service strata and functional fingerprints preserve the different mail, dry-bulk, tow/push and other n.e.c. functions; one parcel, bulk cargo movement and rig tow are never assumed equivalent.

Exclude separately classified refrigerator-vessel function65211, bulk liquid/gas cargo function65212 and container-ship intermodal-container function65213, inland waterways and other finished transport modes. Actual function/equipment/contract resolves overlap once; a dry-bulk carrier, towed barge or postal consignment is not automatically a container service. Exclude integrated end-to-end postal service, independent storage, bare equipment hire, original cargo/tow-target manufacture and independent dredging/drilling/platform installation/production or station-keeping functions. Include actual transport-responsible preparation/handling/port/tow assistance interfaces and causal emergency/recovery duties without treating a commercial towing guidance scope as a universal CPC exclusion.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

Each inventory card represents one independently identifiable product, material/formulation, waste stream, substance or supplied service at a compatible gate, property and unit. A complete supplied assembly or an actually collected mixture may have one identity; its already included constituents are not additional exchanges. The cp_expand_ protocols in section 8 are mandatory identification requirements, never selected flows or quantities to exchange. For every such requirement document actual applicability and enumerate all concrete exchanges with separate row ids. Preserve the full applicable scope and record evidence when no exchange occurs; an unexpanded or unknown requirement is incomplete, never zero. Changing a requirement into a collection protocol does not resolve any missing identity or waive any lifecycle, allocation, measurement or boundary obligation.
