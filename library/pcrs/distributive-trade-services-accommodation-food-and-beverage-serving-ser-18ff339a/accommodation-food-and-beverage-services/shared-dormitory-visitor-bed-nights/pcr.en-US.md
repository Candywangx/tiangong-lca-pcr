# Shared dormitory visitor bed-nights

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Actual short-stay visitor accommodation supplied as one independently assigned SINGLE sleeping bed or bunk berth in a shared dormitory. The actual room inventory is supplied per bed to potentially independent visitors; a room need not contain another actual guest that night to retain this regime. Real bed/room plans, configuration, booking inventory, assignment and fulfillment establish the service. It is not a private whole-room overnight or room capacity divided by nominal beds.

One reference is one actual occupied fulfilled single-berth-night under a sourced local accommodation-night convention. Bunk upper/lower berths are actual separate bed-space IDs; multiple bookings/persons/room moves are reconciled physically. A same-service bed move retains one reference with both resource segments. Exclude whole-room exclusive buyout, unused spare beds, multi-sleeper/double-bed equivalents, merely available capacity, paid no-show/cancellation and fixed24hour assumptions. Student/worker residence and combined recreational-camp packages are different services.

Include actual bed readiness, room/common-facility operation, guest bathroom/kitchen/appliance use, access/response, performed or failed turnover/cleaning, linen care and full-life building/fitout/bed-mattress/equipment/textile responsibility across all users and periods. Necessary vacant/blocked/standby/failed duties remain in responsible pools before actual positive occupied output normalization. Existing ownership or outsourcing does not delete physical stages.

Actual included meals/activities/other ancillary duties are conditional real package components, not universally absent or present. Complete physical provider coverage OR reviewed real production-route expansion must include their upstream material/preparation/resource/waste/assets/transport duties before use; a service label cannot remove them. Guest self-bought food/goods manufacture and ordinary travel are outside selected lodging scope while actual guest-origin waste handling remains where operator responsibility exists. No independent industrial laundry, boiler, generation or kitchen technology is presumed.

Operator examples support physical product and collection questions only. No bed count, price, age, check-in clock, cleaning schedule, default life, laundry intensity, statistical fallback, leak rate or universal numerical range is imported. Missing identities/quantities/coverage remain explicit collection gaps and use limits, never invented values or evidence of zero.
