# Visitor-owned tent pitch-nights

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This method supplies an actually fulfilled, exclusively designated campsite pitch-night for a visitor's own tent. Actual plans, pitch/space boundaries, assignment and occupancy records establish one physical pitch; visitor-owned tent/gear/vehicle is not an operator-supplied overnight shelter. Surface, pitch area, electric/water/drainage hookup, access and common-facility package are actual qualifiers, not separate canonical methods or presumed quantities. No equal service is inferred for RV/caravan/rooftop-vehicle pitches, furnished operator tents/cabins, shared open-field per-person camping, permanent residence/storage-only, or a combined overnight-food-recreation camp package.

Cover actual pitch preparation/inspection/turnover, access/reception, grounds/site/common-facility operation and maintenance, all actually supplied pitch utilities and attributable shared sanitation/hotwater/washing/lighting/support. No hookup does not mean zero common energy or water. Include physical full-life siteworks/drainage/roads/common buildings/equipment/reusables manufacture, installation, maintenance, replacement and end-of-life across all consumers and periods. Native land occupation and actual land-transformation events have explicit boundary/stage/owner evidence, exclusive of identical provider or asset-covered land interfaces.

Visitor travel, personally supplied tent/gear/food/fuel production and independently purchased services are outside this defined pitch-supply product unless actually supplied under the declared operator package. Actual operator-supplied equipment/material/utility obligations and guest-origin waste/sanitation responsibility do not disappear because the guest owns a tent. Guest-operated cooking/fire/fuel and operator-produced services need actual actor/scope evidence; absent hookup is not proof of zero load or emissions. Real independent on-site generation, industrial laundry, biological wastewater/composting or food/recreation production requires reviewed route/module expansion and completed duty coverage before full-package use.

First-party examples support product and operation questions, not universal pitch size, guest/tent count, minimum stay, electrical rating, weather closure, cleaning intensity, soil/grass recipe, treatment technology, lifetime or numerical factors. Actual native records, sources and acceptance remain mandatory; missing measurements, generic numerical ranges or concrete flow identities are disclosed gaps, never invented values or zeros.
