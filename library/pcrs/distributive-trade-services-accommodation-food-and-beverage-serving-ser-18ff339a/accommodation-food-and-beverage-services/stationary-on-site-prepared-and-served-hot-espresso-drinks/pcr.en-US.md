# Stationary on-site prepared-and-served hot espresso drinks

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Actually fulfilled standalone HOT espresso-based drink preparation and serving at one actual STATIONARY onsite barista establishment. Actual coffee receiving/grinding/extraction, water and optional milk/alternative/addition formulation/thermal treatment, presentation and recipient supply gate establish the product. A counter, cafe brand, customer, contract or classification leaf alone does not establish identity. Sources show actual espresso/hot-water/steamed-milk forms and cup presentation; no universal recipe, dose, cup volume, milk, shot count, temperature, nutrition, yield, time, quantity or asset life is imposed. [costa-coffees-current; starbucks-espresso-2025; costa-cups-current]

One reference is one physically fulfilled equivalent recipe/portion/presentation/service-version hot drink service at its actual recipient handoff, including all required components and actual supporting duties. Orders, payment, customers, brew cycles, cups capacity, sales totals and swallowed mass cannot substitute provision. Prepared/uncollected/partial/rejected/replaced/reversed cases remain separately recorded; later leftover does not automatically undo an actually supplied drink. Different actual formulations/portions/configurations are separate profiles unless reviewed physical equivalence is evidenced.

One real node owns actual composition, extraction and optional steaming, serving and cleanup; no invented independent factories. Coffee, milk/alternatives, actual other ingredients and water each retain independent source/state/stock/draw and before/after roles. Actual spent wet grounds, extract/liquid/solids/water, steam condensed into drink versus purge/drain/air, milk foam/remaining milk, packaging/reusables/cleaning and all useful/waste/species fates remain native nonadditive views where they describe the same material. No residual is assigned to emissions.

The selected scope excludes food-related beverages already inside a completed meal service; cold/iced/blended/tea/filter/vending/sealed retail/alcoholic/entertainment service; moving/offsite routes and whole lodging/camp/passenger bundles. Actual meals and other drink/nonfood outputs at the same site remain in the complete all-output/all-user physical resource ledger. New independent industrial production/treatment needs reviewed route expansion; ordinary actual filtering/heating/refrigerating/washing and complete supporting lifecycle/receiver duties stay included.

Include actual upstream supplies and losses, full site/building/bar/equipment/reusable/textile/land life across every actual user/period, real providers/receivers/transport including necessary empty duty, readiness/idle/failure/N0 responsibility. Sources are qualitative examples, not target-site measurements, legal safety limits, equipment identities or quantitative factors. Actual native records and concrete exchange verification remain required before final complete-scope dataset use; unknown quantities and missing identities are never zero.
