# Room or unit accommodation services for visitors, with on-site reactive services

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This record applies to room or unit accommodation services for visitors, with on-site reactive services within the physical product and route defined below. Application requires both this category and every stated physical condition. Other categories discussed below provide methodological context. The bounded reference product is hotel room-nights with daily housekeeping.

Actually operated short-stay PRIVATE HOTEL GUESTROOMS with PRIVATE BATHROOM, a DAILY HOUSEKEEPING service regime and on-site guest-response responsibility. Non-timeshare, non-selfcatering and no shared-bed/dormitory reference. The offered and actual performed/declined cleaning regime, room configuration and lodging package must be evidenced for the actual property and period; a brand, reception desk or self-check-in label alone does not classify the service. Daily service does not mean daily replacement of every linen item or identical cleaning quantities.

This route collects room-related linen washing through actual completed EXTERNAL or CENTRAL laundry services with explicit complete coverage; it does not presume an on-site industrial laundry. A real in-house laundry production route needs its own reviewed operation/resources before using this narrower route. Room surface cleaning, bed-making, actual response, common lodging areas and necessary back-of-house duties are inside. Separately supplied food/drink, parking, meeting, pool/gym/spa and unrelated personal laundry are other outputs; complete physical attribution is required before their shared resources can be excluded. Actual room-included amenities or unresolved shared duties cannot be silently omitted.
