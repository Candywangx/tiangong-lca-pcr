# Member-use whole two-bedroom lock-off villa nights

## 1. Scope and Applicability

For actual visitor accommodation supplied through a member reservation as the WHOLE combined TWO-BEDROOM LOCK-OFF VILLA, with a traceable one-bedroom and studio atomic-space topology and independently lockable connecting/entry arrangements. Both atomic spaces must be exclusively supplied together to the same stay/case/night at the actual assigned property; every bed need not be slept in. This method does not make all timeshare tenure a new physical category. Its independent need is dynamic parent/child physical-space conservation and configuration-specific delivery, distinct from an indivisible apartment unit. Actual plans, assignment and service records establish applicability, not a brand example or an ownership deed alone.

Only fulfilled combined whole-villa nights are reference output. Separate studio/one-bedroom stays are actual other consumers of the same assets/systems and retain their resource attribution; neither a child night nor a dedicated non-lock-off two-bedroom night is the target output. Exclude property-right/point sales, annual dues or loans as reference quantities, unfulfilled reservations, merely available ownership intervals and larger configurations. Actual no-show/early-departure charges do not establish physical occupancy. Declined housekeeping does not cancel a physically fulfilled night.

Cover actual supplied lodging, guest bathroom/kitchen/appliance use, housekeeping/turnover, linen service, utilities and responsibility-matched common facilities, including physically attributable full-life building shell/fitout/FF&E/HVAC/reusables manufacture, installation, maintenance, replacements and end-of-life across all consumers/periods. Owner payment or provider outsourcing does not remove physical stages. Guest travel, food itself and independently supplied meals/retail/recreation are separate unless actually included in the declared package; included common facilities cannot be erased merely because fees or legal ownership differ. No independent generation/boiler/industrial laundry/food-production plant is presumed: real independent conversion or industrial operations require reviewed route/module expansion.

Operator documents support topology, supplied-state and duty questions only. Their points, monetary shares, areas, capacities, check-in times and cleaning day schedules are not universal inventory factors or guarantees. Generic missing numeric ranges and unresolved flow identities remain explicit collection gaps, not fabricated values or proof of zero.
