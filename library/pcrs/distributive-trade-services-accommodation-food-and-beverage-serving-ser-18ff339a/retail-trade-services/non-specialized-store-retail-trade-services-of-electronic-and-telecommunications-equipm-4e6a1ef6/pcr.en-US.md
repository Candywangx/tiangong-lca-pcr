# Retail services for factory-sealed complete Ethernet-switch kits

## 1. Scope and Applicability

A deliberately narrow candidate for unopened complete Ethernet-switch kit retail through an actual non-specialized store. It provides a partial incremental-service core and a fixed completion method; it does not claim full business coverage. Applicability is controlled by b_route and b_absence, not by the broad classification title.
