# Counter handover services for factory-sealed manually pulled transport-cart kits

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This methodology concerns counter pickup of one specified complete hand-pulled goods cart kit in a real mixed-assortment non-specialized store. manually pulled transport-cart is a nonmotorized, nontowable transport vehicle; its garden use does not turn it into powered agricultural machinery. UN62182 includes goods493/495/496/499 except49921; this narrow goods49930 route excludes pleasure boats494 as well as bicycles. Only actual site/lot evidence admits a dataset. The core is deliberately partial pending the executable completion duties; it must not be advertised as a complete retailer footprint.
