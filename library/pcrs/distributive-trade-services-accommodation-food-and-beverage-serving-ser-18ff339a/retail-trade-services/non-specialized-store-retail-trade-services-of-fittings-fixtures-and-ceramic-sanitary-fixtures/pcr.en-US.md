# Non-specialized store pickup retail service for complete glazed ceramic washbasin kits

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This candidate covers one actual homogeneous glazed ceramic washbasin kit SKU/configuration sold by a store with an evidenced mixed merchandise assortment, through ambient receiving, storage, manual picking and customer pickup. A kit includes all original factory-supplied trap, seals, strainer and tools, where supplied, plus original packaging. Different material, dimensions, hole/overflow configuration, finish, included parts or packaging version requires a separate pool. Non-specialized refers to actual assortment, not absence of brand exclusivity.

Plastic basins, freestanding metal fittings, toilets, ceramic pipes, installation, wet product demonstrations, machining, repairs and retailer delivery are outside this route. Intact complete original kits and actual broken mixed-kit carriers have distinct identities. A manufacturer webpage identifies a feasible kit, not a supplier LCI, lot BOM, measured mass or compliance limit. IKEA BJÖRKÅN 595.467.20 illustrates a ceramic basin with a separately packed polymer/rubber/steel trap kit; its two packages are one sale kit, not two equivalent basin services. No nominal catalogue mass is adopted.
