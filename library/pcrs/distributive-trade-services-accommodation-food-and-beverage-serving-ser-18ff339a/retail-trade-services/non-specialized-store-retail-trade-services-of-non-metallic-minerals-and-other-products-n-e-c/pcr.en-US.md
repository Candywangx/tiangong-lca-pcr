# In-store retail service for original 40 lb Solar Naturals solar salt bags in a non-specialized store

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR defines a narrow original-bag salt retail service in an evidenced non-specialized physical store. Method equations are explicit PCR design rules backed by the stated foreground collection; no manufacturer specification is adopted as an activity-data default. Apply the mandatory boundary, allocation and validation tables together, including their source IDs and exceptions.
