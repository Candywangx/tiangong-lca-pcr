# Mixed-store retail of sealed 500-sheet A4 80 gsm white copier-paper reams

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR defines an incremental physical mixed-store retail service for one homogeneous original sealed copier-paper ream configuration, with the material/channel gates in b_route and b_channel. Its reference is final-valid service count. The displayed foreground is a declared core; c_complete fixes mandatory support completion before any complete-business claim. Manufacturer specifications establish a real example, not lot composition, actual stock, LCI quantities or regulatory conformity.
