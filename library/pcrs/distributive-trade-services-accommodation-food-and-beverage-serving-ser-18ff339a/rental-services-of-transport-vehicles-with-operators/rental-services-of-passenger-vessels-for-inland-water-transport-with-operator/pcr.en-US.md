# Rental services of passenger vessels for inland water transport with operator

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Covers rental services of all types of self-propelled passenger vessels for inland water transport with crew. This includes crewed pleasure craft where the actual immediate supplied function is operated hire. Establish a genuine qualified vessel-plus-crew availability obligation from the agreement and actual operation; a charter label, crew presence, invoice or energy payer alone does not establish hire.

Exclude rental/leasing of vessels without crew (CPC 73115) and inland pleasure boats without crew (CPC 73240). Passenger carriage sold as a ticket, journey or transport undertaking, operator-only labour, vessel manufacture and non-inland hire are outside this reference service. Do not impose a blanket ban on crewed pleasure craft or restrict the category to time charter. A downstream transport service is distinct from the operated hire supplied to it.

The parent activity is actual operated inland passenger-vessel hire. Declare the actual self-propelled vessel, hired configuration/capacity, master/crew and hirer responsibilities, navigation/availability gates, inland waterbody and duty. Compatible vessels or hired capacity can share a declared fleet only with evidenced equivalent function; resolve mutually exclusive vessel states and overlapping obligations. Accepted availability may have zero sailing and zero passengers; empty positioning, genuine standby, hotel/auxiliary demand, failure and accepted replacement remain causal activities.
