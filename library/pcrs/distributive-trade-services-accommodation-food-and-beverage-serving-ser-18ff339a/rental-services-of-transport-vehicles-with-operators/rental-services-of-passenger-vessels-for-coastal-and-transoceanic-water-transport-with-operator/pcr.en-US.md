# Rental services of passenger vessels for coastal and transoceanic water transport with operator

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers rental services of all types of self-propelled passenger vessels for coastal and transoceanic water transport with crew. The supplied function is making the actual operated passenger vessel or genuinely hired portion available under an evidenced rental engagement, with declared capacity, time, configuration and operating responsibilities. It is not restricted to time charter, one passenger-vessel design, fuel or propulsion technology. A crewed pleasure vessel is not excluded merely because of that label when its actual function meets this passenger-vessel operated-hire scope.

The reference is one independently accepted operated passenger-vessel hire engagement, with positive actual accepted availability time and original specified vessel/configuration/capacity, operating responsibility, service region/duty and delivery/redelivery gates. This Count reference denotes the actual availability obligation, not a ticket, passenger journey, kilometre, sailing, vessel asset or invoice installment. Actual permitted standby may fulfill availability without passengers or movement. Record hours and capacity explicitly for comparability and causal attribution; do not split one engagement by billing, crew shift, voyage or an accepted replacement vessel, or merge unlike engagements merely because Count is shared.

Exclude vessels without crew, including no-crew vessel rental/leasing and pleasure-boat rental without crew; exclude inland passenger-vessel hire, freight-vessel hire, operator-only labour, bare asset manufacture and passenger carriage sold as transport to the immediate customer. Invoice wording such as charter, crew presence, passenger occupancy or who pays bunkers alone does not decide the function. Determine the actual supplied availability, operational/navigation control, commercial directions, acceptance and counterparties; necessary hired-vessel crew, operating and support duties remain included. Independent downstream ticket/cruise transport or hotel/entertainment services beyond the hire interface remain separate, while actually owed onboard support is retained.

Record original terms and genuine chronological changes for actual vessel availability, acceptable replacement, off-hire/unavailable periods and service completion. Failed, cancelled, interrupted, positioned or recovered duties retain their real burdens and are not fictitious accepted engagements; payment or contractual off-hire alone is not a physical allocation rule. Actual consumed fuel/energy upstream source supply or charging belongs once to the provided operated function regardless of owner, charterer or customer payment/title, after exact provider reconciliation. This PCR supplies no universal charter clause, manning, safety limit, fuel factor or asset lifetime.
