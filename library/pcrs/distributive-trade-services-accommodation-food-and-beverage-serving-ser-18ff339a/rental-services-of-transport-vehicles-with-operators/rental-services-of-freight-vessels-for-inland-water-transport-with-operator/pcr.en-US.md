# Rental services of freight vessels for inland water transport with operator

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers rental of all types of self-propelled freight vessels for inland water transport with crew, including tankers, bulk dry cargo vessels, cargo and freight vessels, tugboats and fishing vessels. The supplied function is actual crew-operated vessel availability under an independently accepted hire obligation. It is not limited to vessels carrying their own cargo, successful carriage, towing completion, catch, one propulsion technology or time charter. A tug or fishing vessel can provide the hire function with no cargo or catch aboard. An unpowered barge rented alone is outside this self-propelled scope; an actually provided powered tug-push/convoy configuration must declare the powered vessel and each included barge, equipment and duty interface, without relabelling an unpowered barge as self-propelled.

The reference is one actual independently accepted operated inland freight-vessel hire engagement: 1 Item(s), with positive actual accepted availability time H, original and genuinely amended vessel/configuration, hired capacity or functional capability, operating responsibility, service region/duty and delivery/redelivery/accepted replacement gates. Availability may be fulfilled by actual permitted standby without sailing, cargo, distance, a completed tow or catch. Capacity/capability and time remain mandatory comparability and causal-attribution qualifiers. Do not count invoices, voyages, crew shifts, tow jobs, catch lots or an accepted replacement as extra engagements; genuine independent obligations remain distinct.

Exclude vessel rental/leasing without crew (CPC 73115), unpowered-barge-only rental, passenger-vessel hire, coastal/transoceanic freight-vessel hire, operator-only labour and vessel manufacture as the reference product. Independent cargo carriage, cargo production, a sold towing/assistance result and fish capture/production are distinct functions; invoice wording, crew presence, fuel payer or fishing-vessel type alone does not determine the immediate product. Establish actual availability, operational/navigation control, commercial direction, customer, acceptance and interfaces. Actually owed pumping, tank conditioning, tow readiness/gear, fishing-vessel gear/handling, crew and support duties remain in hire attribution; independent downstream functions require separate actual handoff and provider coverage.

Retain original terms and genuine chronological changes, positive accepted availability, actual off-hire/unavailable/failure/cancellation/recovery and replacement acceptance. Physical burdens survive payment labels and unsuccessful events. Actual fuel/energy upstream supply or charging belongs once to the provided function regardless owner, customer or payer, including actual consumed initial/opening/customer-owned stock. There is no universal inland fuel, freshwater intake, charter clause, legal/manning rule, discharge factor, asset lifetime or invented numerical range here.
