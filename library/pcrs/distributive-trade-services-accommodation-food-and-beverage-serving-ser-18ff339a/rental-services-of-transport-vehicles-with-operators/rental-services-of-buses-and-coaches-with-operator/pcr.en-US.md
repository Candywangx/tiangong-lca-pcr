# Rental services of buses and coaches with operator

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers buses and coaches rented with an operator for a period of time, generally not dependent on distance; the renter determines how and when the vehicles operate, including schedules, routes and other operational considerations. The supplied product is actual operated vehicle availability under the original hire obligation. Reference is one independently completed and accepted hire engagement with declared positive actual accepted availability time, vehicle or originally specified fleet, configuration, capacity, operator responsibilities, region/duty and delivery/redelivery gates. Permitted standby can fulfill availability with no passengers or distance. Unlike duration/capacity/control/duty profiles are not equivalent merely because each engagement counts as one.

Exclude local/urban/suburban bus or coach charter services 64118 and long-distance charter 64223, passenger carriage sold as transport, vehicle hire without operator, operator-only labour and asset manufacture. A monthly invoice, whole-vehicle booking, driver presence, distance charge or fuel payer alone does not establish operated rental; verify the actual immediate supplied function and renter operational direction. Independent downstream passenger service remains separate. Actual driver work, preparation, accepted standby, positioning, failure/recovery/replacement and required support stay inside the causal rental boundary. No universal hire, legal, vehicle-size or driver-hours clause is imposed.
