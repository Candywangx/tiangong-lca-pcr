# Own-account wholesale of new manual cylinder lawnmowers in actual supplier kits

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Covers new factoryfinished MANUAL PUSH reel/cylinder lawnmowers at the actual complete original supplier SKU/kit and shipping assembly state, through ambient own-account B2B receiving, stock, condition/completeness handling and FINAL buyer handover. Verify actual mower-parent/attached-detached handle/wheel/frame/cutting cylinder/fixed knife/fasteners/gear mechanism if present/factory grease if present/basket if included/original pack. A finished supplier kit may have detached members; factory blade setting does not require a fullyassembled handle/basket gate or merchant assembly. Actual inclusions and optional separately sold goods need SKU evidence, not family images. Exclude powered/battery/robotic/ride-on mowers, unrelated machinery, merchant assembly/mowing test/sharpening/repair/lubricant refill/preservation/repack/consumer mowing, retail/fee agency/storage-only. Extra actual operations require route review.

This physical wholesale add-on excludes all traded commodity and inherited pack manufacturing, including upstream burden of real losses. Separately quantify AND actually integrate required complete original-gate demand outside once after native source assignment/exclusive terminal-loss owners and identical baseline stage/loss coverage, with integration link/evidence before complete-chain readiness. Match actual material/forming/finishing/factory assembly AT ACTUAL SHIPPING STATE/release/originalpack scope, not rawsteel or an assumed fullyassembled machine. Actual physical handling and declared receiver transport/treatment are inside once. Merchandise is not merchant capital, occupied mower service or actual cut-grass output; rated capacity/price/product-piece quantity cannot replace commercial-consignment Count.
