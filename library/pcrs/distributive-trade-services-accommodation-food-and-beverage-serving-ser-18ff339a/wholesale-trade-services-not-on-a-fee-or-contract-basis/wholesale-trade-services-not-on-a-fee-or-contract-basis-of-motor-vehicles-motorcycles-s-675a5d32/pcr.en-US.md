# Ambient own-account wholesale of new one-piece cast-iron brake discs

## 1. Scope and Applicability

For actual factoryfinished NEW single-piece cast-iron MOTOR-VEHICLE brake discs/rotors in original supplier presentation,owned/purchased/resold through ambient physical B2B consignments. Actual SKU/lot/solid or vented geometry,alloy/BOM,declared finish/preservative,disc/pair/carton hierarchy,net complete product mass/pack/condition qualify the method. Exclude two-piece/light-alloy-hub,carbon-ceramic,used/remanufactured or other brake products and merchant machining/coating/rust removal/installation/load-performance test/use/pad-fluid change.
