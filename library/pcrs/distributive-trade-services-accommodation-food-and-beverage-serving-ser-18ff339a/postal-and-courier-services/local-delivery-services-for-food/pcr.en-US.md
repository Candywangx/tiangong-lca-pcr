# Local delivery of hot cooked meals by electric-assist bicycle

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR defines one bounded service methodology for separately charged local delivery of purchased hot cooked meals. Product, qualification, exclusion and completeness requirements are in the normative tables below.
