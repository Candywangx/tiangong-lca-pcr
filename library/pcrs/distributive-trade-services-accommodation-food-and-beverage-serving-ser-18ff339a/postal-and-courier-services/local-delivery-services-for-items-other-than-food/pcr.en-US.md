# Local delivery of ambient non-food retail parcels by battery electric van

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Apply only to ordinary ambient, non-hazardous retail non-food parcels locally dispatched from one retailer staging location by operator-controlled battery electric vans, with no cargo refrigeration. Declare the actual service zone as a postcode list or geofence and the promised handover window; neither a universal distance threshold nor a default service intensity is established. A route may carry multiple clients and parcel sizes, but separate materially different delivery promises and vehicle configurations before allocation. The staging location must have evidenced absence of on-site fuel combustion, thermal utilities and refrigerant equipment; all operational energy is in staging AC or the separately bounded information/cleaning services. Any actual additional resource or emission invalidates completeness until an atomic amendment covers it.

## 7. Allocation and Co-product Handling

| rule_id | Applies to | Rule | source_ids |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `allocation_subdivide` | all shared operations | First directly assign measured parcel-specific materials, events and device time. Split vehicle records into movement and stationary/stop pools using time-stamped traction/auxiliary telemetry; do not allocate an undivided energy pool by an unexplained parcel count. Reconcile both pools to the AC meter including charging loss and battery opening/closing state of charge. If the split cannot be supported, completeness fails; no assumed idle fraction is allowed. |  |
| `allocation_motion` | movement electricity, van and wear | For loaded segment s, allocate motion burden by w_cs = sum packed mass of cohort c parcels on s / sum packed mass of all parcels on s. Use actual gross packed kg and all clients, including failed parcels; no chargeable mass or order count. For empty return/repositioning assign the segment to its causative tour and allocate by that tour total parcel mass-distance including failed attempts; a tour with no attributable loaded movement is unresolved, never zero. The shares must sum to one for each complete pool. | `ghg-scope3-transport-2013` |
| `allocation_stops` | stationary electricity and shared support | Directly assign parcel-attributable stop/wait activity; at a stop serving several parcels, divide its measured stationary burden equally among physical parcels attempted at that stop, including failed attempts, across all clients. Allocate common dispatch/staging/cleaning/terminal activity by measured parcel handling minutes over all handled parcels; allocate storage-area-time by actual parcel occupied area multiplied by staging hours. No success-only denominator may be used inside these allocation shares. Normalize allocated cohort totals once by D only after aggregation. |  |
| `allocation_no_credit` | returns and waste | There are no service co-products. Refused, damaged, lost and returned parcels are not negative outputs or extra reference services. Within the accepted cohort, failed-attempt and final unsuccessful burdens remain in the numerator assigned to successful delivery. Separately report their rates and total burdens; never transfer them to another cohort or claim equivalence across different success rates. Recycling is modelled with actual receiving treatment and no substitution credit. |  |
| allocation_refrigerant_recovery | External R1234yf product or waste handover | An external handover meeting evidenced product acceptance is a separate physical kg output, with no substitution credit and no delivery-reference count. Use the handover cut-off: the service retains necessary vehicle maintenance, compliant recovery and safe handover work through the gate; the recipient bears onward transport, regeneration, upgrading and use.
