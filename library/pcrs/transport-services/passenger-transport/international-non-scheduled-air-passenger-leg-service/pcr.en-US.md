# International non-scheduled air passenger leg service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers actual passenger air transportation on international routes,on a non-scheduled basis,in aircraft of any type including helicopters. Establish actual delivered international service geography,non-scheduled transport function,contract/service responsibility and accepted passenger fulfilment. Aircraft registration,nationality,marketing,range,aircraft/helicopter/air-taxi label,group charter or hourly price does not establish eligibility. Do not infer every air taxi is domestic from a cross-reference to a domestic class;actual international non-scheduled passenger conveyance needs its own function/geography evidence. A prearranged departure time or route does not by itself prove scheduled service or aircraft rental.

The class expressly excludes sightseeing passenger air transport and rental of passenger aircraft with crew. Determine the final delivered product and actual control/fulfilment responsibility from the contract and operations:completed passenger conveyance,an aerial-viewing programme,or aircraft-with-crew use/availability are different functions. Customer route/time requests,an operator calling a service charter,or a whole-group booking alone do not decide. Do not convert fulfilled hire/standby availability with no passenger delivery into completed transport,or remove real support from an actual conveyance product. Booking/dispatch-only brokers,unused rights,primary training/adventure/jump/medical treatment and freight-only functions are distinct. Real accessibility/medical/cabin assistance included in transport stays included.

One reference item is one actual person completing the declared international non-scheduled boarding-to-final-alighting passenger leg,with original promised and chronologically accepted OD,quality,class,contract/inclusions,duration,period and real stages. N is positive actual completed equivalent person legs in that cohort. Tickets,orders,groups,fleet bookings,dispatches,seat/flight capacity,aircraft movements/hours/stages,passenger-km or whole multi-leg itineraries are not this output. Through technical stops,temporary reboarding,aircraft/crew substitutions or marketed codeshares do not automatically repeat the same completed person leg;genuine independently fulfilled connecting legs require actual separate recipient/function/endpoint records.

Preserve original promise and chronological accepted amendments;partial,failed,aborted,diverted,rescue or cancelled duty cannot be retrospectively shortened into successful original transport. A real independently completed compatible leg may use its own evidenced cohort. Retain actual attributable dispatch,pickup,waiting/standby,empty/repositioning/return,holding,cancellation/failure,test/rescue/recovery and necessary ground/airport/cabin support with actual provider/platform/operator owners and all other users. Zero distance or no boarding does not prove zero causal burden. N=0 has no finite passenger-leg intensity;genuinely separately delivered availability is a different output function,not extra completed persons. Infants/nonpay actual travellers count;support crew versus genuinely transported off-duty/dual-role recipients follow actual roles without duplicate people or blanket salary exclusions.

All actual qualifying fixed-wing,rotorcraft or other aircraft mechanisms retain full real technical/native responsibilities,not a Jet-only subset. Actual piston/turbine/APU,electric/hybrid,hydrogen/other fuel/SAF paths need actual grade/formulation/purity/phase/conditioning/storage/conversion/stock/burn/vent/leak/recovery and chemical/water fates. Actual glider tow-return/winch/selflaunch/recovery,balloon burner/fan/lifting-gas/ballast and airship propulsion/ballonet/mooring/ground interfaces are conditional obligations only where a real qualifying transport product uses them;technical sightseeing/training cases do not prove international non-scheduled operation. Missing quantities/UUIDs block completed data,not actual qualifying product applicability.

Retain all actual flight/start/taxi/APU/hover/ground/airport/heliport/site/cabin/handling/deice/cleaning/lavatory/catering/included-care duties and aircraft/ground/site manufacture,installation/refit,use,maintenance/replacements/retirement. The same asset may serve domestic,scheduled,cargo/private or other real functions;complete compatible positive all-user lifetime/stage totals remain required. Provider q_u*actual compatible A_u*only not-yet-attributed share/N once replaces exact covered cells;already matched demand is not occupancy/N divided again. At-plant identities preserve their grade/state/native gate and missing delivery/conditioning/storage/loss bridges. Manufacture is not a lifetime-service identity;whole assemblies/components and whole-blend/replacing components are exclusive.

Actual baggage/otheritems may be free,not universally;included handling/care/provision/fates remain without automatic own manufacture/rearing lifetime of carried objects. Fees alone do not establish independent co-products. Actual chemical species/C-N-S basis/carbon origins/particle size/source/phase/known altitude/receiving media stay explicit;never erase known states to fit unspecified-air UUIDs or double-add nested particle fractions. Non-CO2/contrail/noise physical observations and LCIA gaps remain separate,not zero,CO2-equivalent substance or universal forcing multiplier. No occupancy/cabin/cargo/fuel/lifetime/failure/distance factor or range is prescribed.
