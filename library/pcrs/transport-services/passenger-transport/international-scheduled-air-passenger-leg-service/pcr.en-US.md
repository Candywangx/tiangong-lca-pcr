# International scheduled air passenger leg service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This dedicated PCR covers actual international passenger air transport on pre-determined international routes AND pre-determined schedules, supplied in aircraft of any type including helicopters. CPC64243 is international scheduled air passenger carriage. Establish actual offered/delivered international service geography, passenger boarding/final-alighting OD and served extent, country/territory and route/stop sequence under the stated geographic definition, original route/schedule, quality/class/time/inclusions and carrier responsibility. Aircraft registration, passenger/crew nationality, airline domicile, codeshare marketing, an arbitrary kilometre threshold or a foreign overflight alone does not establish international service. A delay does not by itself make a scheduled product nonscheduled.

One reference is one actual completed qualifying international scheduled person-leg, not a ticket, reservation, seat entitlement, departure, aircraft movement, flight stage, vehicle/hour, passenger-distance or entire multi-flight journey. Define the real independently delivered leg and original promised OD/quality/inclusions, chronological accepted changes and actual final alighting/completion. A technical stop, refuelling, intermediate reboarding or same-obligation aircraft/operating-carrier substitution is not automatically another output; a genuine independently delivered connecting service has its own evidenced leg. Marketing and operating codeshares must not count the same person twice. A domestic add-on or local-only ride is not automatically international because a ticket or aircraft itinerary also contains an international portion. Keep real served extent/geographic responsibility and actual recipient/stage membership.

Preserve original promises, intended-versus-delivered geography/OD/quality/time and chronological actual amendments through cancellation, shortturn, diversion, rescue, failed approach and partial/failed delivery. Do not retrospectively shorten a failed promised endpoint into a completed successful leg or copy an unknown-OD statistical fallback as actual completion proof. A genuinely accepted amended or independent partial product needs real scope, role, acceptance and comparable cohort. Count actual persons including infants/non-revenue passengers by evidenced role; on-duty flight/cabin/ground crew are support, genuinely transported or dual-role crew require explicit nonoverlapping role/time records. Actual accompanying baggage and other items MAY be carried without extra cost; declare the actual conditions rather than requiring every item free. Carried-object manufacture/rearing/lifetime is not automatically an aircraft input; actual handling/care/assistance/sanitation/support and fates remain.

Include the complete actual international service and causal operating-country/airport/heliport/landing-site/provider/period duties: start/taxi/APU/warmup/takeoff/hover/climb/cruise/holding/descent/landing, empty positioning/return, delays/diversion/rescue/failed/zero-passenger duties, ground/airport/cabin/crew/maintenance and real support. Do not cut lifecycle responsibility at a border, fuel-statistics boundary, transfer of operator or marketing geography. Retain actual cargo/mail and other users with measured subdivision and justified compatible physical allocation, class/occupancy/segment distance and uncertainty, without fixed ratios or adding incompatible output units. Close actual causal all-user quantities/stocks/coverage then divide positive comparable completed international scheduled person-leg N once; N=0 has no finite intensity and does not delete burdens.

All actual qualifying aircraft/power/lift/launch configurations remain covered, including real rotor/fixed-wing, electric/hybrid/H2/other-fuel/SAF routes and any other actual mechanism meeting this function. Conditional JetA1/100LL, turboprop/helicopter manufacture and auxiliary examples do not limit scope or prove that every technology operates this service. Apply actual native supply/grade/formulation/phase/gate, factory-to-receipt conditioning/delivery/storage/loss bridges, stock versus burn/use/leak/recovery, AC/DC/conversion/storage, launch/recovery/ground support and full all-user aircraft/site manufacture/use/maintenance/refit/replacement/retirement responsibilities. Supplier q_u times real compatible A_u times only not-yet-attributed evidenced share/N once replaces exactly covered cells; already attributed demand is not allocated again and equivalent completed-person service is not divided by occupancy again.

Preserve every actual substance/carbon origin/compound-versus-C_N_S basis, phase/trajectory/known altitude/receiving medium and waste/runoff/treatment fate. Generic air-unspecified bindings only apply to actually documented justified unspecified states; never erase a known height/receiver to fit a UUID. Particle size/chemical fractions require nonoverlapping compatible observations or explicit overlap conventions, no nested total-plus-contained-fraction double count or invented residual. Actual non-CO2, water/contrail/weather/trajectory and noise observations remain physical records with separate characterization limits; no universal CO2 forcing multiplier, CO2e substance, missing-CF zero, fuel/supply-equals-emission or universal numerical factor/range is supplied.
