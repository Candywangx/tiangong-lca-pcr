# Other inland water passenger leg service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This dedicated PCR covers actual passenger carriage on rivers, canals and other inland waters on a scheduled or non-scheduled basis by vessels other than ferries, cruise ships, sightseeing and excursion boats, including actual water taxis. It is not limited to on-demand launches or a water-taxi brand, and does not make every inland boat transaction eligible. Establish the actual offered/delivered passenger-carriage function, inland waterbody/OD/extent, operator/provider responsibility, dispatch or timetable, duration/class/quality and acceptance. Ferry64121, inclusive inland cruise64122, sightseeing/excursion64133, coastal/ocean carriage and vessel/crew availability as a different final function require their own actual classification and method.

One reference is one actual person completing the declared qualifying other-inland passenger leg from actual boarding to accepted final alighting, with original agreed OD/service extent and quality/time, actual chronological changes and delivery responsibility. Ticket/booking/party, boat departure/voyage, vehicle, crew duty, capacity and passenger-distance are distinct objects. Count actual persons including children/free persons once by real role, not party size assumptions or seats; operating crew are support. Intermediate calls, temporary reboarding, vessel substitution, provider changes and rescue do not automatically add completed legs. Preserve original promise and partial/failed/cancel/no-show outcomes; do not change an endpoint after failure to manufacture a completed promised leg. A genuinely accepted amended or independent partial output has its true extent and comparable cohort.

For actual water taxi or other dispatched service retain requesting/dispatch platforms, pickup docks, positioning/dock-out/return, waiting, unsuccessful pickup/no-show/cancellation, empty/rescue/recovery and zero-passenger operated duties at their real owners, including any continuing delivery obligation. For scheduled or mixed services close all actual voyages/segments and supported vessel/shore uses. Complete causal all-user quantities and period/stock responsibility before normalization by positive actual completed compatible person-leg N once; N=0 gives no finite intensity and never deletes the duty pool. Paid dispatch, party or boat-hours do not prove completion.

A cabin, meal or overnight on a carriage vessel does not automatically turn the actual passenger product into an inclusive cruise. Conversely an actual inclusive cruise/sightseeing/excursion experience cannot disappear by relabelling its movement as ordinary carriage. Define actual included versus genuinely independent meal/cabin/care/cargo functions and their delivered native outputs before subdivision/allocation. On an actual cargo-common vessel the person reference is not the freight journey: measure passenger segment membership/distance and dedicated cabin/hotel/sanitary/boarding duties, cargo/reefer/handling/port-caused dwell and remaining shared hull/propulsion/hotel/port duties with supported causal all-user relationships. No cargo-primary passenger free ride, arbitrary fixed share or addition of Count persons to tonnes/tonne-distance. A separate fee alone does not establish an independent co-product.

Cover every actual qualifying vessel/propulsion configuration and complete ship/shore lifecycle. Actual conventional/alternative gaseous or liquid fuel, hybrid, battery-electric, fuel-cell, sail or operator-propelled carriage needs independently evidenced native fuel/energy/chemical/state/gate, storage/control/hull/rig/auxiliary equipment, operating duty, manufacture/installation/maintenance/refit/replacements and retirement. Conditional water-taxi launch and cargo-passenger ship manufacture cards are supply objects, not evidence that every service has those assets or a restriction against another qualifying configuration. Actual wind/operator work is not automatically purchased energy or food/labour exchange, and no tailpipe-zero claim supplies zero full lifecycle. Missing UUID/data is a completion gap, never product exclusion.

Retain all actual fuel grades/formulations/phases and upstream bunkering/conditioning/loss gaps, external AC/DC/shore charging/battery/storage/conversion gates and actual provider coverage. Keep bilgewater, toilet blackwater, graywater, ballast, scrubber streams, antifoulant/coating residues/leachate, oily materials, garbage and every actual chemical species/receiving state separate. Collected mixtures are wastes at actual handover; direct freshwater/air/soil substance releases are not the same identity, evaporation not collected waste. Accompanying luggage/animals/vehicles/items are transported objects rather than automatically their own manufacturing/rearing/lifetime inputs; actual transport-time handling/care/sanitation/support and fates remain included. No universal composition, occupancy, life, discharge factor, allocation coefficient or numerical range is prescribed.
