# Other coastal and transoceanic water passenger leg service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This dedicated PCR covers actual other coastal and transoceanic water passenger carriage on scheduled or non-scheduled services, regardless of service class, except actual ferry and cruise-ship functions. It includes actual passenger carriage from port to port, including on freight ships, and actual accompanying luggage, animals and other items carried at no additional cost. Establish the offered and delivered residual sea-carriage function, actual coastal/transoceanic waterbody and original boarding/final-alighting ports or actual landing points, extent, schedule/dispatch, class/quality/time and accountable provider. Do not limit this method to one ship type, water taxi, cargo ship, published timetable or numeric distance, and do not substitute inland64129, ferry64231, inclusive cruise64232 or vessel/crew availability for the actual final function.

One reference is one actual person completing the declared qualifying residual sea passenger leg at its original agreed and actually accepted port/landing-point OD, extent, class/quality/time and continuing carrier responsibility. Tickets/booking/party, ship/voyage, capacity, carried objects, working crew, freight journeys and passenger-distance are different objects. Count actual passenger persons including children/free passengers once by real role, not berth/seat/party or vehicle-occupancy assumptions. Intermediate calls, temporary shore leave/reboarding, vessel/provider/crew changes, diversions and rescue do not automatically create repeated successful person-legs. Keep original promises, chronological actual amendments, partial/failed/no-show/cancelled outcomes and accepted delivery evidence; do not retrospectively change a failed endpoint or shorten scope to manufacture the promised completed leg. A genuinely accepted amended or independent partial product has its true extent and comparable cohort.

The entire actual sea leg and its causal ship/shore/booking/provider duties remain accounted across operating countries, port calls and periods. Retain real preparation, pickup/embarkation, manoeuvring, waiting/anchorage/berthing/hotelling, empty/repositioning/return, weather-related modes/diversion, failed service, rescue/recovery, crew rotation/rest and attributable shoreside/operational support under actual owners. Actual passage class, cabin duration/occupancy, meals, sanitation, accessibility and supplied crew support need native records; no every-route cabin/meal/crew-rest-hour or purchased labour recipe is assumed. Paid waiting, unused booking or no-show does not establish passenger completion. Close actual all-user causal quantities, stocks and responsibility then divide positive compatible completed person-leg N once; N=0 has no finite intensity and never deletes the duty pool.

On a real freight/cargo-passenger ship, the freight journey is not this person reference. Establish each genuine native passenger/cargo/vehicle or other independent output and first subdivide measured dedicated boarding, passenger cabin/hotel/food/sanitary support versus cargo gear/reefer/handling/calls/dwell and port operations. Justify residual common hull, propulsion, hotel, shore/site and lifecycle shares with observed causal all-user resource/duty relationships and sensitivity. Cargo as the primary trade does not make passengers burden-free, and Count people cannot be added to tonnes/tonne-distance or equated through arbitrary fare or mass ratios. A fee line alone does not establish a distinct co-product. Actual included services remain complete; an optional bed or meal does not automatically make carriage an all-inclusive cruise, while a real inclusive cruise/sightseeing experience cannot disappear by naming only its movement.

Every actual qualifying sea vessel/drive and ship/shore lifecycle remains applicable, including actual combustion/alternative-fuel/hybrid/battery/fuel-cell/sail/operator-assisted configurations where they deliver this function. Use actual fuel grade/formulation/phase/bunker and upstream conditioning/delivery gates, AC/DC charging/storage/conversion/loss/export, complete native vessel/equipment/site manufacture and supported same-asset all-user useful-duty allocation, maintenance/refit/replacement and retirement. Conditional launch/cargo-passenger manufacture cards are specific supply examples, not scope limits or full-life transport results. Human tasks/work-rest/dedicated support and the disclosed ordinary-subsistence/metabolic boundary/sensitivity continue to apply; wind or human work is not fictitious purchased fuel or proof of zero physical metabolism/lifecycle burden. Missing UUID/quantity/route evidence is incomplete application, not exclusion of a real qualifying product.

Record actual seawater, brackish/estuarine and any freshwater port interfaces separately, with real intake/cooling/ballast treatment/discharge, bilge/toilet blackwater/graywater/scrubber/garbage/antifoulant or coating maintenance/leachate and every actual substance/receiver/known subcompartment. A collected wet mixture at reception is not a direct sea-water elementary emission; chemical compound versus C/N/S, fossil/biogenic origin, dissolved/particulate and wet/dry or density bases remain evidenced. No receiving medium or legal discharge factor is inferred from a convenient UUID. Free accompanying luggage/animals/items actually carried receive their real handling/care/sanitation/support and fates without extra passenger counts; carried objects do not automatically add their own manufacture/rearing/whole lifetime. Actual independently delivered freight/vehicle services have their own native outputs, not an assumed free accompaniment.
