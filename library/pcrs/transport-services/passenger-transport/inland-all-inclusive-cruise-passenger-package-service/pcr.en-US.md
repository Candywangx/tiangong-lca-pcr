# All-inclusive inland cruise passenger package service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers the complete actual all-inclusive inland-water cruise passenger package on rivers, canals and other inland waters: transportation, accommodation, food services and all actual other incidentals supplied in the inclusive function. Declare actual itinerary/waterbody/ports, duration, accommodation, cabin/class, dining, quality and inclusion/acceptance responsibility. Actual recreation, performances, shore excursions, connections, air/ground transfers or extensions remain included when part of that supplied inland cruise package; none is universally presumed. A larger mixed-waterbody holiday must identify its real inland cruise product boundary and any other independently supplied function, not label sea cruising as inland by an arbitrary route-distance fraction.

One reference item is one actual passenger completing one independently declared inland package, not a booking, payment, ticket, ship voyage, boarding, internal sailing leg, cabin, room-night, meal, passenger-kilometre or available berth. Actual compatible itinerary/duration/class/food/stay/inclusions and acceptance define the output before counting. Internal transport, hotel, meals and performances are constituents of the same delivered package, not extra final co-products. Children and infants have no presumed adult/seat equivalent; operating crew or performers do not become passenger-package outputs. Partial/failed/aborted duties retain attributable burdens without being counted as full completion. An actually redefined fulfilled shortened product is separately qualified. N=0 has no finite per-package result.

A boat tour with a snack, a passage with an optional bed or meal, or a night spent aboard is not automatically a cruise package. Assess the actual integrated transport/accommodation/food/incidentals and fulfilment, rather than the word cruise, fare label or a guessed minimum night/meal rule. Standalone sightseeing/excursion boat services are outside this product, including the actual64133 interface; an excursion actually included in the package remains an owned constituent. Exclude pure ferry/water-taxi/freight-ship passenger leg, booking-only, standalone room/meal, coastal/transoceanic cruise product and unrelated holiday function. Actual independent optional outputs can be separated only with evidenced native function/gates and complete shared-resource attribution.

All actual qualifying inland ship/propulsion/energy technologies, sizes and supplied package configurations remain in scope. Missing UUIDs, numerical factors, equipment examples or operating data are completion gates, not reasons to remove a real cruise product. Every actual alternative supply, vessel, recipe, native provider service, waste and receiving fate requires its own atomic identity and quantity method. A transported passenger, luggage, private accompanying vehicle or animal is payload, not automatically a manufactured or reared production asset; actual transport provisions and attributable handling, safety, cleaning and supplied care remain included.

Include actual propulsion and ship/shore energy, hotel/galley/cold-chain/HVAC/hotwater, laundry/linen/housekeeping, sanitation, recreation/incidental work support, crew, port/terminal/connection responsibilities, empty/repositioned/standby/test/partial/failed duties, repair/drydock/antifouling, full ship/fit-out/infrastructure supply and retirement once. Own operation and provider results require exact stage ownership; a provider never makes an uncovered hotel/galley/shipcare responsibility disappear. Sources give bounded context. Completion, expanded capital and native collection/formula rules are explicit PCR choices, not a universal night, meal, occupancy, fuel, ship-life or package factor.
