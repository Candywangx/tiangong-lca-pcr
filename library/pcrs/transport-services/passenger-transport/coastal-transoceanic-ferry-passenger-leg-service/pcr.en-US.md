# Coastal and transoceanic ferry passenger transport leg service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers actual coastal and transoceanic passenger transportation by ferries,scheduled or non-scheduled,including hydrofoils and hovercraft. Establish the real ferry transport function and actual sea route/waterbody,boarding/alighting ports,service responsibility,quality and period. Neither a fixed distance,salinity,ship nationality,registration,marketing name nor a manufacturer case determines this function or extent. Inland ferry,non-ferry sea passenger carriage,inclusive cruise and sightseeing/excursion fulfilment have different product boundaries; a scenic or overnight ferry leg is not automatically a sightseeing or cruise package.

The official class names accompanying luggage,animals and other items that may be carried at no extra cost; it does not explicitly enumerate vehicles as the inland-ferry note does. Do not turn possible free accompaniment into a universal free-service condition,claim the note expressly names cars,or assume actual vehicle carriage has zero burden. Collect every real carried vehicle/item and person,actual inclusion/contract responsibility and any genuinely distinct output function. A fee or separate price alone does not establish a co-product.

One reference item delivers one actual person through the declared completed coastal or transoceanic ferry boarding-to-alighting leg,with actual OD,ports/calls,duration,quality,accessibility and service inclusions. N is the positive count of actual completed compatible person legs in that same cohort/period. Tickets,bookings,unused rights,seats,capacity,voyages,cabins,vehicles,animals and operating crew are not N. Count each actual vehicle occupant once as a person,each physical carried vehicle/item crossing once in its own ledger. Intermediate calls,temporary reboarding or vessel/crew changes do not automatically add another completed final leg. Rescue/diversion/partial or failed service cannot be retrospectively shortened into successful original fulfilment; actual transfers,original promised endpoints,accepted completion and genuinely independent completed legs need explicit evidence. N=0 has no finite per-person intensity.

Retain attributable loaded,empty/repositioning,manoeuvring,berth/port waiting,weather-related,interrupted/failed,rescue/recovery,test and maintenance duties through actual causal pools. Cover actual coastal/open-sea wind,current,wave/weather and navigation operating modes without a universal voyage or sea-state factor. Collect real corrosion/fouling inspection,cleaning,coating,repair/refit,asset/component replacement and material/species fates. Actual long-route crew rest/relief,food/water,accommodation,sanitation and other necessary ship/shore support require documented owners,inclusions,native quantities and all-user allocation; duration alone does not prove any preset accommodation/meal bundle or employee-food-as-engine-fuel assumption. Separately transported off-duty crew require actual recipient-role evidence.

All actual qualifying vessel architectures remain applicable. Hydrofoils need real displacement/transition/foilborne energy,controls/actuators,foil/strut manufacture,repair/replacement and fates. Hovercraft need real lift-fan/thrust energy,skirt inflation/idle/water/shore approach,fan/skirt wear and replacements; lift is not omitted or fabricated as a pollutant flow. Actual cable/towed or other same-function ferry arrangements need real tow/provider/rope/shore mechanisms,external native energy,wear and lifetime ownership rather than assumed onboard propulsion. The three concrete manufacture cards are conditional actual assemblies,not an exhaustive technology list; each other actual assembly/drive/energy mechanism must be independently expanded with real native identity and coverage. Missing quantities/UUIDs block completed data,not applicability to a qualifying product.

Include actual ship/shore fuel supply,propulsion/lift/hotel/auxiliary power,berths/mooring/ramp/terminal/charging,safety/communications/navigation/support and full manufacture,use,maintenance/refit/dry-dock/replacements/retirement. Actual marine fuel grades/formulations/stock/burn/fates and external AC/DC/shore conversion/storage/loss gates require evidence; generic road Diesel and an internal DC bus do not prove them. Use verified provider-native intensity times actual compatible demand and supported all-user share,then divide by N once,with only exact covered cells replacing owned/uncovered duties. A manufactured craft is not a lifetime service. Keep each actual bilge,black/graywater,ballast/sediment,scrubber/coating/antifoulant,waste and chemical species/known receiver separate. Actual marine/brackish/freshwater/air/soil compartments follow real locations and releases,not classification convenience; water vapour is not collected waste,and used lubricating oil is not bilge mixture.
