# Long-distance bus and coach charter mission service

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This dedicated PCR covers actual chauffeur-driven hired bus and motor-coach passenger charter missions between urban and suburban areas OR over long distances. Preserve both actual official extents; the title does not impose a universal kilometre threshold, two non-contiguous-city endpoints or an international journey. Time/distance billing is general, multiple destinations frequent and a predetermined route/timetable typical rather than absolute requirements. Judge the actual offered and fulfilled charter function, agreed group mission and operator-accountable delivery/control; client-full-control renting with operator except chartering remains a separate function.

One reference is one actual completed qualifying whole group charter mission with declared original contract/service extent, recipients/group and passenger roles, actual itinerary/destinations/stops/return, actual duration and quality/class/accessibility, accountable carrier/commissioner/subcontractors and delivery acceptance. Actual vehicles, concurrent coach duties, driver relief/change, replacement vehicles, internal passenger legs, passenger count and stages are support/activity members, not additional final missions. One order may contain several genuinely independent completed missions, and one mission may require multiple vehicles; neither a party label, fleet order, invoice nor number of buses supplies N.

Prove accepted completion against the original agreed extent and chronological actual amendments, promised-versus-delivered itinerary/duration/quality, interruptions, border/terminal conditions and any independently accepted partial or amended product. Do not retrospectively shrink a failed promise to fabricate a complete mission. Partial/failed/cancelled/no-show/empty or stationary standby duty is not automatically a completed charter from payment or car-hire availability. Only independently evidenced qualifying mission obligations actually fulfilled can enter comparable positive completed-mission N. Retain causal unsuccessful/empty/wait/recovery/support burdens; N=0 has no finite mission intensity.

Preserve the entire actually agreed urban-suburban or long-distance itinerary and all actual operating countries/segments/providers, cross-period preparation, loaded/empty positioning/return/recovery, driver change/rest and required actual waiting/border delays and support. Never clip an accountable mission at a national fuel/statistical boundary or treat a subcontractor/driver change as a new final output. Record actual included cabin/sanitary/food/drink/accommodation/guide/accessibility/safety/HVAC/terminal support at real native amounts and coverage. No every-route toilet/bed/meal/hotel/escort/lift/heater, legal resthour or personnel/labour recipe is assumed; actual included necessary obligations cannot be deleted. Independently fulfilled products require justified subdivision/allocation.

All actual qualifying bus/coach propulsion configurations remain applicable: actual diesel/biofuel/gaseous or liquid fuel/hybrid, battery-electric, hydrogen fuel-cell electric and other real chassis/drive mechanisms require complete native supplied fuel/energy/formulation/phase/gate, stock/consumption/leak/vent/recovery, auxiliary/control and every physical species/receiver/waste record. The actual Irizar medium/long-distance fuel-cell/electric coach is a conditional physical example, not proof of this particular charter contract or universal gas purity/phase, consumption, life or zero lifecycle burden. Water vapour is not automatically collected waste. Missing data/UUID never excludes a real qualifying propulsion.

Distinguish actual local urban/suburban charter64118, ordinary public/special scheduled services, full-client-control rental66011 except chartering, sightseeing final experience64132, standalone booking and independent other modes by real product function and geography/control responsibility. Repeat operation, a school/work group, a timetable-looking plan, attraction destination or billing unit alone does not decide the function. Actual operator-accountable charter delivery and full-client-control availability are not interchangeable. Shared assets and provider results retain every actual user and all manufacturing, use, maintenance/refit/replacement and retirement cells exactly once; no universal factors, occupancy, duration, life or numeric ranges are prescribed.
