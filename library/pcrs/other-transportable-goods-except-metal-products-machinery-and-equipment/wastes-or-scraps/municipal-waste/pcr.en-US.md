# Mixed residential residual garbage — first receipt at North Transfer Station

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This method covers one actual whole mixed residual-garbage stream generated by Seattle single-family households after recycling and food/yard diversion, ending at first documented acceptance on the North Transfer Station tipping floor. It describes physical whole-lot supply/handover; collection/treatment services and later secondary products are not renamed Municipal waste. UN supplies the classification heading only; SPU supplies the residential and named-gate context; EPA supplies engineering options. No current route measurement or automatically lawful/nonhazardous mixture is asserted.

The frozen June 2025 CPC entry (p.205) supplies the heading and adjacent subclasses; the October 2026 explanatory worksheet, CPC3 row 1936, explicitly links 39910 to HS 2022 3825.10. HS Chapter 38 note 4 supports mixed household waste and excludes individually segregated materials/articles, industrial waste, waste pharmaceuticals and clinical waste. Sewage sludge (39920), pharmaceutical waste (39931), other clinical waste (39939), waste organic solvents (39940), chemical-industry waste (39950) and other wastes n.e.c. (39990) remain separate. Commercial, multifamily and self-haul loads, street sweepings and construction/demolition loads are outside this method even where the wider HS definition can include them. The narrower CPC mapping is accepted for this collection scope. [un-cpc3-2025; un-cpc3-2026; wco-hs38-2022]

SPU's 2022 plan Table 7.1 and 2025 FEIS Exhibit 3.11-34 identify North Transfer Station as receiving city-contracted residential garbage alongside commercial and separate organic/self-haul streams. They establish a possible route, not a particular single-family load. Require actual route, origin and load tickets, including contractor and physical handover. The 2020 residential study sampled garbage at South Transfer Station; recycling samples were moved to North for sorting. Neither those composition averages nor a self-haul study measures this North first-receipt lot. November 2026 station notices also direct some recycling visitors to the tipping floor during door work: location alone never proves residual-garbage identity. [spu-plan-2022; spu-feis-2025; spu-composition-2020; spu-features]
