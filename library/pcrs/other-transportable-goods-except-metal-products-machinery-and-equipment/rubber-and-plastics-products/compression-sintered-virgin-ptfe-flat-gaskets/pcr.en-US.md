# Cold-compressed and freely sintered virgin PTFE flat gasket manufacture

Global applicability for all following rule families: This attributional method manufactures one drawing-specific unfilled non-expanded virgin PTFE flat annular gasket at a declared CN site. Begin with the whole purchased conventional homopolymer granular moulding powder, verified for cold compression; receive/store/condition/screen, uniformly charge and cold-press a preform, demould, freely sinter in an electrically heated ventilated batch oven, cool under the actual controlled programme, directly dry-turn/bore/part and deburr the billet into solid one-piece gaskets, inspect/test and uniquely first release. Retain real failed batches, warm-up, idle, start/stop, extraction, cleanup, rework and utilities. Subsequent actual FG storage/packing/handling/dispatch is a separately reported original-cohort service account. [daikin-ptfe-moulding-2019; daikin-ptfe-m17-2016; teadit-ptfe-gaskets-2023; doing-ptfe-oven]
