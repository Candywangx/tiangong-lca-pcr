# Slip-cast glazed vitreous-china washbasin bodies

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This attributional method covers complete manufacture of one declared bare glazed vitreous-china washbasin ceramic body: actual separate incoming clay/kaolin and hard mineral materials; wet body preparation, screening/deferrisation and ageing; plaster or actual identified porous-polymer mould slip casting, garnishing and fettling; drying, on-site preparation/application of the actual glaze, gas-fired high-temperature firing, cooling, required mounting-surface grinding and quality tests through unique first quality release. Include actual inbound transport, re-firing before first release, failed and zero-output runs, start/stop, hot hold, idle, cleanup, rework, mould/asset service and pollution controls. Packing, warehouse, handling, dispatch and returns remain a separately disclosed original-cohort service account.

Exclude fine-fireclay/fireclay and stoneware body routes, resin/mineral-cast and metal/plastic sanitaryware, green bodies or slips sold as the reference product, tiles/tableware/refractories/electrical porcelain, toilets/cisterns/baths, assembled WC units, taps/waste fittings/siphons/brackets/furniture, sanitary accessories, decorative transfers, water-repellent post-coatings, installation, use-phase water/energy/cleaning, durability claims and end-of-life of installed basins. Buying fired basins and packing them cannot replace this manufacturing route. A bought full body slip or complete supplied glaze requires a renewed supply-boundary decision and one whole formulation purchase; it cannot be combined with duplicate constituent purchases or silently suppress on-site preparation.

The BREF sanitaryware route and vitreous-china/plaster-mould example, and the manufacturer EPD covering washbasins, are a transparent technical synthesis, not one SKU, factory or present order. The EPD also includes fireclay, average products, omitted accessories, cut-offs, proxies and a case economic allocation; none becomes a default here. No recipe, kiln temperature/time, yield, basin mass, energy intensity, emission factor, flushing performance, life or compliance result is prescribed. Actual product drawings, supplier composition, route and acceptance evidence remain required. `jrc-ceramic-bref-2007`; `duravit-sanitary-ceramic-2022`

## 8. Foreground Data Collection, Calculation, and Quality Rules
