# Dry-cast unreinforced concrete drainage pipe bodies

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Attributional cradle-to-first-quality-release manufacture of complete normal-weight unreinforced unlined concrete drainage pipe bodies from whole Portland cement, natural fine sand, natural coarse gravel and separately supplied water. Include actual receipt/storage and moisture correction, batching/mixing, clean moulds and actual whole release preparation, vertical core vibration with presshead forming, immediate demoulding, transfer into documented non-heated moisture-retention curing, necessary finishing, actual tests/inspection and unique first acceptance of the whole bare pipe. This is a transparent technical synthesis of independent primary product, machinery and production/QC sources; it does not claim one SKU, factory or contemporary order proves every operation. It prescribes no recipe, wall design, reinforcement design, load/hydrostatic/strength threshold, water/cement ratio, cure duration/temperature, density, machine mass or service life. `hawkeye-masterflex`; `mgcpi-nrcp`; `npca-qcm-2026`

Exclude reinforced/fibre-reinforced/prestressed pipe; structural wall/beam/column/slab or paver/block manufacture; fresh-concrete sale; binder manufacture as the foreground terminal; asbestos/cellulose-fibre cement, ceramic, polymer or steel pipe; lined/coated pipe; installed drainage networks, excavation, installation and claimed in-use performance. The selected bare body has no installed gasket, steel sleeve, insert or separate fitting; integral concrete socket/spigot stays in it only when the real drawing includes it. Verify that drawing and supplied-component register before use. Exclude centrifugal spinning, wet casting, autoclaving and deliberate heated/steam/thermal-oil curing from this selected route. Non-heated curing is conditional on the actual manufacturer/specification and climate records establishing suitability, not an instruction to omit real heat. Chemical curing compounds, admixtures, SCM dosing, bonding agents or other actual inputs require applicability reassessment and individually named whole preparations/materials and real energy/losses before completion. Bought-finished-pipe handling cannot replace manufacture.

Masterflex pp.2-3 connect non-reinforced pipe, core vibration, immediate demoulding and transfer to curing; its p.4 C76-based capacity footnote is not this pipe design or a measured production rate. NPCA §4.6.1 explicitly distinguishes dry-cast/machine-made products from wet-cast stripping requirements. The selected moisture-retention cover/actual water route requires real specification/climate evidence rather than imported numerical handbook prescriptions. Original UN 37560 and neighboring titles support a residual-article boundary assessment; the original contains no pipe-specific inclusion/exclusion note. This is a narrower semantic assignment, not complete-leaf coverage. The classification relationship is governed outside this PCR by the authoritative classification mapping and its decision record.
