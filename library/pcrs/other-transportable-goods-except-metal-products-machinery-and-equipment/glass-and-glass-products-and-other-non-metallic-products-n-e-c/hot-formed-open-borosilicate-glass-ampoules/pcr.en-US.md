# Hot-formed open clear Type I borosilicate glass ampoules

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This narrow route begins with whole purchased unworked clear Type I pharmaceutical borosilicate tubing. Include gas/oxygen-air first-bottom/body/neck/stem hot forming, separation/fire finishing and mechanical scoring, cooling, electric annealing, dimensional/cosmetic/chemical/residual-stress/breakforce-related required tests and unique first quality release of bare unprinted uncoated nonsterile unfilled open Form B ampoules. Packing/storage/handling/dispatch remains recorded separately for the original cohort. Natural gas is a declared narrower route condition requiring actual fuel/burner compatibility; equipment evidence says gas, not its chemistry. Another fuel/heating route, colour/coating/printing/OPC-dot/CBR, closed D, washing/sterilization/depyrogenation/filling/sealing for medicine, or primary melting changes scope and requires renewed applicability and all real exchanges.

These complete primary product/equipment sources form a transparent technical synthesis, not one SKU/factory/order or actual inventory. SCHOTT documents share one publisher. Unworked Danner tubing, food jars, lighting envelopes, pressed household ware and fibre mats do not supply this ampoule conversion method. Catalogue dimensions/pieces per tray, breakforce charts, machine speeds/average rates/kW/mass and unlimited-glass-life marketing provide no defaults. Industrial sources do not establish LCA allocation; ILCD supports only the limited attributional hierarchy below.
