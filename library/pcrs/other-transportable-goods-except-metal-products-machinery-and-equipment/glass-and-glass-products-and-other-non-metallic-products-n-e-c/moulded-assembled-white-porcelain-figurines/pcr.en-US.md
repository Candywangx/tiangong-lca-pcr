# Moulded and assembled white hard-porcelain animal figurines

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This attributional manufacturing method covers one declared design of an all-ceramic white hard-porcelain animal figurine: receipt of actual unfired mineral ingredients or a compatible whole supplied slip; wet body preparation, screening and deferrisation where performed; design-specific plaster master/working moulds; individual part forming and dewatering; wet-slurry assembly, seam retouching and hand details; drying, biscuit firing, actual underglaze manufacturer marking and transparent glaze preparation/application; glost firing, cooling, foot finishing and first complete quality release. Track each actual firing/refiring and its heat source separately, including failed/zero-output runs, start/stop, idle, cleanup, mould drying, extraction and control/asset service. Post-release packaging, storage, handling, returns and dispatch remain explicit original-cohort services.

Exclude tableware/kitchenware, sanitaryware, technical/electrical ceramics, grinding media, tiles, refractory products as outputs, flowerpots/vases, bone china/earthenware/stoneware/red-stoneware, resin/plastic imitation ceramics, metal/lighting/electronic assemblies, attached non-ceramic bases, unfired fragments as final product, cold organic coatings, onglaze/inglaze coloured pictures, decals and precious-metal lustres. Those are not made applicable by sharing a kiln or CPC prefix. Purchasing fired figurines and only wrapping them does not implement this method. Actual extra onglaze decoration requires a method extension with each supplied colour/medium and real additional firing, not a zero entry.

The Meissen white Lion product is primary terminal-product evidence; the manufacturer craft pages establish real raw preparation, multipart assembly, spraying/marking and firing operations. The BREF supplies independent ceramic process and adjacent-route context. This is a transparent technical synthesis, not a measured inventory or verified same-SKU bill of materials of that maker. No page establishes the selected supplier, site utility, ingredient recipe, yield, firing schedule, shrinkage, package composition or unit mass for a future dataset. Historical examples and product-page dimensions/weight are not defaults. `meissen-white-lion`; `meissen-raw-preparation`; `meissen-figurine-assembly`; `meissen-glazing`; `meissen-firing`; `jrc-ceramic-bref-2007`

Each lot/fragment/mould/cycle retains lineage, wet/dry mass, measured losses and a burden vector for every exchange. Internal drained slip, slurry joins, recoverable trims, glaze return and recovered kiln heat cancel exactly once while actual collection/reprocessing energy and emissions remain. A complete purchased kiln/mould is acquired once including embodied lining, supports and initial fluids; these composition subledgers are not additional purchases. Separate asset-component rows apply only to genuinely separate supply or repair/replacement. Keep disjoint parent/child physical masses and inherited burdens. Asset cards report only the observed period service share of their compatible upstream asset burden, separately from full physical receipts/retained stocks; current purchase mass is not automatically expensed to current Q.
