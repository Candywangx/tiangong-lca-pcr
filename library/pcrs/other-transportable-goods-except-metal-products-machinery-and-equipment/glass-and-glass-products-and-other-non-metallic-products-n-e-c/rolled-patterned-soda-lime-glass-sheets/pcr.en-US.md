# Unworked continuously rolled patterned soda-lime glass sheets

This attributional PCR manufactures one declared uncoloured non-wired soda-lime-silicate patterned sheet by continuous double-roll forming. Begin with individually supplied mineral batch ingredients and compatible separately traced cullet; include inbound transport, receipt/storage, weighing/mixing, furnace melting and fining, forehearth conditioning, water-cooled smooth/patterned rollers, ribbon transfer, annealing, inspection, stock-sheet scoring/breaking and trim recovery through unique first quality release. Use an air/natural-gas-fired regenerative furnace with actual electric boost when present. Retain actual failed batches, start/stop, hot hold, idle, roller change, cleanup, cullet recovery, cooling and abatement. Subsequent actual packing, warehouse, handling, dispatch and returned-sheet handling remain separate original-cohort services. This is complete raw-to-sheet manufacture, not bought-sheet repacking. [jrc-glass-bref-2013; pilkington-texture; pilkington-product-guide-2017]

Exclude float/tin-bath forming; ground or polished faces; drawn or blown sheets; table casting; wired glass; body-tinted, opacified or borosilicate compositions; dedicated photovoltaic cover products; tempering or heat strengthening; laminating; coating including online coating or SO2 surface treatment; mirrors; insulating glazing assemblies; drilling, edge polishing or fabrication beyond unworked stock-sheet cutting; installation/use/end of life. A real changed route requires renewed applicability and all actual atomic exchanges, never an assumed zero. A pattern embossed into hot glass is not an added coating. Unworked annealed sheets establish no safety-glazing rating. [jrc-glass-bref-2013; pilkington-texture; un-cpc3-2025]

JRC supplies full batch/melting/roller/annealing context; Pilkington independently establishes the rolled patterned terminal and current production. This is a transparent technical synthesis, not one common SKU, factory, recipe or order. Its 2025 shared-furnace example is evidence that rolled and float lines may share a melting source, not proof of allocation or a carbon saving for this dataset. The older product guide supports roller imprinting only; its adjacent performance/installation products are outside scope. The current ATS158 pattern-specific strength and Warwick counterexample prohibit assuming flat-glass strength from minimum or nominal thickness; no source percentage, obscuration scale, size, temperature, composition, yield, cullet fraction, emission factor, service life or numerical default is adopted. [jrc-glass-bref-2013; pilkington-texture; pilkington-rolled-line-2025; pilkington-product-guide-2017; pilkington-strength-2025]

The reference identity is generic Glass (formed & finished), Product/Mass only, with no fixed CAS, composition, route, grade, thickness, surface, performance or geography. Its native CPC37199 classification is inconsistent with the narrower sheet context and supplies no applicability evidence; the coherent broad physical identity is used only for the explicitly qualified sheet subset. It does not establish provider compatibility, an upstream inventory or finished-processing requirements. A plate-glass candidate fixed to float route and2mm cannot substitute merely because it has a37112 classification.
