# Electrically melted pressed soda-lime glass lamp shades

All rules in this PCR apply only to complete manufacture of bare clear uncoloured soda-lime luminaire glass shades from individual mineral ingredients and compatible cullet: electric melting/fining/conditioning, three-part-mould gob pressing, electric annealing and drawing-based first inspection/release of the final as-moulded rim/flange. Exclude open electric-lamp envelopes, complete lamps/luminaires, jars/tableware, spectacle-glass blanks, ampoules, tubing/sheets/glass fibres and cellular glass; exclude borosilicate/quartz/lead/opal/coloured glass, blowing/spinning, normal fossil-fired melting and grinding/drilling/fire-polishing/acid-etching/sandblasting/coating/strengthening/lamination/assembly routes. Use every restriction with the actual composition/drawing and native identity in sections1–3.

The first-release gate and stock/package/normalization equations below are explicit foreground collection/calculation conventions of this PCR; external documents support only their stated process mechanisms and allocation hierarchy. The only Q is original-cohort newly first-quality-released net dry bare-shade kg; same-cohort closing FG is already in Q. Reconcile manufacture and actually observed FG services in separate physical gates and original-cohort vectors, cancelling internal Q on consolidation. Raw quantities remain undivided; after unit conversion and actual assignment/allocation, only calc_reference performs A_i/Q once. Species/water phases, total mass/elements, gross packages and asset physical ledgers remain independent; no numerical default or automatic credit. [jrc-glass-bref-2013; epa-pressed-glass-1975; lampoptics-pressed-shades; jrc-ilcd-2010]

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This attributional PCR begins with individually supplied sand, soda ash, actual limestone and other specific mineral ingredients and compatible cullet, through batch preparation, electric resistive melting/fining/conditioning, gob shearing, matched three-part metallic-mould pressing/cooling/removal, electric annealing/cooling and drawing-based inspection to unique first quality release of a clear uncoloured soda-lime glass luminaire shade. Select a bare configuration whose finished rim/flange is formed directly in the mould; actual drawing acceptance must establish the final interface without secondary finishing. Retain real campaign-start fuel, lubrication, recovery, cooling/compression/extraction/treatment/maintenance and every nonproductive mode. Observe actual later packing/storage/handling/dispatch/return services separately by original cohort. This is complete manufacture rather than bought-finished-shade packing. [jrc-glass-bref-2013; epa-pressed-glass-1975; lampoptics-pressed-shades]

Exclude37192 open electric-lamp envelopes/parts, complete lamps/light sources/luminaires,37191 wide-mouth jars,37193 pressed tableware bowls,37194 spectacle-glass blanks and37195 ampoules. Also exclude37111 tubing,37112 rolled sheet,37114 cold-worked sheet,37115 toughened sheet,37116 insulating units,37117 cellular-glass blocks,37121 direct roving and37129 nonwoven mat. Exclude borosilicate/quartz/lead/opal/coloured glass; ribbon bulb blowing, hand-blowing/spinning, normal fossil-fired melting, grinding/drilling/fire-polishing/acid-etching/sandblasting/coating/decoration/strengthening/lamination and downstream metal/electrical assembly, installation, use and end of life. Real additional materials/operations require renewed applicability and individually named flows, never hidden zero or other bundles.

JRC raw-batch/electric-melting/lamp-pressing mechanisms, independent EPA press/cooling/annealing/inspection context and Lampoptics real shade offer/electric-furnace/electric-annealing capability form a transparent technical synthesis, not one common SKU, plant, recipe or contemporary order. Lampoptics dimensions/tolerances/material and optional finishing depend on approved drawing/process review; none becomes a universal default. JRC Table2.8 is final oxide guidance; Table3.34 gives historical non-electric ceramic/tube/bulb cases per melted glass and cannot supply this shade purchase recipe, energy, emission, yield or Q. Adjacent mineral-wool and container-water figures supply no shade quantities. No temperature, weight, density, lifetime, performance or safety-certification default is prescribed. Industrial documents establish no LCA allocation; ILCD supports only section7 limited attributional hierarchy. [jrc-glass-bref-2013; epa-pressed-glass-1975; lampoptics-pressed-shades; jrc-ilcd-2010]
