# Powder-bonded chopped E-glass strand mat from purchased assembled roving

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Attributional manufacture of random powder-bonded chopped E-glass strand mat from purchased dried sized assembled untwisted roving. Include receipt/inbound freight, creel withdrawal, cylindrical chopping, random conveyor deposition/forming-air suction, actual wetting, whole saturated thermoplastic polyester powder dosing, LPG-heated drying/thermal bonding, cooling/compaction, trim/slit, winding and tests through unique first quality release. Retain actual utilities/abatement and all failed or nonproductive operation. Actual later packing/storage/handling/dispatch/returns remain original-cohort services. This is complete roving-to-nonwoven conversion, not purchased mat repacking. `jrc-glass-bref-2013`; `gasco-csm`; `lipex-csm`; `jushi-p04-csm`

Exclude foreground glass melting/drawing (link upstream roving), unbonded sliver/roving/yarn/chopped strands sold as such, direct continuous-filament mat, wet-laid tissue/veil, woven/knitted/stitched reinforcement, glass-wool insulation/rigid board, emulsion mat, reactive thermoset binder, resin-impregnated composite parts and downstream moulding/use/end of life. Real onsite primary sizing or binder compounding changes scope and requires each purchased ingredient and real energy/loss; do not replace a whole purchased formulation with assay constituents.

JRC gives roving-to-powder-mat mechanism and adjacent tissue/continuous-mat counterexamples; Gasco independently lists complete forming/oven/cooling/trim/winding/exhaust systems; Lipex establishes LPG thermal utility; Jushi documents a real powder-bonded E6 mat-roll terminal. This is transparent technical synthesis, not one common SKU/recipe/factory/current order. E6 chemistry is supplier-specific. Jushi sizing-content expression is ambiguous; its nominal115-yard/4140-inch/approximately105-metre lengths are compatible after unit conversion but cannot establish actual roll mass or binder addition. LOI includes inherited size and retained binder. Lipex approximate consumption/capacity and compressed-air rate are case data, not totals or defaults. No composition, yield, temperature, strength, compatibility, life, moisture, binder fraction or utility/emission factor is prescribed from these sources. Industrial sources do not support LCA allocation; ILCD supports only the declared attributional hierarchy. `jrc-glass-bref-2013`; `gasco-csm`; `lipex-csm`; `jushi-p04-csm`; `ilcd-attributional-2010`
