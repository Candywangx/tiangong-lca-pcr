# Pressed and sintered alumina chemical crucible bodies

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Applies to nominal95% alumina empty chemical-crucible bodies made from unfired raw feed or a whole unfired granulate through pressing drying debinding oxide sintering required finishing cleaning/tests and first final quality release. The declared route is an uncoated body; verify actual supplier surface phase and chemical/thermal terminal acceptance. This is a technical synthesis with no universal recipe or claim of a complete plant record for one SKU. Morgan supplies the material/terminal example and BREF the oxide-ceramic stages; electroporcelain energy and emissions are not transferred.

Excludes silicate porcelain quartz glass SiC nitride metal crucibles raw reagents chemically loaded equipment cookware ornamental ceramics grinding media electrical assemblies sanitaryware and bought-fired-body packing alone. First-release manufacture and later original-cohort service are separate. These product/method boundaries establish the category independently of the classification leaf.
