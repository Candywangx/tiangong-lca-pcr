# Indoor dry-pressed single-fired glazed polished porcelain floor tiles

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This attributional PCR covers one declared design/grade of complete indoor glazed polished porcelain floor tile manufactured from actual unfired supplied raw materials by wet body preparation, spray granulation, hydraulic discrete-die dry pressing, drying, pre-firing glazing, one roller-hearth firing/cooling and actual specified polishing/rectification/testing through unique first quality release. Include all actual preparation, controls, utilities, asset service, rejects, samples, failed/zero-output runs, startup/stop/idle/hot hold/cleanup and genuine rework. Later packing, storage, handling, delivery and return services retain original cohorts over observed H. This is a transparent technical synthesis of complete primary industrial evidence, not a claim that one contemporary factory or SKU proves every generic operation.

Exclude unglazed or non-polished tiles, outdoor-use equivalence, raw ceramic powder, green bodies, ordinary extruded building bricks, refractory goods, natural/artificial stone, tableware, sanitaryware, dry-route granulation, plastic extrusion, slip casting, double/third firing, and installed floor systems. Reassess applicability for actual post-firing coating/sealing treatments or other omitted process routes; add every real atomic exchange rather than hiding a route in zero or an “other” bucket. Bought-finished-tile packing cannot stand in for complete manufacture.

BREF figure 2.20 and §§2.3.5.1–2.3.5.7 distinguish glazed single firing from double firing and unglazed routes; its polishing example is mainly unglazed, not a prohibition on qualified glazed designs. The historical Sassuolo EPD §§3–4 includes glazed porcelain, actual possible polishing and individual quality sorting, but describes a continuous-precompaction/hydraulic-press variant and a product mix; the discrete-die route is independently supported by BREF. The EPD expired in 2024 and supplies no current certificate, recipe, test pass or transferable mass/area/energy/life/recovery default. Its PE/PET packaging wording conflict requires actual material evidence.
