# Wet-process cemented porcelain suspension insulators

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This attributional method covers a complete single ball-and-socket porcelain suspension insulator made by wet mineral preparation, chamber filter pressing, vacuum pugging/extrusion and green turning; drying, glaze and bonded fired-ceramic sand-band application, firing/cooling, required fired finishing and body electrical tests, purchased-metal cap/ball-pin/cotter assembly with onsite qualified Portland-cement mixing and bituminous interface coating, actual curing and final mechanical/electrical acceptance. Start with real kaolin, plastic clay, quartz and feldspar supplied forms, plus actual separately used alumina and process aids. Purchased glaze and coating are whole supplied preparations. The output includes its porcelain shell, malleable-iron cap, forged-steel pin, stainless cotter, retained cement, coating and actual retained water; separable packaging and external string hardware are excluded from net product mass. [jrc-ceramic-bref-2007; hivolt-porcelain-manufacturing; ppc-td-insulators-rev2015]

Exclude bare porcelain shells or unfinished clay bodies; whole suspension strings and line hardware; glass and polymer-composite insulators; pin/post/long-rod/hollow-bushing terminals; clevis couplings; bronze clips, zinc sacrificial sleeves or RTV overcoating; electronic alumina/aluminium-nitride substrates and metallised ceramics; tableware, sanitaryware, ornamental ceramics and grinding media as saleable products; dry/isostatic-press or slip-cast body routes; onsite metal casting/forging/galvanizing and onsite Portland-cement manufacture; installation, use and end of life. Actual route departures require an applicability reassessment with complete new exchanges, not an assumed zero. Additional actual inputs, repairs, species and outlets within the declared route must always be added atomically.

Evidence is a transparent technical synthesis, not one common SKU or measured contemporary plant. JRC distinguishes electrical-porcelain mineral/wet-extrusion manufacture from electronic oxide ceramics and supplies the full process context. Hivolt documents wet processing through cemented hardware, testing and packing, while its illustrated post-shaped products do not independently prove suspension-unit geometry. PPC independently establishes the suspension unit, sand band, malleable-iron cap, galvanized forged ball bolt, stainless locking cotter, Portland cement, bituminous interface and before/after-assembly tests, with clevis and optional zinc-sleeve counterexamples. No historical recipe range, kiln schedule, density, yield, emissions factor, test rating, colour, service life or brand certification is transferred. Manufacturing first release and actual post-release cohort services are separately recorded and can be consolidated without duplicate output.
