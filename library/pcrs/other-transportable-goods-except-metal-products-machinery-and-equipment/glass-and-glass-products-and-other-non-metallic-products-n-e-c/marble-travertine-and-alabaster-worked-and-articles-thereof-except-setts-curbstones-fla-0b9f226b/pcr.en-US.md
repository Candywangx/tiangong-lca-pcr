# Natural marble countertop panel from supplied whole prepared slab

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Attributional LCA methodology for one-piece natural marble countertop panels produced from a burden-bearing whole prepared slab by real inspection/template layout, wet sawing or abrasive-waterjet cutting, sizing, actual core-drilled holes/CNC cutouts, edge profiling, grinding/polishing, cleaning and inspection through unique first factory quality release. Face finish may already be supplied; record actual face reworking rather than assuming a second face-polishing line. Include actual compatible epoxy filling/reinforcement and aqueous impregnator treatment when performed, and all upstream supplier treatments retained in the slab. This is a transparent technical synthesis of distinct sources, not one factory, SKU or order. The declared endpoint can retain explicitly deferred site cutouts only when fully disclosed and appropriate to that purchased panel; never describe a blank as a completed holed panel.

Excluded: Setts; curbstones; flagstones; tiles; cubes and similar paving/tile articles; coloured granules/chippings/powder; raw quarry block as reference; travertine and alabaster variants not established here; granite/sandstone/slate/other lithology; resin-agglomerated engineered stone; ceramic/porcelain/sintered stone; cement artificial stone; multi-panel countertop assembly; cabinets; attached sink/appliance; installation; whole building/kitchen and use-life performance.
