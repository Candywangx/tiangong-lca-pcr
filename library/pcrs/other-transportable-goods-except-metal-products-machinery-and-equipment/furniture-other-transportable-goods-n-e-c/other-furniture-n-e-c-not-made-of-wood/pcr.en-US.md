# Complete powder-coated steel-slat bed kit manufacture

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Manufacture a complete new non-electrical single-level steel bed kit with steel headboard and footboard, both side rails, all actual crossmembers/centre beams/support legs, the entire steel slat system, joining hardware, fitted caps/pads and actual included assembly instructions. Begin with real supplied uncoated cold-rolled low-carbon welded steel tube and conditional sheet plus complete actual purchased parts/formulations. Include cutting, tube bending, punching/drilling/pressing where real, joint preparation and actual welding/bolted connections, dressing, complete real pretreatment/rinsing/drying, whole thermosetting powder application/reclaim and thermal cure, final fitting/sample assembly, configuration-specific QC and complete-kit first quality release. The terminal is an unassembled kit across all its cartons; it is not a single bare welded frame or support component. Supplier-prefabricated pieces retain their complete upstream fabrication/finishing and replace only real onsite steps. Original-cohort storage/packaging/handling/dispatch and actual later work remain separate incremental services through the stated factory gate.

Exclude mattresses, separate mattress supports/box springs, loose slat bases or furniture parts, bought-finished-bed repacking, wooden/wood-slat or aluminium/brass principal structures, upholstery, bunk/loft/child/medical beds, electrical/adjustable/folding/storage mechanisms, onsite steelmaking and onsite polymer/powder synthesis. Liquid paint, galvanizing, cataphoresis and UV-only curing require a different applicability assessment. Customer assembly, transport beyond the declared gate, use, in-use repair and end of life are outside this manufacture reference.

The method is a qualitative technical synthesis. Zehao establishes a complete all-carbon-steel bed terminal with head/foot boards and continuous steel slats plus in-house cutting/welding/powder coating and flat-pack hardware/manual. Its two images show different slat profiles; neither fixes a universal drawing or recipe. SUNSGOODS supplies a broader manufacturing sequence and contrary wood/aluminium/liquid-paint variants; its cup-fed spray-gun image does not verify electrostatic powder coating. TIGER independently supports formulation-specific thermal-cure/suitability and packaging handling context, not proof that either bed maker uses its product. Medex is an explicit wood-slat counterexample despite a steel outer frame. These are not the same SKU, factory or contemporary order. No source size, thickness, bed mass, load/drop threshold, formulation, film thickness, oven temperature/time, yield, energy intensity, certification or life is a default. UN38149 is residual classification context, not permission to treat every remaining leaf as a canonical product.
