# Complete synthetic human skeleton teaching models: supplied-component assembly

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This record covers a new complete synthetic full-body human skeleton teaching model assembled from separately supplied formed replica subassemblies and finished connecting/support articles. The selected foreground route receives and identifies the actual skull, torso/pelvis, left and right arm/hand subassemblies, thigh replicas and lower-leg/foot subassemblies; fits the actual joints and fasteners; mounts the compatible pelvic-support pole and wheeled base; checks completeness, attachment security, intended demonstration movement and stand stability; and records the first complete-model quality release. Include the configured transparent dust cover and any actually supplied functional instructions. This is manufacture by component assembly, not purchase and repacking of an already complete model. Sources: 3b-stan; 3b-assembly; 3b-base; 3b-pole; 3b-safety.

The 3B Stan product description positively supports synthetic replicas and final hand assembly. Its assembly drawing supports separable components and mounting, but is a consumer setup drawing, not a factory make/buy sheet. These sources support a transparent qualitative assembly methodology; they do not prove one contemporary factory order or a universal bill of materials. Supplier manufacture begins with the actual raw material and covers forming, finishing, drilling, coating and subassembly already completed before receipt, once in the corresponding upstream dataset. Do not copy customer assembly time into factory energy. A10 is the bounded reference example; the adjacent A12/A13 ligament/muscle drawings, hanging stands and miniature models do not expand it. Manufacturer sources are one publisher. UN supplies classification context and ILCD supplies conditional allocation methodology independently.

No PVC, PU, ABS or other resin identity, recipe, alloy, bone/part count, model height, net mass, density, coating/solvent proportion, moulding time/temperature/energy, service life or educational-accuracy certification is prescribed. “Life-size” describes the cited configuration only. Marketing “unbreakable” is not a physical guarantee: the manufacturer safety document warns of fragile parts. Exclude natural human/animal tissue and bone, implants, diagnostic or surgical instruments, toys, ordinary furniture, standalone joints/stands, moulds/prints as the terminal, powered simulators and remanufacturing. The declared function is demonstration only; clinical training context does not make this a clinical device. Use, learner activity and end of life are outside this manufacturing reference.

The selected route does not presume local resin moulding, surface coating, adhesive bonding or wet cleaning. If any is actually performed, keep all physical facts and burdens, identify each actual supplied material/formulation, utility, emission species and waste, and extend this same record’s route evidence and atomic inventory before claiming applicability. Performance rejection or lack of repair authorization cannot erase work that occurred. Ordinary supplier/auxiliary identity gaps require resolution in the dataset; they do not create a new PCR identity.

SOMSO QS 10/E independently confirms an artificial complete teaching skeleton with removable skull cover, flexible joints, detachable limbs, wheeled support and dust cover. This corroborates the product form only: SOMSO-PLAST, its catalogue weight and its hand/foot construction are model-specific and cannot supply the 3B bill, M or factory route. Source: somso-qs10. The UNSD entry supplies only the 38996 title, not a detailed assembly inclusion list. Exclude collectors’ anatomical specimens (38963), festive/novelty articles (38991), shop-window animated displays and tailors’ dummies (38999), and unworked natural bone (39110); these neighbouring titles are boundary counterexamples, not alternative accepted mappings. Source: un-cpc3.

## 8. Foreground Data Collection, Calculation, and Quality Rules

Collect the raw registers below before any division. Physical stocks and inherited environmental vectors are separate quantities. Index each observation by article/species, source, phase, medium, stage, side, cohort, provider and time. Retain numerator unit conversions, allocation decisions and duplicate removal as auditable transformations. H is an observation period, not an extra divisor. No factory inventory values are supplied by this PCR. The stock, custody and normalization equations below are explicit modelling synthesis: manufacturer documents support the physical objects and handling context, not these equations or factory quantities; ILCD supports conditional allocation principles only.
