# Unscented cast paraffin pillar candles: complete manufacture and first release

This method covers only complete unscented cylindrical paraffin candles with cotton wicks and actual colour formulation, formed by mould pouring. All rules inherit that scope, the first-release gate, source limitations and actual-cohort accounting. Glitter/adhesive-coated decorative pillars marked not for burning are excluded. Numeric operating or performance defaults are not supplied.

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Cover free-standing unscented cylindrical pillar candles made from purchased petroleum paraffin, complete supplied cotton wick and the actual supplied candle dye formulation. Include the complete wax body and every necessary installed functional component; the evidenced Still Water construction has no bottom wick tab. Its manufacturer describes hand-poured paraffin, natural cotton wick and colour dye, with different sizes and wick configurations. The producer homepage separately labels glitter/adhesive-coated metallic pillars as not for burning and decorative only; those are expressly excluded, even when described as unscented pillar candles. That establishes a real whole product, not a universal recipe, pure-wax assay, specific raw grade or permission to omit a component actually present. [stillwater-unscented-pillars]

The foreground runs from receipt/issue of these actual materials through weighing, melting and temperature control, actual colour incorporation, wick positioning and mould preparation, pouring, cooling/solidification, demoulding, base/surface/wick trimming, completeness/dimension/appearance inspection and actual wick–wax burn trials, to first whole-candle quality release. Include real top-up pours, failed remelts, cleanup, idle/start-stop, maintenance and rejects. Yide's equipment-maker guide supports these generic casting stages and final trials, while distinguishing dipping and extrusion. Combining these sources is a transparent technical synthesis, not proof that Still Water uses Yide equipment, an IGI grade, a particular heat source or one shared factory BOM. [yide-pillar-production; stillwater-unscented-pillars]

IGI distinguishes paraffin building blocks for pillars from prepared soy/paraffin and container blends. This profile uses separately received petroleum paraffin plus actual dye and a supplied wick; it does not infer a mandatory stearic-acid, microcrystalline-wax or vegetable-wax addition. Whole commercial formulations are purchased once; assay, carrier and active fractions are non-additive subaccounts. A real added ingredient or operation must receive its own atomic exchange and evidenced scope extension before conformance is claimed. [igi-candle-waxes]

Exclude glitter/adhesive-coated decorative pillars marked not for burning, scented candles, essential-oil products, containers/votives/tealights, LED/electric candles, tapers made by dipping or extrusion, bulk fuel wax, firelighters, beeswax/plant-wax candles and unrelated decorative articles. Do not mix those configurations to cover CPC 38999. These eligibility limits never delete actual off-route activity or physical losses. Consumer combustion is outside manufacture; actual factory burn tests remain inside their responsible cohort. No default mass per candle, wick count, size, wax melting point, recipe, process temperature/time, burn duration, energy, waste fraction or fossil/biogenic carbon split is adopted. Marketing statements of clean burning or suitability for sensitive users establish no certification, health or performance guarantee.

The representative evidence variant is Still Water Slate Blue Gray, 3 inch diameter by 4 inch height, one integrated cotton wick and no bottom wick tab. Catalogue geometry and wick count identify the configuration, never net mass, packaging or burn duration. Larger three-wick variants are not substituted for this configuration. [stillwater-unscented-pillars]

UNSD CPC 3.0 (2025-06-30), PDF p.198, explicitly includes candles in 38999. Adjacent 38995 combustible articles/lighter parts, 38996 demonstration models, 38997 imitation jewellery and 38998 matches are excluded; nearby 38991 festive/novelty articles and 38994 lighters are not this complete candle. This is a narrower product method, not full leaf coverage. [unsd-cpc3-notes]
