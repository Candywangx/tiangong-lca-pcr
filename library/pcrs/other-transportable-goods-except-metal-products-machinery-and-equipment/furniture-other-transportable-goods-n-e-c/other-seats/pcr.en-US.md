# Single-shot injection-moulded polypropylene monobloc chair manufacture


## 1. Scope and Applicability


Manufacture a specified complete non-upholstered monobloc chair: structural seat, back, legs and support are one non-cellular unfilled virgin-PP single-shot screw-injection moulding. From actual supplied pellet preparations and actual separate colour masterbatch, include receipt/storage, real conditioning if needed, melt/plastication, injection/fill/pack, cooling, ejection, trimming, identified internal regrind and whole-chair inspection to unique first quality release, including every actual glide/minor insert. Retain installed utilities, asset service, failed/zero-output, start-stop/idle and cleanout. A resin pellet, seat panel or bare frame is not a whole chair. `pativin-monobloc-2026`; `ineos-pp-processing`; `ifc-plastics-2007`


Manufacturer evidence supports the complete one-piece PP chair terminal and whole-chair inspection categories; independent polymer/industrial guidance supplies process context. Vitra provides contrary glass-fibre polyester and rigid-PU route history. This is a transparent technical synthesis, not one common SKU/grade/recipe/factory/contemporary order. Marketing is no certification. No source chair mass, density, recipe, setpoint, time, regrind ratio, yield, strength/load threshold, lifetime, emission factor or energy intensity is a default. `pativin-monobloc-2026`; `vitra-panton-history`; `ineos-pp-processing`


Exclude metal/wood structural frames with PP seat/back panels; upholstered, reclining, swivel, folding, medical or vehicle seats; furniture parts; external recyclate; filled/fibre-reinforced, foamed, multilayer/reactive formulations; thermosets; gas-assisted/multi-shot/blow/rotational/compression moulding and sheet thermoforming; applied coating/printing/in-mould decoration. A real departure needs applicability review and all actual exchanges before use. Actual separately purchased filler/stabiliser needs its own specific supply card and mixing/energy/loss evidence; it cannot hide in pp_feed. `ineos-pp-processing`; `ifc-plastics-2007`; `vitra-panton-history`


CPC3.0:38119 is only classification context: original UN notes place it beside metal/wood-frame seats and give no manufacturing rule. This narrow complete-plastic-chair methodology is not the full residual leaf. Its mass declared unit is not equivalence of seating service, floor/seat area or life. Packaging and observed supply services keep separate accounts with original first-release cohort Q. `un-cpc3-2025`; `ilcd-general-2010`
