# Complete flat-pack MFC panel-end office desk manufacture

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Complete manufacture from externally supplied melamine-faced wood particleboard through actual cutting, nonreactive EVA-based hot-melt application of ABS edge strips, trimming/buffing, connector drilling and specified routing, hardware fitting/sample assembly, final kit completeness and surface/dimensional/joint QC, to first-quality release of one complete unassembled flat-pack panel-end office desk kit. The kit contains one top, its two panel-end legs and modesty panel, the entire specified fastening/connection system, floor-contact/levelling feet, included accessories/tools and assembly instructions. All cartons together supply the kit; carton count is not desk count. No numerical board density, kit mass, dimension, life, strength, formaldehyde limit, recipe, adhesive rate, temperature, energy or yield is prescribed.

Exclude seats, kitchen/storage cabinets, loose furniture components or bare tabletops, metal-frame or powered/sit-stand desks, connected multi-desk systems, substrate or resin/facing-paper manufacture onsite, onsite face lamination, MDF/OSB/plywood, reactive PUR/laser edging, spray-painted/veneered routes, purchased finished-desk repacking, assembled-desk market state, customer assembly, use and end of life. Actual in-route optional parts/processes are individually recorded; a changed principal terminal/substrate/edging route requires a fresh applicability assessment.

This is a transparent technical synthesis of independent panel-processing and complete-office-desk sources, not a representation of one common SKU, plant recipe, contemporary order or verified factory inventory. The material-board and furniture-hardware methods concern upstream supplies or parts; they do not cover this complete kit boundary and release method. The broad native finished-wooden-office-furniture identity is physically compatible only with the complete manufactured kit under these conditions. A candidate identity alone establishes no supplier, scientific, translation, mapping or publication approval.
