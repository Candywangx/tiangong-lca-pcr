# First-release assembly of a nine-specimen natural Mohs mineral collection

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This methodology covers the first preparation, identification and assembly of one fixed nine-specimen natural mineralogical collection from separately supplied specimens. Its reference is the measured mass of complete first-released kits, not nine arbitrary purchases or commodity minerals. The mineral roster is talc, gypsum, calcite, fluorite, apatite, feldspar, quartz, topaz and corundum; each has its own inventory card. Functional identifiers, key, tray and box are part of the delivered collection; detachable transport materials have separate accounts. Sources establish a feasible collection product and care principles, not one documented factory order.

Applicability requires genuine natural specimens curated for identifiable mineralogical collectors interest, with a persistent specimen-to-label-to-kit record. Hardness alone or a teaching/test-kit sales name does not establish that interest. Record each specimen’s species/variety, locality evidence and confidence, acquisition chain, original labels, photograph and collection rationale (for example documented mineral habit or locality representation); preserve unknown provenance explicitly and hold applicability when natural identity or collectors interest cannot be established. The nine-specimen seller supports the roster and container only; the collecting society and specialist dealer support provenance practice; the independent fifteen-specimen boxed product is a distinct market example. None proves one actual nine-specimen collector order, buyer or common production site. [michmin-collecting; italmineral-provenance; britishfossils-collection]

UNSD CPC 3.0 full notes (30 June 2025), PDF page 197, lists 38963 without further subclass explanatory prose. Adjacent 38961 art and 38962 philatelic articles are outside this method. Page 198 lists 38996 demonstrational instruments/apparatus/models unsuitable for other uses: a classroom-only model or test apparatus requires separate classification assessment. Exclude solely hardness-test apparatus, artificial teaching models, cut jewellery, single commodity ores and decorative articles lacking mineralogical collectors interest. Educational use does not itself disqualify a documented genuine collection. These are bounded applicability decisions, not extra UNSD verbatim exclusions. [unsd-cpc3-2025]

The terminal handover is the first complete-kit handover at the assembly-site gate. Actual earlier custody/dispatch remains in the observed service ledger; later returns and services are explicitly additional observations tied to the same original Q_c. Outbound carriage is not silently included: any included freight leg requires its own actual carrier/service row, provider and measured distance/load, without a default distance.

The stock and physical equations below are this methodology’s explicit foreground record design. Source citations provide the stated care or attributional principles; no supplier or museum publication is claimed to contain these collection equations or a measured factory inventory. Raw collection remains un-divided and the unique quotient is applied only after unit conversion and actual attribution.

| calc_release | finished_kit | Q_c = sum of newly first-released complete-kit net masses in cohort c. Each release counted once; current closing FG is already included. | calc_raw | all inventory rows | Convert each un-divided raw reading to its actual extensive row unit; sum integrated amounts once, integrate only rates, preserve signed correction with original record. V_r is not stock-corrected or multiplied by an economic fraction. | cp_make; cp_service; cp_pack | V_r | ilcd-attributional-2010 |
