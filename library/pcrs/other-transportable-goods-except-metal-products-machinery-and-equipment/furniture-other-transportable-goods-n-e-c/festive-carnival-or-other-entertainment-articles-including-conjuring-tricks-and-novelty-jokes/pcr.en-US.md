# Printed paperboard party cones: manufacture and first complete-hat release

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This method covers complete non-pyrotechnic printed paperboard party cones with an open bottom, one glued side seam and an attached elastic chin cord. Purchased coated board enters actual conventional sheetfed offset printing, die cutting and hole punching, cone rolling, emulsion-glue bonding, cord attachment, inspection and first complete-article quality release. The functional article includes its real fasteners and cured glue; a flat printed blank or an unattached cord kit is not this release object.

The scope is a narrower festive-product methodology within the retained CPC38991 identity. Hercule describes both assembled nested cones and flat alternatives; M13 supplies a real printed/diecut card-hat product and separately attached barbed cord; Lion Circle independently describes board hats with attached elastic but flat delivery. Their constructions and machinery support a transparent technical synthesis, not one common SKU, factory or order. Fullabond supports a compatible paperboard side-seam emulsion technology; actual bond suitability is tested. Manufacturer options and typical numbers are not a universal recipe. [hercule-party-cone; m13-party-hat; m13-equipment; lion-party-hat; fuller-side-seam]

Exclude ordinary clothing/headgear, raw paper or board as final product, containers, candles and other38999 articles, fireworks/crackers, foam/textile/plastic hats, unassembled kits, digital/UV/heatset printing, added lamination/foil/glitter/flocking and hot-melt seams. Deviations require an explicit method and inventory extension. Actual off-route, failed or unauthorized work remains in the factual physical and burden records; eligibility cannot delete it. The selected route assumes no boiler or steam plant and no onsite wastewater treatment. Identify actual external treatment and add every actual utility or intervention. No child-safety, food-contact, licence or environmental compliance conclusion is inferred from advertising.

The official 38991 entry provides the class heading but no hat-specific inclusion note. Its application here is a narrower semantic interpretation of festive wearable articles, supported by actual party-hat offerings; the narrower association has been independently accepted. Ordinary headgear (2826), toys (385), pyrotechnics (35460), art/collectibles (3896), wigs (3897), baby carriages (38992), cleaning implements (38993), and candles or artificial flowers (38999) are outside this product. The adjacent 38999 heading also mentions entertainment articles, so do not extend this candidate by that word alone. [un-cpc3-2025]
