# Original hand-painted oil paintings on purchased primed stretched cotton canvas

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This method produces one newly made original physical oil painting on a purchased complete acrylic-primed cotton canvas already stretched on a solid-wood support. It selects direct hand painting with complete Gamblin Artist’s Oil Colors Titanium White and Burnt Umber, used as supplied and mixed only with one another on the palette. No added painting medium, solvent wash, new ground, varnish, decorative outer frame, glazing, backing board or hanging hardware is assumed. The delivered artwork includes the original supplied stretcher, fastenings and any retained tensioning pieces, its primed cotton support and the actual retained paint. Its identified configuration is released only after the declared surface/drying and handling acceptance is actually met. [wn-canvas; wn-priming; gamblin-colors; gamblin-solventfree]

This is a transparent technical synthesis of real material supply and painting methods, not observations of one artist, SKU order or factory. The two colours define a bounded manufacturable palette, not a compulsory aesthetic recipe or colour ratio. Other actual colours, media, preparation or finishing require individually named complete exchanges and compatibility review before an extension is used. A required actual step cannot be removed to fit this variant. Source illustrations supply no inventory quantities, dry-film thickness, density, drying duration, oxidation fraction, yield, lifetime, authentication or safety threshold.

Include receiving and checking the complete support, palette dispensing/mixing, all hand application and trial marks, actual ambient oxidative drying, inspection, cleanup, rejected attempts and real rework, and attributable lighting/ventilation/conditioning. Brush cleaning uses separately purchased refined safflower oil and two separate cotton wiping cloth uses; cotton consumption is recorded by the actual cloth stream. The selected variant collects oily residues and any actual wet cleanup liquid for compatible external management. Actual soap or any additional cleaner must be a new specific supplied-formulation row; it cannot be hidden within water. Ordinary operational requirements, legal compliance and real failures remain in the record. [gamblin-solventfree; gamblin-safflower-sds]

Exclude photographic or printed reproductions, digital images, drawings/pastels/engravings/sculptures as alternative outputs, antique trading/restoration, manufacturing pigments/paint or empty frames as outputs, panel/board-supported and water-miscible/alkyd/acrylic painting, aerosol application, applied varnish and decorative framing. Artistic style, price and aesthetic significance establish neither functional equivalence nor allocation. Touch dryness is not complete cure or a lifetime claim; a real premature handover remains a failed event, not qualifying Q. [wn-drying]

The official CPC 3.0 explanatory notes (2025-06-30, PDF p.197) list 38961 without additional inclusion/exclusion prose. The accepted narrower association covers only newly created original physical hand paintings, not mechanically or photographically reproduced pictures (32540), postcards (32530), stamps/postal collectibles (38962), or antiques/collectors’ pieces (38963). Original engravings/prints/lithographs remain in the broader 38961 title but are outside this oil-painting method; “print” alone does not establish a reproduction. Image reproduction/distribution licences (73320), artistic/restoration services (96320), and non-physical digital images are excluded outputs. A contract combining delivery of a painting and rights/services must identify those deliverables separately; neither a licence nor a service fee becomes kg of artwork. These are applicability distinctions, not invented exclusion text for 38961. [unsd-cpc3-2025]
