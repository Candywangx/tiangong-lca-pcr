# Complete plated brass heart necklace finishing and assembly to first quality release

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This methodology covers a complete non-precious-base-metal necklace with a cast brass layered heart pendant and its supplied O-link chain, connected and clasped as one wearable article. The product anchor is Zhefan JWP0940A in its 18K gold-plated brass option. It is a complete necklace, not a pendant sold alone, a bare casting or a jewellery-making component. The narrow foreground receives cut-off unplated brass heart castings and separately supplied chain/findings, performs dimensional correction, polishing, approved engraving and connection/assembly, obtains decorative surface treatment from the actual external plating provider, and inspects the whole necklace before first quality release. Ordering of engraving, plating and assembly follows the approved project, not a universal sequence. Casting and metal production are carried upstream with each supplied casting; this selected receiving route does not assert an onsite foundry.

Brass describes the pendant substrate, not an assumed alloy for every chain link, clasp, jump ring, bail, extender, logo tag or solder. Record each actual component and layer in a disjoint BOM; retain the supplied alloy, forming state, solder/joint, coating and supplier history. The manufacturer distinguishes pre- and post-plating engraving and external plating responsibility. Its QC page shows different generic and gold-plated underlayer branches: none is a universal SKU recipe. The exact deposited metals, coating formulation and partner batch data must be obtained before constructing a numerical inventory. No colour, marketing plating lifetime, nominal thickness, bath concentration, current, temperature, test duration or certification claim becomes a default or regulatory threshold. Sources support a transparent technical synthesis, not an observed production order or a fabricated full chemical BOM.

Exclude precious-metal-base or precious-metal-clad jewellery, vermeil on silver, loose chains/pendants as the reference output, uncompleted castings, toy ornaments, stone-set variants, other base-metal routes, retail repacking of bought finished necklaces and repair sold as new manufacture. Decorative electrodeposited gold over brass does not make the brass substrate precious-metal jewellery. Actual operations outside the selected route remain in physical/burden records and require additional evidence before an expanded dataset is claimed complete. Post-release storage, packaging, dispatch, returns and incremental repair are retained as original-cohort service over observed H; neither dispatch nor reinspection creates new product output. Use and consumer end of life are outside the manufacturing terminal.

Sources: zhefan-heart; zhefan-qc.

The CPC 3.0 explanatory-notes file (30 June 2025, pp.189, 197–198) lists 38997 without additional subclass prose. This method selects personal adornment, excluding the precious-metal/pearl/precious-stone articles of 38240, festive articles (38991), hair accessories (38994), demonstration-only models (38996), matches (38998) and miscellaneous articles (38999). Schedule B 2024 chapter 71 notes 7, 9(a) and 11 independently distinguish plated base-metal adornment from mechanically precious-metal-clad articles; this supports the material distinction, not a CPC crosswalk or legal classification ruling. The maintained CPC mapping and decision establish the narrower relation. Sources: unsd-cpc3-2025; census-jewellery-2024.

| rule_id | applies_to | rule | source_ids |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a_scope | attributional manufacturing | Use this allocation hierarchy only for attributional manufacturing scenarios requiring allocation; it is not a free choice between attributional and consequential modelling. Manufacturer process descriptions authorize no allocation factor. | ilcd-allocation-2010 |
| a_hierarchy | attributional manufacturing | First separate dedicated activities and use actual or virtual subdivision. For each remaining exchange demonstrate its determining physical causality to real co-functions; different exchanges can require different causal bases. Only a genuinely residual common process uses contemporaneous market values at the same process gate and actual supplied state, with downstream transport/packing/processing removed where necessary. Document justification, uncertainty, sensitivity and conservation across all co-functions; never arbitrarily select mass/economic shares or a fixed ratio. | ilcd-allocation-2010 |
| a_scrap | attributional manufacturing | Internal rework metal and returned client substrates are internal flows. Scrap sales alone do not establish a joint function, reduce physical waste, remove burdens or grant substitution credit. Record true waste ownership, treatment and the actual joint process before any justified multifunctionality attribution. No automatic avoided virgin metal or precious-metal credit is allowed. | ilcd-allocation-2010 |

### Calculation Rules

| rule_id | Applies to | Formula or rule | Inputs | Output | source_ids |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| raw_conversion | all raw records | Convert each original total to its actual extensive unit before attribution. Integrate a genuine measured rate over its recorded interval once; never multiply an already accumulated gas/liquid volume by time. Use independent substance-specific measured density and state for volume-to-mass. Electricity kWh times 3.6 gives MJ once. | raw totals; raw units; state; density; intervals | converted raw totals | ilcd-allocation-2010 |
| stock_vectors | manufacturing burden dimensions | U_j = U_direct_j + B_open_store_j + R_store_j - B_close_store_j for disjoint non-WIP stock partitions. U_direct is directly consumed expenditure or already consumption-net data; R_store contains only receipts into the unused stock partition. Never put a receipt in both. A_j = B_open_WIP_j + U_j - B_close_WIP_j - T_other_j, where T_other is an evidenced transfer to a genuine other cohort/function, not waste sale alone. Closing FG is not subtracted. Apply exchange-specific allocation only to the actual residual shared burden and preserve all failure loads. | disjoint stock vectors; actual consumption status; cohort transfers; allocation evidence | manufacturing numerator A_j | ilcd-allocation-2010 |
| service_vectors | original-cohort service burden dimensions | A_cj carries actual performed service plus true incremental F and attributable asset use for original cohort Qc during observed H, preserving historical vectors and evidenced original-dispatch reversal D_rev. Transfer only unused/unassigned charge or service once. H is an observation horizon and is never another divisor. New repair parts/water stay in F even when retained in closing stock; reinspection creates no output. | Qc; H; historical vectors; F; actual service; D_rev | service numerator A_cj | ilcd-allocation-2010 |
| gross_legs | all gross physical rows | V is the full measured event amount before allocation, stock consumption correction or burden transfer. X removes only an identified identical-content duplicate in OTHER on the same side. One whole-wet account or a non-additive constituent bridge is used. Each real outlet has one provider; mixed-waste origin-W subaccounts do not add another outlet. | raw gross V; event direction; original ownership; same-side duplicate evidence | full physical numerator V | ilcd-allocation-2010 |
| single_quotient | all normalized results | normalized_exchange = A / D once. Manufacturing uses A_j and Q; performed original-cohort service uses A_cj and Qc; gross physical disclosure uses full V and its genuine Q or original Qc. These are non-additive dimensions. Q is first-release net kg and includes released closing FG. If D is zero or unknown retain raw quantities and burden without a unit result. Save this single result; every card and aggregation retrieves it without another division. | A; D; dimension; original cohort; numerator evidence | normalized_exchange | ilcd-allocation-2010 |
