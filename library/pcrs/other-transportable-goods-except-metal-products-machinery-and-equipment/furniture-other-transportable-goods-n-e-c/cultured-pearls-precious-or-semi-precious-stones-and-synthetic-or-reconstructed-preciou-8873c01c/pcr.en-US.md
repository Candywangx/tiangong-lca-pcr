# Natural amethyst unmounted faceted gems

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers whole natural purple quartz amethyst manufactured into clean, unmounted, unstrung, undrilled-for-stringing faceted and polished loose gems at their first quality release. The actual route starts from externally supplied natural rough/crystal or simply trimmed pieces with one complete supplier burden vector; selection, color/inclusion orientation, actual trim sawing/shaping, dopping, coarse/fine faceting, prepolish, water-fed cerium-oxide-coated-film polishing, transfer/removal, cleaning and real grading/tests are included. It is one narrow physically evidenced worked-gem category within the broad CPC 38220, not a PCR for all pearls/stones. `un-cpc3-2025`; `gia-fourpeaks-2004`; `ultratec-v5-2015`; `ultratec-ceralap`

The primary sources are a transparent technical synthesis of a natural-amethyst manufacturing case and actual quartz-compatible equipment/article instructions, not a measured same-SKU/same-factory contemporary order. The natural case also documents acid/oil observation and a deliberately heat-lightened subset with failures; the manual contrasts synthetic spinel and several polish materials. This route selects natural unenhanced amethyst and the positively supported coated-cerium-film branch. Exclude deliberate color heat treatment, dye/irradiation/filling, synthetic growth/reconstruction, other gem species, cabochons/carvings/beads, mere rough crystals, chemical silica, industrial/piezo-electric quartz, architectural stone and mounted jewellery as qualifying reference outputs. Actual out-of-scope work/loads are retained, not erased. `gia-fourpeaks-2004`; `gia-amethyst`; `gia-amethyst-care`
