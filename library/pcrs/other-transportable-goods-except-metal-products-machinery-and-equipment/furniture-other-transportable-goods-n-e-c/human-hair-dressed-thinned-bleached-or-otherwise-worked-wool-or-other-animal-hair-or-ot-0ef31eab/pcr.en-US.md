# Natural-colour hand-knotted human-hair lace wig manufacture

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This method covers a complete natural-colour human-hair wig with a sewn lace cap and hand-knotted human hair, from receipt of unworked cut human hair and supplied cap fabric through sorting/alignment, washing, sun-drying, cap cutting/sewing, ventilation, actual trimming/styling, inspection and unique first overall quality release. Actual fixed straps and clips belong to the complete configuration. Keep subsequent storage, packing, handling, returns and dispatch in a separate cohort service account. [adorable-wig-factory; daniel-wig-manufacture]

Adorable independently identifies ready-to-use full-lace wigs made from raw Indian hair and sorting/washing/sun-drying/ventilating/QC. Daniel Alain independently describes lace inspection, cutting, cap sewing on forms, seam reinforcement, hand ventilation, final wash/dry and complete-wig QC; its bleaching/colouring route is contrasting evidence, not imported into this natural-colour route. This is a transparent technical synthesis, not proof of the same SKU, factory, contemporary order, eight-panel geometry or proprietary recipe. Allure demonstrates that a different worked-hair route can formulate shampoo onsite and glue wefts: neither that recipe nor its weights/settings is adopted. Marketing “chemical-free”, “sanitized” and durability claims establish no zero-chemical inventory, disinfection recipe, ethical certification or service life. [adorable-wig-factory; daniel-wig-manufacture; allure-hair-process]

Exclude raw hair sold as the terminal product, loose dressed hair or hair wefts sold separately, toppers, false beards/eyelashes, animal or synthetic hair, glued skin bases, intentional bleaching/dyeing/perming, machine-weft wig construction, and purchased finished-wig repacking. These route exclusions never erase work that actually occurred: retain its full physical inventory and flag applicability for review. Supplier-prepared hair cannot be relabelled unworked hair; link its actual upstream preparation and disclose a different starting state. Existing lace/net/narrow-fabric methods stop at textile products and do not supply hair attachment or whole-wig release rules. [un-cpc3-2025; adorable-wig-factory; daniel-wig-manufacture]

The cap is limited here to a cut-and-sewn uncoated polyamide 6.6 lace-tulle construction. Banbury documents this substrate as a wigmaking supply; its bespoke department independently documents real-hair hand knotting and customer-specific base specifications. Its raw-hair policy supports cut, root-tip-aligned supply and in-house preparation, but does not prove that its bespoke wigs use this particular lace or the natural-colour route. Actual BOM, purchase state and order records must establish that combination. [banbury-lace-substrate; banbury-bespoke; banbury-hair-state]

UNSD's complete explanatory-notes page 197 lists 38971 and 38972 without additional leaf-specific inclusion/exclusion paragraphs. Washing alone does not move terminal unworked hair out of 38971. This terminal is a manufactured complete wig under 38972. Separate hair nets/headgear (28262), textile yardage (27912 where its formation route qualifies), narrow trims (27911) and combs/clips sold independently (38994) do not become complete wigs. [un-cpc3-2025]

Order-specific fitting, cutting and finishing required for first acceptance remain manufacture. Separately sold later fitting, restyling, re-knotting or repair is a service, with no new wig output: disclose a distinct service result. If it is included in the declared combined boundary, attach its incremental exchanges to the original cohort without duplicating them in both results. External returns preserve gross inward/outward custody and original release identity. [banbury-bespoke]
