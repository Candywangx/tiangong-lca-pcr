# Complete flexible PU foam mattress manufacture from supplied foam blocks

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Applies to complete non-spring flexible PU foam mattresses converted from purchased cured slabstock blocks/sheets through actual cutting/profiling, actual multilayer1K waterborne bonding/drying, complete textile cover cutting/quilting/sewing/fitting, whole assembly/testing and unique first quality release. Representative configuration is a knitted-polyester zipper cover with real thin padding/liner/trims as actually specified. Original-cohort services separately include real compression packing, storage, handling, shipment and returns. The sources form qualitative technical synthesis, not the same factory/SKU/order; each concrete dataset proves actual composition and operations.

Mattress supports, spring/hybrid/latex/coir/inflatable/water mattresses, beds/frames, bare blocks as final product, toppers/cushions/quilts, bought-finished mattress repacking, onsite foam/rebond production, reactive/2K/solvent-adhesive and PVC-laminated tape-edge routes. No use-life service or downstream end of life beyond actual factory waste/return services. Actual onsite combustion or purchased heat/steam needs expanded route with each carrier/return/thermal-state/enthalpy/supplier-water-energy agreement, never an invented boiler.
