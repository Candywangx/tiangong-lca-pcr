# Complete single-seat hand-pushed folding stroller assembly and first quality release

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Manufacture of a new complete single-seat nonmotorized hand-pushed compact folding stroller from received finished folding chassis, manufactured wheel assemblies, seat structure and finished sewn soft goods to complete mechanical fitment, configured functional/safety inspection and unique first whole quality release. The selected configuration has a reclining seat and leg rest, five-point restraint, front swivel and rear wheels, working foot-operated parking brake, handlebar and frame locks, canopy and basket. Include the configured wrist/carry straps and matching supplied rain cover once as part of the declared complete set; weigh them separately and include them in the set net mass. The selected example is the Butterfly 2 UK seat configuration described by its manufacturer; it is not a universal BOM or a factory dataset. A bare frame, loose component kit or purchased finished stroller repacking is not this terminal.

The product original supports recycled-aluminium and bio-based-material use without a complete alloy/polymer recipe; seat/canopy outside fabrics are polyester and polyamide, the inlay is polyester laminated with polyurethane foam, and the basket has polyester sides and polyamide bottom. Record actual approved component versions and supplier composition; do not join dimensions, tyre material, alloy grade, mechanisms or finishes from other models into this BOM. Supplier component manufacture carries its metal/plastic/fabric/coating and processing burdens once. Onsite metal cutting/forming/welding/finishing, polymer moulding, lamination and cutting/sewing are outside this receiving final-assembly route; if actually performed, keep all real flows and obtain route evidence before extending this same record. No exclusion permits deletion or a zero for actual work.

The evidence is a transparent technical synthesis. The full manufacturer-authored manual and product page establish the whole stroller, disassembly and component compatibility, not a factory-specific recipe. Natraj describes actual incoming inspection, frame/soft-goods manufacture, final stroller assembly and finished-product brake/folding/fabric checks at its own facility; its upstream operations are not asserted to occur at this receiving assembly gate or at Bugaboo. Manufacturer certification, catalogue mass, age/load/dimensions, test distances, carbon savings and lifespan are not defaults or approval. The manual contains generic bassinet/car-seat warnings and market-specific cleaning language that differs from the product page; those do not add excluded options or prescribe a factory wash.

Exclude motorized or assisted strollers, doubles, running/jogging variants, bassinet-only or car-seat travel systems, newborn nests, separately optional bumper bars and riding boards, bicycle trailers, wheelchairs, toys, detached parts as reference output, repair/remanufacturing as new production and downstream occupied travel/use/end of life. Actual post-release storage, packaging, handling, returns and permitted or unpermitted incremental factory work within observed horizon H remain in original-cohort service accounts. This foreground is a manufacturing method; only verified compatible upstream coverage makes a complete cradle-to-gate dataset.

Sources: bugaboo-butterfly2-product; bugaboo-butterfly2-manual; natraj-stroller-manufacture.

The classification context is CPC 3.0 38992, “Baby carriages and parts thereof”. The official 30 June 2025 notes (p.197) provide the title but no additional subclass text. Dolls’ carriages, including folding types, belong to 38510 (p.191); invalid carriages are separately listed at 49922 (p.276). This method excludes these adjacent terminals. Parts remain within the statistical leaf but are excluded as this method’s reference output. The maintained CPC mapping and decision establish the narrower association. Source: unsd-cpc3-2025.

## 7. Allocation and Co-product Handling

| rule_id | applies_to | rule | source_ids |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a_attributional | all attributable burden dimensions | Use an explicitly attributional goal. First isolate exclusive operations and directly assigned exchanges; subdivide or virtually subdivide shared assembly, testing, handling and asset services wherever real records allow. Then determine exchange-specific physical causality from the operation that actually determines that input/emission/service. A convenient mass share or arbitrary physical/economic choice is not causality. | ilcd-allocation-2010 |
| a_residual | genuine residual multifunctionality | Only the genuinely inseparable remainder may use common-process-gate market values for the real output states and same observation period, with quantities, prices, geography, sensitivity and reason physical causality cannot resolve it disclosed. Do not impose a fixed share, transport-stage final price, recycled-content credit or avoided-production substitution. No presumed coproduct is created by a failed stroller, packaging return, reusable asset, scrap sale or treatment output. | ilcd-allocation-2010 |
| a_vectors | manufacturing and original-cohort services | Conserve per-exchange inherited vectors across raw/WIP/FG/repair/packaging/asset stores and cohort handoffs. A legitimate other-cohort/function transfer requires physical identity, receiving owner and a justified allocation/handover decision once. Physical outward waste or sale alone never subtracts manufacturing/treatment burdens. Service follows its original Qc and horizon H; do not allocate historical services to unrelated new Q. Physical gross V and packaging six legs remain full before any burden allocation. | ilcd-allocation-2010 |

### Calculation Rules

| rule_id | Applies to | Formula or rule | Inputs | Output | source_ids |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| raw_conversion | all raw records | Convert original raw totals to their actual native extensive unit before allocation. Integrate a true rate over its measured intervals once; keep already integrated amounts intact. Mass from volume requires independently measured substance-specific density and state. Electricity kWh to MJ uses exact factor3.6; transport uses real gross payload and distance with declared empty-return convention. | raw totals; actual units; state; density; measured intervals | converted raw totals | ilcd-allocation-2010 |
| stock_vectors | manufacturing burden dimensions | For each exchange j: U_j = U_direct_j + B_open_store_j + R_store_j - B_close_store_j. U_direct is directly consumed nonstored expenditure or an already consumption-net record; R_store includes only receipts into the disjoint unused/unassigned non-WIP store. No receipt or vector can be in both U_direct and R_store. Apply the store correction once only to that store partition, excluding WIP, FG, packaging and separately assigned asset service. Do not correct an already consumption-net record again. A_j = opening WIP vector_j + U_j - closing WIP vector_j - legitimate T_other_j. T_other exists only for genuinely other cohort/function with justified transfer once, never ordinary waste/scrap sale alone. Closing FG is absent from the subtraction.
