# Single-piece solid polypropylene hair comb manufacture to first quality release

## 1. Scope and Applicability


Manufacture a specified complete non-electric one-piece solid PP hair comb: teeth and backbone form one unfilled non-cellular injection-moulded article for manual hair grooming. Start from actual whole supplied virgin PP pellets and actual separate PP-carrier colour concentrate when used; include receipt/storage, conditional conditioning/drying and mixing, screw plastication, injection/packing, cooling, demoulding, gate/flash trimming, real clean internal regrind, inspection and unique first quality release. Later packaging, warehouse, handling, dispatch and returns remain a linked service account. Buying complete combs and repacking them is outside this manufacturing route.


The manufacturer family/material and manufacturing sources establish PP comb end-use and actual injection/inspection stages; independent PP processing and industrial guidance provide technical unit-operation context. This is a transparent technical synthesis, not one common SKU, formulation, factory or contemporary order. “Single-piece solid uncoated PP” is the expressly selected subset, not a claim that every catalogue comb has that configuration. No advertised heat resistance, hygiene, skin-contact safety, certification, cycle time, density, mass, recipe, lifetime or emission factor becomes a site default.


Exclude ABS, PS, PE, acetal, external recyclate, filled/fibre-reinforced or foamed structural bodies, multi-shot/multi-part assemblies, detachable teeth, folding hinges, brushes, lighters, sprays, hair ornaments, heated/electric tools, metal/wood combs and printed/coated/plated products. Surface decoration in the manufacturer source is an optional other route, not an invisible zero operation here. If such work nevertheless occurred, retain its real burden and disposition and hold affected applicability for review; never delete the actual flow to qualify the product.


All rules, cards and calculations inherit this complete product/route scope and each card’s native restrictions; do not consume them outside that context.

All non-reference inventory cards are specific conditional screening exchanges, not a claim that a plant uses every listed auxiliary or emits every listed species. Confirm actual presence or documented absence; add each actual missing formulation, fuel, waste or species as a separate atomic card. An unmeasured amount is unresolved, never zero. The bare-comb manufacturing gate remains first quality release; post-release services are separately linked and must not be merged into a manufacturing-only result.

CPC 3.0 explicitly lists combs in 38994. Brushes fall under adjacent 38993; the leaf excludes electro-thermic apparatus, and 44816 provides nearby electric domestic-appliance context. Generic plastics headings 36940/36990 do not displace this named comb scope. Other articles sharing 38994 remain excluded from this PCR. `unsd-cpc3-2025`

Dynarex 4883 independently documents a complete adult comb whose main material is PP. This establishes terminal product/material evidence only, not virgin fraction, filler absence, plant route, per-item mass, certification or lifetime. `dynarex-adult-comb-4883`

## 8. Foreground Data Collection, Calculation, and Quality Rules


Collect raw observations without division by Q. The aggregation_rule column labels the final target basis only, not collection arithmetic; calc_units converts original units, calc_attribution establishes real attribution and calc_reference alone applies A_i/Q once. Cards and aggregation reference the same stored result. Observation H is not a second divisor; historical service uses original Qc, and physical V_i is retained separately.

For a measured physical output, returned product, asset replacement or heat-carrier crossing, the inventory quantity uses its full V_i at its actual gate/cohort: set its quantity numerator A_i = V_i. Economic or service-burden partitions apply only to associated environmental vectors and never shrink this exchange. The resulting x_i is V_i divided once by its true Q or original Qc. For purchased inputs keep actual measured consumption and attribution separately visible.
