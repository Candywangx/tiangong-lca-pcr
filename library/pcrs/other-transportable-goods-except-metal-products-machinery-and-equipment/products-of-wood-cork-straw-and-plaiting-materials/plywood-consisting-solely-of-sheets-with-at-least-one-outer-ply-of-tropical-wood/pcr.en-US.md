# Plywood with at least one tropical outer ply — integrated rotary-veneer PF route

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This independently identified category requires at least one **outer** ply of tropical wood. Tropical timber hidden only in the core does not qualify. The representative technology is flat, uncoated, untreated, crossed all-veneer plywood with a meranti-group tropical face and tropical hardwood veneer inner plies, made from received untreated logs using rotary peeling, indirect steam drying/redrying, purchased ready-to-spread aqueous resole phenol-formaldehyde (PF) adhesive, hot pressing, cooling, trimming, grade-required sanding, first quality release and shipment packing. Record the botanical species and supply origin of each ply, including the opposite outer ply. A trade name or growth in a warm country alone is insufficient tropical-species evidence. This represents one subset, not every species, adhesive, core or process in the leaf. [unsd-cpc3-2025; itto-tropical-plywood-2014; asia-plywood-pf]

The chosen plant has grid electricity, electric handling, a clean untreated chip-fired steam boiler without electricity generation, indirect steam coils/press platens, purchased tap water and external wastewater treatment. Meter actual utilities, startup, controls and cleaning; these are applicability conditions, not observations of an existing surveyed mill. Other heat or backup fuels, direct-fired drying, cogeneration, direct water abstraction, on-site effluent treatment, purchased veneers, resin-bearing fuel or different glue formulations need an evidenced route extension before use. Veneer repairs in this route use cut veneer patches and the same recorded PF supply; separate putty, thread, tape or repair adhesives are not silently included.

Exclude panels without a tropical outer ply; bamboo; strip/lumber, particle, fibre or cellular cores; parallel-grain veneer lumber; glulam; film-faced, coated, preservative or fire-retardant treated panels; and uncured, unfinished or unaccepted press assemblies as final products. Official explanatory notes also mention other core constructions despite the subclass title. Those constructions are outside this particular all-veneer route; do not claim that this PCR resolves that broader classification tension. [unsd-cpc3-2025]

Tropical face quality, lathe-check depth and loose/tight-side orientation, extractive-contaminated or overdried surfaces, veneer moisture gradients and PF cure must be examined for the actual species and layup. Establish the declared bond/strength and surface grade by actual applicable tests, including conditioning, shear/wood-failure or delamination results as appropriate. No tropical species is approved solely by this PCR. A construction intended for outdoor use is not automatically certified marine or structural plywood. [wood-handbook-2021; epa-plywood-2002]

The ITTO tropical report informs process and sampling questions. Its tabled UF/MF formulations, UF inventory, source cutoffs and assumed background emissions cannot establish this PF recipe or numerical performance. The manufacturer's tropical PF product demonstrates a commercially described construction, not a representative survey, certification verification or default density/moisture. No recipe, species proportion, press setting, heating value, emission factor, yield or allocation fraction is prescribed here. [itto-tropical-plywood-2014; asia-plywood-pf]

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

### Process Map

| process_id | process_name | inclusion | inclusion_condition | role | quantitative_reference |

Retain distinct raw stage ledgers: log receipt/cleaning → debarking/bucking/peeling/clipping → green/dry/redry veneer → face/core selection and patches → PF layup/prepress/hot cure → cooling/trim/sand → quality release → packed finished stocks/dispatch. Track lathe cores, clean clippings and bark separately from PF-bonded trim/dust. Only clean unglued chipped wood feeds the boiler in this representative route; bark is sold or separately disposed. Full-sheet layups and same-PF veneer patches avoid assuming an unrecorded composer or repair chemical. Additional actual materials invalidate a claim of inventory completeness until split and measured.

The cards describe external exchanges and one reference production output. Stage and rework transfers stay in the physical ledger, with no duplicate normalized panel outputs. Each card is one exchange. Missing measurements are collection requirements, not zero quantities; conditional product-versus-waste destinations need documentary evidence. In the stage constituent accounts, reaction-formed and reaction-consumed terms must be paired to the same evidenced reaction and compatible units; physical state changes and internal transfers do not manufacture conserved material. A combustion mass balance also needs measured incoming oxygen/air humidity and exhaust conditions, not fuel mass alone. Unknown reaction or carrier-air terms remain explicit balance gaps.

For every card, its retained per-1-kg Amount and same-first-release denominator apply to the identified manufacturing cohort, not automatically to all exchanges in the calendar period. Keep gross current physical movements X separate from each cohort's inherited exchange-indexed physical inventory vector V, attributed vector A and original provider vectors U_p, including flow/property/unit, provider/gate, phase/medium and period. Apply stock_cohort and normalize_period before combining views: subsequent repacking, inspection, rework, transport, cleaning and losses belong to the original accepted cohort and use its original Qc in a separately labelled service/continued-cohort view. Inherited manufacture is not another current receipt, manufacturing run or provider charge.

For each steel strap, wood runner and any actual additional atomic packaging component, cp_pack distinguishes purchased provision P (including a component bought within a paid whole consignment without a separate invoice), first non-purchased external entry N_ext (borrowed, leased, entrusted or accompanying goods), and genuine same-item return ER. These entries are mutually exclusive; ER requires the same component's documented previous outward event, owner/custodian, original cohort and complete inherited V/A/U_p evidence. A first borrowed item without a previous outward event is N_ext, not ER or free stock. EO is a separate outward return to supplier/owner. Opening stock and matched internal attachment/detachment/reuse are neither P, N_ext nor ER. Record gross dispatch, return, waste and transport/service legs without netting; packaging never creates panel Q. Missing invoices or free custody do not establish zero burden.
