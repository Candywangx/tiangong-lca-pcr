# Non-tropical non-coniferous-faced all-veneer plywood — integrated aqueous resole PF manufacture

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Applies to flat, uncoated, chemically untreated wood plywood consisting entirely of veneer in crossed layers, with at least one outer ply of non-coniferous wood other than tropical and no tropical wood in any ply. Both outer plies of coniferous wood are excluded. Record the face and back separately; the other outer ply may be non-tropical non-coniferous or coniferous wood. Inner veneers may be coniferous, non-tropical non-coniferous or mixtures of those species, subject to per-ply evidence and bond acceptance. The exclusion of tropical wood from every ply preserves this method’s explicitly accepted narrower scope; it is not a claim that CPC 31413 universally forbids tropical inner veneers. The route remains integrated manufacture from untreated logs through conditioning, rotary peeling or slicing, veneer drying/redrying, aqueous resole phenol-formaldehyde (PF) glue preparation/spreading, crossed layup, hot pressing/curing, cooling, trimming, grade-dependent sanding, acceptance and shipment packaging. Heat comes from on-site natural gas or clean untreated wood chips, water from purchased tap water, and wastewater goes to external treatment. Purchased-veneer assembly, other adhesives, direct water abstraction, purchased steam/heat, resin-waste combustion or on-site wastewater treatment require additional atomic inventory and evidence before applicability can be claimed.

Excludes blockboard/lumber-core, cellular board, particleboard/MDF-core veneered panels, bamboo panels, LVL, parallel-grain structural veneer lumber, glulam, coated/film-faced/preservative-treated/fire-retardant panels and curved finished components. Excludes wet uncured layups, press intermediates and rejected or unfinished panels as the final reference output. This retained methodology identity is limited to the matching CPC 31413 integrated PF all-veneer subset; no full-leaf, all-adhesive, all-core or wet/dry-state coverage is implied. [wood-handbook-2021; epa-plywood-2002; unsd-cpc3-2025]

The official CPC 3.0 hierarchy places 31413 under wood-sheet plywood excluding bamboo and distinguishes the outer plies; its explanatory note describes alternate grain directions and also mentions wider central-core structures. Those wider examples do not expand this PCR beyond all-veneer construction. Use the outer-ply conditions above to keep this category separate from both-coniferous-faced and tropical-faced products. Verify actual species and positions rather than infer botanical class from a trade name or supply region. [unsd-cpc3-2025]

Applicability is established for the actual ply-by-ply species and harvest/supply origin, cutting defects, conditioning, inlet/outlet moisture, drying/redrying and surface history, complete PF formulation and pressing/cure schedule. Test bond strength/durability and wood failure or delamination under the declared grade's acceptance method for the actual layup; retain rejected batches and rework. Pine pitch, tropical oils/acidic extractives and overdried surfaces can affect wetting or cure; no species or mixed layup automatically qualifies. A failed bond cannot be made applicable by silently changing adhesive, coating or chemically treating the panel. Historical tropical observations are counterevidence to automatic species transfer: they do not establish an accepted extension to tropical inner veneers, observed non-tropical-faced factory data, a recipe or universal species approval. [wood-handbook-2021; fao-tropical-timber]

The single broad plywood flow describes crossed glued wood veneers, at-plant gluing/pressing and a building-material semi-finished use. This permits an accepted factory panel that will later enter construction, subject to the cure/cooling/finishing/grade and provider gate checks below. It does not establish that wet uncured work-in-progress is shipment-ready. No supplier/background gate or volume convention is inferred from the flow identity. Finishing after hot pressing is supported by EPA and FAO; the CORRIM softwood case uses a cubic-metre shipment declared unit, not a species-general density. [epa-plywood-2002; fao-plywood-manufacture; corrim-western-plywood-2025]

Both CORRIM report covers show March 2025 submission, April/May revisions and July 22 editing; primary facility surveys are from 2022/2023. They are regional cases of the same survey program, not independent methodology approval. Their softwood-faced panels are outside this PCR's outer-ply target, while purchased veneer and mixed boiler fuel extend beyond its integrated-log/clean-fuel boundary. Regional recipes, densities, energy, yields, emissions and whole-life scenarios are case-check questions only. No numeric recipe, press temperature/time, moisture, density or emission factor is prescribed; matching site/product records provide these values. [corrim-western-plywood-2025; corrim-southern-plywood-2025]

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

### Process Map

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Internal stages: logs → debark/condition → green veneer → dry/redry → dry veneer → PF layup/press/cure → cool/trim/grade-dependent sand/accept → shipped panel. Unglued chips may feed the internal boiler; heat feeds conditioning/dryer/press; bonded trim and captured sanding dust go to external treatment. Record matched lot/state/mass/water/carbon and equal quantities at both ends of every internal transfer, without additional purchased inputs or sold outputs. Preserve boiler ash, rework, stock changes, fugitive and captured outlets.

For every row retain actual species/layup/recipe cohort, state and destination; physical wood/resin/water/carbon ledgers remain in their original mass units even though normalization is by V_net. Changes in species trigger inlet/outlet moisture, conditioning/drying and bond-acceptance checks, plus row-specific provider compatibility, not substitution of regional factors.

The following atomic inventory implements the declared route. Conditional absence needs evidence of non-applicability; unreported values are not zero. Add a specific row and verify its identity for additional chemicals/materials/species discovered in site formulation or equipment surveys; do not fold them into another row.
